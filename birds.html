Bird Emergency Warning Signs

Seek immediate avian veterinary care if your bird shows:

  • Labored breathing, tail bobbing, or open-mouth breathing
  • Blood in droppings, from feathers, or from the beak
  • Extreme lethargy or inability to perch
  • Seizures or loss of balance
  • Egg binding (swollen abdomen, straining in female birds)
  • Trauma or suspected poisoning

Note: Birds hide illness until severely sick. If you notice any changes, consult an avian vet promptly.
