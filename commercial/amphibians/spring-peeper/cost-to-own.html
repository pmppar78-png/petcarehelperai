First-Year Cost Breakdown for Spring Peeper

The first year of Spring Peeper ownership involves significant upfront investment beyond the acquisition cost. Initial setup for a Spring Peeper includes a quality vivarium ($50-$300 depending on size and type), misting system ($30-$150), food and water dishes ($15-$40), and initial supply of species-appropriate food ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for Spring Peeper include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Spring Peeper may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Spring Peeper typically range from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
