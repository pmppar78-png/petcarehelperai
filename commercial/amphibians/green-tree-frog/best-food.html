Life-Stage Feeding Guide for American Green Tree Frog

American green tree frogs eat insects their whole lives, and the prey simply gets a little larger. Froglets take fruit flies and pinhead crickets dusted with calcium at most feedings, plus a multivitamin a couple of times a month. Adults eat small crickets or dubia roaches several times a week, still dusted, and they do not need a fatty waxworm habit. Feed at night when they are hunting. A frog that is round and always sitting in the food dish is overfed. An older frog may eat less often. Offer the same insects, watch the body, and keep the dusting. Gut-load the insects. Wild-caught bugs can carry pesticides.
