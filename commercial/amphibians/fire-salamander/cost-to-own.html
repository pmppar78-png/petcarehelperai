Hidden Costs Most Fire Salamander Owners Overlook

Adult fire salamanders are terrestrial. They need a moist hide, a shallow water dish, cool temperatures often in the 60s Fahrenheit, and a substrate that stays damp without going sour. The repeating costs are earthworms and other soft invertebrates, treated water, and a thermometer you actually read.

A deep swimming pool drowns an adult that cannot climb out. A secure lid matters because they wander. A second tub with damp moss is the quarantine. Heat lamps sized for a desert lizard will cook this animal. The food bill is worms, and the welfare bill is keeping the enclosure cool and clean.
