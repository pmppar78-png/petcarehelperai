What to feed this Blue Green Chromis

Blue-green chromis in the trade are usually Chromis viridis, the green chromis, a reef damsel that schools in the water column. A group needs 30 gallons or more. They eat zooplankton. A small marine pellet and frozen mysis or brine shrimp, fed in the water column, match that. Nori is a tang food. These chromis need the pellet and the mysis. Two meals a day suit a school. They are peaceful and get eaten out of their rations by faster wrasses and damsels.

Watch the school eat. A chromis that hangs at the back, darkens, and shows a hollow belly is the one missing food or the one getting sick. These fish are often lost in groups after a move, with ich and a refusal to eat arriving together. Treat the water and the parasite, and keep offering mysis in small amounts. A heavier dump of pellets will not restart a fish that is flashing.

Chromis are active and they waste food by spitting pellets that are too large. Choose a small pellet. Older blue-green chromis keep eating mysis and small pellets.
