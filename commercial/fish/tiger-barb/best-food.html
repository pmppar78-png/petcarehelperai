What to feed this Tiger Barb

Tiger barbs, Puntigrus tetrazona, are fast, schooling omnivores that reach about 3 inches and need 30 gallons or more. They eat eagerly: flakes, pellets, frozen foods, and greens. Two meals a day, a pinch the school finishes quickly, are the right amount. They will eat past fullness if you keep adding food, and a 30-gallon tank cannot absorb that waste.

The famous fin-nipping gets worse in a small group and when the fish are bored and underfed. Keep six or more and feed them on time. It will not make them safe with long-finned bettas.
