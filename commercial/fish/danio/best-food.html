What to feed

Danios in the hobby are mostly Danio rerio, the zebra danio, and a few close species. They are fast surface and midwater fish from streams, and a school needs about 10 gallons or more with a lid because they jump. Feed a flake or small pellet, plus frozen brine shrimp or daphnia, twice a day. They eat insects in the wild and will take a hatch of fruit flies if you offer one. They finish a meal in seconds. Anything still floating a few minutes later is too much.

Zebras are hardy and easy to overfeed. The tank looks fine until ammonia rises. A danio with a sunken belly in this genus is unusual and means the fish is sick or is the runt of a school that is too small. Keep six or more. A single danio paces and eats poorly.

Leopard danios are a spotted pattern of the same species and eat the same foods. Fry need a fine powder. Adults will eat eggs. A varied flake and brine menu is the whole plan. They do not need a color food, and they do not need a bottom wafer. Older danios eat the same small meals. Slow one down only if the belly starts to bulge, and check the water if a fish clamps or flashes.
