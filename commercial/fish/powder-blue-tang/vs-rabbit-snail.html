Rabbit Snail: Breed Background and Character

Rabbit Snails (Tylomelania species) are unique freshwater snails from the ancient lakes of Sulawesi, Indonesia. Named for their rabbit-like faces with long, droopy "antennae" and wrinkled skin, these large, elongated snails have become increasingly popular in the aquarium hobby. In contrast, Rabbit Snail brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their friendly personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their 20+ gallons build.
