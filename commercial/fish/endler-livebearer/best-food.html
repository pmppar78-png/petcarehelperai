What to feed this Endler Livebearer

Endler's livebearers are smaller than fancy guppies and do well in 5 to 10 gallons as a group. They graze continuously. A small flake, a vegetable-based flake, and frozen brine shrimp, crushed or tiny, fit the mouth. Two small meals a day cover an Endler colony. They will also pick at biofilm and soft algae. A large group still needs the flake and the brine shrimp.

Endlers breed readily. Fry survive in a planted tank and eat the same fine foods. If the colony is exploding, you are feeding more than the adults need. Cut the pinch. A fish with a sunken belly in a tank full of flakes is usually a fry that cannot reach the adult piece, so crush it. A swollen pinecone body is dropsy and needs a water check the same day.

Males stay colorful on ordinary food and clean water. You do not need a separate color pellet. Keep Endlers and fancy guppies from hybridizing if you care about the line. The diet is the same idea either way: small, partly vegetable, twice a day, nothing left to rot.
