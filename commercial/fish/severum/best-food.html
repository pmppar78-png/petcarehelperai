Feeding Guidelines for Severum Cichlid

Severums are omnivorous cichlids. Feed a cichlid pellet, blanched greens or fruit in small amounts, and shrimp or bloodworms a few times a week, once or twice a day. They will eat plants, so do not count on a decorative planting as their salad. Skip feeder goldfish. Remove uneaten food before it fouls the warm water they prefer.

What to Look For

  • Quality protein: A named meat (not "animal protein") as ingredient #1 ensures your Severum Cichlid gets bioavailable amino acids.
  • Right formula for the life stage: Growing, adult, and senior Severum Cichlids have different caloric and nutrient requirements.

What to feed this Severum

Severums, Heros, are large South American cichlids with a more vegetarian bent than an Oscar. Adults need 55 gallons or more and reach 8 inches or so. Feed a cichlid pellet made with vegetable matter, blanched spinach or zucchini, and a smaller share of frozen brine shrimp or bloodworms. A meat-only diet is the wrong emphasis, and a flake meant for a carnivore will not replace the greens.

They eat plants in the tank. If you want the plants, feed the greens first. A severum that shreds every sword and still looks hollow is underfed. One that ignores zucchini and has a tight, swollen belly may be blocked or reacting to a rich frozen food. Ease off the bloodworms. They are calmer feeders than a green terror and will take food from the midwater if it sinks slowly.

Juveniles eat the same foods in smaller pieces, more often. Color bars fade when the fish is stressed or the water is stale. Clean water brings them back more reliably than a new jar of food. Keep the ration measured. Severums are messy once they are large. Remove the zucchini before it breaks apart on the sand.
