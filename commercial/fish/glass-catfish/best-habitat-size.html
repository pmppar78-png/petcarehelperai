Glass Catfish Space Requirements

Best for Small Living Spaces

For Glass Catfishs in small homes, organise the space around three zones: a rest zone (crate or bed, quiet, low traffic), an activity zone (feeding, toys, interactive play), and a transition zone (near the door for exits and returns).

Choosing the Right Aquarium Size for Glass Catfish

The aquarium should be approximately 1.5 to 2 times your Glass Catfish's body length in the primary dimension. Material quality matters: invest in a durable aquarium that will last throughout your Glass Catfish's 7-8 years lifespan rather than replacing cheaper options repeatedly.
