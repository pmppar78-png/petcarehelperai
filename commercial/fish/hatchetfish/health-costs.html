Ask a pet-care question

These unique surface dwellers are the only freshwater fish capable of true powered flight - they can literally fly above the water surface using their large pectoral muscles to escape predators.

Their unusual appearance and interesting behavior make them popular among experienced aquarists, though their specialized care requirements mean they're not ideal for beginners.

They spend virtually all their time at the water's surface, making them perfect for filling the top level of a community tank.
