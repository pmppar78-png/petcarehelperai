Choose Hatchetfish If...

  • The Hatchetfish's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Hatchetfish's than the Guppy's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Hatchetfish's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.

Choose Guppy If...

  • Your weekly schedule reliably absorbs the Guppy's exercise, training, and enrichment minimums — not just on good weeks.
  • The Guppy's social and behavioural baseline lines up with the people, kids, or other pets already in the home.
  • You can plan around the Guppy's known health predispositions without that planning crowding out other priorities.
