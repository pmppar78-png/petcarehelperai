French Angelfish: Breed Background and Character

The French Angelfish is a large (15 in) saltwater species that requires advanced knowledge and a well-established aquarium. With a lifespan of 10-15 years, this semi-aggressive species requires a minimum tank size of 250 gal. These characteristics make French Angelfish a distinctive fish with specific care requirements. Their semi-aggressive nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their 250 gal frame. Prospective owners should note that French Angelfish's expert care level requires consistent attention throughout their 10-15 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.

Flowerhorn Cichlid: Breed Background and Character

The Flowerhorn Cichlid is a man-made hybrid fish developed in Malaysia and Taiwan during the late 1990s. Created by crossing various Central and South American cichlids, Flowerhorns are prized for their vivid colors, distinctive nuchal hump (kok), and interactive personalities. In contrast, Flowerhorn Cichlid brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their highly aggressive personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their 75+ gallons minimum build. The intermediate care demands of Flowerhorn Cichlid span their 10-12 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
