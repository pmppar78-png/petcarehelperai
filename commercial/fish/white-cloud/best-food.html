Feeding Guidelines for White Cloud Mountain Minnow

White cloud mountain minnows take a crushed flake, a micro pellet, and frozen daphnia or baby brine, once or twice a day. They live cooler than tropical tetras, often in the mid sixties to low seventies Fahrenheit, and they still need small portions. A big tropical pellet goes uneaten. Feed what the school clears, and keep the tank from cooking under a strong heater.

What to Look For

  • Quality protein: A named meat (not "animal protein") as ingredient #1 ensures your White Cloud Mountain Minnow gets bioavailable amino acids.
  • Right formula for the life stage: Growing, adult, and senior White Cloud Mountain Minnows have different caloric and nutrient requirements. Match the food to the stage.

White Cloud Mountain Minnow Nutritional Profile

Nutrition for White Cloud Mountain Minnow must account for this species's 10 gal frame and naturally peaceful disposition. Across a lifespan of 5-7 years, dietary consistency directly influences vitality and longevity. White Cloud Mountain Minnow fish with moderate exercise demands need a caloric intake carefully calibrated to prevent both underweight and overweight conditions. A diet rich in animal-based proteins should make up 25-35% of total calories for this species, with fat content adjusted for activity level. Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids are particularly beneficial for White Cloud Mountain Minnow to maintain fin health and coloration.

Expert Feeding Tips for White Cloud Mountain Minnow Owners

Long-time White Cloud Mountain Minnow owners consistently recommend these practices for optimal nutrition. Stick to a fixed feeding schedule—same times daily—because digestive regularity improves nutrient absorption. Introduce any new food gradually over 7-10 days by mixing increasing proportions with the current diet. Avoid feeding human leftovers, which disrupt balanced nutrition and can introduce harmful ingredients. Store dry food in an airtight container away from heat and humidity to preserve nutrient integrity. Weigh food portions with a kitchen scale rather than using a scoop, as volume-based measuring can vary by 20% or more. Keep a monthly weight log and share trends with your aquatic veterinarian at each visit.

What to feed this White Cloud

White cloud mountain minnows, Tanichthys albonubes, are cool-water schoolers from Chinese streams. A group belongs in 10 gallons or more and does not need a heater in a temperate room. They eat small insects and crustaceans. A crushed flake, a micro pellet, and frozen daphnia or baby brine shrimp, twice a day, match the mouth. They are often overfed in unheated tanks where leftovers sit and the filter is doing less biological work.

A white cloud with a hollow belly and a faded stripe is the one missing meals, often because a warmer-water fish in the same tank is faster. These minnows do their best without tropical tankmates. Feed a pinch the school finishes. The red on the fins and the stripe look stronger in clean, cool water than they do from a color flake.

They spawn in plants and the fry take infusoria and then fine powdered food. Adults will pick at eggs. Keep the adult ration small enough that the tank stays clear. A white cloud gasping at the surface in warm water is in the wrong temperature. The gasp comes from water that is too warm for this minnow. Move the temperature down and keep the daphnia on the menu.

Tanichthys albonubes comes from cool hill streams around White Cloud Mountain, and the farmed fish in shops still do best without a tropical heater. Feed a crushed flake in the morning and frozen daphnia in the evening, and keep both meals small because an unheated tank breaks leftover food down slowly. A school of eight shows the behavior better than a pair: they feed in a loose group near the surface and the midwater. The red on the tail and the pale stripe sharpen in clean, cool water over a few weeks of steady meals. Fry hide in moss and need a powdered food the adults are also willing to eat, Put that powder in the moss as well as in the open water. A white cloud that stays with the school and keeps a slight belly curve is eating. One that separates and thins needs the competition reduced or the food crushed finer.
