Health and Lifespan Comparison

Common Pleco has a typical lifespan of 10-15 years, while Platy Fish lives approximately 3-5 years. Health profiles differ significantly between these fish. Common Pleco is predisposed to Ich (White Spot Disease), Malnutrition, Fin Rot, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Platy Fish faces its own health challenges including Ich, Fin Rot, Swim Bladder Issues. Both breeds have about the same number of documented health predispositions, though the specifics and management differ. Insurance considerations differ between the two fish based on these risk profiles. Prospective owners should discuss species-specific health screening with an aquatic veterinarian before making their decision.
