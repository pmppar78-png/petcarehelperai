What to feed

Ember tetras, Hyphessobrycon amandae, are tiny orange schoolers, under an inch, from Brazil. A group needs about 10 gallons of planted water. Give them micro pellets, a crushed flake, and baby brine shrimp or cyclops. Two small meals a day cover an ember school. A standard flake ring is too big, and the embers only get the dust that falls off it.

They are peaceful and easy to miss at feeding time if larger tetras are in the tank. Watch whether the orange school actually reaches the pinch. A fish that fades from orange to a dull wash and looks head-heavy is not eating. Crush the food further or feed the embers on the far side of a plant. They graze a little biofilm. That is a snack.

Fry are extremely small. If you are raising them, they need infusoria or a liquid fry food before baby brine. Adults will not raise them for you. Remove uneaten brine the same day. A 10-gallon tank with a school of embers has almost no margin for a rotten feeding. Older embers eat the same micro foods. There is no larger pellet they grow into.
