DIY Enrichment Ideas for Electric Blue Acara

Electric blue acaras use a cave and a patch of sand. A clay pot on its side and a smooth stone they can hover under are the useful additions. They are cichlids and will dig, so seat any rock so it cannot topple. Skip delicate stem plants in the territory. Feed at the cave mouth so the fish has a reason to come out.
