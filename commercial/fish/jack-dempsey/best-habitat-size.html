Adapting your Jack Dempsey Cichlid's living environment to seasonal changes protects both health and comfort. Summer adjustments for a 55+ gallons (single), 75+ gallons (pair) fish: increase water availability, add cooling surfaces, ensure the aquarium has adequate airflow, and never expose your Jack Dempsey Cichlid to direct sun in enclosed spaces. For Jack Dempsey Cichlid with moderate exercise needs, adjust indoor enrichment to compensate when weather limits outdoor activities. Track how your Jack Dempsey Cichlid responds to seasonal shifts and maintain a seasonal setup checklist for efficient transitions.
