Cichlid Nutritional Profile

Every Jack Dempsey Cichlid has nutritional demands driven by its 55+ gallons (single), 75+ gallons (pair) build, aggressive energy, and expected 10-15 years lifespan.

Life Stage Feeding

Jack Dempsey Cichlid nutritional needs shift meaningfully across life stages. Young Jack Dempsey Cichlids need nutrient-dense food with higher protein and fat to support growth — typically 20-40% more calories per pound than adults. What to feed

Jack Dempseys, Rocio octofasciata, are Central American cichlids that grow to about 8 to 10 inches and need a tank in the 55- to 75-gallon range for an adult. The menu runs from a carnivore or cichlid pellet to meaty frozen food, earthworms, and krill. Two meals a day work for growing fish. Adults can sit at one or two. Skip feeder goldfish.

They feed hard and then sulk in a cave. A pellet that lands in the open may be ignored until dusk. Feed when the fish is out. A Dempsey with a sunken flank and clamped fins is off its food. In this tough species that usually means bad water or a bully. Check both before you change foods. Check the tank before you buy a new jar.

The dark body and the blue spangles look their best on clean water and a steady diet. Overfeeding washes that out with nitrate and a bloated gut. Pull leftover seafood the same day. A Jack Dempsey will also eat small tankmates. Eating small tankmates is predation. The meal you measure out is the pellet and the worm. Give it the pellet and the worm on purpose.
