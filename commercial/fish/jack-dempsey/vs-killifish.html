Both Jack Dempsey Cichlid and Killifish are similarly sized at 55+ gallons (single), 75+ gallons (pair), so recurring costs for food and supplies are comparable between the two species. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (55+ gallons (single), 75+ gallons (pair) vs 5-20+ gallons (species dependent)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with species-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Jack Dempsey Cichlid's 10-15 years expected life and Killifish's 1-5 years (species dependent) expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived fish accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Jack Dempsey Cichlid if your lifestyle accommodates their moderate activity needs, moderate maintenance requirements, and you're prepared for their aggressive temperament. Choose Killifish if you prefer their moderate energy level, can manage moderate maintenance, and appreciate their varies (peaceful to semi-aggressive) personality.
