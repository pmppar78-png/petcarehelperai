Crystal Red Shrimp (CRS): Breed Background and Character

Crystal Red Shrimp (Caridina cantonensis) are stunning ornamental shrimp prized for their striking red and white banded patterns. Developed in Japan through selective breeding of the wild Bee Shrimp, CRS have become icons of the freshwater shrimp hobby. In contrast, Crystal Red Shrimp (CRS) brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their friendly personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their 10+ gallons build. The intermediate to advanced care demands of Crystal Red Shrimp (CRS) span their 1.5-2 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
