Best for Budget-Conscious Frontosa Cichlid Owners

Frontosas are big cichlids that swallow prey whole, and bloat after a fatty diet or a filthy tank is the expensive outcome. Test the water the day the fish stops eating. Structure the food spend around a cichlid pellet and frozen seafood for a frontosa, a few times a week for an adult, sized to a fish that swallows prey whole. An aquatic veterinarian comes after that test, when the numbers are fine and the fish is still off feed. You cannot buy a useful insurance plan for a frontosa. Save for the water change supplies and the fish consult.

The habits that help are dim light, a cave the fish can enter, hard alkaline water checked with a test kit, and a sinking pellet fed when the fish is actually awake.
