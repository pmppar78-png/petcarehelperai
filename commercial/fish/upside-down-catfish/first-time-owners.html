Common Mistakes New Upside-Down Catfish Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding Upside-Down Catfish's moderate exercise needs, moderate maintenance requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Skipping early socialization limits your Upside-Down Catfish's comfort in varied environments. Inconsistent rules and boundaries confuse fish with peaceful nocturnal temperaments.
