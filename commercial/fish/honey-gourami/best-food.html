Best for Transitioning Honey Gourami's Diet

What to feed

Honey gouramis, Trichogaster chuna, are small, peaceful labyrinth fish, around 2 inches, that belong in 10 gallons or more. They eat tiny invertebrates. A small pellet, crushed flake, frozen daphnia, and baby brine shrimp are the right scale. They are less greedy than dwarf gouramis and easier to underfeed in a community of faster fish.

Watch a feeding. A honey gourami that never leaves the plants when the flake hits is missing it. Feed a second, smaller pinch into the cover. Males turn a deep orange when they are in good condition. A pale, hollow fish may be a male out of color or a fish that has stopped eating. Check the belly either way. Females are plainer, but both sexes should have a gently filled belly. A wasted honey gourami can also be sick. Check the water if it clamps and refuses daphnia.

They gulp air at the surface. Leave access. A tight lid should still allow a pocket of air, and the lid stops a jump. Fry need very fine food. Adults do fine on the small pellet and daphnia for years. The daily meal stays the small pellet and the daphnia. Too many of them swell the belly. Pull uneaten food. This species is quiet, and a dirty tank bothers it more than the fish's manners suggest.

Trichogaster chuna stays near 2 inches and feeds slowly enough that a danio or a barb will clear the surface before a honey gourami arrives. Feed once in the open and a second time beside the plants where the honeys actually hang. Frozen daphnia is the food most of them take during the first week, with a micro pellet added once the belly stays rounded. Males in breeding color build a small nest of bubbles at the surface and may ignore a meal while they guard it. Offer the pellet again the next day. The labyrinth organ means the fish still needs to reach the air, so leave a gap under the lid and do not fill the tank to the rim. A honey gourami that eats, shows a warm amber body, and returns to the same plant is settled. One that gasps at the surface for hours and refuses daphnia needs a water test before the ration changes.
