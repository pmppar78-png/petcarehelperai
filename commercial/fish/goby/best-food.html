What to feed this Goby

Gobies are a huge family. The ones on this page range from small sand-sifting species to fish that need 55 gallons. Most take meaty foods. They pick at small invertebrates, and in a tank they need frozen mysis, brine shrimp, and a small pellet placed where that species actually lives. A sand-sifting goby starves in a new tank with no pods and no targeted feeding. A watchman-style goby needs the food at the burrow entrance.

Identify the goby before you trust a generic pinch. A fish that hovers on the sand and picks needs the food to land on the sand. A fish that perches needs the food in the current at perch height. A goby with a hollow belly and a faded color is missing those meals, often because a wrasse takes them. Feed after the lights dim or with a baster.

Many gobies are poor swimmers and will not cross the tank for a flake. Two small targeted meals beat one broadcast feeding. Pull leftovers off the sand. A goby that gasps or shows white spots needs a water and disease check. Food will not treat ich. A goby that eats at the baster, keeps a rounded belly, and returns to its hole is on a workable plan.
