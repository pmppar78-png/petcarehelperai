Fiddler Crab: Breed Background and Character

Fiddler Crabs are charming semi-terrestrial crabs famous for the male's dramatically oversized claw, which they wave in elaborate displays to attract mates and establish dominance. Named because this waving motion resembles playing a fiddle, these crabs are fascinating to observe. These characteristics make Fiddler Crab a distinctive fish with specific care requirements. Their friendly nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their 10+ gallons (paludarium style) frame.
