What to feed

Kuhli loaches, Pangio, are eel-shaped nocturnal fish that stay slim and need a group in about 20 gallons with sand and hiding places. They eat small worms and insect larvae in the leaf litter. Sinking micro pellets, frozen bloodworms, and brine shrimp, fed after dark, are the foods that reach them. A daytime flake feeding never does.

A kuhli with a hollow belly is easy to miss because the body is naturally thin. Look for a fish that gets thinner behind the head and stops joining the evening swarm. That one is losing the food to faster tankmates or is sick. Place a worm in the cave entrance. They can live a decade on the same evening pellets and worms.

They will take a crushed wafer. Whole algae wafers are the wrong size and the wrong idea. Remove uneaten bloodworms in the morning so they do not rot in the sand the loaches are trying to sift. A group that piles together and then scatters to hunt when the food hits is the behavior you want. A single kuhli rarely thrives. Feed the whole kuhli school at the caves.
