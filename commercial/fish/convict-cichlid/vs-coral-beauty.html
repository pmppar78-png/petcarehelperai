Convict Cichlid: Breed Background and Character

The Convict Cichlid, also known as the Zebra Cichlid, is one of the hardiest and most prolific cichlids in the aquarium hobby. Named for their distinctive black vertical stripes resembling prison bars, these fish are perfect for beginners interested in keeping cichlids. These characteristics make Convict Cichlid a distinctive fish with specific care requirements. Their aggressive (especially when breeding) nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their 30+ gallons (single), 40+ gallons (pair) frame. Coral Beauty Angelfish: Breed Background and Character
