What to feed

Ramshorn snails eat soft algae, decaying leaves, and leftover food, and they will browse tender plants when the tank is crowded with snails and short on those foods. A few snails in 5 gallons or more are easy to feed. A population explosion means the tank is being overfed. Cut the extra fish food and the new shells slow down. Cut the fish food and the shells stop appearing on every leaf.

They are useful scavengers and a poor reason to add more wafers. If you keep them on purpose, a small algae wafer or a blanched spinach leaf a couple of times a week is enough for a modest group. Remove the leaf the next day. A ramshorn with a cracked, white shell needs calcium in the water. A snail that smells and falls out of the shell has died.

Fish often eat small ramshorns, so a population can crash when a loach or an assassin arrives. The remaining adults still need the biofilm. Do not pour in extra food to save them or you will bring the boom back. Watch the plants. Holes in soft leaves, with the snails sitting on them, mean the grazing has run ahead of the algae.
