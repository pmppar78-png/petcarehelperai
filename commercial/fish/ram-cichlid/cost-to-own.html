First Year Cost

Best for Budget

Ram cichlids fail in cool, hard, or dirty water. A thermometer and an ammonia test are the first invoice, and they are cheap next to a dead pair. Structure the food spend around small pellets and frozen brine for a ram cichlid, twice a day, in warm clean water so the meals get eaten. Call an aquatic veterinarian when the tank is held warm, the test is clean, and the fish is still off feed. Insurers do not cover a ram. Keep emergency money for heaters, tests, and the consult.

The habits that keep rams alive on a small budget are a heater you verify with a thermometer, small water changes, and a test for ammonia any day a ram turns dark.

Buy fish that are eating, with intact fins and a rounded belly, and keep them in their own 20-gallon tank or with calm tankmates that can handle the heat.

A heater that can hold 82°F in a cool room, a lid to keep that heat in, and a test kit you actually use are the equipment that matters.
