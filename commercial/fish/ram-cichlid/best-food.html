Best for Transitioning Ram Cichlid's Diet

Feed small omnivore foods: crushed pellets, frozen cyclops, baby brine shrimp, and a little vegetable matter.

What to feed

Ram cichlids in the shops are usually German blue rams or a close Mikrogeophagus, small fish that want warm, clean water and a 20-gallon tank for a pair. Crushed pellets, frozen cyclops, baby brine shrimp, and a little vegetable matter suit a ram. They pick food off the sand and from the water column. Two small meals beat one heavy dump that the fish cannot finish.

A ram that fades, clamps, and shows a hollow stomach is the classic failure, and it starts with water as often as with the brand of pellet. Get the fish eating frozen cyclops first. Transition toward a pellet only after the belly rounds. Peas and large wafers are a poor match for a 2-inch mouth. Crush them or skip them.

Ram fry need newly hatched brine shrimp and a powdered fry food. Newly hatched brine shrimp and a powdered fry food at the spawning site work. The adults will compete for it. Remove uneaten brine before it dies in the sand. A ram kept on one food for months loses condition faster than a tetra does. Rotate two frozen items and one small pellet through the week.

Age the aquarium, plant it, and confirm ammonia at 0 ppm and nitrite at 0 ppm for weeks before they arrive.
