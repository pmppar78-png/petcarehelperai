Amano Shrimp: Breed Background and Character

Amano Shrimp (Caridina multidentata), named after legendary aquascaper Takashi Amano who popularized their use, are the gold standard for algae control in planted aquariums. These larger shrimp are voracious algae eaters, capable of consuming problem algae that other species won't touch. In contrast, Amano Shrimp brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their friendly personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their 10+ gallons build. The easy care demands of Amano Shrimp span their 2-3 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
