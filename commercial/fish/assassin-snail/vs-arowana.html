Assassin Snail: Breed Background and Character

Assassin Snails (Clea helena) are the aquarist's secret weapon against pest snail infestations. These striking yellow and brown banded snails are carnivorous, actively hunting and consuming other snails - making them a natural, chemical-free solution for snail population control. These characteristics make Assassin Snail a distinctive fish with specific care requirements. Their friendly nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their 10+ gallons frame. Prospective owners should note that Assassin Snail's easy care level requires consistent attention throughout their 2-3 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.

Arowana: Breed Background and Character

Arowanas are often called "dragon fish" due to their elongated bodies, large metallic scales, and barbels resembling dragon whiskers. These ancient fish are living fossils that have remained largely unchanged for millions of years, making them fascinating additions to large aquariums. In contrast, Arowana brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their predatory, semi-aggressive personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their 250+ gallons minimum build. The expert care demands of Arowana span their 15-20+ years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
