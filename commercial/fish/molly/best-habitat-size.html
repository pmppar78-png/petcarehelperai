Climate and Environment

Best for Climate

Most Mollys do well in the 40–60% relative humidity range, and seasonal humidifiers or dehumidifiers are worth the modest cost in climates that fall outside this band.

Multi Pet Household

For Molly Fish with their peaceful temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact.

Safety Proofing Your

For a 20 gallons fish like Molly Fish, pay special attention to items at their height level that could be pulled down, heavy objects that could fall, and access to countertops or high shelves.

Seasonal Habitat Adjustments

Your Molly Fish's habitat needs shift with the seasons. Never leave Molly Fish in unventilated spaces during heat. Transitional seasons require attention to indoor air quality—spring water quality changes and autumn mold can affect Molly Fish's respiratory health. These seasonal adjustments, while modest in effort, make a measurable difference in your Molly Fish's comfort and health across their 3-5 years lifespan.
