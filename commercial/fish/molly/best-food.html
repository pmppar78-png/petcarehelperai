What to feed this Molly

Mollies, Poecilia, are livebearers that eat algae and small invertebrates. A group needs about 20 gallons, and many types do better with a little salt or at least hard, alkaline water. Feed a flake or pellet with vegetable matter and spirulina, plus frozen brine shrimp. A meat-only carnivore pellet is a bad staple for a fish that grazes.

Sailfin mollies get larger and need the full 20 gallons and then some. They are heavy eaters and heavy polluters. A molly that shimmies in place and clamps its fins is often in the wrong salt level or in bad water. Check those before you change the flake. A pinched belly plus that shimmy means it has also stopped eating. Fix the water, then offer a small spirulina feeding.

Fry eat powdered food and will also graze. Adults outcompete them if the only food is a large floating flake. Crush a portion. Blanched zucchini clipped in the tank gives the grazers a station. Remove it the next day. Older mollies eat the same vegetable flake and brine, in amounts that still leave the belly rounded without a bulge.
