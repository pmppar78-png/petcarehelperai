Hillstream Loach Nutritional Profile

The Hillstream Loach has specific dietary requirements shaped by its 20+ gallons build and peaceful temperament. A diet rich in animal-based proteins should make up 25-35% of total calories for this species, with fat content adjusted for activity level. Best for Weight Management

A Hillstream Loach on a weight-management protocol does well on a formulation with higher protein, higher fibre, and lower calorie density. The protein preserves lean mass during caloric deficit; the fibre extends satiety between meals; the lower calorie density allows feeding a similar volume while reducing intake. Combined with structured portion control, this formulation shifts the Hillstream Loach toward a healthy weight without the frustration of visibly smaller meals.

What to feed this Hillstream Loach

Hillstream loaches such as Sewellia live in fast, oxygen-rich water and scrape biofilm and algae off smooth stone. A tank of 20 gallons or more with strong flow and a mature film on rounded rock is the feeding station. Blanched vegetables, a gel food, and an algae wafer can supplement that film. Flakes that float at the surface mostly miss them.

A new tank with bare rock will not feed a hillstream loach, no matter how often you add a wafer in the open current where other fish grab it. Weight the food under a stone. These loaches have a flat body and a sucker underside, and a sunken belly is easy to miss until the fish stops clinging and tumbles in the flow. That is a feeding failure.

Keep the water cool and moving. A hillstream loach in still, warm water breathes hard and stops grazing. Feed small amounts through the week, and add a little vegetable or gel on the days the film is thin. The wild pattern is continuous scraping, so a tank that grows a little algae on purpose is doing more for them than a sterile aquascape.
