Best for Budget-Conscious Blenny Owners

A lawnmower blenny starves on clean new rock long before it needs a prescription. Test salinity, ammonia, and nitrate, and confirm there is film algae, before you book a vet. Structure the food spend around dried seaweed clips and the film algae on mature rock, which is what a lawnmower blenny actually combs all day. An aquatic veterinarian is appropriate once the water is right and the body is still thinning. Insurance for a blenny does not exist in any practical form. Save cash for the water tests and a fish-experienced consult.

The habits that matter for a blenny are a mature reef with algae to graze, a lid, and a check that it has a hole of its own before you add another bottom fish.
