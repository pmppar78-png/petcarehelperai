Feeding Guidelines for Blenny

A lawnmower blenny, Salarias fasciatus, combs film algae off rock. Feed a grazing clip of dried seaweed and leave mature rock it can rasp, every day. In a new, bare tank it thins and dies while you offer only mysis. Meaty foods can be a side, a few times a week. The daily meal is the film and the seaweed.

Understanding Blenny's Dietary Heritage

The Blenny's evolutionary background directly influences modern dietary needs. As a 20-55+ gallons (species dependent) fish with peaceful to semi-aggressive character traits, Blenny has metabolic patterns shaped by generations of selective development. Owners who understand Blenny's heritage make better nutritional choices because they anticipate requirements rather than reacting to deficiency symptoms. The connection between Blenny's peaceful to semi-aggressive personality and dietary preference is well documented—fish with higher energy temperaments tend to self-regulate intake more effectively, while calmer fish may overeat if portions are uncontrolled.

Best for Transitioning Blenny's Diet

Feed nori and algae-based foods once the rocks are clean, or the fish declines while the rest of the reef still looks fed.

What to feed this Blenny

Many blennies sold as algae eaters, including lawnmower blennies, graze film off rock. The tank size runs from about 20 gallons for a small species to 55 or more for a larger one. Once the rocks are bare, a daily clip of nori and an algae pellet are what keep a grazing blenny from fading. A lawnmower in a bare quarantine tank needs algae sheets or it wastes. Two offerings a day, a clip of seaweed and an algae pellet, match a fish that grazes all day in the wild.

A blenny that gets thin behind the head, loses color, and stops scraping the rock is starving. That happens in new reefs and in tanks that are scrubbed daily. Meaty foods such as mysis can be a side for omnivorous species. The daily food is still the algae and the nori. Know which blenny you have. A fang blenny is a different animal and will bite fish. It still needs to be fed on purpose.

Pull uneaten nori before it decays in a corner. A healthy grazer leaves a clean patch and keeps a thick waist. Ich can show as white spots and scratching on marine fish that are stressed and thin. The food does not treat ich. It does keep the blenny from being an easy target. Older blennies eat the same algae foods. They just need the clip refreshed even when the rocks look fuzzy to you.
