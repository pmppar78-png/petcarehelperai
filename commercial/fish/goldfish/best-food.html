Feeding Guidelines for Goldfish

Feed goldfish a sinking pellet or a gel food, plus a shelled pea or blanched greens, once or twice a day. Give only what they finish in about two minutes. Bread, crackers, and tropical high-protein sticks are a poor diet for a fish that should live for years. Floating flake as the only food leaves them gulping air at the surface. Soak or sink the meal.

What to Look For

  • Balanced macronutrients: Protein, fat, and carbohydrate ratios should suit your Goldfish's size, age, and energy level.
