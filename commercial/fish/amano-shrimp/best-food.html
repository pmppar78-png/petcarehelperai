Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Amano Shrimp

Feeding an Amano Shrimp is not a one-size-fits-all proposition — it changes over their 2-3 year lifespan. Growth-phase diets emphasize protein, fat, and calcium in controlled ratios. Adult diets focus on maintaining lean body mass and steady energy. Senior diets address the declining metabolism and environmental stress that come with age. The common thread: choose quality ingredients at every stage, and adjust portions as your Amano Shrimp's body and activity level change.

Ideal Portion Control for Amano Shrimp

Judge a Amano Shrimp by foraging and by molts that finish, not by a scale weight. Feed what this Amano Shrimp finishes and remove leftovers the same day. A failed molt or a Amano Shrimp that stays hidden needs a water test (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature) before the ration changes.

Best for Weight Management

The extra food that puts weight on a Amano Shrimp is the second helping and the wafer left in the tank. Feed one measured portion this Amano Shrimp finishes, then remove what remains.

Extra wafers and leftover food are what usually overfeed a Amano Shrimp. Change the amount a little at a time. A sharp cut can leave a Amano Shrimp ignoring the feeding spot, and a stalled molt is a health sign rather than a cue to pile on more food.
