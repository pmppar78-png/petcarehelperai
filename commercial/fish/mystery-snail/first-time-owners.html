Local Vet & Care Considerations

Routine veterinary care for First Time Owners — Mystery Snail varies more by region than many owners realize. Standard preventive care tends to range from $180 to $450 a year depending on local pricing, and bundled wellness plans can soften the cost if you commit to a single clinic.
