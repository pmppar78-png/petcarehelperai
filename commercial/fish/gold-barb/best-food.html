What to feed

Gold barbs, Barbodes semifasciolatus, are active omnivores that reach about 3 inches and need 20 gallons or more for a school. Feed a flake or small pellet, a vegetable flake, and frozen daphnia or brine shrimp, twice a day. They eat insects and plant matter in the wild and will graze soft algae in the tank. A school finishes food quickly. What remains is waste.

The gold color and the green bars look best on clean water and a varied flake. A gold barb that stays pale and hollow is losing meals or is sick. These barbs are peaceful for barbs and still outcompete tiny nano fish. Feed the fish you intended to keep. A pinched belly plus clamped fins needs a water check the same day.

They spawn in plants and the fry need a fine food. The adults' diet does not change. Offer the vegetable flake often enough that the menu is not all brine shrimp. A fish stuffed on frozen food trails stringy feces and clouds the water. Alternate the rich days with the plain flake days.
