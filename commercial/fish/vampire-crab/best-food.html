What to feed this Vampire Crab

Vampire crabs, Geosesarma, are small land crabs from forest streams. They need a humid paludarium of about 10 gallons or more, with most of the space as land, leaf litter, and a shallow pool. They eat leaf detritus, biofilm, small pellets, a bit of fruit or vegetable, and the occasional frozen bloodworm or similar animal food. A tank that is all water will not let them feed the way they do in the wild.

Scatter a tiny pinch in the leaf litter at night. Crabs that only get a pile in a dish may ignore it. A crab out in the open all day, pale and slow, is usually too dry or too exposed. Molts happen in a burrow. Leave the crab alone if it seals itself in. A failed molt is a crab dead in a split shell, often when humidity crashed.

They will prey on tiny tankmates. Feed a mixed menu of pellet, vegetable, and a little animal food. Moldy food in the litter has to come out. A vampire crab with a deep color, a quick sidestep, and time spent foraging under leaves is on a workable diet. They drink from the pool, so the water has to stay clean even though they do not swim in it for long.
