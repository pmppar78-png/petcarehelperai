Best for Budget-Conscious Spotted Python Owners

Spotted pythons, Antaresia maculosa, eat frozen-thawed rodents. A hatchling takes pinky mice. An adult takes a mouse or a small rat about as wide as the snake's mid-body, every one to two weeks. Buy prey frozen, thaw it in the refrigerator, and warm it in water before feeding. The enclosure costs are a thermostat, a heat source, a lock, and a hide. A reptile exam is an occasional bill. The rodents are the repeating one.

Weigh a spotted python every few weeks and replace a thermostat that drifts. A snake that refuses a thawed rodent after previously feeding well needs the temperatures checked and a call to a veterinarian who sees reptiles.
