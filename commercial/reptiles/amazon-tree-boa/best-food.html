Amazon Tree Boa Nutritional Profile

Every Amazon Tree Boa has nutritional demands driven by its 5-7 feet build, variable energy, and expected 15-20 years lifespan.

Life Stage Feeding

Amazon tree boas eat in the branches, and the interval stretches as the snake grows. Neonates start on pinky mice or small anoles-sized prey only if you are using frozen-thawed rodents they will accept, offered about every five to seven days. Juveniles take fuzzies and hoppers from a perch. Adults eat an appropriately narrow mouse or rat every ten to fourteen days, still up in the branches, because a bowl on the floor is the wrong place. Thaw prey in the refrigerator and warm it in water. Skip live prey. A senior snake may stretch the interval further. A snake that refuses for a month in the right heat is a weight-and-vet question. A snake that refuses for a week in good heat is often between meals.
