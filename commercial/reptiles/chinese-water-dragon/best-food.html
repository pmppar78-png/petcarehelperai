Common Dietary Sensitivities

Dietary sensitivities affect a notable proportion of reptiles, and Chinese Water Dragon is no exception given the species's association with Snout/Rostral Damage, Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD), Respiratory Infections.

Ideal Portion Control

Use the manufacturer's guidelines as a starting point, then adjust based on your Chinese Water Dragon's body condition — the body should appear healthy and well-filled without visible fat deposits or protruding bones. Split daily food into two meals for adults, three to four for growing Chinese Water Dragons, and keep treats under 10% of total daily calories.

Best for Weight

Chinese Water Dragons on a weight programme benefit from a modest, consistent increase in daily activity rather than dramatic exercise bursts.
