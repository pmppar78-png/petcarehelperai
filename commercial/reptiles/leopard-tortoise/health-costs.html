Building a Vet Fund

Set the vet fund up once and let it work. Target $60 per month automated into a dedicated high-yield savings account. After twenty-four months, the balance typically sits around $1,500 including interest, which absorbs most one-off events for a Leopard Tortoise. After forty-eight months, the balance approaches $3,200, a threshold at which the household effectively self-insures against non-catastrophic veterinary spend.

Pair the fund with even an accident-only insurance policy for catastrophic coverage. The combined monthly cost is typically $80–$120, and the combined financial protection is stronger than either component alone.
