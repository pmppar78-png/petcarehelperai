Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Frilled Dragon (Frilled Neck Lizard)

Frilled dragons eat insects and a salad, closer to a bearded dragon than to a snake. Hatchlings need small, calcium-dusted insects daily or near it, and a little finely chopped green. Juveniles keep the insects frequent and start taking more salad. Adults eat gut-loaded roaches or crickets several times a week plus a daily leafy salad, with fruit rare. UVB makes the calcium usable. A frilled that eats only insects and no greens, or the reverse, gets into trouble. Seniors may slow down. Offer the same foods, watch the body, and have a reptile vet look at a jaw that feels soft. Fireflies are toxic. Skip them.
