Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Ball Python

Ball pythons change how often they eat, and the prey stays a whole rodent about as wide as the mid-body. Hatchlings usually take a pinky or fuzzy every five to seven days. Juveniles move to hoppers and small mice on a similar rhythm. Adults commonly eat a small rat every ten to fourteen days, sometimes longer if the body condition is already solid. Thaw in the refrigerator. Warm in water. Never leave a live rodent in the cage. A breeding female needs a closer eye on weight. An older python that skips a meal in correct heat is often fine. One that loses weight, wheezes, or has stuck eye caps needs a reptile vet and a look at the temperatures, before anyone force-feeds.
