Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Uromastyx

Uromastyx are herbivores from baby to old age. Hatchlings eat finely chopped greens, lentils or a few seeds, and edible flowers, with calcium. Adults eat a big daily salad of dark leafy greens, squash, a few seeds, and flowers, and they bask hot to digest it. Animal protein is a mistake at every stage. Leave the insects to other lizards. This one eats plants. Fruit should be rare. A juvenile that is growing needs the same plant foods, offered where it can reach them after basking. An older uromastyx may eat more slowly and still needs the heat and the greens. A soft jaw means the UVB or the calcium failed. See a reptile vet. Do not add meat to fix it.
