Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Bull Snake (Gopher Snake)

Bull snakes are rodent eaters with a big appetite and a long body, and they should not be fed like a pet that gets a rat whenever it looks hungry. Hatchlings start on pinkies or fuzzies every five to seven days. Juveniles take hoppers and small mice weekly. Adults eat a mouse or a small rat every seven to fourteen days, sized to the mid-body. Overfeeding makes a heavy snake that still acts starved. Thaw prey properly. Use frozen-thawed. A winter cool-down, if you brumate at all, changes the feeding pause and should be planned with someone who knows colubrids. An old bull snake eats the same prey a little less often. Track weight. A skinny, active snake is underfed. A sausage-shaped one is overfed.
