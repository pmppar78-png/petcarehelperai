Mourning Gecko Space Requirements

Best for Small

Mourning Geckos adapt to small living spaces when the environment provides stable conditions and species-appropriate enrichment, not based on square footage alone.

Choosing the Right

The terrarium should be approximately 1.5 to 2 times your Mourning Gecko's body length in the primary dimension. Material quality matters: invest in a durable terrarium that will last throughout your Mourning Gecko's 10-15 years lifespan rather than replacing cheaper options repeatedly.

Multi Pet Household

For Mourning Gecko with their docile, communal, parthenogenic temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact.

Safety Proofing Your

For Mourning Gecko at 3-4 inches (7-10 cm) size, check for gaps or spaces where they could become trapped or escape. Regular safety audits of your Mourning Gecko's environment every few months catch new hazards as household items and arrangements change over time.

Seasonal Habitat Adjustments

Adapting your Mourning Gecko's living environment to seasonal changes protects both health and comfort. Summer adjustments for a 3-4 inches (7-10 cm) reptile: increase water availability, add cooling surfaces, ensure the terrarium has adequate airflow, and never expose your Mourning Gecko to direct sun in enclosed spaces. Seasonal parasite prevention affects habitat management too—mite and parasite concernss may require more frequent cleaning of your Mourning Gecko's terrarium and resting areas.
