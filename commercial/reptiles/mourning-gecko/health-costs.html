Building a Vet Fund

Building a vet fund for a Mourning Gecko is a discipline problem disguised as a savings problem. The savings math is simple: $60 per month for three years produces a $2,160 reserve, enough to absorb most non-catastrophic events. The discipline is harder: keeping the fund untouched during routine financial pressure, replenishing it after unavoidable drawdowns, and resisting the temptation to cancel the auto-transfer during lean months.
