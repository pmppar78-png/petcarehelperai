Common Mistakes New Musk Turtle Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding Musk Turtle's moderate exercise needs, moderate grooming requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; Musk Turtle reptiles at Small (3-5 in) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding. Skipping early socialization limits your Musk Turtle's comfort in varied environments. Inconsistent rules and boundaries confuse reptiles with hardy temperaments.
