Common Mistakes New Greek Tortoise Owners Make

First-time Greek Tortoise owners frequently make avoidable errors that impact their reptile's wellbeing. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; Greek Tortoise reptiles at Small-Medium (5-8 in) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding.
