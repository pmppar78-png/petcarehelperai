First-Year Cost Breakdown for Giant Day Gecko

Year one with a Giant Day Gecko carries the highest costs due to one-time setup expenses. Budget for a properly sized terrarium ($50-$300), essential heat lamp and UVB light ($30-$150), food and water provisions ($15-$40), and a quality initial food supply ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for Giant Day Gecko include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Giant Day Gecko may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Giant Day Gecko typically range from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
