Best for Budget-Conscious Leachianus Gecko Owners

Leachianus geckos are large, long-lived, and the bills are egg binding, mouth rot, and a calcium problem that shows up as a soft jaw. Structure the food spend around a powdered leachianus or crested gecko diet mixed fresh, replaced daily, with insects as a smaller side than they are for a crested gecko. Book a reptile veterinarian through the ARAV directory for a mouth check before the gecko is a decade old and huge. See if Nationwide's avian and exotic plan covers a leachianus in your state, and keep a fund for that dental-style abscess care.
