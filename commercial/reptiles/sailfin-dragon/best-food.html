Best for Weight Management

Weight management for Sailfin Dragon is a calorie accounting problem. Most overweight Sailfin Dragons receive the right-looking portion plus the un-tracked calories from treats, chews, table scraps, and training rewards. A weight-management formula with L-carnitine and elevated fibre helps satiety, but it does not fix the accounting. Measure daily food by gram rather than scoop, count treat calories into the daily total, and restrict treats to 10% of daily intake.

Set a target weight with the veterinarian and reassess monthly. Weight loss of roughly 1% of body weight per week is safe and sustainable; faster loss risks lean-mass depletion, particularly for adult and senior Sailfin Dragons. Re-measure body condition score at each monthly check-in, because weight alone can mislead when lean mass is shifting alongside fat.
