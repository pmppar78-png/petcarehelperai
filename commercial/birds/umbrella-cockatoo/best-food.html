Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Umbrella Cockatoo

Umbrella cockatoos gain weight easily and will trade vegetables for noodles if you let them. Start weaned birds on a large-parrot pellet and a vegetable chop. Keep nuts and seed as earned bites. Adults that scream for extras are often already heavy. An older umbrella may develop arthritis and move less, so the calorie need drops while the pellet-and-vegetable pattern stays. Soften foods if the beak is long, and have a vet trim that beak. Do not retire the bird onto sunflower seed. Skip avocado and chocolate. A kitchen scale, used monthly, is the feeding tool that matters.
