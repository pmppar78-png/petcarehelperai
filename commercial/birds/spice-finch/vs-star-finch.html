Choose Spice Finch If...

  • You already enjoy the kind of human-bird interaction style the Spice Finch is known for — the Star Finch's style would feel like a stretch.
  • The Spice Finch's long-term health outlook is one you can support with consistent preventive care and appropriate insurance.

Choose Star Finch If...

  • The Star Finch's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Star Finch's than the Spice Finch's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Star Finch's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.
