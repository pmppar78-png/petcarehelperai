Store cleaning chemicals, medications, and small ingestible objects out of reach. Cover or redirect electrical cords that a curious Spice Finch might investigate. Install appropriate barriers to prevent access to dangerous areas like balconies, pools, or garages. For Spice Finch at 24x12x18 inches minimum (flight cage preferred) size, check for gaps or spaces where they could become trapped or escape. Spice Finch's cage setup requires seasonal modifications to maintain optimal comfort and safety year-round.
