First Year Cost

Best for Budget

Nandays get expensive when sunflower seed becomes the whole diet, or when a broken blood feather will not stop bleeding. Structure the food spend around a pellet and vegetables for a nanday, with sunflower and safflower measured out as treats so the bird stays lean. Line up an avian veterinarian via the AAV directory before the first molt crisis. Look up whether Nationwide's avian and exotic plan enrolls nandays where you live, and keep a fund for the avian emergency exam.
