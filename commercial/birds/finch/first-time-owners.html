Common Mistakes New Finch Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding Finch's moderate exercise needs, moderate grooming requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; Finch birds at Tiny (3-6 inches, 10-30 grams) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding. Skipping early socialization limits your Finch's comfort in varied environments.
