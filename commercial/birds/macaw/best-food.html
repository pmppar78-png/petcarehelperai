Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Macaw

Macaws, as a group, wean from formula to a pelleted diet and vegetables, and the nut bowl is the thing owners overdo for the next few decades. Feed a size-appropriate macaw pellet, a daily vegetable chop, and nuts only as training rewards. Juveniles can have a bit more food while they grow. Adults in a cage need fewer nuts than a bird flying outdoors. Seniors often need softer produce and a calorie cut if they stop climbing. Palm fruit is a natural food for some species. Filling the bowl with fat is still a bad idea. Weigh the bird. Avocado stays out.
