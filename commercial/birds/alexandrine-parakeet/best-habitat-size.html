Alexandrine Parakeet Space Requirements

Best for Small

For Alexandrine Parakeets in small homes, organise the space around three zones: a rest zone (crate or bed, quiet, low traffic), an activity zone (feeding, toys, interactive play), and a transition zone (near the door for exits and returns).

Choosing the Right

The cage should be approximately 1.5 to 2 times your Alexandrine Parakeet's body length in the primary dimension. Material quality matters: invest in a durable cage that will last throughout your Alexandrine Parakeet's 25-30 years lifespan rather than replacing cheaper options repeatedly.

Adapting your Alexandrine Parakeet's living environment to seasonal changes protects both health and comfort. Summer adjustments for a 8-10 oz bird: increase water availability, add cooling surfaces, ensure the cage has adequate airflow, and never expose your Alexandrine Parakeet to direct sun in enclosed spaces.
