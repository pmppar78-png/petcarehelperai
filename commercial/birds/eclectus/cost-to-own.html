First-Year Cost Breakdown for Eclectus Parrot

The first year of Eclectus Parrot ownership involves significant upfront investment beyond the acquisition cost. Initial setup for a Eclectus Parrot includes a quality cage ($50-$300 depending on size and type), perches and toys ($30-$150), food and water dishes ($15-$40), and initial supply of species-appropriate food ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for Eclectus Parrot include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Eclectus Parrot may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Eclectus Parrot typically range from $1,800 to $4,500, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
