Climate and Environment Factors for Gouldian Finch

Best for Climate Control

Most Gouldian Finchs do well in the 40–60% relative humidity range, and seasonal humidifiers or dehumidifiers are worth the modest cost in climates that fall outside this band.

Seasonal Habitat Adjustments for Gouldian Finch

Your Gouldian Finch's habitat needs shift with the seasons. In warmer months, a 24x12x18 inches minimum (flight cage preferred) bird needs cooling options: frozen treats, cooling mats, and increased air circulation around the cage. Transitional seasons require attention to indoor air quality—spring allergens and autumn mold can affect Gouldian Finch's respiratory health. These seasonal adjustments, while modest in effort, make a measurable difference in your Gouldian Finch's comfort and health across their 6-8 years lifespan.
