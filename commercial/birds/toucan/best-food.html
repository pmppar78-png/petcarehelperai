Common Dietary Sensitivities in Toucan

Some Toucans develop food sensitivities that show up as persistent itching, ear infections, loose stools, or vomiting after meals.

Signs Your Toucan Is Thriving on Their Diet

You will know your Toucan's diet is working when you see steady energy levels, a plumage with a healthy sheen, firm and regular stools, and a stable weight.
