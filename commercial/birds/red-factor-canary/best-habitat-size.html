Best Cage Setup

Quick Answer

A red-factor canary is a color canary, and the red comes from carotenoids in the diet during molt, not from a dye poured on the feathers. They need a canary seed mix, greens, cuttlebone, and a cage wide enough to hop. They are not a handling pet. Drafts and a dirty cage floor cause the respiratory disease that kills canaries. Color food is optional. Clean air is not.

Choosing the Right

The cage should be approximately 1.5 to 2 times your Red Factor Canary's body length in the primary dimension. Material quality matters: invest in a durable cage that will last throughout your Red Factor Canary's 10-15 years lifespan rather than replacing cheaper options repeatedly.

Nutrition for Young

Adjustable or expandable cage options accommodate Red Factor Canary's growth from juvenile to adult size, saving money while ensuring appropriate space at every life stage.

Climate and Environment

Environmental conditions significantly affect Red Factor Canary's health and comfort. Air quality matters: ensure adequate ventilation in your Red Factor Canary's space without creating drafts. If your geographic region experiences extreme seasons, plan seasonal adjustments to your Red Factor Canary's cage setup including heating, cooling, and humidity management.

For a 24x12x18 inches minimum (flight cage preferred) bird like Red Factor Canary, pay special attention to items at their height level that could be pulled down, heavy objects that could fall, and access to countertops or high shelves.
