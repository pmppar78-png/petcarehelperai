Best Food for Button Quail: What to Feed, Portions & Mistakes to Avoid

Quick Answer

Start with a life-stage appropriate food that meets AAFCO standards, then adjust portions for Button Quail's size, activity, body condition, and any veterinary restrictions. Parasites, Respiratory Infections, and Seeds, insects, greens belong in the food choice for the Button Quail, and the type line reads Quail.

Button quail, the Chinese painted quail, are ground birds the size of a golf ball. They need a low enclosure so a flush does not break a neck, a fine game-bird or finch diet, and grit. They are not a pet to hold. Females can exhaust themselves laying. A heat lamp used carelessly cooks them. If you want a bird that sits on a finger, this is the wrong species.
