Best Pet Insurance for Green Wing Macaw: Coverage, Costs & Red Flags

Quick Answer

Pellets, nuts, fruits, vegetables and South America belong in the insurance quote for the Green-Wing Macaw.

A Green-wing Macaw is one of the giant macaws, close to a hyacinth in presence and louder than a person expects from a photo. They live for decades, they chew furniture, and they need a room-sized space and a diet of pellets, vegetables, and some nuts, not a bucket of sunflower seed. Beak and feather disease testing and a legal source matter. This is not a step up from a cockatiel.
