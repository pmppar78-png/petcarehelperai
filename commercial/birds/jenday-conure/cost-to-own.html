Jenday Conure Cost to Own: First-Year, Monthly & Vet Budget

Quick Answer

Psittacine Beak and Feather Disease and Chlamydiosis belong in the cost sheet for the Jenday Conure.

A Jenday Conure is a loud Aratinga, orange and green, and it is a poor secret in an apartment. They need hours out, things to destroy, and a pellet-and-vegetable diet. They bite when they are hormonal or under-slept. Budget the noise and the chew toys before the color. An avian vet visit once a year, and sooner if the bird is fluffed or thin, is the health plan.

Best for Budget-Conscious Jenday Conure Owners

Jenday medical bills cluster around fatty liver after a seed habit, bite wounds, and a hen that cannot finish laying. Structure the food spend around a conure pellet plus pepper, carrot, and leafy greens, with sunflower seed limited to training. Book the new-bird exam with an avian veterinarian listed in the AAV directory. Check whether an exotic policy, Nationwide's avian and exotic plan among them, covers a jenday where you live, and hold cash for an after-hours beak or egg visit.
