Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Cockatoo

Cockatoos wean onto pellets and vegetables and then spend adulthood trying to convert that into nuts and pasta. Hold the line. Juveniles need a formulated pellet, chopped vegetables, and a few nuts. Adults of the big white species gain weight and feather-pick when the diet is sunflower and table food. Keep nuts as training payment. An aging cockatoo still needs the pellet. Soften it if the beak is worn, and cut calories if the bird is only sitting and screaming. Avocado, chocolate, and pits stay out of the kitchen at every age. A gram scale tells you more than the look of the chest feathers.
