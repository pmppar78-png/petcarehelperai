Climate and Environment

Environmental conditions significantly affect Canary's health and comfort. Air quality matters: ensure adequate ventilation in your Canary's space without creating drafts. If your geographic region experiences extreme seasons, plan seasonal adjustments to your Canary's cage setup including heating, cooling, and humidity management.

For Canary at Small (4.5-5.5 inches, 12-30 grams) size, check for gaps or spaces where they could become trapped or escape. Regular safety audits of your Canary's environment every few months catch new hazards as household items and arrangements change over time.

Seasonal Habitat Adjustments

Your Canary's habitat needs shift with the seasons. In warmer months, a Small (4.5-5.5 inches, 12-30 grams) bird needs cooling options: frozen treats, cooling mats, and increased air circulation around the cage. Transitional seasons require attention to indoor air quality—spring allergens and autumn mold can affect Canary's respiratory health. These seasonal adjustments, while modest in effort, make a measurable difference in your Canary's comfort and health across their 10-15 years lifespan.
