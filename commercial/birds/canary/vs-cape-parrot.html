Canary: Breed Background and Character

The Canary has been a beloved companion bird for over 500 years, prized primarily for the male's beautiful, melodious singing. These small finches are ideal for those who want to enjoy the beauty and song of a bird without the handling requirements and noise of parrots. These characteristics make Canary a distinctive bird with specific care requirements. Their friendly nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Small (4.5-5.5 inches, 12-30 grams) frame. Prospective owners should note that Canary's beginner-friendly care level requires consistent attention throughout their 10-15 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
