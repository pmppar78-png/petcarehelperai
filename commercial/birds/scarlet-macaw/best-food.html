Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Scarlet Macaw

Scarlet macaws eat more fruit in the wild than some other macaws, and in a house that fact gets misread as a fruit-only diet. Wean chicks onto a pellet and vegetables, and keep fruit as one portion beside the pellet and the vegetables. Adults need the pellet measured, vegetables daily, fruit in moderation because of sugar, and nuts as training rewards. A senior scarlet that is less active needs fewer nuts and the same vegetables. Pull fresh food within a few hours in warm weather. Avocado, chocolate, and pits are off the menu at every age. Weigh the bird. Feathers hide both a heavy body and a thin one.
