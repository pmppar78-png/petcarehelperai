Puzzle feeders force Hawk-Headed Parrot (Red-Fan Parrot) to work for their food, engaging natural foraging instincts and extending mealtime from minutes to 20-30 minutes of focused mental activity. For this species, species-appropriate puzzle difficulty should be gradually increased as your Hawk-Headed Parrot (Red-Fan Parrot) masters each level. Physical activity for Hawk-Headed Parrot (Red-Fan Parrot) should reflect their moderate exercise needs and 6-10 oz build.
