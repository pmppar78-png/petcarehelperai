What to feed this Foxface Rabbitfish

Foxface rabbitfish, Siganus vulpinus and close species, are reef herbivores that reach several inches and need about 75 gallons. Clip seaweed in the tank every day, and keep spirulina, algae pellets, and blanched vegetables in the rotation so a foxface keeps grazing after the rock film is gone. They will sample meaty foods. The staple is the seaweed. Two or three offerings a day match a grazing metabolism. A foxface in a bare tank starves with a full-looking freezer nearby if nobody actually clips the nori.
