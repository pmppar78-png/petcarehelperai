Pre-existing Condition Awareness for Royal Gramma

Understanding pre-existing condition policies is crucial for Royal Gramma owners. Most insurers exclude conditions diagnosed or showing symptoms before enrollment. For Royal Gramma, this is particularly important because some species-specific conditions like respiratory issues can present subtle early signs. During the waiting period (typically 14 days for illness, 48 hours for accidents), no claims can be filed. Some insurers will cover curable pre-existing conditions after a symptom-free period of 12-18 months. To maximize your Royal Gramma's coverage, enroll as early as possible, ideally within the first few months of bringing your Royal Gramma home, and maintain continuous coverage without lapses.
