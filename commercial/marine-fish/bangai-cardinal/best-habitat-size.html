Multi-Pet Household Setup for Bangai Cardinalfish

If introducing Bangai Cardinalfish into a home with existing marine fish or other animals, careful space planning prevents territorial conflicts and stress. For Bangai Cardinalfish with their peaceful, slow-moving, schooling temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact. Resource guarding is common during transitions; provide duplicate resources (food bowls, water sources, enrichment items) in separate locations. Monitor interactions closely during the first several weeks, and be prepared to separate marine fish if signs of aggression or excessive stress appear.
