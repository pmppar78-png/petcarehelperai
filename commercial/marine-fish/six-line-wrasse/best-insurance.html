Pre-existing Condition Awareness for Six-Line Wrasse

A six-line wrasse that is already chasing every fish in the shop will do the same at home. That behavior is what you bought. Ich or a torn fin on arrival is the medical pre-existing note. Photograph both. Insurance will not rehome the tankmates it harasses, and a fin that arrived split will be called an old injury.
