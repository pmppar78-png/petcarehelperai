Building a Vet Fund

A vet fund is a separate, liquid savings balance earmarked for Flame Hawkfish veterinary expenses and nothing else. Treat it as non-discretionary: a monthly auto-transfer of $40–$80 from the operating account into a dedicated sub-account. The mechanism matters more than the amount. Size the fund to cover one significant event plus one ongoing chronic treatment. For most Flame Hawkfishs, that is a target balance of $2,500–$4,000.
