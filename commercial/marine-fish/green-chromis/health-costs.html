Building a Vet Fund

Building a vet fund for a Green Chromis is a discipline problem disguised as a savings problem. The discipline is harder: keeping the fund untouched during routine financial pressure, replenishing it after unavoidable drawdowns, and resisting the temptation to cancel the auto-transfer during lean months.
