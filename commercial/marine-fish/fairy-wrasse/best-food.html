What to feed

Fairy wrasses, Cirrhilabrus, are colorful reef wrasses that live in harems over rubble and eat zooplankton. A small group needs about 55 gallons with a sand bed they can dive into at night. Mysis, enriched brine, and pellets they recognize are the staple, twice a day, broadcast in the water column where the males display. They are peaceful plankton feeders. They are not pest-control wrasses, and they should not be expected to live on rock fauna alone.

Males in full color that suddenly hide and fade may be sleeping, or they may be bullied by another wrasse. A fish that also looks hollow is missing the mysis. In a mixed reef a fairy wrasse often reaches the mysis second. Damsels and larger wrasses will steal the cloud of food. Spread it. A pinched belly on a fairy wrasse shows as a knife edge behind the head, which ruins the profile before the color goes.

They jump when startled. Keep a lid. Pull uneaten mysis. Two feedings a day keep the males displaying. A single daily dump is more waste and less behavior. Older fairy wrasses eat the same plankton foods. The color is the point of the fish and it holds when the meals are regular and the sand is there for the night dive. A wrasse on the sand in the afternoon, breathing hard, needs a water check.

Cirrhilabrus fairy wrasses live over rubble slopes and pick zooplankton from the current, and the males hold a harem if the tank is long enough. A 55-gallon tank with open sand lets them dive under the sand when the lights go out, which is how they sleep. Broadcast a cloud of mysis twice a day across the open water. A single corner is a spot one damsel can own. Enriched brine shrimp can be the second food on alternate days. Males in full color that display and then eat are the picture of a fed group. A male that loses color and rests on the sand in the middle of the day is stressed or underfed. Check the belly before you add another male. These wrasses jump when the lid is off during a water change. Feed them, then close the tank. They do not need nori, and they will ignore it while they wait for the mysis.
