For a high-energy Fairy Wrasse, the enrichment budget should skew toward activities with variable outcomes rather than predictable ones. Two targeted twenty-minute cognitive sessions a day, bracketed by standard physical exercise, produce better behavioural outcomes than a single hour of high-intensity play. The cognitive fatigue compounds through the day and translates into a materially calmer Fairy Wrasse by evening.

Weekly Enrichment Schedule for Fairy Wrasse

A structured enrichment calendar prevents both over-stimulation and boredom for Fairy Wrasse. This rotation ensures every enrichment category gets regular attention without overwhelming either you or your Fairy Wrasse. Within each day, distribute enrichment across morning and evening sessions rather than concentrating all stimulation in one period. Track your Fairy Wrasse's engagement and behavioral indicators to optimize the schedule over time for your individual marine fish's needs and preferences.
