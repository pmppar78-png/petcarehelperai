First-year veterinary costs for Fairy Wrasse include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Fairy Wrasse may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Fairy Wrasse typically range from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
