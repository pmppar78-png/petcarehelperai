What to feed

Regal blue tangs, Paracanthurus hepatus, are active herbivores that need a very large tank, on the order of 180 gallons, with open swimming room. In the wild they graze algae all day. Nori on a clip, spirulina, and algae-based foods have to be available every day, with mysis or a prepared herbivore food as a smaller side. A blue tang in a tank you have scraped clean will waste away while the fish around it look fed. Three grazing opportunities a day match the metabolism better than one heavy meal.

Head and lateral line erosion, the pitted face some tangs develop, is linked to a poor diet and to carbon and water-quality problems. A varied algae diet is the feeding half of that prevention. White spots and flashing are ich, which blue tangs catch easily when they are stressed or newly added. Food does not cure ich. A thin tang is a harder patient. Keep the nori coming for any fish that is still eating.

They are not reef-safe in the sense of ignoring every coral, and a hungry tang is more likely to pick. Feed the algae first. Pull old nori. A regal tang with a deep body, a constant grazing posture, and intact skin is on the right plan. A pinched belly behind the head means the clip is empty or another fish owns it. Older blue tangs eat the same nori and spirulina. They do not move to a meaty diet with age.

Paracanthurus hepatus grazes algae for most of the day in the ocean, and a single regal tang needs on the order of 180 gallons of swimming room. Clip nori in the tank every day, and offer spirulina or an algae pellet as well, with mysis only as a side. A blue tang in a scraped-clean tank loses weight behind the head even when the freezer is full of meaty food. Refresh the clip when the sheet is gone. Head and lateral line erosion, the pitted skin some tangs develop, is less common when the algae diet is steady and the water is clean. Ich, the white spots and the scratching, is separate and common in this species after a move. Keep feeding any tang that will still pick while you treat the parasite. A deep-bodied regal that spends the day on the clip is the goal.
