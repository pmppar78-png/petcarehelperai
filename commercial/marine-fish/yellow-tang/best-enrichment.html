DIY Enrichment Ideas for Yellow Tang

Yellow tangs, Zebrasoma flavescens, need swimming length and grazing. Place a seaweed clip at the far end of the tank from their favorite rock so they cross open water all day. Mature live rock with film algae is the other half of the setup. A short cube with one ornament leaves them pacing the glass. Quarantine them. Stressed tangs spot with ich.

Ask a pet-care question

A yellow tang's day is grazing. Long open water, rock with a film of algae, and a clip of dried seaweed give it that work. A bare glass box with a single pellet feeding leaves a tang pacing and thin, however bright the yellow looks under the light.
