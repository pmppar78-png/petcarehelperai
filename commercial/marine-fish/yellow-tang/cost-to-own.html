First Year Cost

Best for Budget

Yellow tangs need swimming room and daily seaweed. Ich shows up when that grazing life is skipped. Check salinity and ammonia, and look at the body condition, before treatment. Structure the food spend around dried seaweed clipped in the tank daily for a yellow tang, plus a spirulina food, because this fish grazes plants all day. Talk to an aquatic veterinarian once the water is credible and the spots or the thin body remain. Pet insurance does not cover Zebrasoma. The emergency fund is quarantine, nori, and the clinic fee.

The habits that matter are grazing space, a seaweed clip, and quarantine. A yellow tang on pellets alone in a bare tank gets thin while the color still looks fine under the lights.

Regardless of source, budget for an immediate comprehensive aquatic-experienced veterinarian examination ($75-$200) to establish your Yellow Tang's baseline health profile.

Zebrasoma flavescens grazes algae all day and grows to about 8 inches. A long tank of 70 to 100 gallons or more, live rock with a film, and dried seaweed on a clip are the husbandry. A yellow tang kept on pellets alone in a bare box thins out. Quarantine new tangs. They carry ich into a display more often than the color suggests.
