Best for Transitioning Mandarin Dragonet's Diet

What to feed

Mandarin dragonets, Synchiropus splendidus, eat amphipods and copepods off rock and sand. A 30-gallon mature reef that already has a pod population is the minimum honest setup, and bigger is safer. A mandarin that is not eating frozen or prepared foods will starve in a new tank no matter how pretty the rock is. Many never learn to eat mysis or pellets. Those fish need a refugium and a tank that produces pods, or they waste away with a pinched belly and a sunken face.

If you buy a mandarin, watch it eat before it comes home. A fish that picks at the glass and the rock all day is hunting pods. A fish that sits still and gets thinner is failing. You can train some individuals onto live brine and then frozen mysis, a few bites several times a day. A pinch of flake in the water column is not.
