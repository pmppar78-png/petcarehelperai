Feeding Guidelines for Belgian Tervuren

A Belgian Tervuren is the fawn, long-haired Belgian shepherd. The drive matches a Malinois more than a collie-mix photo suggests. They need work, they are often noise-sensitive, and epilepsy and hip dysplasia are the screening topics. Feed a performance diet in amounts that match actual work, not the appetite of a dog that would train all day.

What to Look For

  • Life-stage appropriate: Puppy, adult, and senior formulas are not interchangeable — pick the one that matches your Belgian Tervuren's current stage.
  • Calorie density match: The right calorie content for your Belgian Tervuren's size and activity level prevents both under- and over-feeding.
