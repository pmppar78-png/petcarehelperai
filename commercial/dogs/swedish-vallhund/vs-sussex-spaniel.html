Swedish Vallhund
Ownership and health

Temperament and Personality

Swedish Vallhund's friendly, energetic, watchful character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Sussex Spaniel's friendly, calm, cheerful nature.

Best for Families

Swedish Vallhund's friendly nature and Sussex Spaniel's friendly temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Sussex Spaniel faces its own health challenges including orthopedic problems, Heart Conditions, Other Concerns. Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Intervertebral Disc Disease for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Sussex Spaniel's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Swedish Vallhund.

Exercise and Activity

Swedish Vallhund requires high levels of exercise and engagement, while Sussex Spaniel needs moderate (45-60 minutes daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small to Medium (20-35 lbs) vs Medium (35-45 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Swedish Vallhund's 12-15 years expected life and Sussex Spaniel's 13-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Swedish Vallhund: high engagement vs Sussex Spaniel: moderate (45-60 minutes daily)), grooming tolerance (moderate vs moderate), and personality preference (friendly vs friendly).

Best for First

Feeding and Nutrition

Swedish Vallhund (Small to Medium (20-35 lbs), high activity) has different caloric and macronutrient needs than Sussex Spaniel (Medium (35-45 lbs), moderate (45-60 minutes daily) activity). Health-condition-specific dietary needs also differ—Swedish Vallhund's associations with orthopedic problems may warrant targeted nutrition, while Sussex Spaniel's genetic predisposition to joint conditions calls for different dietary strategies.

Living Space and

Swedish Vallhund requires crate space suited to a Small to Medium (20-35 lbs) dog with high exercise demands and a friendly, energetic, watchful disposition. Sussex Spaniel needs space accommodating their Medium (35-45 lbs) build, moderate (45-60 minutes daily) activity needs, and friendly, calm, cheerful behavioral style.

Insurance and Health

Sussex Spaniel's risk factors (orthopedic problems and Heart Conditions) require different policy features.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Swedish Vallhund's friendly, energetic, watchful temperament and high activity needs must remain compatible with your lifestyle through potential moves, career changes, and family growth. Sussex Spaniel's friendly, calm, cheerful character and moderate (45-60 minutes daily) demands create a different long-term compatibility profile. Care complexity evolves with age: Swedish Vallhund's health predispositions (orthopedic problems) and Sussex Spaniel's risks (orthopedic problems) may require increasing management in later years.
