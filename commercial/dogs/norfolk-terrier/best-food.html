Understanding Norfolk Terrier's Dietary Heritage

The Norfolk Terrier's evolutionary background directly influences modern dietary needs. As a Small (11-12 lbs) dog with fearless character traits, Norfolk Terrier has metabolic patterns shaped by generations of selective development. Owners who understand Norfolk Terrier's heritage make better nutritional choices because they anticipate requirements rather than reacting to deficiency symptoms. The connection between Norfolk Terrier's fearless, lovable, alert personality and dietary preference is well documented—dogs with higher energy temperaments tend to self-regulate intake more effectively, while calmer dogs may overeat if portions are uncontrolled.
