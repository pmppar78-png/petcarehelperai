The Standard Poodle has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Large (40-70 lbs) build and intelligent temperament. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Standard Poodle's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.
