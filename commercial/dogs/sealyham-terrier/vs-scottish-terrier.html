The temperament contrast between Sealyham Terrier and Scottish Terrier is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. In daily life, this means Sealyham Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward outgoing behavior, while Scottish Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward independent tendencies.
