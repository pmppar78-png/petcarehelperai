Is Irish Setter Right for You? A Lifestyle Assessment

This breed's outgoing and active personality thrives with high (1-2 hours daily) engagement and structured routines. Irish Setter is considered a lower-maintenance breed, making it a reasonable choice for first-time dog owners who are committed to basic care routines.

Best for Active Owners

Active households should still build deliberate rest into the Irish Setter's week.

Your First 30 Days with an Irish Setter

Irish Setters need miles and a fence in the first month, plus a comb behind the ears where the long feathering mats. They are slow to mature and quick to run off on a bird. Enroll a class, use a long line, and book the vet visit. Schedule real mileage outside the yard. A lap of the fence leaves this breed under-exercised.
