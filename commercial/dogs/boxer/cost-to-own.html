Boxers are known for arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy, mast cell tumors, and bloat. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, split into meals so a deep chest carries a lighter load. Use a general practitioner who will listen longer than a quick puppy check and who knows the bloat emergency. The policy has to be active before a heart arrhythmia or a skin mass is recorded. Ask the insurer about a cancer rider or a high annual limit. Mast cell tumors in this breed are rarely a one-visit bill.
