Boxer Nutritional Profile

The Boxer has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Large (50-80 lbs) build and fun-loving temperament. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Boxer's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.
