Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Large (50-80 lbs) vs Large (70-110 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs low (but high grooming needs)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Boxer's 10-12 years expected life and Bouvier des Flandres's 10-12 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Boxer if your lifestyle accommodates their high activity needs, moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their fun-loving temperament. Choose Bouvier des Flandres if you prefer their high (1-2 hours daily) energy level, can manage low (but high grooming needs) maintenance, and appreciate their loyal personality.
