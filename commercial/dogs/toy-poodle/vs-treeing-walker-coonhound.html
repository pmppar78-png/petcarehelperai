Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Toy (4-6 lbs) vs Large (50-70 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (very low (hypoallergenic) vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. The exercise gap is significant: Toy Poodle demands moderate (30-45 minutes daily) activity versus Treeing Walker Coonhound's high (1-2 hours daily) needs—this alone dictates different daily routines. Toy Poodle's intelligent, active, alert temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Treeing Walker Coonhound's smart, confident, loving nature creates different environmental needs.
