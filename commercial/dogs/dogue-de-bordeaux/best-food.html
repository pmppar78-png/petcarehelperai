Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Dogue de Bordeaux

Dogues de Bordeaux have short lives and heavy bodies, so every extra pound is costly. Puppies must grow slowly on a large-breed puppy food for well over a year. A wrinkly, barrel-shaped puppy is overfed. Adults eat two or three small meals of a complete food, never one enormous dinner, because bloat is a real risk in this chest. Keep the waist. Wrinkles show loose skin. Condition shows up at the waist and the ribs. Seniors often need fewer calories early, a dental plan around a short muzzle, and pain control that works better if the dog is lean. Ask the vet before adding joint chews that are mostly calories. Water should be available. Exercise after a huge meal is a bad combination in this breed.
