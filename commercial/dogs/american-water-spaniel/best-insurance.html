What to Look For in Pet Insurance

  • Coverage breadth: Accidents, illnesses, hereditary conditions, and emergency care.
  • Reimbursement rate: Most plans offer 70-90% reimbursement after deductible.
  • Annual limits: Choose unlimited or high annual limits for comprehensive protection.
  • Deductible options: Lower deductibles mean higher premiums but less out-of-pocket per incident.
  • Waiting periods: Understand how long before coverage begins for different conditions.

Coverage Types Explained

  • Accident-only plans: Cover injuries from accidents like broken bones, lacerations, and ingestion of foreign objects.
  • Comprehensive plans: Cover both accidents and illnesses including cancer, infections, and chronic conditions.
  • Wellness plans: Add-on coverage for routine care like vaccinations, dental cleanings, and annual checkups.

Pre-existing Condition Awareness for American Water Spaniel

Understanding pre-existing condition policies is crucial for American Water Spaniel owners. Most insurers exclude conditions diagnosed or showing symptoms before enrollment. For American Water Spaniel, this is particularly important because some breed-specific conditions like respiratory issues can present subtle early signs. During the waiting period (typically 14 days for illness, 48 hours for accidents), no claims can be filed. Some insurers will cover curable pre-existing conditions after a symptom-free period of 12-18 months. To maximize your American Water Spaniel's coverage, enroll as early as possible, ideally within the first few months of bringing your American Water Spaniel home, and maintain continuous coverage without lapses.
