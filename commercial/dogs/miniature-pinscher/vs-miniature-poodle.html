Miniature Poodle: Breed Background and Character

The Miniature Poodle is the middle-sized variety of the Poodle breed, standing between 10-15 inches tall. Like their Standard Poodle cousins, Miniature Poodles are exceptionally intelligent, elegant, and athletic dogs with a non-shedding coat that makes them popular with allergy sufferers. In contrast, Miniature Poodle brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience.
