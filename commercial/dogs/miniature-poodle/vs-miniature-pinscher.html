The temperament contrast between Miniature Poodle and Miniature Pinscher is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. Miniature Poodle is characterized by an intelligent, active, trainable personality, while Miniature Pinscher tends toward fearless, fun-loving, proud traits.
