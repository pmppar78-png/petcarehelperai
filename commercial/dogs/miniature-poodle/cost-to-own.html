Like their Standard Poodle cousins, Miniature Poodles are exceptionally intelligent, elegant, and athletic dogs with a non-shedding coat that makes them popular with allergy sufferers.

Originally bred down from Standard Poodles, Miniature Poodles were popular as circus performers and truffle hunters due to their intelligence and trainability.
