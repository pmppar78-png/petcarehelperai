Best for Budget-Conscious Shih-Poo Owners

Shih Poos inherit crowded teeth, slipping patellas, and a flat face that overheats and snores. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, in toy-dog portions, because the coat makes a fat dog look fluffy. A general practitioner who grades the teeth and the patella at the wellness visit will preview the real costs. Enroll before a dental or luxating-patella diagnosis. Daily face wiping and a groomer on a schedule prevent the tear-stain skin infections that become the real invoice.
