Shih-Poo requires low to moderate (20-30 minutes daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Shih Tzu needs low-moderate (30-60 min daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Shih-Poo has low (often hypoallergenic) grooming needs, while Shih Tzu requires low (hair, not fur) maintenance.

Cost of Ownership
