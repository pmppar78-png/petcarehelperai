Temperament and Personality

Goldendoodle is characterized by a friendly, intelligent, devoted personality, while Gordon Setter tends toward affectionate, confident, bold traits.

Best for Families

Goldendoodle's friendly nature and Gordon Setter's affectionate temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Ask the veterinarian about Sebaceous adenitis, an inflammatory skin disease of Poodles, is worth a question for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Goldendoodle's predispositions typically require specific screening tests, while Gordon Setter has its own set of conditions to monitor.

Exercise and Activity

Goldendoodle requires moderate to high (45-90 min daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Gordon Setter needs high activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Goldendoodle has low to moderate (varies by coat type) grooming needs, while Gordon Setter requires high maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Both Goldendoodle and Gordon Setter are similarly sized at Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs), so recurring costs for food and supplies are comparable between the two breeds. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs) vs Large (45-80 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low to moderate (varies by coat type) vs high), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Goldendoodle's 10-15 years expected life and Gordon Setter's 12-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Goldendoodle: moderate to high (45-90 min daily) engagement vs Gordon Setter: high), grooming tolerance (low to moderate (varies by coat type) vs high), and personality preference (friendly vs affectionate).

Feeding and Nutrition

Goldendoodle at Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate to high (45-90 min daily) activity level, while Gordon Setter at Large (45-80 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their high energy output. Goldendoodle's genetic predisposition to joint conditions may require specialized dietary formulations, while Gordon Setter may benefit from diets supporting joint health and mobility.

Living Space and

Goldendoodle (Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs), friendly, intelligent, devoted) occupies space differently than Gordon Setter (Large (45-80 lbs), affectionate, confident, bold). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Goldendoodle's moderate to high (45-90 min daily) energy creates one footprint, while Gordon Setter's high activity level creates another.

Insurance and Health

Gordon Setter's risk factors (hip and joint issues and dental disease, skin conditions, and breed-related eye problems) require different policy features.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Goldendoodle (Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs), excellent care demands) and Gordon Setter (Large (45-80 lbs), moderate care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Goldendoodle's moderate to high (45-90 min daily) exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Gordon Setter's high activity needs cannot be neglected.

Best for Making

Reading about a breed only goes so far; real interaction reveals whether Goldendoodle's personality or Gordon Setter's energy aligns with your daily life.
