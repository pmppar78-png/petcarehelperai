Temperament and Personality Differences

Understanding how Irish Doodle and Ibizan Hound differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. Irish Doodle's friendly, active, intelligent character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Ibizan Hound's even-tempered, playful, active nature. In daily life, this means Irish Doodle owners typically experience a dog that leans toward friendly behavior, while Ibizan Hound owners find their dog more inclined toward even-tempered tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences.
