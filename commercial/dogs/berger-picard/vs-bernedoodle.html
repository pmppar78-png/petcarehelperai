Bernedoodle: Breed Background and Character

The Bernedoodle is a stunning designer breed created by crossing a Bernese Mountain Dog with a Poodle. First intentionally bred in 2003, Bernedoodles combine the loyal, gentle nature of the Bernese with the intelligence and hypoallergenic coat of the Poodle. In contrast, Bernedoodle brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their loyal, goofy, affectionate personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate (45-60 min daily) energy management, low to moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs) build. The very good care demands of Bernedoodle span their 12-18 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
