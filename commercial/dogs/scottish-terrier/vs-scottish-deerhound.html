Exercise and Activity Level Differences

Scottish Deerhound: Breed Background and Character

The Scottish Deerhound, known as the "Royal Dog of Scotland," has been hunting red deer in the Scottish Highlands for centuries. So prized was this noble breed that during the Age of Chivalry, no one below the rank of earl was permitted to own one, and wars were fought over their possession. In contrast, Scottish Deerhound brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience.
