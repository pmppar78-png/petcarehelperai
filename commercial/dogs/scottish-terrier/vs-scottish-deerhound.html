  • Your weekly schedule reliably absorbs the Scottish Terrier's exercise, training, and enrichment minimums — not just on good weeks.
  • The Scottish Terrier's social and behavioural baseline lines up with the people, kids, or other pets already in the home.
  • You can plan around the Scottish Terrier's known health predispositions without that planning crowding out other priorities.

Choose Scottish Deerhound If...

  • The Scottish Deerhound's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Scottish Terrier's independent, confident, spirited character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Scottish Deerhound's gentle, dignified, polite nature. In daily life, this means Scottish Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward independent behavior, while Scottish Deerhound owners find their dog more inclined toward gentle tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Scottish Terrier's independent nature and Scottish Deerhound's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Scottish Terrier is predisposed to Bleeding Disorders, Cancer, Other Conditions, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Scottish Deerhound faces its own health challenges including Major Health Concerns, breed-related eye, dental, and skin conditions that benefit from early detection. Ask the veterinarian about Von Willebrand's Disease (vWD) and Hemophilia B for the first animal and about Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) and Osteosarcoma for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.
