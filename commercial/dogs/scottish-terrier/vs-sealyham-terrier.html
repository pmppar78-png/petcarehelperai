Choose Scottish Terrier If...

  • The Scottish Terrier's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Scottish Terrier's than the Sealyham Terrier's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Scottish Terrier's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.

Choose Sealyham Terrier If...

  • You already enjoy the kind of human-dog interaction style the Sealyham Terrier is known for — the Scottish Terrier's style would feel like a stretch.
  • The Sealyham Terrier's long-term health outlook is one you can support with consistent preventive care and appropriate insurance.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Scottish Terrier is characterized by an independent, confident, spirited personality, while Sealyham Terrier tends toward outgoing, calm, fearless traits. In daily life, this means Scottish Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward independent behavior, while Sealyham Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward outgoing tendencies.
