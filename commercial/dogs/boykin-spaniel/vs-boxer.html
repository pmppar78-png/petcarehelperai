Boykin Spaniel: Breed Background and Character

The Boykin Spaniel is the state dog of South Carolina, developed in the early 1900s as a versatile hunting companion for the Wateree River swamp region. This medium-sized spaniel excels at flushing and retrieving waterfowl and wild turkey.
