Best for Budget-Conscious Boykin Spaniel Owners

Boykin Spaniels show up with hip dysplasia, eye disease, and ear infections after a day in the water. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, portioned for a spaniel who will hunt and then beg. A general practitioner comfortable with flushing spaniel ears is the right clinic. Enroll before the first hip radiograph or chronic-ear diagnosis. Rinse and dry the ears after every swim. That habit is cheaper than a year of ear-drop refills.
