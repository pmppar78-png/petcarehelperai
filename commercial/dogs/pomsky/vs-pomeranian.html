Temperament and Personality Differences

Pomsky is characterized by a playful, intelligent, spirited personality, while Pomeranian tends toward lively, bold, inquisitive traits. In daily life, this means Pomsky owners typically experience a dog that leans toward playful behavior, while Pomeranian owners find their dog more inclined toward lively tendencies.

Best for Families with Children
