Temperament and Personality Differences

Cavachon's gentle, cheerful, affectionate character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Caucasian Shepherd's powerful, alert, fearless nature. In daily life, this means Cavachon owners typically experience a dog that leans toward gentle behavior, while Caucasian Shepherd owners find their dog more inclined toward powerful tendencies. The household has to live with gentle, cheerful, affectionate on one side and powerful, alert, fearless on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Cavachon's gentle nature and Caucasian Shepherd's powerful temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Caucasian Shepherd faces its own health challenges including hip and elbow dysplasia, heart conditions such as dilated cardiomyopathy, and other breed-related health concerns.
