Temperament and Personality Differences

Understanding how Pumi and Puli differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. Pumi's lively, alert, intelligent character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Puli's loyal, agile, obedient nature. In daily life, this means Pumi owners typically experience a dog that leans toward lively behavior, while Puli owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies. Both temperaments are legitimate — the better choice depends on the specific household, not any absolute measure.
