Common Mistakes New Pumi Owners Make

Mistake one: choosing Pumi based on appearance rather than lifestyle fit—this breed's high (60+ minutes daily) energy and excellent (eager to work) care demands must match your reality. Mistake three: socializing too aggressively or not at all—Pumi's lively temperament requires gradual, positive exposure to new experiences. Mistake four: comparing your Pumi's progress to other dogs online, which creates unrealistic expectations and unnecessary anxiety.

Building a Care Team for Your Pumi

A professional groomer experienced with Pumi's coat and maintenance requirements saves time and ensures proper care. A qualified trainer or behaviorist who understands Pumi's excellent (eager to work) trainability provides invaluable early guidance. Finally, identify reliable pet sitters or boarding facilities that can accommodate Pumi's specific needs for times when you're unavailable. Building this team proactively means every aspect of your Pumi's care is covered.
