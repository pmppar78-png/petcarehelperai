Temperament and Personality Differences

Papillon's alert, friendly, happy character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Parson Russell Terrier's bold, friendly, athletic nature. In daily life, this means Papillon owners typically experience a dog that leans toward alert behavior, while Parson Russell Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward bold tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Papillon's alert nature and Parson Russell Terrier's bold temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Parson Russell Terrier faces its own health challenges including Eye Conditions, hip and joint issues, Other Concerns.
