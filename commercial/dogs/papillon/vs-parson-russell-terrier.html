Parson Russell Terrier faces its own health challenges including Eye Conditions, hip and joint issues, Other Concerns. Ask the veterinarian about Patellar Luxation and Legg-Calve-Perthes for the first animal and about Primary Lens Luxation and Late Onset Ataxia for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Exercise and Activity

Papillon requires moderate (30-45 min daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Parson Russell Terrier needs very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity.

Cost of Ownership

Over a complete lifespan, Papillon's 14-16 years expected life and Parson Russell Terrier's 13-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Papillon: moderate (30-45 min daily) engagement vs Parson Russell Terrier: very high (1-2+ hours daily)), grooming tolerance (moderate vs moderate), and personality preference (alert vs bold).

Feeding and Nutrition

Papillon's Small (5-10 lbs) frame and moderate (30-45 min daily) energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Parson Russell Terrier's Small (13-17 lbs) build and very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Papillon's health profile (hip and joint issues, Other Conditions) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Parson Russell Terrier's predispositions (Eye Conditions, hip and joint issues) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Papillon at Small (5-10 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and moderate (30-45 min daily) activity pattern, while Parson Russell Terrier at Small (13-17 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and very high (1-2+ hours daily) energy level. Papillon's alert, friendly, happy temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Parson Russell Terrier's bold, friendly, athletic nature creates different environmental needs.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Papillon (Small (5-10 lbs), excellent (highly intelligent) care demands) and Parson Russell Terrier (Small (13-17 lbs), good (but independent) care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Papillon's moderate (30-45 min daily) exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Parson Russell Terrier's very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.
