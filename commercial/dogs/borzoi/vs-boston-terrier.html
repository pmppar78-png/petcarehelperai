Borzoi
Detailed breed guide

Temperament and Personality

Borzoi's gentle, affectionate, athletic character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Boston Terrier's friendly, bright, amusing nature.

Best for Families

Borzoi's gentle nature and Boston Terrier's friendly temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Ask the veterinarian about Bloat (GDV) and heart conditions for the first animal and about Ulcers, cherry eye, and cataracts are common for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Boston Terrier's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Borzoi.

Exercise and Activity

Borzoi requires moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Boston Terrier needs moderate (30-60 min daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Borzoi has moderate to high (seasonal) grooming needs, while Boston Terrier requires low-moderate maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

The size difference between Borzoi (Large (60-105 lbs)) and Boston Terrier (Small-Medium (12-25 lbs)) significantly impacts costs across food, supplies, and veterinary care. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Large (60-105 lbs) vs Small-Medium (12-25 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate to high (seasonal) vs low-moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Borzoi's 9-14 years expected life and Boston Terrier's 11-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Borzoi: moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) engagement vs Boston Terrier: moderate (30-60 min daily)), grooming tolerance (moderate to high (seasonal) vs low-moderate), and personality preference (gentle vs friendly).

Best for First

Feeding and Nutrition

Borzoi at Large (60-105 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) activity level, while Boston Terrier at Small-Medium (12-25 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their moderate (30-60 min daily) energy output. Borzoi's predisposition to Major Health Concerns may require specialized dietary formulations, while Boston Terrier may benefit from diets supporting Brachycephalic Concerns.

Living Space and

Borzoi (Large (60-105 lbs), gentle, affectionate, athletic) occupies space differently than Boston Terrier (Small-Medium (12-25 lbs), friendly, bright, amusing). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Borzoi's moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) energy creates one footprint, while Boston Terrier's moderate (30-60 min daily) activity level creates another.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Borzoi (Large (60-105 lbs), moderate (independent but willing) care demands) and Boston Terrier (Small-Medium (12-25 lbs), good (eager to please) care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Borzoi's moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Boston Terrier's moderate (30-60 min daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.
