Dandie Dinmont Terrier's independent, smart, proud character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Doberman Pinscher's loyal, alert, intelligent nature. In daily life, this means Dandie Dinmont Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward independent behavior, while Doberman Pinscher owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies.
