Common Mistakes New Basenji Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding Basenji's moderate to high (1+ hours daily) exercise needs, low (short coat) grooming requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; Basenji dogs at Small to Medium (22-24 lbs) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding. Skipping early socialization limits your Basenji's comfort in varied environments.
