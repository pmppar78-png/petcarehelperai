Otterhound Nutritional Profile

The Otterhound has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Large (80-115 lbs) build and amiable temperament. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Otterhound's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.

Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Otterhound

Otterhounds are large, rare, shaggy hounds with a hunting appetite and a deep chest. Puppies need a large-breed puppy food and a lean outline you can find under the coat. Adults eat two measured meals. A single huge feed followed by a run is a bloat pattern to avoid. If the dog actually swims and hunts, raise the calories. If it is a pet, lower them. The coat will not tell you which. Seniors need a calorie cut, ear care after water, and a dental look. People overfeed rare breeds because every meal feels like care. This large, shaggy hound was developed in medieval England for the specific purpose of hunting otters, which were depleting fish stocks in rivers and streams.

With their webbed feet, waterproof double coat, and exceptionally keen nose (considered one of the most sensitive among all dog breeds), Otterhounds are superbly adapted for aquatic tracking.
