Common Mistakes New Australian Terrier Owners Make

Mistake one: choosing Australian Terrier based on appearance rather than lifestyle fit—this breed's moderate (30-60 min daily) energy and good care demands must match your reality. Mistake three: socializing too aggressively or not at all—Australian Terrier's spirited temperament requires gradual, positive exposure to new experiences. Mistake four: comparing your Australian Terrier's progress to other dogs online, which creates unrealistic expectations and unnecessary anxiety.
