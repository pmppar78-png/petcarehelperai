Australian Terrier vs Azawakh: Cost, Temperament, Health & Which Fits Better

Quick Answer

The better choice is the one whose daily care, health risks, and lifetime cost fit your household consistently. Compare Australian Terrier and Azawakh on routine first, then use temperament, grooming load, and vet-risk planning to break the tie.

Choosing between Australian Terrier and Azawakh works best when the decision starts with daily constraints: time, space, handling comfort, likely vet costs, and how much structure the household can maintain.

The goal is not to crown one choice. It is to identify which set of needs fits the life the owner can actually provide.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Personality is where Australian Terrier and Azawakh diverge most clearly. Australian Terrier brings a spirited, affectionate, courageous energy to the household, compared to Azawakh's loyal, reserved, proud disposition. These differences shape every daily interaction. In daily life, this means Australian Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward spirited behavior, while Azawakh owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies. There is no objectively better personality here; pick the one that suits your household.

Best for Families with Children

Activity requirements differ minimally between Australian Terrier and Azawakh. Australian Terrier requires moderate (30-60 min daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Azawakh needs high (1-2 hours daily) activity. Australian Terrier owners should plan for 60-90 minutes of daily activity, compared to 60-90 minutes for Azawakh. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small (15-20 lbs) vs Large (33-55 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low vs low), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Australian Terrier's 11-15 years expected life and Azawakh's 12-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.
