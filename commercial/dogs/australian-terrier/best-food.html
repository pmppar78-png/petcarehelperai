Australian Terrier Nutritional Profile

Begin any Australian Terrier feeding conversation with the basics of the breed: a Small (15-20 lbs) physique and a spirited character. Australian Terrier's compact build means calorie needs are lower in absolute terms but higher per pound of body weight than larger dogs. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Australian Terrier's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.

Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Australian Terrier

Young Australian Terriers need nutrient-dense food with higher protein and fat to support growth — typically 20-40% more calories per pound than adults.

Ideal Portion Control for Australian Terrier

Use the manufacturer's guidelines as a starting point, then adjust based on your Australian Terrier's body condition — you should be able to feel the ribs without seeing them, and there should be a visible waist from above. Split daily food into two meals for adults, three to four for growing Australian Terriers, and keep treats under 10% of total daily calories.

Best for Weight Management

Australian Terriers on a weight programme benefit from a modest, consistent increase in daily activity rather than dramatic exercise bursts.
