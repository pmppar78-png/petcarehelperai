Understanding how Whippet and West Highland White Terrier differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. In daily life, this means Whippet owners typically experience a dog that leans toward gentle behavior, while West Highland White Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences.
