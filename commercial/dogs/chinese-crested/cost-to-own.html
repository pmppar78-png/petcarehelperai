Best for Budget-Conscious Chinese Crested Owners

Hairless Chinese Cresteds need dental care for missing and crowded teeth, plus sunburn treatment on bare skin, and the powderpuff coat mats. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, portioned for a toy dog with a fast metabolism. A general practitioner who looks at the gums of a hairless dog at every visit will catch the real bill early. Enroll before the first dental grade or skin infection is charted. Sunscreen for bare skin and a sweater in winter are real supplies. A sunburned hairless back is a veterinary visit.

Recurring Annual Expenses for Chinese Crested

Grooming needs for Chinese Crested, given their very low (hairless) or low (powderpuff) shedding/maintenance profile, run $0-$600 per year depending on professional grooming frequency.
