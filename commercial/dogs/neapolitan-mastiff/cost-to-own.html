Initial setup for a Neapolitan Mastiff includes a quality crate ($50-$300 depending on size and type), collar and leash ($30-$150), food and water dishes ($15-$40), and initial supply of species-appropriate food ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for Neapolitan Mastiff include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Neapolitan Mastiff may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Neapolitan Mastiff typically range from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
