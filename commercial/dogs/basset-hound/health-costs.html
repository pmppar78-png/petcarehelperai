Wellness Monitoring and Early Detection for Basset Hound

Establish baseline health metrics during your Basset Hound's first comprehensive examination: weight, body condition score, bloodwork panels, and any species-appropriate screening tests for this breed. For Basset Hound with predispositions to Structural Issues, ask your veterinarian about targeted early-detection protocols—these often cost $100-$300 per screening but can identify problems months before symptoms appear. A health journal documenting your Basset Hound's normal behaviors and measurements provides invaluable comparison data when something changes.
