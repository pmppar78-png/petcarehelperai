Social Enrichment for Basset Hound

This breed's patient, low-key, charming personality means they benefit from appropriately structured social experiences. However, respect your individual Basset Hound's social preferences; forcing interaction causes stress rather than enrichment.

DIY Enrichment Ideas for Basset Hound
