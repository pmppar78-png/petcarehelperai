Choose Irish Water Spaniel If...

  • The Irish Water Spaniel's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Irish Water Spaniel's than the Irish Terrier's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Irish Water Spaniel's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.

Choose Irish Terrier If...

  • You already enjoy the kind of human-dog interaction style the Irish Terrier is known for — the Irish Water Spaniel's style would feel like a stretch.
  • The Irish Terrier's long-term health outlook is one you can support with consistent preventive care and appropriate insurance.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Irish Terrier's bold, dashing, spirited nature. In daily life, this means Irish Water Spaniel owners typically experience a dog that leans toward playful behavior, while Irish Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward bold tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Irish Water Spaniel's playful nature and Irish Terrier's bold temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.
