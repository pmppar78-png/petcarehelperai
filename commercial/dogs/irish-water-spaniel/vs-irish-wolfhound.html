Choose Irish Water Spaniel If...

  • The Irish Water Spaniel's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Irish Water Spaniel's than the Irish Wolfhound's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Irish Water Spaniel's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.

Choose Irish Wolfhound If...

  • Your weekly schedule reliably absorbs the Irish Wolfhound's exercise, training, and enrichment minimums — not just on good weeks.
  • The Irish Wolfhound's social and behavioural baseline lines up with the people, kids, or other pets already in the home.
  • You can plan around the Irish Wolfhound's known health predispositions without that planning crowding out other priorities.

Temperament and Personality

Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Irish Wolfhound's gentle, dignified, courageous nature.

Best for Families

Irish Water Spaniel's playful nature and Irish Wolfhound's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Irish Wolfhound faces its own health challenges including Major Health Concerns, eye conditions, skin allergies, and age-related joint deterioration. Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the first animal and about Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) and Osteosarcoma for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Exercise and Activity

Irish Water Spaniel requires high levels of exercise and engagement, while Irish Wolfhound needs moderate (30-60 min daily) activity.

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Large (45-68 lbs) vs Giant (105-180 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Irish Water Spaniel's 12-13 years expected life and Irish Wolfhound's 6-8 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Irish Water Spaniel: high engagement vs Irish Wolfhound: moderate (30-60 min daily)), grooming tolerance (moderate vs moderate), and personality preference (playful vs gentle).

Feeding and Nutrition

Irish Water Spaniel's Large (45-68 lbs) frame and high energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Irish Wolfhound's Giant (105-180 lbs) build and moderate (30-60 min daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Irish Water Spaniel's health profile (hip and joint concerns along with other health conditions common in this breed) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Irish Wolfhound's predispositions (Major Health Concerns, breed-related eye, dental, and skin conditions that benefit from early detection) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Irish Water Spaniel at Large (45-68 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and high activity pattern, while Irish Wolfhound at Giant (105-180 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and moderate (30-60 min daily) energy level. Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Irish Wolfhound's gentle, dignified, courageous nature creates different environmental needs.

Insurance and Health

Irish Water Spaniel faces health risks from joint and skeletal conditions and hereditary conditions including potential eye, dental, and metabolic issues that generate specific claim patterns, while Irish Wolfhound's Major Health Concerns and eye conditions, skin allergies, and age-related joint deterioration drives different insurance utilization. Over Irish Water Spaniel's 12-13 years lifespan, expected veterinary costs may differ significantly from Irish Wolfhound's 6-8 years cost horizon.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Irish Water Spaniel (Large (45-68 lbs), moderate care demands) and Irish Wolfhound (Giant (105-180 lbs), good (eager to please) care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Irish Water Spaniel's high exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Irish Wolfhound's moderate (30-60 min daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.
