Irish Wolfhound: Breed Background and Character

The Irish Wolfhound is the tallest of all dog breeds, with a history dating back over 2,000 years in Ireland. Originally bred to hunt wolves, elk, and wild boar, these magnificent dogs were so valued by Irish chieftains that they were given as royal gifts and their export was once restricted by law. In contrast, Irish Wolfhound brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their gentle, dignified, courageous personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate (30-60 min daily) energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Giant (105-180 lbs) build.
