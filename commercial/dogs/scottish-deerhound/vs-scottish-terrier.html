Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Scottish Deerhound and Scottish Terrier is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. Scottish Deerhound is characterized by a gentle, dignified, polite personality, while Scottish Terrier tends toward independent, confident, spirited traits. In daily life, this means Scottish Deerhound owners typically experience a dog that leans toward gentle behavior, while Scottish Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward independent tendencies.
