Chinese Shar-Pei Energy Profile and Enrichment Needs

Shar-Pei enrichment has to respect heat and the wrinkles. Short training games indoors, a sniff walk in cool air, and a puzzle feeder beat a noon game of fetch. They overheat, and swimming soaks the folds, which then need to be dried so the skin does not infect. Watch the hocks: a sudden swollen hock can be Shar-Pei fever and needs a veterinarian the same day.
