Exercise and Activity Level Differences

Activity requirements differ notably between Chinese Shar-Pei and Chinese Crested. Chinese Shar-Pei requires moderate (45-60 min daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Chinese Crested needs low to moderate (20-30 minutes daily) activity.
