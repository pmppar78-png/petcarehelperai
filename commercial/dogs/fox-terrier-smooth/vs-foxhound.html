Best for Families

Smooth Fox Terrier's friendly nature and American Foxhound's independent temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Smooth Fox Terrier has a typical lifespan of 12-15 years, while American Foxhound lives approximately 11-13 years. Smooth Fox Terrier is predisposed to orthopedic problems such as ligament injuries and other genetic predispositions, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Ask the veterinarian about Patellar Luxation and Legg-Calve-Perthes for the first animal and about Ear Infections and Bloat for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Exercise and Activity

Activity requirements differ minimally between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound. Smooth Fox Terrier requires high levels of exercise and engagement, while American Foxhound needs high activity. Smooth Fox Terrier owners should plan for 60-90 minutes of daily activity, compared to 60-90 minutes for American Foxhound.

Choosing between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound requires weighing daily lifestyle impact over emotional preference. Smooth Fox Terrier's friendly personality will define your household's dynamic differently than American Foxhound's independent character.

Best for First

Between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound, the one with a more patient temperament and simpler grooming routine reduces the learning curve substantially.

Feeding and Nutrition

Nutrition planning for Smooth Fox Terrier versus American Foxhound involves different considerations. Smooth Fox Terrier (Small to Medium (15-19 lbs), high activity) has different caloric and macronutrient needs than American Foxhound (Large (60-70 lbs), high activity). Health-condition-specific dietary needs also differ—Smooth Fox Terrier's associations with hip and joint issues may warrant targeted nutrition, while American Foxhound's tendency toward hip dysplasia and other orthopedic problems calls for different dietary strategies.

Living Space and

Habitat compatibility is a practical differentiator between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound. Smooth Fox Terrier requires crate space suited to a Small to Medium (15-19 lbs) dog with high exercise demands and a friendly, bold, independent disposition. American Foxhound needs space accommodating their Large (60-70 lbs) build, high activity needs, and independent, easy-going, sweet behavioral style. Beyond the primary crate, consider exercise space: Smooth Fox Terrier needs substantial active space, while American Foxhound demands significant room for exercise.

Insurance and Health

Insurance considerations differ between Smooth Fox Terrier and Foxhound based on their respective health profiles and life expectancies.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

The long-term view reveals important differences between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound. A 12-15 years commitment to Smooth Fox Terrier versus 11-13 years with American Foxhound means different duration but also different intensity curves. Smooth Fox Terrier (Small to Medium (15-19 lbs), moderate care demands) and American Foxhound (Large (60-70 lbs), moderate care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Smooth Fox Terrier's high exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as American Foxhound's high activity needs cannot be neglected.
