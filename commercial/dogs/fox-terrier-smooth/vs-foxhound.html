Smooth Fox Terrier has a typical lifespan of 12-15 years, while American Foxhound lives approximately 11-13 years. Activity requirements differ minimally between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound. Nutrition planning for Smooth Fox Terrier versus American Foxhound involves different considerations. Living Space and

Habitat compatibility is a practical differentiator between Smooth Fox Terrier and American Foxhound. American Foxhound needs space accommodating their Large (60-70 lbs) build, high activity needs, and independent, easy-going, sweet behavioral style. Beyond the primary crate, consider exercise space: Smooth Fox Terrier needs substantial active space, while American Foxhound demands significant room for exercise.
