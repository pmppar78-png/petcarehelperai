Cost Levers Worth

First Year Cost

Best for Budget

Chihuahua costs cluster on the mouth, a slipping patella, a collapsing trachea, and a soft spot in the skull of some puppies. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, in frequent small meals so a tiny puppy does not crash between feeds. See a general practitioner who pairs a harness conversation and a dental probe with the scale. Insure before the first tracheal or patella note. Use a harness and a warm layer in cold weather. A tracheal irritation or a shiver that is actually pain both end at the clinic.

Recurring Annual Expenses

Toys, treats, and enrichment items for a Chihuahua with low (20-30 minutes daily) activity needs average $100-$300 per year.

Chihuahua costs cluster in the mouth, the knees, and emergency care for a dog that is easy to injure. Dental cleanings, patella surgery in some dogs, and a sweater are the realistic list. A tiny puppy that will not eat can become hypoglycemic within a day. Budget an exam early and a dental by middle age. The food bill is small. The dental bill follows the mouth, and this mouth is crowded.
