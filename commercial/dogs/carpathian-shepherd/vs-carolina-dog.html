Carpathian Shepherd: Breed Background and Character

The Carpathian Shepherd Dog (Ciobanesc Romanesc Carpatin) is an ancient Romanian breed that has protected livestock in the Carpathian Mountains for centuries. These powerful dogs have defended flocks against wolves, bears, and other predators in some of Europe's most rugged terrain. These characteristics make Carpathian Shepherd a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their loyal, brave, calm nature means daily life involves moderate (1-1.5 hours daily) activity management, high (dense double coat) grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Large to Giant (70-100 lbs) frame.
