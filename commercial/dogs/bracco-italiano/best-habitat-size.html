Multi-Pet Household Setup for Bracco Italiano

If introducing Bracco Italiano into a home with existing dogs or other animals, careful space planning prevents territorial conflicts and stress. Each animal should have their own crate, feeding station, and resting area. For Bracco Italiano with their affectionate, intelligent, enthusiastic temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact. Shared common areas should have multiple exit points so no animal feels trapped. Resource guarding is common during transitions; provide duplicate resources (food bowls, water sources, enrichment items) in separate locations. Monitor interactions closely during the first several weeks, and be prepared to separate dogs if signs of aggression or excessive stress appear.
