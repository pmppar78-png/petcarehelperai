Understanding how Lhasa Apso and Maltese differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. Lhasa Apso's confident, smart, comical character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Maltese's gentle, playful, charming nature. In daily life, this means Lhasa Apso owners typically experience a dog that leans toward confident behavior, while Maltese owners find their dog more inclined toward gentle tendencies.
