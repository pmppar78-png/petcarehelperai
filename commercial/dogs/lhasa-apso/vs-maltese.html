Temperament and Personality Differences

Lhasa Apso's confident, smart, comical character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Maltese's gentle, playful, charming nature. In daily life, this means Lhasa Apso owners typically experience a dog that leans toward confident behavior, while Maltese owners find their dog more inclined toward gentle tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Lhasa Apso's confident nature and Maltese's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Maltese faces its own health challenges including Dental Issues, Joint Issues, Other Conditions. Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Eye Conditions for the first animal and about Portosystemic shunts, patellar luxation, and tracheal collapse are the three to know by name for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Maltese's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Lhasa Apso.

Exercise and Activity Level Differences

Lhasa Apso requires moderate levels of exercise and engagement, while Maltese needs low-moderate (30 minutes daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance Comparison

Lhasa Apso has high grooming needs, while Maltese requires very low (hypoallergenic) maintenance.

Best for Low-Maintenance Owners

Cost of Ownership Comparison

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small (12-18 lbs) vs Toy (4-7 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (high vs very low (hypoallergenic)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Lhasa Apso's 12-15 years expected life and Maltese's 12-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Which Is Right for Your Family?

The exercise gap is significant: Lhasa Apso demands moderate activity versus Maltese's low-moderate (30 minutes daily) needs—this alone dictates different daily routines. Lhasa Apso's confident personality will define your household's dynamic differently than Maltese's gentle character.

Best for First-Time Owners

Feeding and Nutrition Comparison

Lhasa Apso at Small (12-18 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate activity level, while Maltese at Toy (4-7 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their low-moderate (30 minutes daily) energy output. Lhasa Apso's genetic predisposition to joint conditions may require specialized dietary formulations, while Maltese may benefit from diets supporting Dental Issues.

Living Space and Habitat Requirements

Lhasa Apso (Small (12-18 lbs), confident, smart, comical) occupies space differently than Maltese (Toy (4-7 lbs), gentle, playful, charming). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Lhasa Apso's moderate energy creates one footprint, while Maltese's low-moderate (30 minutes daily) activity level creates another.

Insurance and Health Coverage Comparison

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Lhasa Apso (Small (12-18 lbs), moderate care demands) and Maltese (Toy (4-7 lbs), good (can be stubborn) care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Lhasa Apso's moderate exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Maltese's low-moderate (30 minutes daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.
