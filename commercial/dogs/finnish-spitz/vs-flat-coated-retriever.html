Temperament and Personality Differences

Finnish Spitz brings a friendly, alert, playful energy to the household, compared to Flat-Coated Retriever's cheerful, optimistic, good-humored disposition. In daily life, this means Finnish Spitz owners typically experience a dog that leans toward friendly behavior, while Flat-Coated Retriever owners find their dog more inclined toward cheerful tendencies. The household has to live with friendly, alert, playful on one side and cheerful, optimistic, good-humored on the other.
