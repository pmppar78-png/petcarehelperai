Norfolk Terrier: Breed Background and Character

The Norfolk Terrier is one of the smallest working terriers, distinguished by its folded, V-shaped ears (unlike its prick-eared cousin, the Norwich Terrier). Originally developed in East Anglia, England, these compact hunters were bred to work in packs hunting rats and foxes. In contrast, Norfolk Terrier brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their fearless, lovable, alert personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate (30-45 minutes daily) energy management, low (wire coat) maintenance routines, and space planning for their Small (11-12 lbs) build. The good (eager but independent) care demands of Norfolk Terrier span their 12-16 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
