Temperament and Personality Differences

Personality is where Border Collie and Bohemian Shepherd diverge most clearly. Border Collie brings an affectionate, smart, energetic energy to the household, compared to Bohemian Shepherd's friendly, intelligent, active disposition. These differences shape every daily interaction. In daily life, this means Border Collie owners typically experience a dog that leans toward affectionate behavior, while Bohemian Shepherd owners find their dog more inclined toward friendly tendencies.
