Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Border Collie and Border Terrier is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. Border Collie is characterized by an affectionate, smart, energetic personality, while Border Terrier tends toward affectionate, happy, plucky traits. In daily life, this means Border Collie owners typically experience a dog that leans toward affectionate behavior, while Border Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward affectionate tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences.
