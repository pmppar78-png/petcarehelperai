Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Large (61-85 lbs) vs Medium (31-49 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs low (curly, corded coat)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Spinone Italiano's 10-12 years expected life and Spanish Water Dog's 12-14 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Spinone Italiano if your lifestyle accommodates their moderate activity needs, moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their sociable temperament. Choose Spanish Water Dog if you prefer their high (60-90 minutes daily) energy level, can manage low (curly, corded coat) maintenance, and appreciate their playful personality.

Spinone Italiano at Large (61-85 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate activity level, while Spanish Water Dog at Medium (31-49 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their high (60-90 minutes daily) energy output. Spinone Italiano's tendency toward hip dysplasia and other orthopedic problems may require specialized dietary formulations, while Spanish Water Dog may benefit from diets supporting joint health and mobility.

Living Space and

Spinone Italiano (Large (61-85 lbs), sociable, docile, patient) occupies space differently than Spanish Water Dog (Medium (31-49 lbs), playful, loyal, athletic, hardworking). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Spinone Italiano's moderate energy creates one footprint, while Spanish Water Dog's high (60-90 minutes daily) activity level creates another.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Spanish Water Dog's 12-14 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (hip and joint issues) and different care demands (good to excellent versus moderate).
