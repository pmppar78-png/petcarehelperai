Ownership and health

Temperament and Personality

Gordon Setter's affectionate, confident, bold character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Goldendoodle's friendly, intelligent, devoted nature.

Best for Families

Gordon Setter's affectionate nature and Goldendoodle's friendly temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the first animal and about Sebaceous adenitis, an inflammatory skin disease of Poodles, is worth a question for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Gordon Setter's predispositions typically require specific screening tests, while Goldendoodle has its own set of conditions to monitor.

Exercise and Activity

Gordon Setter requires high levels of exercise and engagement, while Goldendoodle needs moderate to high (45-90 min daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Gordon Setter has high grooming needs, while Goldendoodle requires low to moderate (varies by coat type) maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Large (45-80 lbs) vs Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (high vs low to moderate (varies by coat type)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Gordon Setter's 12-13 years expected life and Goldendoodle's 10-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Gordon Setter: high engagement vs Goldendoodle: moderate to high (45-90 min daily)), grooming tolerance (high vs low to moderate (varies by coat type)), and personality preference (affectionate vs friendly).

Feeding and Nutrition

Gordon Setter's Large (45-80 lbs) frame and high energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Goldendoodle's Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs) build and moderate to high (45-90 min daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Gordon Setter's health profile (joint-related conditions and other breed-specific health issues) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Goldendoodle's predispositions (hip and joint issues, Eye Conditions) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Gordon Setter at Large (45-80 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and high activity pattern, while Goldendoodle at Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and moderate to high (45-90 min daily) energy level. Gordon Setter's affectionate, confident, bold temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Goldendoodle's friendly, intelligent, devoted nature creates different environmental needs.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Gordon Setter (Large (45-80 lbs), moderate care demands) and Goldendoodle (Standard (50-90 lbs), Medium (30-45 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs), excellent care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Gordon Setter's high exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Goldendoodle's moderate to high (45-90 min daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.

Best for Making

Reading about a breed only goes so far; real interaction reveals whether Gordon Setter's personality or Goldendoodle's energy aligns with your daily life.
