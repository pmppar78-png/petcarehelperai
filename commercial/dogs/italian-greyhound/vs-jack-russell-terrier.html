Temperament and Personality Differences

Italian Greyhound is characterized by an affectionate, playful, alert personality, while Jack Russell Terrier tends toward fearless, energetic, intelligent traits. In daily life, this means Italian Greyhound owners typically experience a dog that leans toward affectionate behavior, while Jack Russell Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward fearless tendencies.
