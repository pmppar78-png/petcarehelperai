Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small (7-14 lbs) vs Small (13-17 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Feeding and Nutrition

Italian Greyhound at Small (7-14 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate (30-45 min daily) activity level, while Jack Russell Terrier at Small (13-17 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their very high (1-2+ hours daily) energy output. Italian Greyhound's predisposition to Orthopedic Issues may require specialized dietary formulations, while Jack Russell Terrier may benefit from diets supporting Eye Conditions.

Living Space and

Italian Greyhound (Small (7-14 lbs), affectionate, playful, alert) occupies space differently than Jack Russell Terrier (Small (13-17 lbs), fearless, energetic, intelligent). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Italian Greyhound's moderate (30-45 min daily) energy creates one footprint, while Jack Russell Terrier's very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity level creates another.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Italian Greyhound's affectionate, playful, alert temperament and moderate (30-45 min daily) activity needs must remain compatible with your lifestyle through potential moves, career changes, and family growth. Jack Russell Terrier's fearless, energetic, intelligent character and very high (1-2+ hours daily) demands create a different long-term compatibility profile. Care complexity evolves with age: Italian Greyhound's health predispositions (Orthopedic Issues) and Jack Russell Terrier's risks (Eye Conditions) may require increasing management in later years.
