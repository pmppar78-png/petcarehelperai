Norwich Terrier: Breed Background and Character

The Norwich Terrier is one of the smallest working terriers, distinguished by its erect, pointed ears (unlike its drop-eared cousin, the Norfolk Terrier). Developed in East Anglia, England, these compact hunters were bred to hunt vermin and bolt foxes from their dens. In contrast, Norwich Terrier brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience.
