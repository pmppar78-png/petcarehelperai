Your First 30 Days with a Skye Terrier

A Skye Terrier is long and low, with a coat that covers the eyes and a back that does not love repeated jumps off the sofa. The first month is floor traction, ramps, a comb that reaches the skin, and a vet visit. Tie the hair out of the eyes so the dog can see the stairs. Keep the puppy off the furniture until the ramps are in place.
