Skye Terrier's loyal, courageous, dignified character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Sloughi's noble, reserved, graceful nature. In daily life, this means Skye Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward loyal behavior, while Sloughi owners find their dog more inclined toward noble tendencies. The household has to live with loyal, courageous, dignified on one side and noble, reserved, graceful on the other.
