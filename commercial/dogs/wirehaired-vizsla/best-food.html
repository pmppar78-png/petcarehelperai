Ideal Portion Control for Wirehaired Vizsla

Use the manufacturer's guidelines as a starting point, then adjust based on your Wirehaired Vizsla's body condition — you should be able to feel the ribs without seeing them, and there should be a visible waist from above. Split daily food into two meals for adults, three to four for growing Wirehaired Vizslas, and keep treats under 10% of total daily calories.
