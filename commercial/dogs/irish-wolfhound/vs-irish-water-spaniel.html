Temperament and Personality Differences

Irish Wolfhound brings a gentle, dignified, courageous energy to the household, compared to Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave disposition.

Best for Families with Children

Irish Wolfhound's gentle nature and Irish Water Spaniel's playful temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Irish Wolfhound is predisposed to Major Health Concerns, hereditary conditions including potential eye, dental, and metabolic issues, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Ask the veterinarian about Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) and Osteosarcoma for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Living Space and Habitat Requirements

Irish Water Spaniel needs space accommodating their Large (45-68 lbs) build, high activity needs, and playful, hardworking, brave behavioral style.
