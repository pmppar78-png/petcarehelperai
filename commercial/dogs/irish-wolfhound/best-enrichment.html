Originally bred to hunt wolves, elk, and wild boar, these magnificent dogs were so valued by Irish chieftains that they were given as royal gifts and their export was once restricted by law.

Despite their imposing size and fierce hunting heritage, Irish Wolfhounds are famously described as "gentle giants" - calm, patient, and remarkably sweet-natured.
