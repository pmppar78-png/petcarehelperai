Common Mistakes New Basenji Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding Basenji's moderate exercise needs, low grooming requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; Basenji dogs at Small to Medium (22-24 lbs) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding.
