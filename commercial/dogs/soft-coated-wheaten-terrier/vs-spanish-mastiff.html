Temperament and Personality

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier is characterized by a friendly, happy, devoted personality, while Spanish Mastiff tends toward affectionate, determined, noble traits.

Best for Families

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier's friendly nature and Spanish Mastiff's affectionate temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier is predisposed to Protein-Losing Conditions, Other Conditions, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Ask the veterinarian about Protein-Losing Nephropathy (PLN) and Protein-Losing Enteropathy (PLE) for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Elbow Dysplasia for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Exercise and Activity

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier requires moderate to high (1 hour daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Spanish Mastiff needs low activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier has low (single coat) grooming needs, while Spanish Mastiff requires high maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Medium (30-40 lbs) vs Large (140-200 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low (single coat) vs high), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier's 12-14 years expected life and Spanish Mastiff's 10-12 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier if your lifestyle accommodates their moderate to high (1 hour daily) activity needs, low (single coat) grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their friendly temperament. Choose Spanish Mastiff if you prefer their low energy level, can manage high maintenance, and appreciate their affectionate personality.

Best for First

Feeding and Nutrition

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier at Medium (30-40 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their moderate to high (1 hour daily) activity level, while Spanish Mastiff at Large (140-200 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their low energy output. Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier's predisposition to Protein-Losing Conditions may require specialized dietary formulations, while Spanish Mastiff may benefit from diets supporting joint health and mobility.

Living Space and

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier (Medium (30-40 lbs), friendly, happy, devoted) occupies space differently than Spanish Mastiff (Large (140-200 lbs), affectionate, determined, noble). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier's moderate to high (1 hour daily) energy creates one footprint, while Spanish Mastiff's low activity level creates another.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier's 12-14 years expected life will include a vibrant youth, stable adulthood, and eventual senior phase with increasing health needs related to Protein-Losing Conditions. Spanish Mastiff's 10-12 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (skeletal and joint concerns) and different care demands (moderate versus good (can be stubborn)).
