Temperament and Personality Differences

East European Shepherd's loyal, protective, balanced character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than English Bulldog's calm, courageous, friendly nature. In daily life, this means East European Shepherd owners typically experience a dog that leans toward loyal behavior, while English Bulldog owners find their dog more inclined toward calm tendencies.
