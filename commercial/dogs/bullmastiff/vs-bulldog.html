Bullmastiff: Breed Background and Character

The Bullmastiff was developed in 19th century England by crossing Mastiffs (60%) with Bulldogs (40%) to create the perfect gamekeeper's dog. Their job was to track poachers silently, then knock them down and hold them without mauling - earning them the nickname "Gamekeeper's Night Dog." These characteristics make Bullmastiff a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their loyal, brave, affectionate nature means daily life involves moderate (30-45 minutes daily) activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Large (100-130 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Bullmastiff's good (with experienced handler) care level requires consistent attention throughout their 7-9 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
