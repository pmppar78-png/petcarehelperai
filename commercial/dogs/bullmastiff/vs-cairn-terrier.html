Temperament and Personality Differences

Bullmastiff is characterized by a loyal, brave, affectionate personality, while Cairn Terrier tends toward alert, busy, cheerful traits. In daily life, this means Bullmastiff owners typically experience a dog that leans toward loyal behavior, while Cairn Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward alert tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Bullmastiff's loyal nature and Cairn Terrier's alert temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Elbow Dysplasia for the first animal and about Legg-Calve-Perthes Disease and Patellar Luxation for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Cairn Terrier's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Bullmastiff.
