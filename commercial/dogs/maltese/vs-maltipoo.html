Choose Maltese If...

  • You already enjoy the kind of human-dog interaction style the Maltese is known for — the Maltipoo's style would feel like a stretch.
  • The Maltese's long-term health outlook is one you can support with consistent preventive care and appropriate insurance.

Choose Maltipoo If...

  • The Maltipoo's daily care load — exercise, grooming, mental stimulation — fits into the rhythm your household already has.
  • The temperament you want around dinner, on walks, and during stressful weeks is closer to the Maltipoo's than the Maltese's.
  • You're prepared to fund the Maltipoo's typical insurance, screening, and preventive-care profile through senior years.

Ownership and health

Temperament and Personality

Maltese is characterized by a gentle, playful, charming personality, while Maltipoo tends toward affectionate, playful, gentle traits.

Best for Families

Maltese's gentle nature and Maltipoo's affectionate temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Maltese is predisposed to Dental Issues, Joint Issues, Other Conditions, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Maltipoo faces its own health challenges including orthopedic problems, Eye & Dental Issues, Other Concerns. Ask the veterinarian about Portosystemic shunts, patellar luxation, and tracheal collapse are the three to know by name for the first animal and about Luxating Patella and Legg-Calve-Perthes Disease for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Maltipoo's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Maltese.

Exercise and Activity

Maltese requires low-moderate (30 minutes daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Maltipoo needs low to moderate (20-40 min daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Maltese has very low (hypoallergenic) grooming needs, while Maltipoo requires very low maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

The size difference between Maltese (Toy (4-7 lbs)) and Maltipoo (5-20 lbs (typically 8-14 lbs)) significantly impacts costs across food, supplies, and veterinary care. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Toy (4-7 lbs) vs 5-20 lbs (typically 8-14 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (very low (hypoallergenic) vs very low), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Maltese's 12-15 years expected life and Maltipoo's 12-16 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Maltese: low-moderate (30 minutes daily) engagement vs Maltipoo: low to moderate (20-40 min daily)), grooming tolerance (very low (hypoallergenic) vs very low), and personality preference (gentle vs affectionate).

Feeding and Nutrition

Maltese's Toy (4-7 lbs) frame and low-moderate (30 minutes daily) energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Maltipoo's 5-20 lbs (typically 8-14 lbs) build and low to moderate (20-40 min daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Maltese's health profile (Dental Issues, Joint Issues) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Maltipoo's predispositions (orthopedic problems, Eye & Dental Issues) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Maltese at Toy (4-7 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and low-moderate (30 minutes daily) activity pattern, while Maltipoo at 5-20 lbs (typically 8-14 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and low to moderate (20-40 min daily) energy level. Maltese's gentle, playful, charming temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Maltipoo's affectionate, playful, gentle nature creates different environmental needs.

Insurance and Health

Maltese faces health risks from Dental Issues and Joint Issues that generate specific claim patterns, while Maltipoo's orthopedic problems and Eye & Dental Issues drives different insurance utilization. Over Maltese's 12-15 years lifespan, expected veterinary costs may differ significantly from Maltipoo's 12-16 years cost horizon. Size-driven cost differences (Toy (4-7 lbs) versus 5-20 lbs (typically 8-14 lbs)) affect medication dosing, surgical complexity, and equipment costs—all factors that influence insurance claim amounts.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Maltese's gentle, playful, charming temperament and low-moderate (30 minutes daily) activity needs must remain compatible with your lifestyle through potential moves, career changes, and family growth. Maltipoo's affectionate, playful, gentle character and low to moderate (20-40 min daily) demands create a different long-term compatibility profile. Care complexity evolves with age: Maltese's health predispositions (Dental Issues) and Maltipoo's risks (orthopedic problems) may require increasing management in later years.
