The temperament contrast between Maltese and Maltipoo is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. In daily life, this means Maltese owners typically experience a dog that leans toward gentle behavior, while Maltipoo owners find their dog more inclined toward affectionate tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences.
