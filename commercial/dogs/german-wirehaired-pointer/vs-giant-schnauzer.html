German Wirehaired Pointer is characterized by an affectionate, eager, determined personality, while Giant Schnauzer tends toward loyal, alert, trainable traits. In daily life, this means German Wirehaired Pointer owners typically experience a dog that leans toward affectionate behavior, while Giant Schnauzer owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies.
