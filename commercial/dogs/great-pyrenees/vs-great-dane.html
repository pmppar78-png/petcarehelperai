Choose Great Dane if you prefer their moderate (1-2 hours daily) energy level, can manage moderate maintenance, and appreciate their friendly personality.

Great Dane needs space accommodating their Giant (110-175 lbs) build, moderate (1-2 hours daily) activity needs, and friendly, patient, dependable behavioral style.
