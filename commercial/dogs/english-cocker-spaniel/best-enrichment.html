Quick Answer

An English Cocker Spaniel is a hunting spaniel, lighter and more work-oriented than many American cockers kept as pets. Rage syndrome, a rare sudden aggression linked in some lines to a genetic finding, and ear disease are the topics to raise with a breeder. They need exercise and coat care. A spaniel ear that smells sweet is yeast until proven otherwise.

Social Enrichment for English Cocker Spaniel

This breed's merry, affectionate, busy personality means they benefit from appropriately structured social experiences. However, respect your individual English Cocker Spaniel's social preferences; forcing interaction causes stress rather than enrichment.
