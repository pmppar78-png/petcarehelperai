Best for Budget-Conscious English Cocker Spaniel Owners

English Cockers bring primary glaucoma, chronic ear disease under heavy leather, and familial nephropathy in some lines. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, measured, because a spaniel face looks hungry even when the ribs are buried. A general practitioner who measures eye pressure and flushes ears is the one to keep. Insure before glaucoma or a kidney value is recorded. Weekly ear checks at home are the cheap prevention. The expensive path is a total ear-canal surgery after years of yeast.
