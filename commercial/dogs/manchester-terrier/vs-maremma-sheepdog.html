Cost of Ownership

The size difference between Manchester Terrier (Standard: 12-22 lbs; Toy: under 12 lbs) and Maremma Sheepdog (Large (66-100 lbs)) significantly impacts costs across food, supplies, and veterinary care. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Standard: 12-22 lbs; Toy: under 12 lbs vs Large (66-100 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low vs high (long white coat)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Manchester Terrier's 15-17 years expected life and Maremma Sheepdog's 11-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.
