Best for Budget-Conscious Chesapeake Bay Retriever Owners

Chesapeakes are large retrievers with hip dysplasia, bloat risk, and eye disease on the screening list. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, matched to a dog who swims hard and then wants the whole bowl. A general practitioner who does hip screening and knows gastric dilatation-volvulus is the clinic to keep. Start the policy before those screening results exist. A drying routine after water work belongs next to the food bill. Wet ears in this retriever become a standing pharmacy cost.
