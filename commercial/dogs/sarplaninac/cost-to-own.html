Best for Budget-Conscious Sarplaninac Owners

A Sarplaninac is a giant livestock guardian, so plan for hip disease, bloat, and wounds from other animals. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, fed for a working mountain dog and reduced if the dog is only pacing a yard. Keep a general practitioner who can sedate a guardian breed calmly and who will discuss gastropexy. Insure before the first orthopedic note or bite-wound repair. Budget serious fencing and livestock-guardian insurance questions. A roaming guardian creates liability far above the food bill.
