Black and Tan Coonhound: Breed Background and Character

The Black and Tan Coonhound is one of America's earliest native breeds, developed from Bloodhounds and Foxhounds brought to the colonies. They were the first coonhound breed to be recognized by the AKC (in 1945) and are known for their exceptional tracking ability and distinctive appearance. These characteristics make Black and Tan Coonhound a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their easygoing, friendly, bright nature means daily life involves high (1-2 hours daily) activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Large (65-110 lbs) frame.
