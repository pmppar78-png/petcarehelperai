Jack Russell Terrier: Breed Background and Character

The Jack Russell Terrier is a small, fearless, and incredibly energetic breed developed in England in the 1800s by Reverend John Russell for fox hunting. These compact terriers were bred to be bold enough to chase foxes from their dens while being small enough to follow them underground. These characteristics make Jack Russell Terrier a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their fearless, energetic, intelligent nature means daily life involves very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Small (13-17 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Jack Russell Terrier's good (but independent) care level requires consistent attention throughout their 13-16 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
