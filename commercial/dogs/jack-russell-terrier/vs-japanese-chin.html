Temperament and Personality Differences

Jack Russell Terrier's fearless, energetic, intelligent character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Japanese Chin's charming, noble, loving nature. In daily life, this means Jack Russell Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward fearless behavior, while Japanese Chin owners find their dog more inclined toward charming tendencies. The household has to live with fearless, energetic, intelligent on one side and charming, noble, loving on the other.
