Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Jack Russell Terrier and Italian Greyhound is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. Jack Russell Terrier is characterized by a fearless, energetic, intelligent personality, while Italian Greyhound tends toward affectionate, playful, alert traits. In daily life, this means Jack Russell Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward fearless behavior, while Italian Greyhound owners find their dog more inclined toward affectionate tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences.

Italian Greyhound faces its own health challenges including Orthopedic Issues, Dental Problems, Other Conditions.
