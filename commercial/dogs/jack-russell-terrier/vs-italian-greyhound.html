Jack Russell Terrier is predisposed to Eye Conditions, orthopedic problems, Other Concerns, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Italian Greyhound faces its own health challenges including Orthopedic Issues, Dental Problems, Other Conditions. Exercise and Activity

Jack Russell Terrier requires very high (1-2+ hours daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Italian Greyhound needs moderate (30-45 min daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small (13-17 lbs) vs Small (7-14 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs low), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Jack Russell Terrier's 13-16 years expected life and Italian Greyhound's 14-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Jack Russell Terrier if your lifestyle accommodates their very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity needs, moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their fearless temperament.

Feeding and Nutrition

Jack Russell Terrier's Small (13-17 lbs) frame and very high (1-2+ hours daily) energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Italian Greyhound's Small (7-14 lbs) build and moderate (30-45 min daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Jack Russell Terrier's health profile (Eye Conditions, orthopedic problems) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Italian Greyhound's predispositions (Orthopedic Issues, Dental Problems) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Jack Russell Terrier requires crate space suited to a Small (13-17 lbs) dog with very high (1-2+ hours daily) exercise demands and a fearless, energetic, intelligent disposition. Italian Greyhound needs space accommodating their Small (7-14 lbs) build, moderate (30-45 min daily) activity needs, and affectionate, playful, alert behavioral style.

Insurance and Health

Jack Russell Terrier is predisposed to Eye Conditions and orthopedic problems, making coverage for hereditary conditions essential. Italian Greyhound's risk factors (Orthopedic Issues and Dental Problems) require different policy features. Wellness coverage value also differs: Jack Russell Terrier's very high (1-2+ hours daily) activity level versus Italian Greyhound's moderate (30-45 min daily) demands mean different injury risk profiles.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Jack Russell Terrier's 13-16 years expected life will include a vibrant youth, stable adulthood, and eventual senior phase with increasing health needs related to Eye Conditions. Italian Greyhound's 14-15 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (Orthopedic Issues) and different care demands (moderate (can be stubborn) versus good (but independent)).
