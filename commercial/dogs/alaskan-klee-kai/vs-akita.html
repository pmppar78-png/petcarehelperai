In daily life, this means Alaskan Klee Kai owners typically experience a dog that leans toward intelligent behavior, while Akita owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies. The household has to live with intelligent, energetic, loyal on one side and loyal, courageous, dignified on the other.
