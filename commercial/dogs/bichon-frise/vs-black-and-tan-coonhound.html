Black and Tan Coonhound: Breed Background and Character

The Black and Tan Coonhound is one of America's earliest native breeds, developed from Bloodhounds and Foxhounds brought to the colonies. They were the first coonhound breed to be recognized by the AKC (in 1945) and are known for their exceptional tracking ability and distinctive appearance. In contrast, Black and Tan Coonhound brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their easygoing, friendly, bright personality creates a daily dynamic involving high (1-2 hours daily) energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Large (65-110 lbs) build.
