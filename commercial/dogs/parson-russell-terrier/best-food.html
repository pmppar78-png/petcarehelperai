Parson Russell Terrier Nutritional Profile

The Parson Russell Terrier has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Small (13-17 lbs) build and bold temperament. Parson Russell Terrier's compact build means calorie needs are lower in absolute terms but higher per pound of body weight than larger dogs.

Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Parson Russell Terrier

Parson Russell Terriers are the leggier, fox-bolting type, and they need fuel for work without a round belly. Puppies eat a complete small-breed puppy food on a schedule, with a waist. Adults that hunt or do a lot of sport eat more than adults that only walk. Keep two written portions. Seniors live a long time and collect dental disease. Brush if the dog allows, and budget cleanings. A senior that skips meals may have a painful tooth. See the vet before you entice it with fatty add-ins. Use the daily food for training so biscuits stay inside the ration. These terriers are persistent raiders. Latch the bin. A lean Parson jumps cleanly. A heavy one still jumps and lands harder.
