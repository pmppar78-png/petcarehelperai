Temperament and Personality Differences

Cairn Terrier brings an alert, busy, cheerful energy to the household, compared to Bullmastiff's loyal, brave, affectionate disposition. In daily life, this means Cairn Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward alert behavior, while Bullmastiff owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Cairn Terrier's alert nature and Bullmastiff's loyal temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.
