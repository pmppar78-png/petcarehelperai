Cairn Terrier: Breed Background and Character

The Cairn Terrier is one of Scotland's oldest working terrier breeds, developed to hunt vermin among the cairns (stone piles used as landmarks and memorials) of the Scottish Highlands. Bullmastiff: Breed Background and Character

The Bullmastiff was developed in 19th century England by crossing Mastiffs (60%) with Bulldogs (40%) to create the perfect gamekeeper's dog. Their job was to track poachers silently, then knock them down and hold them without mauling - earning them the nickname "Gamekeeper's Night Dog." In contrast, Bullmastiff brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their loyal, brave, affectionate personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate (30-45 minutes daily) energy management, moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Large (100-130 lbs) build. The good (with experienced handler) care demands of Bullmastiff span their 7-9 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
