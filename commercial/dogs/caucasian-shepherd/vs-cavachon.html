Temperament and Personality Differences

Caucasian Shepherd brings a powerful, alert, fearless energy to the household, compared to Cavachon's gentle, cheerful, affectionate disposition. In daily life, this means Caucasian Shepherd owners typically experience a dog that leans toward powerful behavior, while Cavachon owners find their dog more inclined toward gentle tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

Caucasian Shepherd's powerful nature and Cavachon's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Caucasian Shepherd is predisposed to joint problems including hip dysplasia, cardiac conditions, and other health issues common in larger breeds, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Elbow Dysplasia for the first animal and about Mitral Valve Disease (MVD) and Patent Ductus Arteriosus for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.
