Norwich Terrier: Breed Background and Character

The Norwich Terrier is one of the smallest working terriers, distinguished by its erect, pointed ears (unlike its drop-eared cousin, the Norfolk Terrier). Developed in East Anglia, England, these compact hunters were bred to hunt vermin and bolt foxes from their dens. These characteristics make Norwich Terrier a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their affectionate, alert, curious nature means daily life involves moderate (30-45 minutes daily) activity management, low (wire coat) grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Small (12 lbs) frame.
