Boerboel: Breed Background and Character

The Boerboel (pronounced "boo-r-bull") is a powerful South African mastiff developed by Dutch, German, and Huguenot settlers to protect their homesteads against dangerous predators including lions, leopards, and hyenas. The name translates to "farmer's dog" in Afrikaans. These characteristics make Boerboel a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their confident, protective, calm nature means daily life involves moderate (45-60 minutes daily) activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Giant (150-200 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Boerboel's good (needs experienced handler) care level requires consistent attention throughout their 9-11 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
