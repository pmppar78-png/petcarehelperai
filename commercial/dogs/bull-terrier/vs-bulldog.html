Temperament and Personality Differences

Bull Terrier brings a playful, charming, mischievous energy to the household, compared to Bulldog (English Bulldog)'s calm, courageous, friendly disposition. In daily life, this means Bull Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward playful behavior, while Bulldog (English Bulldog) owners find their dog more inclined toward calm tendencies. The household has to live with playful, charming, mischievous on one side and calm, courageous, friendly on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Bull Terrier's playful nature and Bulldog (English Bulldog)'s calm temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.
