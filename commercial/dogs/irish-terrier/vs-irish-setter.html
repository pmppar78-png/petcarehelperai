Choose Irish Terrier If...

  • Your weekly schedule reliably absorbs the Irish Terrier's exercise, training, and enrichment minimums — not just on good weeks.
  • The Irish Terrier's social and behavioural baseline lines up with the people, kids, or other pets already in the home.
  • You can plan around the Irish Terrier's known health predispositions without that planning crowding out other priorities.

Choose Irish Setter If...

  • You already enjoy the kind of human-dog interaction style the Irish Setter is known for — the Irish Terrier's style would feel like a stretch.
  • The Irish Setter's long-term health outlook is one you can support with consistent preventive care and appropriate insurance.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Irish Terrier brings a bold, dashing, spirited energy to the household, compared to Irish Setter's outgoing, active, sweet-natured disposition. In daily life, this means Irish Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward bold behavior, while Irish Setter owners find their dog more inclined toward outgoing tendencies. The household has to live with bold, dashing, spirited on one side and outgoing, active, sweet-natured on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Irish Terrier's bold nature and Irish Setter's outgoing temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Irish Setter faces its own health challenges including joint and skeletal conditions, Digestive Issues, Other Conditions. Ask the veterinarian about Patellar Luxation and Legg-Calve-Perthes for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Osteochondrosis (OCD) for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Irish Setter's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Irish Terrier.

Feeding and Nutrition Comparison

Irish Terrier at Medium (25-27 lbs) needs caloric intake calibrated to their high activity level, while Irish Setter at Large (60-70 lbs) requires nutrition matched to their high (1-2 hours daily) energy output. Irish Terrier's tendency toward hip dysplasia and other orthopedic problems may require specialized dietary formulations, while Irish Setter may benefit from diets supporting joint health and mobility.

Living Space and Habitat Requirements

Irish Terrier (Medium (25-27 lbs), bold, dashing, spirited) occupies space differently than Irish Setter (Large (60-70 lbs), outgoing, active, sweet-natured). Daily activity patterns influence space usage—Irish Terrier's high energy creates one footprint, while Irish Setter's high (1-2 hours daily) activity level creates another.
