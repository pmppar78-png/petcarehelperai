Temperament and Personality Differences

Irish Terrier's bold, dashing, spirited character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave nature. In daily life, this means Irish Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward bold behavior, while Irish Water Spaniel owners find their dog more inclined toward playful tendencies. The household has to live with bold, dashing, spirited on one side and playful, hardworking, brave on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Irish Terrier's bold nature and Irish Water Spaniel's playful temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.
