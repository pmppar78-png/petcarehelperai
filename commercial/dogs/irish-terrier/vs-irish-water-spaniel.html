Irish Terrier's bold nature and Irish Water Spaniel's playful temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Ask the veterinarian about Patellar Luxation and Legg-Calve-Perthes for the first animal and about Hip Dysplasia and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Grooming and Maintenance

Cost of Ownership

Over a complete lifespan, Irish Terrier's 13-15 years expected life and Irish Water Spaniel's 12-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Irish Terrier if your lifestyle accommodates their high activity needs, moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their bold temperament. Choose Irish Water Spaniel if you prefer their high energy level, can manage moderate maintenance, and appreciate their playful personality.

Feeding and Nutrition

Irish Terrier's Medium (25-27 lbs) frame and high energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Irish Water Spaniel's Large (45-68 lbs) build and high activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Irish Terrier's health profile (orthopedic problems such as ligament injuries and other genetic predispositions) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Irish Water Spaniel's predispositions (joint-related conditions and other breed-specific health issues) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Irish Terrier at Medium (25-27 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and high activity pattern, while Irish Water Spaniel at Large (45-68 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and high energy level. Irish Terrier's bold, dashing, spirited temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Irish Water Spaniel's playful, hardworking, brave nature creates different environmental needs.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Irish Terrier (Medium (25-27 lbs), moderate care demands) and Irish Water Spaniel (Large (45-68 lbs), moderate care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Irish Terrier's high exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Irish Water Spaniel's high activity needs cannot be neglected.
