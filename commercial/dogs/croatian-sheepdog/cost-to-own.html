Best for Budget-Conscious Croatian Sheepdog Owners

Croatian Sheepdogs are herding dogs, so budget hip screening, eye exams, and the occasional injury from real stock work. Structure the food spend around a mid-tier premium brand purchased in 30- to 40-pound bags, adjusted when the dog is working and when it is only walking the block. A general practitioner who will radiograph hips in a rare breed is the relationship to build. Insure before the first orthopedic film. Budget a coat rake and a tick check if the dog works outdoors. A hidden hotspot under that coat is a shave-and-treat bill.
