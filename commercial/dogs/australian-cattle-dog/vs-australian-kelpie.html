Australian Cattle Dog is characterized by an alert, curious, loyal personality, while Australian Kelpie tends toward intelligent, alert, energetic traits. In daily life, this means Australian Cattle Dog owners typically experience a dog that leans toward alert behavior, while Australian Kelpie owners find their dog more inclined toward intelligent tendencies. The household has to live with alert, curious, loyal on one side and intelligent, alert, energetic on the other.
