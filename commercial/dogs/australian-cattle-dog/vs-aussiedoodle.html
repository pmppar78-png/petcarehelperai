The Australian Cattle Dog, also known as the Blue Heeler, Red Heeler, or Queensland Heeler, was developed in Australia in the 19th century to herd cattle over vast distances in harsh conditions. Aussiedoodle: Breed Background and Character

The Aussiedoodle (also known as Aussiepoo) is a striking designer breed created by crossing an Australian Shepherd with a Poodle. This combination produces an incredibly intelligent, athletic, and often stunningly beautiful dog that excels in active households. In contrast, Aussiedoodle brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their intelligent, energetic, loyal personality creates a daily dynamic involving high (1-2 hours daily) energy management, low to moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Standard (45-70 lbs), Mini (15-35 lbs), Toy (10-15 lbs) build. The excellent care demands of Aussiedoodle span their 10-14 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
