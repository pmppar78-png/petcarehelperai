Social Enrichment for Australian Cattle Dog

This breed's alert, curious, loyal personality means they benefit from appropriately structured social experiences. For Australian Cattle dogs that enjoy company of their own kind, supervised playdates or group activities can provide valuable peer interaction. However, respect your individual Australian Cattle Dog's social preferences; forcing interaction causes stress rather than enrichment.
