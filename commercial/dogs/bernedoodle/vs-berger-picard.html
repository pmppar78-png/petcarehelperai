Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Bernedoodle and Berger Picard is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these dogs. Bernedoodle is characterized by a loyal, goofy, affectionate personality, while Berger Picard tends toward loyal, observant, good-natured traits. In daily life, this means Bernedoodle owners typically experience a dog that leans toward loyal behavior, while Berger Picard owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies. The better temperament is the one that matches you — there is no universal winner.
