Ownership and health

Temperament and Personality

Bernedoodle is characterized by a loyal, goofy, affectionate personality, while Berger Picard tends toward loyal, observant, good-natured traits.

Best for Families

Bernedoodle's loyal nature and Berger Picard's loyal temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Berger Picard faces its own health challenges including Eye Conditions, hip and joint concerns along with other health conditions common in this breed. Ask the veterinarian about Hip Dysplasia and Elbow Dysplasia for the first animal and about Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA) and Cataracts for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Berger Picard's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Bernedoodle.

Grooming and Maintenance

Cost of Ownership

The size difference between Bernedoodle (Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs)) and Berger Picard (Medium to Large (50-70 lbs)) significantly impacts costs across food, supplies, and veterinary care. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs) vs Medium to Large (50-70 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low to moderate vs low to moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Bernedoodle's 12-18 years expected life and Berger Picard's 12-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Bernedoodle: moderate (45-60 min daily) engagement vs Berger Picard: high (1-2 hours daily)), grooming tolerance (low to moderate vs low to moderate), and personality preference (loyal vs loyal).

Feeding and Nutrition

Bernedoodle's Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs) frame and moderate (45-60 min daily) energy demands require specific caloric targeting, while Berger Picard's Medium to Large (50-70 lbs) build and high (1-2 hours daily) activity level call for different nutritional proportions. Bernedoodle's health profile (joint and skeletal conditions, Other Concerns) may necessitate prescription or limited-ingredient diets, while Berger Picard's predispositions (Eye Conditions, joint and skeletal conditions) have their own dietary implications.

Living Space and

Bernedoodle at Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and moderate (45-60 min daily) activity pattern, while Berger Picard at Medium to Large (50-70 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and high (1-2 hours daily) energy level. Bernedoodle's loyal, goofy, affectionate temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Berger Picard's loyal, observant, good-natured nature creates different environmental needs.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Bernedoodle's 12-18 years expected life will include a vibrant youth, stable adulthood, and eventual senior phase with increasing health needs related to joint and skeletal conditions. Berger Picard's 12-13 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (Eye Conditions) and different care demands (good (can be stubborn) versus very good).
