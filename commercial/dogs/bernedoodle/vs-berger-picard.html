Bernedoodle: Breed Background and Character

The Bernedoodle is a stunning designer breed created by crossing a Bernese Mountain Dog with a Poodle. First intentionally bred in 2003, Bernedoodles combine the loyal, gentle nature of the Bernese with the intelligence and hypoallergenic coat of the Poodle. These characteristics make Bernedoodle a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their loyal, goofy, affectionate nature means daily life involves moderate (45-60 min daily) activity management, low to moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Standard (70-90 lbs), Mini (25-49 lbs), Tiny (10-24 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Bernedoodle's very good care level requires consistent attention throughout their 12-18 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
