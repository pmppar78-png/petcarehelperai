Physical Exercise Recommendations for Border Terrier

Border Terrier dogs with affectionate, happy, plucky traits often enjoy varied exercise routines over repetitive ones. Patellar Luxation, Affectionate, Happy, Plucky, and Small 11-16 lbs belong in the enrichment plan for the Border Terrier.

Social Enrichment for Border Terrier

This breed's affectionate, happy, plucky personality means they benefit from appropriately structured social experiences. However, respect your individual Border Terrier's social preferences; forcing interaction causes stress rather than enrichment.

Best for Social Border Terrier

Even Border Terriers that are less social by temperament benefit from brief, low-intensity exposures to novel stimuli, because the interpretive work itself is cognitively engaging.

DIY Enrichment Ideas for Border Terrier

Activity-based DIY enrichment includes obstacle courses built from household items, sensory exploration stations using different safe textures and surfaces, and hide-and-seek games that leverage Border Terrier's natural affectionate instincts.
