King Shepherd: Breed Background and Character

The King Shepherd is a relatively new breed developed in the 1990s by American breeders Shelley Watts-Cross and David Turkheimer. Their goal was to create a larger, healthier shepherd with the German Shepherd's intelligence and trainability but with fewer health problems and a gentler temperament. These characteristics make King Shepherd a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their gentle, loyal, confident nature means daily life involves moderate to high (1-2 hours daily) activity management, high (heavy double coat) grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Giant (90-150 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that King Shepherd's excellent care level requires consistent attention throughout their 10-11 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
