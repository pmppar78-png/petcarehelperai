Best Food by

Life Stage Feeding

Young Mudis need nutrient-dense food with higher protein and fat to support growth — typically 20-40% more calories per pound than adults.

Growth-Phase Diet

Prime-of-Life Nutrition

Maintenance formulas for Mudi should reflect their high (60+ minutes daily) activity level with complete and balanced nutrition meeting AAFCO standards for adult dogs.

Adjusting Diet With

Ideal Portion Control

Use the manufacturer's guidelines as a starting point, then adjust based on your Mudi's body condition — you should be able to feel the ribs without seeing them, and there should be a visible waist from above. Split daily food into two meals for adults, three to four for growing Mudis, and keep treats under 10% of total daily calories.

Signs Your Is

Look for these signs that your Mudi's diet is working: steady weight maintenance without effort, well-formed stools with no persistent gas or loose bowel movements, a coat that stays shiny between grooming sessions, calm and consistent energy levels, and enthusiasm at mealtimes without obsessive food-seeking behavior.

Understanding Mudi's Dietary Heritage

With a natural versatile disposition and high (60+ minutes daily) activity pattern, Mudi converts calories to energy in characteristic ways that differ from other dogs. Owners who research Mudi's background gain insights that translate directly into better feeding decisions throughout every stage of their dog's life.
