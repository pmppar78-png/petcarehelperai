West Highland White Terrier Nutritional Profile

The West Highland White Terrier has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Small (15-20 lbs) build and loyal temperament. West Highland White Terrier's compact build means calorie needs are lower in absolute terms but higher per pound of body weight than larger dogs.

Life Stage Feeding

West Highland White Terriers are small, long-lived, and often itchy, and food is only sometimes the reason. Puppies need a complete small-breed puppy food, measured, with a waist under the white coat. Adults eat two measured meals. If the skin is allergic, a diet trial is a veterinary plan with one food, for weeks, with no sneaked treats. Buying a new bag every fortnight is how those trials fail. Seniors need dental care and often a lower calorie count. Pulmonary and skin disease are medical. A heavier bowl does not treat them. Part the coat and find ribs. These terriers gain in quiet homes. Use the kibble as the training treat. Wash the face. Tear staining and yeasty folds get worse if you add fatty scraps around the mouth.

Signs Your Is

You will know your West Highland White Terrier's diet is working when you see steady energy levels, a coat with a healthy sheen, firm and regular stools, and a stable weight.

Understanding West Highland White Terrier's Dietary Heritage

What worked for a West Highland White Terrier's ancestors — the activity types, the protein sources, the eating patterns — still influences what your West Highland White Terrier does best on today.
