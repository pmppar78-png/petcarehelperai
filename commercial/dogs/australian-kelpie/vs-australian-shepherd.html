Australian Kelpie: Breed Background and Character

The Australian Kelpie is a highly intelligent and tireless working dog developed in Australia specifically for herding livestock in the harsh Australian outback. Known for their remarkable endurance, these dogs can work sheep for hours in extreme heat, covering vast distances without tiring. These characteristics make Australian Kelpie a distinctive dog with specific care requirements. Their intelligent, alert, energetic nature means daily life involves very high (2+ hours daily) activity management, moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Medium (31-46 lbs) frame.
