Physical Exercise Recommendations

Chi-Poo dogs with energetic, playful, loving traits often enjoy varied exercise routines over repetitive ones.

Social Enrichment for

This breed's energetic, playful, loving personality means they benefit from appropriately structured social experiences. However, respect your individual Chi-Poo's social preferences; forcing interaction causes stress rather than enrichment.

DIY Enrichment Ideas

Build simple agility obstacles from household items: cushion tunnels, blanket tents, and cardboard mazes scaled for Chi-Poo's Small (5-20 lbs) frame.

Weekly Enrichment Schedule

Track your Chi-Poo's engagement and behavioral indicators to optimize the schedule over time for your individual dog's needs and preferences.
