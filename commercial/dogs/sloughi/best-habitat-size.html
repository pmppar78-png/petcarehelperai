Best for Climate Control

Outdoor climate considerations for Sloughi depend on physiology. Coated breeds manage cold better than heat; short-coated and brachycephalic breeds manage heat poorly. Build the exercise schedule around the daily temperature profile: early-morning and late-evening walks in hot weather, midday walks in cold weather. Skip outdoor exercise entirely at temperature extremes and substitute indoor enrichment.

Multi-Pet Household Setup for Sloughi

If introducing Sloughi into a home with existing dogs or other animals, careful space planning prevents territorial conflicts and stress. Each animal should have their own crate, feeding station, and resting area. For Sloughi with their noble, reserved, graceful temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact. Shared common areas should have multiple exit points so no animal feels trapped. Resource guarding is common during transitions; provide duplicate resources (food bowls, water sources, enrichment items) in separate locations. Monitor interactions closely during the first several weeks, and be prepared to separate dogs if signs of aggression or excessive stress appear.

Safety-Proofing Your Home for Sloughi

Safety-proofing for Sloughi is an ongoing process, not an one-time task. Start with the critical hazards: toxic household plants (over 700 common plants are toxic to dogs), accessible medications (even a single dropped pill can be dangerous), and unsecured cleaning chemicals. For a Large (35-50 lbs) dog like Sloughi, pay special attention to items at their height level that could be pulled down, heavy objects that could fall, and access to countertops or high shelves. Electrical cords should be covered or routed out of reach. Recheck safety measures every season as household items shift and new hazards emerge. Regular safety audits of your Sloughi's environment every few months catch new hazards as household items and arrangements change over time.

Seasonal Habitat Adjustments for Sloughi

Sloughi's crate setup requires seasonal modifications to maintain optimal comfort and safety year-round. During warm months, ensure adequate ventilation and cooling for your Large (35-50 lbs) dog—dogs of this breed can be sensitive to heat stress. Provide shaded rest areas and consider cooling accessories appropriate for Sloughi's size. Cold weather demands insulated resting spots, draft elimination around the crate, and potentially supplemental heating rated safe for dogs. Spring and autumn transitions often bring allergens and temperature fluctuations; monitor your Sloughi's comfort during these periods and adjust bedding and environmental controls accordingly.
