Temperament and Personality Differences

Sloughi's noble, reserved, graceful character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Skye Terrier's loyal, courageous, dignified nature. In daily life, this means Sloughi owners typically experience a dog that leans toward noble behavior, while Skye Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward loyal tendencies.

Best for Families with Children
