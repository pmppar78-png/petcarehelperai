Temperament and Personality Differences

Understanding how Rat Terrier and Redbone Coonhound differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. Rat Terrier's friendly, curious, lovable character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than Redbone Coonhound's even-tempered, amiable, mellow nature. In daily life, this means Rat Terrier owners typically experience a dog that leans toward friendly behavior, while Redbone Coonhound owners find their dog more inclined toward even-tempered tendencies. Neither is better in the abstract; pick the one that matches your personality and household rhythm.

Best for Families with Children

Evaluate each breed's interaction style with children. Rat Terrier's friendly nature and Redbone Coonhound's even-tempered temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Rat Terrier has a typical lifespan of 12-18 years, while Redbone Coonhound lives approximately 12-15 years. Health profiles differ significantly between these dogs. Rat Terrier is predisposed to orthopedic problems, Other Concerns, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Redbone Coonhound faces its own health challenges including hip and joint concerns along with other health conditions common in this breed. Similar predisposition counts, different specific risks, different management requirements. Insurance considerations differ between the two dogs based on these risk profiles. Prospective owners should discuss breed-specific health screening with a veterinarian before making their decision.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Redbone Coonhound's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Rat Terrier. That said, consistent preventive care is non-negotiable for both — the real question is which breed's health demands better fit your schedule and budget.

Exercise and Activity Level Differences

Activity requirements differ minimally between Rat Terrier and Redbone Coonhound. Rat Terrier requires moderate to high (40-60 min daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Redbone Coonhound needs high activity. Because the two breeds have similar activity levels, the time commitment is comparable — other criteria should decide. Rat Terrier owners should plan for 60-90 minutes of daily activity, compared to 60-90 minutes for Redbone Coonhound. Under-exercised dogs of either breed develop behavioral issues, but the consequences and management strategies differ.

Grooming and Maintenance Comparison

Daily and periodic maintenance requirements differ between Rat Terrier and Redbone Coonhound. Rat Terrier has moderate (seasonal) grooming needs, while Redbone Coonhound requires low maintenance. Professional grooming costs reflect these differences: Rat Terrier owners typically spend $200-$400 annually on grooming, compared to $0-$200 for Redbone Coonhound. Keep up with brushing, bathing, nails, and dental care at home; professional grooming supplements rather than replaces these. The time commitment for daily grooming and general home environment management is an important lifestyle consideration. Factor grooming costs and time into your total ownership commitment when deciding between these dogs.
