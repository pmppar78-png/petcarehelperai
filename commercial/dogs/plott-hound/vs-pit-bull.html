Temperament and Personality Differences

Plott Hound's bold, loyal, alert character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than American Pit Bull Terrier's confident, smart, good-natured nature. In daily life, this means Plott Hound owners typically experience a dog that leans toward bold behavior, while American Pit Bull Terrier owners find their dog more inclined toward confident tendencies.
