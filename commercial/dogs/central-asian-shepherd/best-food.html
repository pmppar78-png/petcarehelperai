Ideal Portion Control

Use the manufacturer's guidelines as a starting point, then adjust based on your Central Asian Shepherd's body condition — you should be able to feel the ribs without seeing them, and there should be a visible waist from above. Split daily food into two meals for adults, three to four for growing Central Asian Shepherds, and keep treats under 10% of total daily calories.

Best for Weight

Most overweight Central Asian Shepherds receive the right-looking portion plus the un-tracked calories from treats, chews, table scraps, and training rewards.

Understanding Central Asian Shepherd's Dietary Heritage

A Central Asian Shepherd's physical frame requires a specific calorie-to-nutrient ratio that changes across their 12-15 years lifespan.
