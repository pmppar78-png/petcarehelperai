Best for Families

Lagotto Romagnolo's affectionate nature and Lakeland Terrier's bold temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan

Lagotto Romagnolo is predisposed to Neurological Conditions, hip and joint issues, Other Concerns, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Lakeland Terrier faces its own health challenges including Eye Conditions, Orthopedic Issues, Other Concerns. Ask the veterinarian about Benign Familial Juvenile Epilepsy (BFJE) and Lagotto Storage Disease (LSD) for the first animal and about Lens Luxation and Cataracts for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low

Lagotto Romagnolo's predispositions typically require specific screening tests, while Lakeland Terrier has its own set of conditions to monitor.

Exercise and Activity

Lagotto Romagnolo requires moderate to high (60+ minutes daily) levels of exercise and engagement, while Lakeland Terrier needs moderate (30-60 minutes daily) activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Lagotto Romagnolo has low (hypoallergenic coat) grooming needs, while Lakeland Terrier requires low (wire coat) maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Medium (24-35 lbs) vs Small (15-17 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low (hypoallergenic coat) vs low (wire coat)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Lagotto Romagnolo's 15-17 years expected life and Lakeland Terrier's 12-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Lagotto Romagnolo if your lifestyle accommodates their moderate to high (60+ minutes daily) activity needs, low (hypoallergenic coat) grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their affectionate temperament. Choose Lakeland Terrier if you prefer their moderate (30-60 minutes daily) energy level, can manage low (wire coat) maintenance, and appreciate their bold personality.

Living Space and

Lagotto Romagnolo at Medium (24-35 lbs) needs a crate appropriately scaled to their dimensions and moderate to high (60+ minutes daily) activity pattern, while Lakeland Terrier at Small (15-17 lbs) requires crate sizing matched to their own build and moderate (30-60 minutes daily) energy level. Lagotto Romagnolo's affectionate, keen, undemanding, trainable temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Lakeland Terrier's bold, friendly, confident nature creates different environmental needs.

Insurance and Health

Lagotto Romagnolo faces health risks from Neurological Conditions and hip and joint issues that generate specific claim patterns, while Lakeland Terrier's Eye Conditions and Orthopedic Issues drives different insurance utilization. Over Lagotto Romagnolo's 15-17 years lifespan, expected veterinary costs may differ significantly from Lakeland Terrier's 12-15 years cost horizon. Size-driven cost differences (Medium (24-35 lbs) versus Small (15-17 lbs)) affect medication dosing, surgical complexity, and equipment costs—all factors that influence insurance claim amounts.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Lagotto Romagnolo (Medium (24-35 lbs), excellent care demands) and Lakeland Terrier (Small (15-17 lbs), good (independent thinker) care demands) each require sustained dedication but in different ways. Lagotto Romagnolo's moderate to high (60+ minutes daily) exercise requirements must be met consistently, just as Lakeland Terrier's moderate (30-60 minutes daily) activity needs cannot be neglected.
