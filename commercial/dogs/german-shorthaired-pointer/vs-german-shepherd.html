German Shorthaired Pointer has moderate grooming needs, while German Shepherd requires very high (year-round) maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Medium-Large (45-70 lbs) vs Large (50-90 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs very high (year-round)), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, German Shorthaired Pointer's 10-12 years expected life and German Shepherd's 9-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived dog accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

German Shorthaired Pointer's tendency toward hip dysplasia and other orthopedic problems may require specialized dietary formulations, while German Shepherd may benefit from diets supporting joint health and mobility.
