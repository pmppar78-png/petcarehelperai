Temperament and Personality Differences

Havanese is characterized by an intelligent, outgoing, funny personality, while Harrier tends toward friendly, outgoing, active traits. In daily life, this means Havanese owners typically experience a dog that leans toward intelligent behavior, while Harrier owners find their dog more inclined toward friendly tendencies.
