Quick Answer

Feeding Guidelines for Alaskan Malamute

What to Look For

  • Life-stage appropriate: Puppy, adult, and senior formulas are not interchangeable — pick the one that matches your Alaskan Malamute's current stage.
  • Calorie density match: The right calorie content for your Alaskan Malamute's size and activity level prevents both under- and over-feeding.

Alaskan Malamute Nutritional Profile

Feeding planning for an Alaskan Malamute rests on two easy-to-observe inputs, the Large (75-100 lbs) build and the affectionate behavioral profile, both translate directly into calorie and macronutrient choices. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Alaskan Malamute's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.

Ideal Portion Control for Alaskan Malamute

Measure portions, track weight, adjust every 2-4 weeks — portion control for an Alaskan Malamute is mostly about not skipping any of those steps. Two meals a day works for most adult Alaskan Malamutes.
