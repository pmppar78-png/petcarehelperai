In daily life, this means Irish Red and White Setter owners typically experience a dog that leans toward courageous behavior, while Irish Doodle owners find their dog more inclined toward friendly tendencies. Neither temperament is objectively better; the right choice depends on your personality and lifestyle preferences. Irish Red and White Setter may suit owners who appreciate courageous companions, while Irish Doodle appeals to those who value friendly characteristics in their dog.
