The first year with a retrieving house dog

UKC's character line is the one to believe: friendly, outgoing, eager to please, extremely intelligent, and easy to teach complex tasks. Shyness in a mature dog is a serious fault in that standard. VCA matches the picture from the clinic side, a social dog who usually does well with children and other pets once socialized, with high energy and a moderate tolerance for being alone only after exercise and training. The table on this page already says the time commitment runs from 30 minutes to two hours and more. A first-time owner who pictured a calm couch dog will feel that range in the first week. The dog is doing the job it was built for, carrying objects and asking for the next round.

Size arrives fast. AKC's males finish at 22.5 to 24.5 inches and 65 to 80 pounds, females at 21.5 to 23.5 inches and 55 to 70. The puppy who fits in a lap at ten weeks is the dog who will lean his full chest into a visitor at two years. Teach a sit for greetings while the puppy is still light. VCA's retrieving note, chewing and mouthing and sometimes swallowing objects, is the other first-month project. A soft mouth, which UKC lists as essential, is a retrieve to hand. It is also a puppy who grabs wrists. Trade the wrist for a toy every time, and keep the laundry behind a door. The supplies you replace are the cheap part. A swallowed sock is the expensive one.

Exercise has a ceiling as well as a floor. VCA wants significant daily work: walking, retrieving, swimming, training games. Merck's 2026 review of exercise-induced collapse describes weakness and collapse 5 to 20 minutes into intense exercise, with a rapid recovery and a normal dog in between. The Minnesota lab found the DNM1 variant common enough that roughly 30 to 40 percent of tested Labradors carry one copy. A first dog does not need a five-mile run at ten months. Short retrieves, a sniff walk, and a nap protect growing joints, which VCA already flags for hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, and osteochondritis dissecans in the puppy year, and they also avoid the intense-plus-excitement pattern that brings on collapse in dogs with two copies of the variant. Ask the breeder for the parents' EIC results along with hips, elbows, and eyes. The breed page on this site already names those papers. A seller who has never heard of dynamin-1 is handing you the research project.

Home layout matters more than a perfect yard. VCA says Labs do best with outdoor access and that they are adaptable about climate, with a short dense coat that sheds heavily in season. A first-time household can succeed in a house with a fenced yard and a person who is actually home for the midday break. The same household fails when everyone leaves for ten hours and the dog's exercise is a single dark walk. Time alone is a skill you build after the dog is tired, in a crate long enough for the adult size, placed where the family sits. UKC's ears are drop ears. After a muddy walk, wipe them. That thirty-second habit is the ear care VCA singles out for swimming and active Labs, and it starts in week one so the dog does not treat the ear as a fight.

Children and other pets go well in the VCA description when the dog is socialized, and they go badly when the retrieve drive has no rules. A toddler waving food is a moving dummy. A cat who runs is quarry. Introduce both with the Labrador on a leash, fed, and asked for a settle on a bed. Supervise. The standard's friendly temperament is the raw material. The sit at the doorway is the finished dog. Visitors get the same pattern: the dog sits, the guest enters, the retrieve toy comes out only after the greeting is calm. Skipping that because "Labs love everyone" is how a 70-pound chest knocks someone down in month four, when the puppy weight becomes the AKC adult weight.

Food and weight are a first-year skill, and they get harder to repair once the dog is grown. VCA says controlled growth and weight management matter because orthopedic disease gets worse when the dog is heavy. Feed the amount that keeps a waist, using the sex-specific AKC weights as the adult target, and keep treats inside that amount. A first-time owner who tops the bowl every time the dog stares will meet the joint list early. The cost page prints food at $30 to $100 a month for the ordinary plan. Stay there until a veterinarian changes the diet. Atopy and other medical diets are a response to a diagnosis, which shows up in the adult chapter of VCA's handout, after the first year has already taught you what this dog's ribs feel like.

Plan the people, too. A groomer is optional for this coat if you brush weekly and bathe after mud. A trainer is useful in the first month because the breed learns quickly and will also learn the wrong game quickly. A boarding kennel or a sitter needs a note that this dog eats non-food items and may be a swimmer, so socks and small toys are picked up and the pool gate stays shut. Insurance, priced before any limp is in the record, sits on the cost page at $15 to $60 a month. Buy it while the puppy is still a blank chart. The first year is busy because the dog is built that way. It is also the year the paperwork, the crate, and the retrieve rules are cheap compared with the adult who never learned them.
