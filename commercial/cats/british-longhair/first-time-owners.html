Common Mistakes New British Longhair Owners Make

The most common mistake is inadequate research: understanding British Longhair's low to moderate exercise needs, high grooming requirements, and health predispositions before acquisition prevents mismatched expectations. Overfeeding is another frequent issue; British Longhair cats at Medium to Large (8-18 lbs) require carefully measured portions, not free-feeding. Skipping early socialization limits your British Longhair's comfort in varied environments. Inconsistent rules and boundaries confuse cats with calm temperaments.
