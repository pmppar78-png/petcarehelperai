Arabian Mau Nutritional Profile

Understanding the Arabian Mau as a Medium to Large (8-16 lbs)-framed, affectionate breed is the right starting point for any feeding decision, size and temperament do most of the work in setting nutritional targets. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Arabian Mau's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.
