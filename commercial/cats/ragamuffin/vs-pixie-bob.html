Pixie-bob Cat: Breed Background and Character

The Pixie-bob is a domestic cat breed developed in Washington State in the 1980s to resemble the North American Bobcat. Despite legends suggesting wild bobcat heritage, DNA testing has confirmed they are fully domestic cats. The breed was founded by Carol Ann Brewer with a cat named Pixie. In contrast, Pixie-bob Cat brings different strengths and considerations to the ownership experience. Their loyal, intelligent, dog-like personality creates a daily dynamic involving moderate energy management, low to moderate maintenance routines, and space planning for their Medium to Large (8-17 lbs) build. The moderate care demands of Pixie-bob Cat span their 13-16 years lifespan, requiring owners to commit to ongoing engagement and health monitoring throughout.
