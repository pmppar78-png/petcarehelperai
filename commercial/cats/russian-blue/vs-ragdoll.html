Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Russian Blue Cat and Ragdoll Cat is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these cats. Russian Blue Cat is characterized by a gentle, loyal, reserved personality, while Ragdoll Cat tends toward docile, affectionate, relaxed traits. In daily life, this means Russian Blue Cat owners typically experience a cat that leans toward gentle behavior, while Ragdoll Cat owners find their cat more inclined toward docile tendencies. Neither temperament beats the other on an absolute basis; pick for fit with your life.
