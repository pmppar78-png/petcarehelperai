Pixie-bob Cat: Breed Background and Character

The Pixie-bob is a domestic cat breed developed in Washington State in the 1980s to resemble the North American Bobcat. Despite legends suggesting wild bobcat heritage, DNA testing has confirmed they are fully domestic cats. The breed was founded by Carol Ann Brewer with a cat named Pixie. These characteristics make Pixie-bob Cat a distinctive cat with specific care requirements. Their loyal, intelligent, dog-like nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, low to moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Medium to Large (8-17 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Pixie-bob Cat's moderate care level requires consistent attention throughout their 13-16 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
