Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Ragdoll Cat and Ragamuffin is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these cats. Ragdoll Cat is characterized by a docile, affectionate, relaxed personality, while Ragamuffin tends toward calm, friendly, sweet traits. In daily life, this means Ragdoll Cat owners typically experience a cat that leans toward docile behavior, while Ragamuffin owners find their cat more inclined toward calm tendencies. Pick based on personality and lifestyle fit, not on an imagined ranking of the two temperaments.

Best for Families with Children
