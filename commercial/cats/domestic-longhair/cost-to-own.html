Cost-Saving Strategies for Domestic Longhair Care

Best for Value-Conscious Owners

A domestic longhair is a mixed-breed cat with a long coat, not a pedigree. The useful facts are the ones you can see: weight usually in the 8 to 15 pound range, a lifespan often into the teens if the cat is indoors, and a coat that mats if nobody combs it. Dental disease, obesity, and not knowing the parents' hearts are the real risks. Comb to the skin and keep the cat inside.
