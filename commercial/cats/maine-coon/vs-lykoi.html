Living Space and

Maine Coon at Large (8-18 lb) needs an indoor space appropriately scaled to their dimensions and moderate to high activity pattern, while Lykoi Cat at Small to Medium (6-12 lbs) requires indoor space sizing matched to their own build and high energy level. Maine Coon's gentle, friendly, intelligent temperament influences how they interact with their living space, while Lykoi Cat's intelligent, loyal, playful nature creates different environmental needs.

Lykoi Cat's intelligent, loyal, playful character and high demands create a different long-term compatibility profile.
