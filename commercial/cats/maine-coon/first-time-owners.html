Common Mistakes New Maine Coon Owners Make

Mistake one: choosing Maine Coon based on appearance rather than lifestyle fit—this breed's moderate to high energy and moderate care demands must match your reality. Mistake three: socializing too aggressively or not at all—Maine Coon's gentle temperament requires gradual, positive exposure to new experiences. Mistake four: comparing your Maine Coon's progress to other cats online, which creates unrealistic expectations and unnecessary anxiety.
