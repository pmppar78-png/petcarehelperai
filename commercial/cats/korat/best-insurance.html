Pre-existing Condition Awareness for Korat Cat

Understanding pre-existing condition policies is crucial for Korat Cat owners. Most insurers exclude conditions diagnosed or showing symptoms before enrollment. For Korat Cat, this is particularly important because some breed-specific conditions like Genetic Conditions can present subtle early signs. During the waiting period (typically 14 days for illness, 48 hours for accidents), no claims can be filed. Some insurers will cover curable pre-existing conditions after a symptom-free period of 12-18 months. To maximize your Korat Cat's coverage, enroll as early as possible, ideally within the first few months of bringing your Korat Cat home, and maintain continuous coverage without lapses.
