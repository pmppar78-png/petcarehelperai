Temperament and Personality Differences

American Curl brings an affectionate, playful, curious energy to the household, compared to American Shorthair's easygoing, affectionate, adaptable disposition. In daily life, this means American Curl owners typically experience a cat that leans toward affectionate behavior, while American Shorthair owners find their cat more inclined toward easygoing tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

American Curl's affectionate nature and American Shorthair's easygoing temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

American Curl is predisposed to Ear-Related Concerns, General Health, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. American Shorthair faces its own health challenges including Cardiac Conditions, Weight-Related Issues, hereditary conditions including potential eye, dental, and metabolic issues. Ask the veterinarian about Ear Canal Narrowing and Ear Infections for the first animal and about Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and, in some lines, polycystic kidney disease are worth asking about for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but American Shorthair's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to American Curl.
