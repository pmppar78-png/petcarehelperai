Neither breed is truly "low maintenance" health-wise, but Cornish Rex's longer lifespan and different condition profile may mean fewer intensive interventions in middle age compared to Colorpoint Shorthair.

Cost of Ownership

Over a complete lifespan, Colorpoint Shorthair's 12-16 years expected life and Cornish Rex's 12-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived cat accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Colorpoint Shorthair if your lifestyle accommodates their high activity needs, low grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their vocal temperament. Choose Cornish Rex if you prefer their very high energy level, can manage low maintenance, and appreciate their playful personality.
