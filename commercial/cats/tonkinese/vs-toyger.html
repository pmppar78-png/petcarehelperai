Temperament and Personality Differences

The temperament contrast between Tonkinese Cat and Toyger Cat is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these cats. Tonkinese Cat is characterized by a social, intelligent, affectionate personality, while Toyger Cat tends toward friendly, intelligent, laid-back traits. In daily life, this means Tonkinese Cat owners typically experience a cat that leans toward social behavior, while Toyger Cat owners find their cat more inclined toward friendly tendencies. Neither temperament beats the other on an absolute basis; pick for fit with your life.
