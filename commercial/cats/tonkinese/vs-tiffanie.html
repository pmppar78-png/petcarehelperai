Tonkinese Cat is predisposed to Genetic Conditions, hereditary conditions including potential eye, dental, and metabolic issues, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Tiffanie Cat needs space accommodating their Medium (8-14 lbs) build, moderate activity needs, and gentle, affectionate, intelligent behavioral style.
