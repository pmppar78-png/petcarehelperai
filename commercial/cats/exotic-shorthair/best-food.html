Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Exotic Shorthair

Exotic Shorthairs are the flat-faced, plush version of a Persian, and extra weight makes the breathing and the face folds worse. Kittens should grow on a complete kitten food without being pushed to look round. Adults need measured meals, often with a raised dish if the flat face makes a deep bowl awkward. Wet food can help the water intake. Seniors commonly need dental care, and a painful mouth means smaller, softer complete meals, still measured. A constantly full dry bowl is how these cats become heavy and then struggle in warm rooms. Feel the ribs through the plush coat. Onion and garlic stay out. Count the treats.
