American Wirehair: Breed Background and Character

The American Wirehair is one of the rarest cat breeds in existence, the result of a spontaneous genetic mutation that occurred in a litter of barn cats in Verona, New York in 1966. The distinctive wiry, crimped coat sets this breed apart from all others and is unique to American cats. These characteristics make American Wirehair a distinctive cat with specific care requirements. Their easygoing, affectionate, playful nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, low (special care) grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Medium (8-12 lbs) frame.
