Life-Stage Feeding Guide for American Wirehair

The wiry coat does not change what an American Wirehair needs to eat. Kittens grow on a complete kitten diet, wet or dry, in meals you can measure. Adults eat a complete cat food twice a day, and you judge the portion by the ribs under that coat. A harsh jacket hides a thick waist. Seniors may want a softer texture if the mouth is sore, and the nutrient need is still a cat food with taurine and vitamin A already formed.
