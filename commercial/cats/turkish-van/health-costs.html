Age Related Health

Senior Nutrition Needs

Senior Turkish Vans — typically age seven and up — benefit from a distinct approach to preventive care.

Senior Turkish Vans do better on a proactive plan; reactive care tends to trail the problem and cost more to resolve. The conditions most likely to drive veterinary spend in the Turkish Van's senior years — dental disease, orthopedic change, renal or hepatic drift — are detectable early with routine bloodwork and physical exam.

Managing Chronic Conditions

Chronic conditions in Turkish Van—including Genetic Conditions, hyperthyroidism, urinary tract conditions, and skin sensitivities—require a long-term management mindset rather than a cure-and-forget approach. Building a routine that accommodates your Turkish Van's health needs becomes second nature within a few months and significantly improves quality of life.

Wellness Monitoring and

Create a baseline profile during your Turkish Van's initial veterinarian evaluation including weight, vital ranges, and species-appropriate lab values. For Turkish Van cats predisposed to Genetic Conditions and specific genetic predispositions that regular veterinary screening can catch early, your veterinarian may recommend condition-specific screening intervals more frequent than annual visits. Trends in your Turkish Van's health data over months and years reveal gradual changes that single-point measurements miss entirely—making consistent tracking one of the most cost-effective health investments for this breed.
