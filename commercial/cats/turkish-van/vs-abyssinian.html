The temperament contrast between Turkish Van and Abyssinian Cat is one of the most significant factors in choosing between these cats. Turkish Van is characterized by an active, intelligent, water-loving personality, while Abyssinian Cat tends toward active, curious, playful traits. In daily life, this means Turkish Van owners typically experience a cat that leans toward active behavior, while Abyssinian Cat owners find their cat more inclined toward active tendencies.
