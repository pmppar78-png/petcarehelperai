Temperament and Personality Differences

Oriental Longhair is characterized by a social, vocal, intelligent personality, while Oriental Shorthair tends toward social, vocal, intelligent traits. In daily life, this means Oriental Longhair owners typically experience a cat that leans toward social behavior, while Oriental Shorthair owners find their cat more inclined toward social tendencies. The household has to live with size on one side and social, vocal, intelligent on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Oriental Longhair's social nature and Oriental Shorthair's social temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.
