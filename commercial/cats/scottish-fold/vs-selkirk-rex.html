Cost of Ownership

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Medium (6-13 lbs) vs Medium to Large (6-16 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Scottish Fold's 11-14 years expected life and Selkirk Rex's 15-20 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived cat accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Choose Scottish Fold if your lifestyle accommodates their moderate activity needs, moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their sweet temperament. Choose Selkirk Rex if you prefer their moderate energy level, can manage moderate maintenance, and appreciate their patient personality.

Feeding and Nutrition

Scottish Fold (Medium (6-13 lbs), moderate activity) has different caloric and macronutrient needs than Selkirk Rex (Medium to Large (6-16 lbs), moderate activity). Health-condition-specific dietary needs also differ—Scottish Fold's associations with Cartilage & Bone Issues may warrant targeted nutrition, while Selkirk Rex's predisposition to Genetic Conditions calls for different dietary strategies.

Living Space and

Scottish Fold requires indoor space space suited to a Medium (6-13 lbs) cat with moderate exercise demands and a sweet, calm, adaptable disposition. Selkirk Rex needs space accommodating their Medium to Large (6-16 lbs) build, moderate activity needs, and patient, loving, playful behavioral style.

Insurance and Health

Scottish Fold is predisposed to Cartilage & Bone Issues and Cardiac Conditions, making coverage for hereditary conditions essential. Selkirk Rex's risk factors (the breed-typical condition profile flagged in veterinary literature for this lineage) require different policy features.

Long-Term Commitment Assessment

Scottish Fold's 11-14 years expected life will include a vibrant youth, stable adulthood, and eventual senior phase with increasing health needs related to Cartilage & Bone Issues. Selkirk Rex's 15-20 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (Genetic Conditions) and different care demands (moderate versus moderate).
