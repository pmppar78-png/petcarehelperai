Temperament and Personality Differences

Scottish Fold brings a sweet, calm, adaptable energy to the household, compared to Selkirk Rex's patient, loving, playful disposition. In daily life, this means Scottish Fold owners typically experience a cat that leans toward sweet behavior, while Selkirk Rex owners find their cat more inclined toward patient tendencies. The household has to live with size on one side and sweet, calm, adaptable on the other.
