The comparison below works through each of those in turn — costs, exercise, grooming, training, health, and lifestyle fit — so the decision rests on lived constraints rather than first impressions.

The aim here is to surface the operationally meaningful gaps between the two so the right choice is obvious by the end.

Temperament and Personality Differences

Understanding how American Bobtail and American Curl differ in temperament is essential for making the right choice. American Bobtail's friendly, adaptable, intelligent character creates a fundamentally different ownership experience than American Curl's affectionate, playful, curious nature. In daily life, this means American Bobtail owners typically experience a cat that leans toward friendly behavior, while American Curl owners find their cat more inclined toward affectionate tendencies. There is no objectively better personality here; pick the one that suits your household.

Best for Families with Children

Evaluate each breed's interaction style with children. American Bobtail's friendly nature and American Curl's affectionate temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

American Bobtail has a typical lifespan of 13-18 years, while American Curl lives approximately 12-16 years. Health profiles differ significantly between these cats. American Bobtail is predisposed to Tail-Related Concerns, General Health Concerns, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. American Curl faces its own health challenges including Ear-Related Concerns, General Health. Both breeds show similar numbers of documented predispositions, though the conditions and their management paths diverge. Insurance considerations differ between the two cats based on these risk profiles. Prospective owners should discuss breed-specific health screening with a veterinarian before making their decision.

Best for Low-Maintenance Health

To minimise vet visits, compare hereditary health risks and typical lifespan for each breed before temperament and aesthetics. American Bobtail's predispositions typically require specific screening tests, while American Curl has its own set of conditions to monitor. The breed with fewer hereditary risks and a straightforward preventive care plan will be easier to manage long-term.

Exercise and Activity Level Differences

Activity requirements differ minimally between American Bobtail and American Curl. American Bobtail requires moderate levels of exercise and engagement, while American Curl needs moderate activity.
