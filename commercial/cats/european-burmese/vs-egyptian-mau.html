Temperament and Personality Differences

European Burmese brings a social, intelligent, affectionate energy to the household, compared to Egyptian Mau's athletic, loyal, sensitive disposition. In daily life, this means European Burmese owners typically experience a cat that leans toward social behavior, while Egyptian Mau owners find their cat more inclined toward athletic tendencies.

Best for Families with Children

European Burmese's social nature and Egyptian Mau's athletic temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

European Burmese is predisposed to Genetic Conditions, specific genetic predispositions that regular veterinary screening can catch early, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Egyptian Mau faces its own health challenges including Genetic Conditions, dental disease, kidney conditions, and breed-specific eye issues.
