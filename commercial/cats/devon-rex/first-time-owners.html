Is Devon Rex Good for First-Time Owners? Fit, Cost & Care Load

Quick Answer

Luxating patella, Playful, Mischievous, Affectionate, and Small to Medium 5-10 lbs belong in the first-week plan for the Devon Rex.

A Devon Rex has a fragile, pixie coat and large ears, and the breed carries a hereditary myopathy that causes muscle weakness, especially in the neck, and a risk of laryngeal spasm under stress. They also get hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. A Devon that cannot hold its head up needs a vet. They are people-obsessed and they chill easily. This is not a sturdy barn cat in a cute coat.
