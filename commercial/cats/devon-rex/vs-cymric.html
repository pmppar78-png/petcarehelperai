Temperament and Personality Differences

Devon Rex brings a playful, mischievous, affectionate energy to the household, compared to Cymric Cat's gentle, playful, loyal disposition. In daily life, this means Devon Rex owners typically experience a cat that leans toward playful behavior, while Cymric Cat owners find their cat more inclined toward gentle tendencies. The household has to live with size on one side and playful, mischievous, affectionate on the other.

Best for Families with Children

Devon Rex's playful nature and Cymric Cat's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Health and Lifespan Comparison

Devon Rex is predisposed to Genetic Conditions, Coat and Skin Issues, additional hereditary conditions including allergies and age-related changes, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Cymric Cat faces its own health challenges including Manx Syndrome, General Health Concerns. Grooming and Maintenance Comparison

Cost of Ownership Comparison

Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Males: 7-10 lbs, Females: 5-7 lbs vs Medium (8-12 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (low to moderate vs moderate to high), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Devon Rex's 12-15 years expected life and Cymric Cat's 9-13 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived cat accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Which Is Right for Your Family?

Choose Devon Rex if your lifestyle accommodates their very high activity needs, low to moderate grooming requirements, and you're prepared for their playful temperament. Choose Cymric Cat if you prefer their moderate energy level, can manage moderate to high maintenance, and appreciate their gentle personality.

Feeding and Nutrition Comparison

Devon Rex (Males: 7-10 lbs, Females: 5-7 lbs, very high activity) has different caloric and macronutrient needs than Cymric Cat (Medium (8-12 lbs), moderate activity). Health-condition-specific dietary needs also differ—Devon Rex's associations with Genetic Conditions may warrant targeted nutrition, while Cymric Cat's predisposition to Manx Syndrome calls for different dietary strategies.

Living Space and Habitat Requirements

Devon Rex requires indoor space space suited to a Males: 7-10 lbs, Females: 5-7 lbs cat with very high exercise demands and a playful, mischievous, affectionate disposition. Cymric Cat needs space accommodating their Medium (8-12 lbs) build, moderate activity needs, and gentle, playful, loyal behavioral style.

Devon Rex's 12-15 years expected life will include a vibrant youth, stable adulthood, and eventual senior phase with increasing health needs related to Genetic Conditions. Cymric Cat's 9-13 years trajectory follows a similar arc but with different condition profiles (Manx Syndrome) and different care demands (moderate versus moderate).
