Best for Families

Lykoi Cat's intelligent nature and LaPerm's gentle temperament each present different dynamics with younger family members.

Lykoi Cat is predisposed to Coat-Related Concerns, General Health Considerations, with associated veterinary costs for monitoring and treatment. Ask the veterinarian about Temperature Sensitivity and Skin Oil Buildup for the first animal and about Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) and Progressive Retinal Atrophy for the second before you decide, and keep both lifespan figures in the health section above beside that list.

Exercise and Activity

Lykoi Cat requires high levels of exercise and engagement, while LaPerm needs moderate activity.

Grooming and Maintenance

Lykoi Cat has moderate (special care) grooming needs, while LaPerm requires low maintenance.

Cost of Ownership

Both Lykoi Cat and LaPerm are similarly sized at Small to Medium (6-12 lbs), so recurring costs for food and supplies are comparable between the two breeds. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small to Medium (6-12 lbs) vs Small to Medium (5-10 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate (special care) vs low), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Lykoi Cat's 12-15 years expected life and LaPerm's 10-15 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived cat accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Consider your daily schedule (Lykoi Cat: high engagement vs LaPerm: moderate), grooming tolerance (moderate (special care) vs low), and personality preference (intelligent vs gentle).
