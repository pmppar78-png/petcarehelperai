Burmilla Cat: Breed Background and Character

The Burmilla is a relatively new breed that originated in the United Kingdom in 1981 from an accidental mating between a Chinchilla Persian and a Lilac Burmese. The resulting kittens were so beautiful that a breeding program was established to develop this striking silver-coated breed. These characteristics make Burmilla Cat a distinctive cat with specific care requirements. Their gentle, playful, affectionate nature means daily life involves moderate activity management, low to moderate grooming commitment, and environmental setup appropriate for their Medium (8-12 lbs) frame. Prospective owners should note that Burmilla Cat's moderate care level requires consistent attention throughout their 10-15 years lifespan to maintain optimal health and happiness.
