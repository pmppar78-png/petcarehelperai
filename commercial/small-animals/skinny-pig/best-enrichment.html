DIY enrichment for Skinny Pig (Hairless Guinea Pig) taps into natural behaviors without expensive commercial products. Build simple agility obstacles from household items: cushion tunnels, blanket tents, and cardboard mazes scaled for Skinny Pig (Hairless Guinea Pig)'s Medium (1.5-2.5 lbs / 0.7-1.2 kg) frame. For an intelligent breed like Skinny Pig (Hairless Guinea Pig), increase DIY puzzle complexity over time—start with single-step challenges and progress to multi-step sequences.
