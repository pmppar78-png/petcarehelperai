First-Year Cost Breakdown for American Rabbit

Year one with a American Rabbit carries the highest costs due to one-time setup expenses. Budget for a properly sized enclosure ($50-$300), essential bedding and hideout ($30-$150), food and water provisions ($15-$40), and a quality initial food supply ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for American Rabbit include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new American Rabbit may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for American Rabbit typically range from $1,800 to $4,500, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
