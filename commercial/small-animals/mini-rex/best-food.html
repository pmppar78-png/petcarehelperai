Feeding Guidelines for Mini Rex Rabbit

Choose a high-quality food appropriate for your Mini Rex Rabbit's age, size, and activity level. Look for whole protein as the first ingredient. Avoid fillers like corn and soy.

What to Look For

  • Species-appropriate protein: The primary ingredient should be a quality protein source suitable for Mini Rex Rabbit.
  • No harmful additives: Avoid artificial colors, flavors, and preservatives.
  • Complete nutrition: Ensure the food meets all nutritional requirements for this species.
  • Age-appropriate formula: Choose a formula matched to your pet's life stage.
  • Digestibility: High-quality ingredients are more easily absorbed and produce less waste.
