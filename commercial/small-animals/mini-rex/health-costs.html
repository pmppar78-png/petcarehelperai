Managing Chronic Conditions

Chronic conditions in Mini Rex Rabbit—including Sore Hocks (Pododermatitis), Dental Problems, Digestive Issues—require a long-term management mindset rather than a cure-and-forget approach. Many Mini Rex Rabbit owners underestimate the importance of environmental management alongside medication—temperature regulation, activity modification, and stress reduction all influence chronic condition outcomes. Building a routine that accommodates your Mini Rex Rabbit's health needs becomes second nature within a few months and significantly improves quality of life.
