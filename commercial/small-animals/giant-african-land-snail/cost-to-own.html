Best for Budget-Conscious Giant African Land Snail Owners

Giant African land snails are illegal to keep in much of the United States without a permit, and where they are legal the health issues are shell damage and a dry enclosure. Structure the food spend around vegetables, a calcium source such as cuttlebone, and a bit of fruit, with salted snacks and processed human food kept away from the shell. An exotics veterinarian is the person who should see a cracked shell or a snail that stays sealed up. The ARAV directory directory is a way to find clinics used to unusual patients. Pet insurance for a snail does not exist in practice. Ask anyway whether Nationwide's avian and exotic plan lists this species in your state, and keep a small emergency fund for the exam.

The habits that matter are a daily humidity check, calcium always available, and greens that are actually eaten. A dry tank and a bare shell are the expensive combination.
