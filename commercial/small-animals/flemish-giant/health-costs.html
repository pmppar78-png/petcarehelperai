A Simple Vet-Care Savings Plan

Building a vet fund for a Flemish Giant is a discipline problem disguised as a savings problem. The savings math is simple: $60 per month for three years produces a $2,160 reserve, enough to absorb most non-catastrophic events. The discipline is harder: keeping the fund untouched during routine financial pressure, replenishing it after unavoidable drawdowns, and resisting the temptation to cancel the auto-transfer during lean months.

The most reliable way to enforce the discipline is to place the fund in an account that is inconvenient to access — a separate institution, a different app login, no debit card. Friction on withdrawal dramatically increases the odds of the fund being available when it is actually needed.
