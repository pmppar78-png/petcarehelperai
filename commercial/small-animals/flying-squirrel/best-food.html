The Sugar Glider / Flying Squirrel has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Very Small (2-5 oz) build and friendly temperament. As a tiny small animal, Sugar Glider / Flying Squirrel has a fast metabolism requiring calorie-dense food in small, frequent portions.
