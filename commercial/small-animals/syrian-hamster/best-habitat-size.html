Multi-Pet Household Setup for Syrian Hamster (Golden Hamster)

If introducing Syrian Hamster (Golden Hamster) into a home with existing small animals or other animals, careful space planning prevents territorial conflicts and stress. Each animal should have their own enclosure, feeding station, and resting area. For Syrian Hamster (Golden Hamster) with their friendly temperament, introduction should be gradual over days to weeks, starting with scent exchange before visual or physical contact. Shared common areas should have multiple exit points so no animal feels trapped. Resource guarding is common during transitions; provide duplicate resources (food bowls, water sources, enrichment items) in separate locations. Monitor interactions closely during the first several weeks, and be prepared to separate small animals if signs of aggression or excessive stress appear.

Your Syrian Hamster (Golden Hamster)'s habitat needs shift with the seasons.
