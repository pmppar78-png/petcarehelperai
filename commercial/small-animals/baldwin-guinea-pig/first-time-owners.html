Common Mistakes New Baldwin Guinea Pig Owners Make

New Baldwin Guinea Pig owners commonly stumble in predictable ways. The biggest error is underestimating time commitment—even with moderate needs, daily interaction is non-negotiable. Many new owners also buy equipment before researching what Baldwin Guinea Pig actually needs, wasting money on wrong-sized enclosure setups or inappropriate accessories. Another critical mistake is delayed veterinary establishment: your Baldwin Guinea Pig should see an exotic veterinarian within the first week, not the first month. Inconsistent boundaries during the initial weeks create behavioral problems that become exponentially harder to correct later. Underestimating costs results in difficult decisions when exotic veterinarian bills arrive. Finally, many new owners don't establish an exotic veterinarian relationship early enough, missing critical early health screening windows.
