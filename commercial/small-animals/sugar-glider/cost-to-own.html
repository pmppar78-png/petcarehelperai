Lifetime Cost Projection for Sugar Glider

Over a typical 12-15 years in captivity lifespan, total Sugar Glider ownership costs break down approximately as follows: acquisition ($300-$3,000+), first-year setup and care ($1,300 to $3,500), annual recurring costs multiplied by remaining years ($900-$2,600 per year), and end-of-life care ($500-$2,000). This investment yields immeasurable companionship and joy, but prospective owners should ensure they can sustain these costs comfortably throughout the Sugar Glider's entire life.
