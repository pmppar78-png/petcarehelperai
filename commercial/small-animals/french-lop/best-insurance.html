What the French lop quote has to cover

French lop insurance is the bill for a heavy lop with wet ears and a hay-dependent mouth. Lifetime veterinary costs quoted for this breed reach $15,000 to $45,000, while the comprehensive premiums across the same years add up to $5,000 to $12,000. At 80 percent reimbursement, one $3,000 emergency returns most of a single year's premium.

The claims worth reading the exclusion list for are the damp ear canals under a lop, cheek teeth that overgrow when hay runs short, and hocks carrying a 10 to 15 pound body. A policy that drops dental disease or gastrointestinal stasis leaves the expensive French lop visits unpaid. Ask an exotic veterinarian who already treats lop rabbits which of those three they see most, and match the policy to that answer.
