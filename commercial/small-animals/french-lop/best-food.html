What to Look

  • Life-stage appropriate: Young and adult small animal formulas are not interchangeable — pick the one that matches your French Lop Rabbit's current stage.
  • Calorie density match: The right calorie content for your French Lop Rabbit's size and activity level prevents both under- and over-feeding.
  • Digestive tolerance: A food your French Lop Rabbit digests well (firm stools, no gas, no vomiting) beats a "superior" food that causes GI problems.
