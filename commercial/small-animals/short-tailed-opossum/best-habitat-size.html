Seasonal Habitat Adjustments for Short-Tailed Opossum

Adapting your Short-Tailed Opossum's living environment to seasonal changes protects both health and comfort. Summer adjustments for a 4-6 inches small animal: increase water availability, add cooling surfaces, ensure the enclosure has adequate airflow, and never expose your Short-Tailed Opossum to direct sun in enclosed spaces. Seasonal parasite prevention affects habitat management too—mite and parasite concernss may require more frequent cleaning of your Short-Tailed Opossum's enclosure and resting areas.
