First-Year Cost Breakdown for Campbell's Dwarf Hamster

The first year of Campbell's Dwarf Hamster ownership involves significant upfront investment beyond the acquisition cost. Initial setup for a Campbell's Dwarf Hamster includes a quality enclosure ($50-$300 depending on size and type), bedding and hideout ($30-$150), food and water dishes ($15-$40), and initial supply of species-appropriate food ($40-$100). First-year veterinary costs for Campbell's Dwarf Hamster include the initial health examination ($50-$150), vaccination series or wellness checks ($100-$300), spay/neuter or initial procedures if applicable ($200-$600), and microchipping ($40-$60). Training or behavioral guidance for a new Campbell's Dwarf Hamster may add $100-$400. Total first-year costs for Campbell's Dwarf Hamster typically range from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on acquisition source and care choices.
