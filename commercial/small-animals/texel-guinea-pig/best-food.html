Life-Stage Feeding Guide for Texel Guinea Pig

Texel guinea pigs have a curly coat and the same non-negotiable diet as other cavies: hay and vitamin C. Grow pups on alfalfa, then shift adults to unlimited grass hay, a measured vitamin C pellet, and a daily handful of greens. The curl collects debris and the animal swallows hair. That makes hay intake and grooming both part of feeding. Seniors need dental checks and the same fiber. A Texel that eats slowly, hunches, or stops producing droppings needs an exotics vet the same day. Vitamin C deficiency looks like a limp or a rough coat. Do not answer it with rabbit food or a seed mix. Fruit and yogurt drops are optional at most.
