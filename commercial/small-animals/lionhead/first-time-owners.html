Your First 30 Days with a Lionhead Rabbit

The first month with a lionhead rabbit is unlimited grass hay, a vet visit, and a daily comb of the mane so the wool does not mat or get swallowed. Learn the signs of gut stasis: a rabbit that stops eating and produces no droppings needs a veterinarian who sees exotics the same day. Skip a bath. Use a pen with a solid floor, bigger than a dwarf-rabbit cage.
