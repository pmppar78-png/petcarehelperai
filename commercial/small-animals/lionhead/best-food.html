Rabbit Nutritional Profile

Every Lionhead Rabbit has nutritional demands driven by its Small (2.5-3.75 lbs / 1.1-1.7 kg) build, friendly energy, and expected 7-10 years lifespan. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Lionhead Rabbit's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions.
