Cost of Ownership Comparison

Both Degu and Dumbo Rat are similarly sized at Small (6-10 oz), so recurring costs for food and supplies are comparable between the two breeds. Key cost differentials include: food costs scale with size (Small (6-10 oz) vs Small-Medium (0.5-1.5 lbs)), grooming costs reflect maintenance requirements (moderate vs moderate), and veterinary costs correlate with breed-specific health risks. Over a complete lifespan, Degu's 6-9 years expected life and Dumbo Rat's 2-3 years expected life mean different total cost horizons—the longer-lived small animal accumulates more total costs but potentially offers more years of companionship.

Degu (Small (6-10 oz), very high activity) has different caloric and macronutrient needs than Dumbo Rat (Small-Medium (0.5-1.5 lbs), high activity). Health-condition-specific dietary needs also differ—Degu's associations with breed-specific conditions may warrant targeted nutrition, while Dumbo Rat's predisposition to breed-specific conditions calls for different dietary strategies.

Living Space and Habitat Requirements

Degu requires enclosure space suited to a Small (6-10 oz) small animal with very high exercise demands and a friendly disposition. Dumbo Rat needs space accommodating their Small-Medium (0.5-1.5 lbs) build, high activity needs, and friendly behavioral style. Beyond the primary enclosure, consider exercise space: Degu needs substantial active space, while Dumbo Rat demands significant room for exercise.
