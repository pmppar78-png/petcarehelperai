Hissing Cockroach Cost to Own: First-Year, Monthly & Vet Budget

Quick Answer

Docile, Hardy, Madagascar, and 2-3 inches belong in the cost sheet for the Madagascar Hissing Cockroach.

A Madagascar hissing cockroach is a large insect that needs a warm, humid tub, hiding bark, and a diet of vegetables and a protein source. They are not a sterile classroom toy: they can live for years, the males ram each other, and some people are allergic to cockroach proteins. They are restricted in a few places. A dry, cold tank is how they die. They do not need a heat lamp sitting on the lid.
