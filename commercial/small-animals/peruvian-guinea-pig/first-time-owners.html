Common Mistakes New Peruvian Guinea Pig Owners Make

Mistake one: choosing Peruvian Guinea Pig based on appearance rather than lifestyle fit—this breed's moderate energy and advanced (high grooming needs) care demands must match your reality. Mistake three: socializing too aggressively or not at all—Peruvian Guinea Pig's friendly temperament requires gradual, positive exposure to new experiences. Mistake four: comparing your Peruvian Guinea Pig's progress to other small animals online, which creates unrealistic expectations and unnecessary anxiety.

Building a Care Team for Your Peruvian Guinea Pig

For grooming, find a professional who knows Peruvian Guinea Pig's specific maintenance profile rather than a general groomer learning on the job. Given Peruvian Guinea Pig's demanding care level, a professional trainer or behaviorist is strongly recommended rather than optional. Building this team proactively means every aspect of your Peruvian Guinea Pig's care is covered.
