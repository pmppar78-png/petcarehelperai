Pet Mouse Nutritional Profile

The Pet Mouse has specific dietary requirements shaped by its Very Small (0.5-1 oz) build and friendly temperament. As a tiny small animal, Pet Mouse has a fast metabolism requiring calorie-dense food in small, frequent portions. A diet rich in animal-based proteins at 28-35% of total calories fuels Pet Mouse's active lifestyle, with fat content elevated slightly to sustain energy through longer activity sessions. Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids are particularly beneficial for Pet Mouse to maintain coat health and joint function.
