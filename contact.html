Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pet Care Helper AI a veterinary service?

No. Pet Care Helper AI provides educational information only. We are not a veterinary clinic, and our AI assistant does not replace professional veterinary care. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical decisions about your pet.

How can I advertise on Pet Care Helper AI?

We partner with pet care brands, veterinary service providers, and pet product companies. For advertising inquiries, partnership proposals, or sponsored content opportunities, please use the form above or email advertise@petcarehelperai.com.

Is the information on this site free?

Yes. All breed guides, care guides, tools, and AI chat features on Pet Care Helper AI are completely free to use. The site is supported by advertising and affiliate partnerships.

How do I report an error in your content?

We take accuracy seriously. If you find an error or outdated information, please use the contact form above and select "Content Correction" as the subject. Include the page URL and a description of the issue. We investigate all reports and aim to correct confirmed errors within one week.
