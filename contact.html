Get in Touch

Whether you spotted an error on one of our pages, have a question about your pet, or just want to suggest a topic we have not covered yet — we would like to hear from you. Every message lands in the same inbox that Paul checks personally. Most people hear back within one to two business days; occasionally it takes a bit longer during busy stretches, but nothing gets ignored.

We read every message that comes through this form. If you are dealing with an urgent pet health concern right now, please skip the form and contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal hospital directly — they can help your pet faster than we can.

Other Ways to Reach Us

The form above is the quickest route for most messages. For a few specific situations, it helps to know which inbox you are writing to and what information to include.

Business & Partnerships

advertise@petcarehelperai.com

Sponsorships, advertising, media inquiries, and partnership proposals. We typically respond to business emails within 48 hours. If your outreach relates to an affiliate or promotional arrangement, please include the product category up front — we decline most pharma, supplement, and unregulated-remedy pitches as a matter of editorial policy.

Veterinary Professionals

If you are a licensed veterinarian, veterinary technician, or exotics specialist and you have spotted something inaccurate or outdated, we especially want to hear from you. Corrections from professionals get fast-tracked: we aim to acknowledge receipt within one business day, and to have a revised version of the page live within five. Use the form above or email us at advertise@petcarehelperai.com with "Vet Correction" in the subject line and, if possible, the specific URL and the source (journal article, guideline document, textbook edition) you want us to align with.

Reporting a Factual Error

The fastest path is the contact form above with "Report an error or outdated info" selected from the dropdown. If you can paste the exact sentence that looks wrong and either a corrected version or a source, we can usually turn it around the same day. Our Editorial Standards page documents the full correction workflow, including when we add a visible note to the article.

Privacy and Data Requests

For a data access, correction, or deletion request under CCPA, GDPR, or any state-level consumer privacy law, use the form above or email advertise@petcarehelperai.com. We process privacy requests within the statutory window (typically 30 days). Our Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how long we retain it, and which third-party processors touch it. We do not maintain user accounts, so most requests resolve quickly.

What We Cannot Do Over Email

We cannot diagnose your pet, triage a current illness, or advise on dosages. The AI chat on the Chat page offers educational information only; if your pet is actively unwell, please contact a veterinarian or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435. We also cannot write prescriptions, refer you to a specific local vet (we publish location directories, not personal referrals), or recommend a product tailored to your pet's exact weight and history.

Mail, phone, and in-person meetings are not currently offered. Pet Care Helper AI is a small editorial operation based in Boston, MA, and we keep the contact surface intentionally narrow so that nothing falls through the cracks. Every message is read by a person; nothing goes to an automated triage bot.
