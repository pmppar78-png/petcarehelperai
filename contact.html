About Pet Care Helper AI

Pet Care Helper AI is an informational pet care resource based in Boston, MA. We provide free, editorially reviewed educational content covering dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, fish, amphibians, and small animals. Our platform includes breed guides, care guides, interactive tools, and a veterinary service directory to help pet owners make informed decisions about their pets' wellbeing.

All content on this site is produced for educational purposes only and does not constitute veterinary advice. For more about our editorial practices, please visit our Editorial Standards and Medical Disclaimer pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pet Care Helper AI a veterinary service?

No. Pet Care Helper AI provides educational information only. We are not a veterinary clinic, and our content does not replace professional veterinary care. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical decisions about your pet.

How can I advertise on Pet Care Helper AI?

We partner with pet care brands, veterinary service providers, and pet product companies. For advertising inquiries, sponsorship proposals, or partnership opportunities, please email advertise@petcarehelperai.com.

Is the information on this site free?

Yes. All breed guides, care guides, tools, and AI chat features on Pet Care Helper AI are completely free to use. The site is supported by advertising and affiliate partnerships.
