What to Look for in Pet Services

Choosing a Veterinarian

  • Credentials: Look for AAHA-accredited practices
  • Specialties: Consider if you need exotic pet experience
  • Hours: Check if they offer evening or weekend appointments
  • Emergency protocols: Know where they refer after-hours emergencies
  • Reviews: Read recent patient reviews on Google and Yelp

Emergency Vet Considerations

  • 24/7 availability: True emergencies happen at all hours
  • Location: Know the fastest route before an emergency
  • Services: Confirm they have surgery and critical care capabilities
  • Payment: Understand costs and payment options upfront

Protect Your Pet with Insurance

Emergency vet visits can cost thousands of dollars. Get peace of mind with pet insurance from Spot Pet Insurance, Lemonade Pet, or Trupanion.
