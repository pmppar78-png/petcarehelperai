Find Vets & Pet Services in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Philadelphia has a thriving pet community across its diverse neighborhoods. From Center City to Fishtown, University City to Manayunk, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Philadelphia metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Philadelphia Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Philadelphia

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Philadelphia has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals, including the renowned Penn Vet.

Tips for Philadelphia Pet Emergencies

  • Penn Vet's Ryan Hospital offers world-class emergency care
  • BluePearl and VCA also have emergency locations in the metro area
  • Parking can be challenging - know where you can park quickly
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $150-300 before treatment
