Seasonal Pet Care in Portland

Leptospirosis risk is elevated due to wet conditions. Flea prevention is recommended year-round. Salmon poisoning (Neorickettsia helminthoeca) is a unique PNW threat to dogs.

Year-Round Safety in Portland, Oregon

  • Summer: Summers are generally mild but extreme heat events are increasing. The Pacific Northwest is less prepared for heat — few homes have AC. Watch for heat stress during heat domes.
  • Winter: Mild but very wet winters. Persistent rain and mud can cause skin infections in pets. Ice storms are rare but devastating. Monitor for hypothermia in prolonged wet conditions.
  • Spring: Extended rain season continues into June. Slug bait (metaldehyde) used in Pacific NW gardens is highly toxic to pets. Tick populations are present though lower than eastern regions.
  • Fall: Mushroom season brings toxic species into yards and trails. Salmon poisoning disease from eating raw fish is unique to the Pacific NW and can be fatal to dogs.
  • Severe Weather: Atmospheric rivers bring heavy rain and flooding November-March. Windstorms can topple trees. Keep pets indoors during major storm events.

How to Choose a Veterinarian in Portland

Selecting the right veterinarian is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your pet. The American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA) accreditation indicates a practice voluntarily meets higher-than-average care standards — only about 12-15% of veterinary practices in North America achieve this certification.

Key Factors When Choosing a Portland Vet

  • Accreditation: AAHA-accredited practices undergo rigorous evaluations of their medical protocols, facilities, and continuing education
  • Emergency protocols: Understand how the practice handles after-hours emergencies — do they have a referral relationship with a 24-hour hospital?
  • Communication style: A good vet explains diagnoses and treatment options clearly, welcomes questions, and respects your role in healthcare decisions
  • Specialties: If you have an exotic pet (bird, reptile, small mammal), verify the practice has experience with your species
  • Fear-free practices: Fear Free certification indicates staff trained to minimize stress, anxiety, and fear during veterinary visits
  • Reviews and reputation: Check Google reviews, Yelp, and ask for recommendations from local Portland pet owners and breed-specific groups
