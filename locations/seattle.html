Dog Parks in

Seattle has an incredible number of off-leash dog parks - more per capita than almost any other US city. From waterfront parks to wooded trails, there's a perfect spot for every dog.

Tips for Dog

  • Bring towels and be prepared for muddy conditions
  • Check park rules as some have specific hours or regulations

    Washington licenses and the Seattle Animal Shelter
