Find Vets & Pet Services in Seattle, Washington

Seattle is consistently ranked as one of the most dog-friendly cities in America, with an incredible community of pet lovers. From Capitol Hill to Ballard, Queen Anne to West Seattle, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Seattle metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Seattle Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Seattle

When your pet has an emergency, quick action is essential. Seattle has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals throughout the Puget Sound region.

Tips for Seattle Pet Emergencies

  • BluePearl Pet Hospital has multiple emergency locations in the Seattle area
  • Animal Critical Care & Emergency Services (ACCES) serves the region
  • Traffic across bridges can be unpredictable - know multiple routes
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $150-300 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Seattle

Seattle has a wonderful selection of veterinarians, from neighborhood clinics to specialty practices. The city's pet-friendly culture means high-quality care is widely available.

Dog Parks in Seattle

Seattle has an incredible number of off-leash dog parks - more per capita than almost any other US city. From waterfront parks to wooded trails, there's a perfect spot for every dog.

Tips for Seattle Dog Parks
