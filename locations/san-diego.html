Emergency Veterinarians in

San Diego has several 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals throughout the county. Know your nearest emergency facility before an emergency happens.

Tips for Pet

  • VCA and BluePearl have multiple San Diego locations
  • UC Davis veterinary specialists offer referral services in the area
  • Beach hazards include stingrays and saltwater ingestion
  • Keep emergency vet addresses saved in your phone

Regular Veterinarians in

San Diego has veterinary practices throughout the county, from beach communities to inland areas. The mild climate means year-round outdoor activities with pets.

Tips for Finding

  • Many San Diego vets offer weekend appointments
  • Ask about foxtail awareness - common in San Diego
  • House-call vets are popular throughout the county
  • Flea and tick prevention is important year-round

Exotic Pet Veterinarians

San Diego's climate makes it popular for reptile and bird keeping. Several excellent exotic animal veterinarians serve the area.

Tips for Exotic

  • The San Diego Zoo has created demand for exotic specialists
  • Several practices specialize exclusively in exotic animals
  • Ask about emergency exotic care availability
  • Exotic appointments may need to be booked in advance

Pet Groomers in

San Diego has groomers for every style and budget. Mobile grooming is popular throughout the county.

  • Beach dogs may need more frequent baths for salt and sand

  • Book holiday grooming appointments in advance

Pet Stores in

San Diego has pet stores ranging from major retailers to boutique shops specializing in natural and organic products.

  • Many local stores focus on natural and organic products
  • Beach gear and water toys are popular items
  • Several specialty aquarium stores serve the fishkeeping community

Dog Parks and Dog Beaches in San Diego

San Diego is famous for its dog beaches and numerous dog parks. The mild weather means year-round outdoor activities with your pet.

Tips for San Diego Dog Parks and Beaches

  • Dog Beach in Ocean Beach is one of the most famous dog beaches
  • Fiesta Island has large off-leash areas
  • Rinse dogs after beach visits to remove salt
  • Watch for stingrays in the water - shuffle your feet
