Find Vets & Pet Services in Austin, Texas

Austin is one of the most pet-friendly cities in America, known for its "Keep Austin Weird" culture that extends to its love for pets. From South Congress to East Austin, Downtown to the suburbs, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Austin metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Austin Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Austin

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Austin has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals serving the metro area.

Tips for Austin Pet Emergencies

  • Texas A&M Veterinary Medical Teaching Hospital is a regional referral resource
  • Multiple emergency clinics serve North, South, and Central Austin
  • Be aware of heat-related emergencies - they're common in Texas summers
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $100-250 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Austin

Austin has excellent veterinary care options, with many vets experienced in treating both common pets and those affected by Texas heat and outdoor hazards.

Tips for Finding an Austin Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Many Austin vets are experienced with heat-related conditions
  • Consider proximity to your neighborhood
  • Ask about their emergency referral protocols
  • Many practices offer wellness plans for routine care

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Austin

Austin has good options for exotic pet care, with specialists available for birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other exotic animals.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in Austin

Pet Groomers in Austin

Austin offers grooming services from neighborhood shops to boutique salons. The hot Texas climate means regular grooming is important for keeping pets comfortable.

Tips for Austin Pet Grooming

  • Summer clips can help keep dogs cool in Texas heat
  • Many groomers are experienced with outdoor/swimming dogs
  • Mobile grooming is popular throughout Austin neighborhoods
  • Prices typically range from $40-120 depending on service and pet size
  • Book ahead for holiday periods

Dog Parks in Austin

Austin is famous for its dog parks and outdoor spaces. Zilker Park, Red Bud Isle, and numerous neighborhood parks offer off-leash areas for dogs.

Tips for Austin Dog Parks

  • Red Bud Isle is a popular off-leash destination with water access
  • Avoid peak heat hours - early morning and evening visits are best
  • Bring plenty of water for your pet
  • Always clean up after your pet
  • Many parks have water features where dogs can cool off

Pet Boarding Facilities in Austin

Austin offers excellent boarding options from ranch-style facilities with acres of space to urban pet hotels with webcam access.

Tips for Pet Boarding in Austin

  • Book well in advance, especially for SXSW and ACL periods
  • Ensure the facility has adequate cooling for Texas summers
  • Tour the facility before your pet's first stay
  • Many facilities offer swimming and outdoor play
  • Consider in-home pet sitting as an alternative
