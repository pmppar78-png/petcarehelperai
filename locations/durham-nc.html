Seasonal Pet Care in Durham

Year-Round Safety in Durham, North Carolina

  • Summer: Extended extreme heat from May through October with high humidity. Heatstroke is a leading cause of preventable pet death. Limit outdoor time to early morning and evening.
  • Winter: Generally mild but occasional ice storms catch pet owners off guard. Short-coated breeds may still need protection during cold snaps below 40°F.
  • Spring: Flea and tick pressure begins early and is intense. Heartworm-carrying mosquitoes are active nearly year-round in the Southeast.
  • Fall: Hurricane season extends through November. Falling acorns and certain mushroom species can be toxic if ingested by curious pets.
  • Severe Weather: Hurricanes and severe thunderstorms are significant risks. Have evacuation plans that include pets, and never leave animals behind during mandatory evacuations.
