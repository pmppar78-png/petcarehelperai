Find Vets & Pet Services in Boston, Massachusetts

Boston is a wonderfully pet-friendly city with a strong community of pet owners across neighborhoods like Back Bay, South End, Cambridge, Somerville, and beyond. This guide helps you find quality pet services throughout Greater Boston, from emergency vets to groomers and specialty care.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Boston Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Boston

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Boston has excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals, including the renowned Angell Animal Medical Center.

Tips for Boston Pet Emergencies

  • Angell Animal Medical Center (MSPCA) is one of the premier emergency facilities in the country
  • Several VCA and BluePearl locations also offer 24/7 emergency care
  • Boston traffic and limited parking can delay arrival - plan routes in advance
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $150-300 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Boston

Boston has an excellent selection of veterinarians, benefiting from proximity to world-class veterinary schools and medical institutions.

Tips for Finding a Boston Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Many Boston vets are affiliated with Tufts or other veterinary schools
  • Consider proximity to your neighborhood, as parking can be challenging
  • Ask about their after-hours emergency protocols
  • Many practices offer wellness plans for routine care

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Boston

Boston has excellent options for exotic pet care, with specialists available for birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other exotic animals.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in Boston

  • Angell Animal Medical Center has an excellent avian and exotic department
  • Tufts Veterinary School offers specialty exotic care
  • Ask specifically about experience with your pet's species
  • Exotic vet visits often cost more than standard visits
  • Keep a list of exotic-certified vets before you need one urgently

Pet Groomers in Boston

Boston offers a wide range of grooming services from neighborhood shops to upscale salons. Many offer pickup services, which is helpful given Boston's parking challenges.

Tips for Boston Pet Grooming

  • Book popular groomers in advance, especially before holidays
  • Many groomers offer pickup and delivery in Boston neighborhoods
  • Mobile grooming vans are popular for convenience
  • Prices typically range from $50-150 depending on service and pet size
  • Winter grooming should maintain coat for warmth

Dog Parks in Boston

Boston has numerous dog parks and off-leash areas throughout the city and surrounding communities. The Esplanade and Boston Common area are popular spots.

Tips for Boston Dog Parks

  • Peters Park in the South End is one of the most popular fenced dog parks
  • Many parks have specific off-leash hours - check posted rules
  • Bring water and cleanup bags
  • Winter conditions may affect park accessibility
  • Some parks have separate areas for small and large dogs

Pet Boarding Facilities in Boston

Boston offers various boarding options from traditional kennels to luxury pet hotels, many with webcam access so you can check on your pet.

Tips for Pet Boarding in Boston

  • Book well in advance, especially for holidays and long weekends
  • Tour the facility before your pet's first stay
  • Ensure vaccinations are up to date - most require proof
  • Ask about indoor play areas for winter months
  • Consider in-home pet sitting as an alternative
