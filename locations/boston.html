Massachusetts licenses and Boston animal control

The Board of Registration in Veterinary Medicine licenses veterinarians in Massachusetts. Mass.gov lists the board at mass.gov/orgs/board-of-registration-in-veterinary-medicine and gives the board phone as 617-701-8723, at One Federal Street, Suite 600, Boston.

Shelter and animal-control calls for the city go to Boston Animal Care and Control, the department published at boston.gov/departments/animal-care-and-control.
