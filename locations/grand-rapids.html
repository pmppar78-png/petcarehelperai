Year-Round Safety in Grand Rapids, Michigan

  • Summer: Hot and humid summers with heat waves common in July-August. Thunderstorms can be severe. Firework season in summer causes pet anxiety and escapes.
  • Winter: Harsh winters with extended sub-zero temperatures. Limit outdoor time during wind chills below 0°F. Road salt irritates and cracks paw pads — use pet-safe booties or wax.
  • Spring: Rapid flooding from snowmelt creates hazards. Tornado season begins in spring. Tick populations are expanding northward across the Midwest.
  • Fall: Hunting season increases risks for outdoor pets — use bright vests. Antifreeze toxicity risk rises as temperatures drop.
  • Severe Weather: Tornadoes are a significant risk spring through early fall. Include pets in your tornado safety plan and identify pet-safe shelter areas in your home.
