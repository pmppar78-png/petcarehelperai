Find Vets & Pet Services in San Francisco, California

San Francisco is one of the most dog-friendly cities in America, with more dogs than children! From the Marina to the Mission, SOMA to Noe Valley, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout SF and the Bay Area.

Emergency Veterinarians in San Francisco

When your pet has an emergency, quick action is essential. San Francisco and the Bay Area have several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals.

Tips for SF Pet Emergencies

  • SFSPCA and Pet Emergency & Specialty Center offer emergency services
  • UC Davis Veterinary Hospital is available for complex referral cases
  • Traffic and parking can delay travel - plan routes in advance
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $175-350 before treatment

Tip: Save the nearest emergency vet's number in your phone now — during a crisis, you won't have time to search.

Tip: Emergency clinics in surrounding suburbs may have shorter wait times during peak hours.

Seasonal Pet Care in San Francisco

Valley fever affects dogs in central and southern California. Flea prevention is needed year-round. Heartworm transmission occurs in irrigated agricultural areas.

Year-Round Safety in San Francisco, California

  • Summer: Southern and inland California face extreme heat. Wildfires are a major seasonal threat — have pet evacuation plans. Foxtail grass seeds can lodge in ears, noses, and paws.
  • Winter: Mild coastal winters but mountain areas see snow. Atmospheric river storms can cause flooding in valleys. Northern California gets significantly more rain and cold than the south.
  • Spring: Foxtail grass season begins in spring and is an unique California hazard. Rattlesnake activity increases March-October. Wildflower superbloom years increase outdoor pet exposure to wildlife.
  • Fall: Peak wildfire season runs through November. Smoke and poor air quality affect pets — keep them indoors during smoky conditions. Santa Ana/Diablo winds increase fire danger.
  • Severe Weather: Atmospheric rivers cause flooding. Earthquake preparedness should include pets — keep carriers accessible and maintain emergency supplies. Wildfires require rapid evacuation plans.
