Find Vets & Pet Services in San Francisco, California

San Francisco is one of the most dog-friendly cities in America, with more dogs than children! From the Marina to the Mission, SOMA to Noe Valley, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout SF and the Bay Area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for SF Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in San Francisco

When your pet has an emergency, quick action is essential. San Francisco and the Bay Area have several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals.

Tips for SF Pet Emergencies

  • SFSPCA and Pet Emergency & Specialty Center offer emergency services
  • UC Davis Veterinary Hospital is available for complex referral cases
  • Traffic and parking can delay travel - plan routes in advance
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $175-350 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in San Francisco

San Francisco has excellent veterinary care options, with many practices catering to the city's passionate pet parent community.

Tips for Finding an SF Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Many SF vets offer fear-free and cat-friendly certifications
  • Consider proximity to your neighborhood - parking can be challenging
  • Ask about their emergency referral protocols

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in San Francisco

San Francisco has excellent options for exotic pet care, with specialists available for birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other exotic animals.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in SF

  • Mobile grooming is popular given parking challenges
  • SF has many eco-friendly and organic grooming options
  • Prices typically range from $60-175 depending on service and pet size

Dog Parks in San Francisco

San Francisco has numerous off-leash dog parks and beaches. The city's famous dog culture means well-maintained parks with active communities.

Tips for SF Dog Parks

  • Be prepared for microclimates - bring layers for foggy conditions
  • Always clean up after your pet
  • Some parks have specific off-leash hours

Pet Boarding Facilities in San Francisco

San Francisco offers boarding options from cozy neighborhood facilities to luxury pet hotels with webcam access and special amenities.
