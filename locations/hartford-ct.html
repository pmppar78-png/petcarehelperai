  • Winter: Harsh winters with heavy snow and ice. Road salt and chemical de-icers are toxic to pets — always wipe paws after walks. Hypothermia risk for small and short-coated breeds.
  • Spring: Peak tick season begins in April. The Northeast has high rates of Lyme disease — maintain tick prevention year-round. Seasonal allergies often flare as pollen counts rise.
  • Fall: Falling leaves can hide hazards. Antifreeze use increases in fall — even small amounts are lethal to pets. Continue flea and tick prevention through first hard frost.
