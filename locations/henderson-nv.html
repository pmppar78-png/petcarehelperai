Seasonal Pet Care in Henderson

Valley fever is a major concern for dogs in Arizona, Nevada, and New Mexico. Heartworm prevention is recommended year-round despite arid conditions.

Year-Round Safety in Henderson, Nevada

  • Summer: Extreme heat is the primary danger, with temperatures exceeding 110°F in summer. Pavement can reach 170°F. Walk pets only before 7 AM or after 8 PM. Carry water on every walk.
  • Winter: Mild winters in low-elevation areas. Higher elevations can see snow and freezing temperatures. Desert temperature swings between day and night can be dramatic.
  • Spring: Rattlesnake season begins in March as snakes emerge from brumation. Consider rattlesnake avoidance training for dogs. Valley fever (coccidioidomycosis) is endemic and affects dogs.
  • Fall: Scorpion activity peaks in fall. Coyote encounters increase as urban expansion meets wildlife habitat. Monsoon season (July-September) can cause flash flooding.
  • Severe Weather: Monsoon thunderstorms cause flash flooding July-September. Haboobs (dust storms) reduce visibility and can cause respiratory issues for pets.

How to Choose a Veterinarian in Henderson

Selecting the right veterinarian is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your pet. The American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA) accreditation indicates a practice voluntarily meets higher-than-average care standards — only about 12-15% of veterinary practices in North America achieve this certification.

Key Factors When Choosing a Henderson Vet

  • Accreditation: AAHA-accredited practices undergo rigorous evaluations of their medical protocols, facilities, and continuing education
  • Emergency protocols: Understand how the practice handles after-hours emergencies — do they have a referral relationship with a 24-hour hospital?
  • Communication style: A good vet explains diagnoses and treatment options clearly, welcomes questions, and respects your role in healthcare decisions
  • Specialties: If you have an exotic pet (bird, reptile, small mammal), verify the practice has experience with your species
  • Fear-free practices: Fear Free certification indicates staff trained to minimize stress, anxiety, and fear during veterinary visits
  • Reviews and reputation: Check Google reviews, Yelp, and ask for recommendations from local Henderson pet owners and breed-specific groups
