Find Vets & Pet Services in Denver, Colorado

Denver is one of the most pet-friendly cities in America, with an active outdoor culture that includes pets in everything from hiking to brewery visits. From LoDo to Cherry Creek, Capitol Hill to the suburbs, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Denver metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Denver Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Denver

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Denver has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals serving the Front Range.

Tips for Denver Pet Emergencies

  • Colorado State University Veterinary Teaching Hospital is a regional referral center
  • BluePearl and VCA emergency hospitals serve the Denver metro area
  • Be aware of altitude-related issues for visiting pets
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $125-275 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Denver

Denver has excellent veterinary care options, with many vets experienced in treating active, outdoor pets and altitude-related health concerns.

Tips for Finding a Denver Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Many Denver vets are experienced with hiking and outdoor injuries
  • Consider vets with knowledge of altitude-related health issues
  • Ask about their emergency referral protocols
  • Many practices offer wellness plans for routine care

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Denver

Denver has good options for exotic pet care, with specialists available for birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other exotic animals.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in Denver

  • Colorado State University offers specialty exotic care
  • Several clinics specialize in exotic animals in the metro area
  • Ask specifically about experience with your pet's species
  • Exotic vet visits often cost more than standard visits
  • Altitude can affect some exotic species differently

  • Many groomers are experienced with outdoor/active dogs
  • Mobile grooming is available throughout Denver neighborhoods
  • Prices typically range from $45-130 depending on service and pet size

Dog Parks in Denver

Denver has fantastic dog parks throughout the city and suburbs. The city's dog-friendly culture means well-maintained, popular off-leash areas everywhere.

Tips for Denver Dog Parks

  • Cherry Creek State Park has one of the largest off-leash areas
  • Many parks have water features for dogs
  • Bring water - hydration is important at altitude
  • Always clean up after your pet
  • Be aware of wildlife in some park areas

Pet Boarding Facilities in Denver

Denver offers excellent boarding options from traditional kennels to adventure-focused facilities that include hiking and outdoor activities.

Tips for Pet Boarding in Denver

  • Book well in advance, especially for ski season and holidays
  • Tour the facility before your pet's first stay
  • Ensure vaccinations are up to date - most require proof
  • Many facilities offer outdoor adventure add-ons
  • Consider in-home pet sitting as an alternative
