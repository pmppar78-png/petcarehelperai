Find Vets & Pet Services in Dallas-Fort Worth

The Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex is one of the largest metro areas in the US, with excellent pet services spread throughout. From Uptown Dallas to Fort Worth's Stockyards, from Plano to Arlington, this guide helps you find quality pet care across the DFW area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for DFW Pet Owners

Skip metroplex traffic and get instant vet advice online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Dallas-Fort Worth

The DFW metroplex has multiple 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals. Given the area's size, identify the closest emergency vet to your home before an emergency happens.

Tips for DFW Pet Emergencies

  • Multiple emergency hospitals serve both Dallas and Fort Worth
  • Texas A&M veterinary specialists offer referral services
  • Heat emergencies are common in Texas summers
  • Keep emergency vet addresses saved in your navigation app
  • Emergency visits typically start at $125-275 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Dallas-Fort Worth

DFW has veterinary practices throughout the metroplex, from neighborhood clinics to large specialty hospitals. Finding one convenient to your daily routine makes regular care easier.

Tips for Finding a DFW Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest standards
  • Many DFW vets offer evening and Saturday appointments
  • Consider vets near both home and work given commute times
  • House-call vets are available throughout the metroplex
  • Ask about heartworm prevention - essential in Texas

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Dallas-Fort Worth

DFW has several exotic animal specialists who treat birds, reptiles, and small mammals. Texas's warm climate makes reptile keeping popular, creating demand for qualified exotic vets.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in DFW

  • Several practices specialize exclusively in exotic animals
  • Exotic appointments often need to be booked weeks ahead
  • Ask about emergency exotic care availability
  • Keep exotic pets temperature-regulated during hot summers
  • Exotic vet visits typically cost more than standard visits

Pet Groomers in Dallas-Fort Worth

DFW has groomers for every budget, from basic neighborhood shops to luxury pet spas. Mobile grooming is popular throughout the metroplex.

Tips for DFW Pet Grooming

  • Mobile grooming keeps pets cool during hot months
  • Book holiday grooming appointments well in advance
  • Summer cuts help dogs stay cool but don't shave double-coated breeds
  • Prices typically range from $40 to $120+ depending on service
  • Ask about flea and tick treatments - important year-round in Texas

Dog Parks in Dallas-Fort Worth

DFW has numerous dog parks throughout the metroplex. Many feature water elements and shaded areas for hot Texas summers.

Tips for DFW Dog Parks

  • Always bring water for your dog
