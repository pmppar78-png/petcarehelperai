Seasonal Pet Care in Oklahoma City

Year-round heartworm prevention is standard due to extended mosquito seasons. Flea populations remain active nearly year-round.

Year-Round Safety in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

  • Summer: Extreme heat from June through September. Temperatures frequently exceed 100°F. Pets can develop heatstroke within 10 minutes on hot surfaces.
  • Winter: Variable winters — mild most years but ice storms can be severe. Power grid vulnerabilities mean extended outages are possible during winter storms.
  • Spring: Tornado season is a major concern March through June. Severe thunderstorms with large hail can endanger outdoor pets.
  • Fall: Warm fall temperatures keep snake activity high into November. Fire ant mounds expand after fall rains and can cause serious allergic reactions in pets.
  • Severe Weather: Tornado Alley runs through this region. Have a pet safety plan for severe weather. Identify interior rooms or storm shelters where pets can take cover.

How to Choose a Veterinarian in Oklahoma City

Selecting the right veterinarian is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your pet. The American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA) accreditation indicates a practice voluntarily meets higher-than-average care standards — only about 12-15% of veterinary practices in North America achieve this certification.

Key Factors When Choosing a Oklahoma City Vet

  • Accreditation: AAHA-accredited practices undergo rigorous evaluations of their medical protocols, facilities, and continuing education
  • Emergency protocols: Understand how the practice handles after-hours emergencies — do they have a referral relationship with a 24-hour hospital?
  • Communication style: A good vet explains diagnoses and treatment options clearly, welcomes questions, and respects your role in healthcare decisions
  • Specialties: If you have an exotic pet (bird, reptile, small mammal), verify the practice has experience with your species
  • Fear-free practices: Fear Free certification indicates staff trained to minimize stress, anxiety, and fear during veterinary visits
  • Reviews and reputation: Check Google reviews, Yelp, and ask for recommendations from local Oklahoma City pet owners and breed-specific groups
