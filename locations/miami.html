Find Vets & Pet Services in Miami, Florida

Miami is a vibrant, pet-loving city where pets enjoy the tropical lifestyle alongside their owners. From Miami Beach to Coral Gables, Brickell to Coconut Grove, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Miami metro area, including emergency care, regular vets, and specialty services.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Miami Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Miami

When your pet has an emergency, quick action is essential. Miami has several 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals throughout the metro area. Know your nearest emergency vet before you need one.

Tips for Miami Pet Emergencies

  • Several BluePearl and VCA emergency hospitals serve the Miami area
  • Be aware of heat-related emergencies - they're common in Miami's climate
  • Traffic can be challenging - consider keeping multiple emergency vet options
  • Keep your pet's medical records on your phone for quick access
  • Emergency visits typically start at $125-275 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Miami

Finding a great regular vet in Miami means considering location, language options (many vets offer bilingual services), and their experience with your type of pet.

Tips for Finding a Miami Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Many Miami vets offer Spanish-language services
  • Consider vets with experience in heat-related conditions
  • Ask about their emergency referral protocols
  • Check if they offer wellness plans for routine care

Dog Parks in Miami

Miami has numerous dog parks where pets can enjoy off-leash play. Many parks have separate areas for large and small dogs, and some even offer beach access.

Tips for Miami Dog Parks

  • Avoid peak heat hours - early morning and evening visits are best
  • Always bring water for your pet in Miami's warm climate
  • Some beaches have designated dog-friendly areas
  • Always clean up after your pet
  • Watch for posted hours and rules at each park
