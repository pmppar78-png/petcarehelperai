Seasonal Pet Care in Miami

Year-round prevention for heartworm, fleas, and ticks is non-negotiable in Florida. Mosquito-borne heartworm transmission occurs 365 days a year.

Year-Round Safety in Miami, Florida

  • Summer: Extreme heat and humidity May through October. Pavement temperatures can exceed 150°F. Walk pets only in early morning or after sunset. Watch for signs of heatstroke.
  • Winter: Mild winters rarely require cold-weather precautions, though sudden cold fronts in northern Florida can drop temperatures quickly. Monitor tropical fish outdoor ponds during cold snaps.
  • Spring: Flea, tick, and mosquito populations surge with spring rains. Bufo toads emerge after rain and are highly toxic to dogs — learn to recognize them.
  • Fall: Peak hurricane season August-October. Toxic blue-green algae blooms in lakes and canals can be lethal to pets who drink or swim in affected water.
  • Severe Weather: Florida leads the nation in hurricane risk. Every pet owner should have an evacuation plan including pet-friendly shelters. Never leave pets behind during evacuations.
