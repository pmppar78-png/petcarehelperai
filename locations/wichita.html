Year-Round Safety in Wichita, Kansas

  • Summer: Hot summers with intense sun exposure. Wind can mask heat danger. Severe thunderstorms and hail are common. Firework anxiety is heightened by thunder-prone summer weather.
  • Winter: Brutally cold winters with strong winds. Wind chill can drop effective temperature well below -20°F. Blizzard conditions can develop rapidly on open plains.
  • Spring: Severe tornado season March-June. Flooding from rapid snowmelt. Tick populations are expanding westward across the Great Plains.
  • Fall: Hunting season requires high-visibility gear for outdoor pets. Early blizzards can occur by October. Rattlesnake activity continues through warm fall days.
  • Severe Weather: Tornado Alley runs through the Great Plains. Have a tornado safety plan for pets. Identify interior rooms for shelter. Keep pet carriers accessible during storm season.
