Find Vets & Pet Services in Chicago

Chicago and its surrounding suburbs offer excellent pet care options throughout Chicagoland. From the Loop to Lincoln Park, from Evanston to Oak Park, this guide helps you find quality pet services across the metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Chicago Pet Owners

Get instant vet advice without braving Chicago weather. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Chicago

Chicago has several 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals throughout the city and suburbs. Knowing the closest one to your home can save precious time in an emergency.

Tips for Chicago Pet Emergencies

  • MedVet and BluePearl have multiple Chicagoland locations
  • Keep emergency vet addresses saved for quick navigation
  • Winter weather can slow travel - have a backup plan
  • Some emergency vets require payment upfront
  • Emergency visits typically start at $125-300 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Chicago

Chicago neighborhoods each have their own character, and you'll find veterinary practices to match. Many offer weekend and evening hours for busy professionals.

Tips for Finding a Chicago Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest standards
  • Consider vets accessible by CTA if you don't have a car
  • House-call vets are available throughout Chicagoland
  • Ask about their cold-weather policies for outdoor waits
  • Many vets offer payment plans or accept CareCredit

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Chicago

Chicago has several veterinarians who specialize in exotic animals including birds, reptiles, and small mammals. These specialists are essential for proper exotic pet care.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in Chicago

  • Chicago Exotics Animal Hospital is well-known for exotic care
  • Some exotics vets require referrals from regular vets

  • Keep exotic pets warm during transport in winter
  • Exotic vet visits typically cost more than standard visits

Dog Parks in Chicago

Chicago has numerous dog-friendly areas (DFAs) in parks throughout the city. These fenced areas allow dogs to play off-leash year-round.

Tips for Chicago Dog Parks

  • The Montrose Dog Beach is a popular lakefront destination
  • Many DFAs have separate areas for large and small dogs
  • Parks can be icy in winter - watch for salt on paws
  • City parks require dogs to be registered and vaccinated
  • Early morning and evening visits are usually less crowded
