Year-Round Safety in Honolulu, Hawaii

  • Summer: Year-round warm temperatures. Leptospirosis is a significant risk, especially after heavy rains. Keep pets away from standing water and muddy areas.
  • Winter: Mild winters require no cold-weather precautions. Heavy rains increase flash flood risk and leptospirosis exposure.
  • Spring: Consistent warm weather means year-round parasite pressure. Flea prevention is essential. Cane toad encounters increase after rain.
  • Fall: Hurricane season runs June-November. Vog (volcanic fog) from active volcanic areas can affect pet respiratory health.
  • Severe Weather: Hurricanes, tsunamis, and volcanic activity require specific preparedness. Keep pet emergency supplies including carriers and documentation ready at all times.
