Find Vets & Pet Services in Columbus, Ohio

Columbus is a growing, pet-friendly city with excellent resources for pet owners. From German Village to the Short North, Upper Arlington to Clintonville, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout the Columbus metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Columbus Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Columbus

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Columbus has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals, including the renowned Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center.

Tips for Columbus Pet Emergencies

  • Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center offers world-class emergency care
  • MedVet and other emergency clinics also serve the metro area
  • Highway traffic can be unpredictable - plan routes in advance
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $100-250 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Columbus

Pet Boarding Facilities in Columbus

Columbus offers various boarding options from traditional kennels to modern pet hotels with webcam access.

Tips for Pet Boarding in Columbus

  • Book well in advance, especially for OSU football weekends and holidays
