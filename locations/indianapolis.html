Seasonal Pet Care in Indianapolis

Heartworm prevention is recommended at minimum April through November, though many vets now recommend year-round protection. Lyme disease is increasing in the upper Midwest.

Year-Round Safety in Indianapolis, Indiana

  • Summer: Hot and humid summers with heat waves common in July-August. Thunderstorms can be severe. Firework season in summer causes pet anxiety and escapes.
  • Winter: Harsh winters with extended sub-zero temperatures. Limit outdoor time during wind chills below 0°F. Road salt irritates and cracks paw pads — use pet-safe booties or wax.
  • Spring: Rapid flooding from snowmelt creates hazards. Tornado season begins in spring. Tick populations are expanding northward across the Midwest.
  • Fall: Hunting season increases risks for outdoor pets — use bright vests. Antifreeze toxicity risk rises as temperatures drop.
  • Severe Weather: Tornadoes are a significant risk spring through early fall. Include pets in your tornado safety plan and identify pet-safe shelter areas in your home.

Typical Vet Costs in Indianapolis

Veterinary pricing in Indianapolis generally tracks with national averages. Expect to pay between $50 and $150 for a standard wellness exam, $300 to $800 for dental cleanings (pre-anesthetic bloodwork extra), and $200 or more just for the exam fee at an emergency clinic. Core vaccinations typically run $75 to $200 annually. Costs climb with pet size and service complexity. Pet insurance, with average monthly premiums around $56 for dogs and $32 for cats, can help absorb the unexpected bills.
