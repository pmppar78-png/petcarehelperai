Emergency Veterinarians in San Jose

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. San Jose and the South Bay have several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals.

Tips for San Jose Pet Emergencies

  • Multiple emergency clinics serve the South Bay area
  • UC Davis is available for complex referral cases

  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $150-300 before treatment
