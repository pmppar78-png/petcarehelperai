Year-Round Safety in Jackson, Mississippi

  • Summer: Prolonged extreme heat and oppressive humidity from May through September. Heat index frequently exceeds 110°F. Pets can develop heatstroke in minutes.
  • Winter: Generally mild but occasional hard freezes occur. Short-coated pets may need protection during cold snaps. Frozen water bowls can leave outdoor pets without water.
  • Spring: Heavy rains increase mosquito populations dramatically. Flea pressure is intense from early spring onward.
  • Fall: Hurricane season peaks August-October. Late-season storms can bring flooding and displacement. Venomous snake activity increases in warm fall weather.
  • Severe Weather: Hurricanes and flooding are primary threats. Always include pets in evacuation plans. Keep vaccination records accessible for emergency shelter admission.
