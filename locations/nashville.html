Nashville is a growing, vibrant city with a strong pet-loving community. From East Nashville to the Gulch, Green Hills to Germantown, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout Music City and the surrounding Davidson County area.

Emergency Veterinarians in Nashville

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go is critical. Nashville has several excellent 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals serving the metro area.

Tips for Nashville Pet Emergencies

  • BluePearl Pet Hospital has an emergency location in Nashville
  • Nashville Pet Emergency Clinic serves the area 24/7
  • Traffic on I-65 and I-24 can be heavy - plan alternate routes
  • Keep your pet's medical records accessible on your phone
  • Emergency visits typically start at $100-225 before treatment
