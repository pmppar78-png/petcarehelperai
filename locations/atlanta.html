Find Vets & Pet Services in Atlanta, Georgia

Atlanta is a vibrant, pet-friendly city with a thriving community of pet owners across neighborhoods like Midtown, Buckhead, Decatur, and beyond. Whether you need emergency veterinary care, a trusted regular vet, or quality pet services, this guide helps you find the best pet care options throughout the Atlanta metro area.

24/7 Online Vet Care for Atlanta Pet Owners

Can't get to a vet right away? Get instant advice from licensed veterinarians online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Atlanta

When your pet has an emergency, knowing where to go can save precious time. Atlanta has several 24-hour emergency veterinary hospitals serving the metro area. Keep these resources handy before an emergency strikes.

Tips for Atlanta Pet Emergencies

  • BluePearl Pet Hospital has multiple emergency locations around Atlanta
  • Georgia Veterinary Specialists offers 24/7 emergency care
  • Traffic on I-285 and I-85 can be unpredictable - know multiple routes
  • Keep your pet's vaccination records and medical history accessible
  • Emergency visits typically range from $125-250 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Atlanta

Finding a great regular vet in Atlanta means considering location, expertise, and whether they fit your pet's specific needs. Many Atlanta veterinarians offer extended hours and weekend appointments.

Tips for Finding an Atlanta Vet

  • Look for AAHA-accredited practices for highest care standards
  • Consider proximity to both your home and workplace
  • Many Atlanta vets offer fear-free certification for anxious pets
  • Ask about their emergency referral network
  • Check if they offer wellness plans for routine care

Exotic Pet Veterinarians in Atlanta

Atlanta has excellent options for exotic pet care, including specialists for birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other exotic animals. Not all vets treat exotics, so finding a specialist is important.

Tips for Exotic Pet Owners in Atlanta

  • UGA Veterinary Teaching Hospital is a regional resource for exotic cases
  • Ask specifically about experience with your pet's species
  • Exotic vet visits often cost more than standard pet visits
  • Some exotic specialists require referrals
  • Keep a list of exotic-certified vets before you need one urgently
