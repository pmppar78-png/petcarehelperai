Find Vets & Pet Services in Los Angeles

Los Angeles County spans a massive area with diverse neighborhoods, from the San Fernando Valley to the South Bay. Whether you're in Hollywood, Santa Monica, Pasadena, or Long Beach, this guide helps you find quality pet services throughout LA.

24/7 Online Vet Care for LA Pet Owners

Beat LA traffic and get instant vet advice online. Pawp offers unlimited 24/7 consultations plus a $3,000 emergency fund, Airvet provides on-demand video consultations, and Vetster lets you choose your own vet.

Emergency Veterinarians in Los Angeles

LA's sprawling geography means knowing your nearest emergency vet is crucial. Traffic can add significant time to any trip, so identify the closest 24-hour facility to both your home and workplace.

Tips for LA Pet Emergencies

  • VCA and Access specialty hospitals have multiple LA locations
  • Know emergency vets near both home and work due to traffic
  • Keep emergency vet addresses saved in your maps app
  • Some areas have mobile emergency vet services
  • Emergency visits typically range from $150-400 before treatment

Regular Veterinarians in Los Angeles

LA has thousands of veterinary practices ranging from neighborhood clinics to state-of-the-art specialty hospitals. Finding one convenient to your daily routine makes regular care much easier.

Tips for Finding an LA Vet

  • Consider vets near your commute route for easier visits
  • Many LA vets offer extended evening and weekend hours

Dog Parks in Los Angeles

LA has numerous dog parks and beaches where your pup can run off-leash. Many parks have separate areas for large and small dogs.

Tips for LA Dog Parks

  • Runyon Canyon is famous but can be crowded - go early
  • Several beaches allow off-leash dogs during certain hours
  • Bring water - LA parks can get hot
  • Many parks require dogs to be spayed/neutered
  • Watch for foxtails in grassy areas during summer
