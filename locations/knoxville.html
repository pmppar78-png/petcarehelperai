Year-Round Safety in Knoxville, Tennessee

  • Summer: Hot and humid summers with temperatures regularly in the 90s°F. Thunderstorms are frequent. Heat index can exceed 105°F. Limit outdoor exercise to early morning and evening.
  • Winter: Variable winters with occasional ice storms that can be more dangerous than snow. Power outages from ice accumulation are common. Have backup heating plans for pets.
  • Spring: Flood risk from spring rains. Tornado risk increases March-May. Flea and tick pressure begins early in the upper South.
  • Fall: Extended warm fall means continued tick activity. Hunting season requires caution in rural areas. Acorns and black walnuts can be toxic to dogs.
  • Severe Weather: Severe thunderstorms and tornadoes are possible spring through fall. Some areas face remnant hurricane impacts. Have weather alerts enabled and a pet emergency plan ready.
