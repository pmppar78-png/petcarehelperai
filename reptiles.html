Reptile Emergency Warning Signs

Seek immediate exotic veterinary care if your reptile shows:

  • Open-mouth breathing, wheezing, or respiratory distress
  • Prolapse (tissue protruding from the vent)
  • Severe burns from heating elements
  • Trauma or suspected broken bones
  • Complete refusal to move or severe lethargy
  • Seizures or tremors
  • Mouth rot (white, cheesy discharge in mouth)

Note: Reptiles often hide illness. Changes in behavior, appetite, or appearance warrant prompt veterinary evaluation.
