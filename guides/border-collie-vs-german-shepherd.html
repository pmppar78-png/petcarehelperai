Personality Overview

The Border Collie is known for being a high-energy herding breed with a distinctive personality. Herding breeds are intelligent, alert, and may try to herd family members, especially children.

Weighing around 30-55 lbs and lifespan of 12-15 yrs, the Border Collie comes with breed-specific considerations worth understanding early. The practical information below will help guide your decisions.
