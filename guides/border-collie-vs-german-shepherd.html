Cost of Ownership

A Border Collie stays nearer 30 to 55 pounds and works in bursts of focus. A German Shepherd is commonly 50 to 90 pounds, with a longer growth and a hip history that lasts into adulthood. The Shepherd takes more floor space and a larger crate. The Collie takes more of the handler's attention during the day. Choose the limit you can actually keep.
