How Big Do Cane Corsos Get? Size & Growth Guide

Cane Corso full size: 88-120 lbs, large breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Cane Corsos are a large breed, reaching 88-120 lbs at full maturity. Large breeds take 12-24 months to reach their adult size, with most of their height achieved by 12 months and filling out continuing until 18-24 months.

At 88-120 lbs and with a typical 9-12 yrs lifespan, the Cane Corso rewards owners who understand the breed's specific quirks rather than treating it as a generic pet. The following sections address key owner concerns.

Many experienced Cane Corso owners recommend a balanced mix of physical activities and brain games.

Mental stimulation is as important as physical exercise for Cane Corso. Boredom is the root cause of most destructive behavior — not disobedience. Puzzle feeders, scent work, and novel experiences challenge your Cane Corso's mind in ways that a standard walk cannot. Change up the routine regularly: the same toys and the same routes lose their enrichment value quickly.
