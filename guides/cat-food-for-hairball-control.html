    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    Owners often call a cough a hairball. Asthma and heart disease can look like that hack, which is why an adult cat is still examined about once a year and a cat from about 10 years old is often examined every six months. Kittens are examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, and a kitten hack should not be written off as a hairball at those visits. A hairball diet does not sort those causes out. Repeated retching, weight loss, or a cat who stops passing stool should be seen now.
