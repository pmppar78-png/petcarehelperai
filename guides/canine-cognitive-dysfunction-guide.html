    Key Questions

    Disorientation in an older dog gets an exam that separates cognitive change from pain, sensory loss, and organ disease. The AAHA Senior Care Guidelines describe that senior exam.

    Disorientation, a reversed sleep cycle, or new house soiling in an older dog needs an exam that separates cognitive dysfunction from pain, hearing loss, vision loss, and organ disease. Book that visit when the change starts. The background calendar is puppies every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, adults at least yearly, and many dogs from about 7 years, giant breeds earlier, every six months, which is often when these signs are first obvious. A cognition supplement does not skip the bloodwork.

    A stack of cognition supplements, bought before bloodwork, can be treating pain, organ disease, or sensory loss with a powder. The AAHA senior exam is the purchase that finds a cause you can actually do something about. Add a supplement only if the veterinarian still wants it after that visit. The unopened jars are the savings.
