Compare plans covering HCM, PRA, luxating patella, monthly costs, and coverage recommendations.

Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Bengals is particularly important given their predisposition to HCM, PRA, luxating patella.

A Bengal is priced as a cat. The breed-specific claims are hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, progressive retinal atrophy (PRA-b), pyruvate kinase deficiency, and, in some cats, a luxating patella.

Quick Answers

For a Bengal, the claims that move the quote are hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, progressive retinal atrophy, and pyruvate kinase deficiency.

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in a Bengal is medical: echocardiogram, then medication. Progressive retinal atrophy and pyruvate kinase deficiency are not operations. A luxating patella is the one that sometimes needs surgery, and only when the grade makes the cat lame.

For Bengals, common pre-existing concerns include HCM and PRA. .
