Aging and Senior

Start German Shepherd coverage before hip and elbow radiographs are scored. Abnormal films are the pre-existing orthopedic record. Degenerative myelopathy that is already making the dog wobble will not become a new covered illness. A SOD1 copy count is only a risk marker, not a diagnosis. Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency confirmed with a TLI can be excluded if it predates the policy.

German Shepherd hip and elbow films are the screening that predicts more real-world problems than a cheek swab. Degenerative myelopathy testing looks for the SOD1 risk variant. Two copies raise risk. They do not diagnose a dog that is already wobbly, and one copy is common in dogs that never become ataxic. Hemophilia and a few other clotting variants exist in some lines. Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency is a blood TLI test, not a gene result. Buy the radiographs and the cardiac auscultation, then add SOD1 if you are choosing breeding stock.
