Border Collie vs Australian Shepherd: Side-by-Side

Border Collie and Australian Shepherd look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Australian Shepherd is larger at 40-65 lbs, while Border Collie typically runs 30-55 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Both breeds share a high energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around hip dysplasia, epilepsy. Border Collie carries additional risk for collie eye anomaly. Australian Shepherd is more notably predisposed to cataracts. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.
