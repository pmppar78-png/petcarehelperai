How much does Chartreux pet insurance cost?

NAPHIA's 2025 State of the Industry report, compiled by Willis Towers Watson from year-end 2024 premiums, puts US cat accident-and-illness coverage at $32.21 a month ($386.47 a year) and accident-only coverage at $9.17 a month. Enrollment age, ZIP code, deductible, reimbursement percentage, and annual limit set the quote. For a Chartreux, the claims that move the quote are patellar luxation and urinary stones.
