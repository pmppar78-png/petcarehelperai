How Big Do Golden Retrievers Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

These notes cover a Golden Retriever's growth until the adult gundog outline is in place. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Feathering makes a Golden Retriever look larger than the tape, and a female and a male can still take different space in a corridor.

Parents predict the landing spot better than a puppy photo does. A pair of 23 inch parents is a different forecast from a tall male and a small female. Weigh through the first year, and add another weigh-in whenever the youngster is racing high in the band or stalling while eating well. The gundog outline is athletic, built in Scotland for retrieving, and a wide soft middle is extra mass the joints carry. Golden Retrievers will eat past a healthy stop.

Paws look generous early and the chest arrives later, which fools people into buying a tiny harness. A doorway makes a decent monthly station: dog square, cloth tape at the withers, scale on the same tile. Write the sex on the card. Ears and skull stay out of the number. AKC describes a medium-long neck that merges into well laid-back shoulders, and feet that are medium, round, compact, and well knuckled, with thick pads. The backline stays level from the shoulder to a slightly sloping croup, and a side photo each month is how a household sees that line. VCA notes sensitivity to heat because the coat is dense, so a growth walk on a hot afternoon stays shorter than the same route on a cool morning.

Male vs Female Size

The adult coat hides the waist, so use your hands: ribs felt, a waist from above, a tuck from the side. Ask for a calorie number at the spay or neuter visit, when the requirement drops.

Factors Affecting Size

A Golden Retriever needs a puzzle or a retrieve after the walk. A bored dog chews shoes and baseboards. Swap the toy and the route before both go stale, and end the retrieve so the dog can lie down. A retrieve in the yard after breakfast, then a quiet chew, uses the mouth the breed was built for.

When They Stop Growing

Most height is in by 12 to 14 months. The tail plume and the thighs fill in between 18 and 24 months. A Golden Retriever who already stands at the shoulder mark can keep gaining after that height has settled, and the scale is what shows it. Use the same scale every month, ahead of the morning meal, and record the date beside the figure. A heavy week of extra kibble shows up on that card before the feathering does. Calling the dog finished when the shoulder looks level, then acting surprised at a later weigh-in, is the miss this timeline is meant to prevent. The monthly card is what separates a heavy adolescent from a dog whose outline has settled. Height and the later fill are different events, and the card is how a household tells them apart. A square look in a photo can still fall short of the adult outline, and only the next month's tape shows which way the body went.

What scale reading fits a female Golden Retriever, and what fits a male?

A female weighs 55 to 65 lb. A male weighs 65 to 75 lb. On the AKC standard a female stands 21.5 to 22.5 inches and a male stands 23 to 24 inches.
