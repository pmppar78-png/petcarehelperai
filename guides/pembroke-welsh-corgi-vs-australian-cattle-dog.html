Pembroke Welsh Corgi vs Australian Cattle Dog: Side-by-Side

Pembroke Welsh Corgi and Australian Cattle Dog look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Australian Cattle Dog is larger at 35-50 lbs, while Pembroke Welsh Corgi typically runs 25-30 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Both breeds share a high energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around hip dysplasia. Pembroke Welsh Corgi carries additional risk for degenerative myelopathy, obesity. Australian Cattle Dog is more notably predisposed to progressive retinal atrophy, deafness. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.

Grooming effort is meaningfully different: Pembroke Welsh Corgi sheds at a heavy level, Australian Cattle Dog at moderate. That drives brush frequency, vacuum load, and whether the coat tolerates a week between sessions or demands daily attention during peak seasons.
