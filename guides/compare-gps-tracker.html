GPS Trackers vs. Bluetooth Tags

Understanding the difference is crucial for choosing the right device.

True GPS Trackers (Fi, Whistle, Tractive)

  • Use cellular networks for real-time location
  • Work anywhere with cell coverage
  • Require monthly subscription for cellular service
  • Continuous, accurate tracking
  • Best for: Active tracking, rural areas, escape-prone pets

Bluetooth Tags (AirTag, SmartTag)

  • Rely on network of other users' phones to report location
  • No subscription fees
  • Limited range without other devices nearby
  • Best in urban areas with many Apple/Samsung users
  • Best for: Urban environments, backup tracking, budget option

AirTag Warning

Apple AirTags are NOT designed for pets and don't provide real-time tracking. They only update location when another Apple device passes by. In rural areas or if your pet runs far, an AirTag may not update for hours or days. For true pet safety, use a dedicated GPS pet tracker.

Tips for Using GPS Trackers

Maximize Battery Life

  • Use WiFi base stations at home (reduces GPS pings)
  • Set appropriate tracking frequency for your needs
  • Charge regularly before battery depletes completely
  • Use power-saving mode when not traveling

Set Up for Success

  • Configure geofence zones (home, dog park, etc.)
  • Test Lost Mode before you actually need it
  • Add multiple family members to the account
  • Ensure collar fits snugly but comfortably
  • Check that ID tags and microchip info are also current

If Your Pet Goes Missing

  • Immediately activate Lost Mode for real-time tracking
  • Share location with family members
  • Have a charged phone ready for tracking
  • GPS tracker is one tool - also notify neighbors, shelters, and post on social media
