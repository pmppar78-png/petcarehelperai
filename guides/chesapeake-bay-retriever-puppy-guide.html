First-Year Health Milestones

Early intervention consistently produces better outcomes and lower costs than reactive treatment for breed-associated conditions. Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Chesapeake Bay Retrievers are prone to.

Chesapeake Bay Retrievers should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 1-2 years of age, as large breeds develop structural issues early. Proactive testing tends to pay for itself in avoided complications.
