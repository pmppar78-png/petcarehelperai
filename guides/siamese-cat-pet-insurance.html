Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Siameses is particularly important given their predisposition to amyloidosis, dental disease, asthma.

Hepatic amyloidosis, bronchial asthma, and juvenile or chronic gingivitis are the conditions that generate claims.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies amyloidosis, dental disease, asthma as conditions with higher prevalence in Siameses.

Amyloidosis Risk and

For a Siamese, the claims that move the quote are hepatic amyloidosis, asthma, and gingivitis.

Hepatic amyloidosis in a Siamese is a liver and inflammatory disease treated medically. Asthma is treated with inhaled or oral medication the veterinarian prescribes. Gingivitis needs dentistry, often more than once.

For Siameses, common pre-existing concerns include amyloidosis and dental disease. .
