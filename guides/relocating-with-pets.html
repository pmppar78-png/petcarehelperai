Where can I learn more?

Crossing a state line or a border is paperwork. CDC dog-import rules and USDA APHIS pet-travel pages name the health certificate and rabies requirements, and the destination state's own rules can be tighter. Ask the clinic for the health certificate and rabies record the destination state requires. Book the exam early enough that the paperwork is dated correctly.

How can I save money on pet care?

A health certificate or rabies document outside the destination's dating window has to be redone, which is a second exam fee. Read the CDC or USDA APHIS rule, and the destination state's rule, before you book the appointment and the movers. Update the address on the microchip registration before a lost dog is searched for under the old city. Buy food the pet already eats, enough to cover the days you cannot shop.
