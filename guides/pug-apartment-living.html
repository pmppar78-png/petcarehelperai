Pug in an Apartment

Can a Pug thrive in an apartment?

Apartment Suitability Score

Can a Pug live in an apartment? Pugs can adapt well to apartment living. Their small size and low energy levels make them more suitable for smaller spaces.

Expect 14-18 lbs at maturity and 13-15 yrs of life with a Pug; the combination of its health pattern and temperament profile is where owner attention pays the largest dividends.
