Should I go to the emergency vet?

A dog that hides and also cannot stand, has a swollen belly, or is bleeding needs the emergency clinic now. Hiding plus a fever, pale gums, or a bite wound that is hot and oozing should be seen the same day.

How much will treatment cost?

The exam looks for pain, a fever, and a source such as a bite, a back injury, or an upset abdomen. Bloodwork is added when the dog is also off food. Ask what the estimate includes before you approve a panel.
