Where can I learn more?

Domestic trips still follow the airline's pet rules and any state health certificate the destination requires. International trips use CDC dog-import rules and USDA APHIS pet-travel pages. A general packing list does not name the breed embargo or the rabies timing. Read the rule that matches the actual border.

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

Book the pre-travel exam early enough for the destination's health certificate and for a parasite plan that matches the region. A dog that gets carsick may need a prescribed drug tried at home first. The annual exam counts only if it falls inside the certificate window the airline or the state requires.

How can I save money on pet care?

Read the rule that matches the border you will actually cross before you pay for tickets, crates, and an exam on the wrong day. CDC and USDA APHIS pages cover imports. A domestic trip may only need the airline's crate rule and a health certificate in a set window. Doing the exam twice because the first one expired is the fee to avoid. Pack the food you already use so you are not buying a new diet in a city where it also upsets the stomach.
