How often should I take my pet to the vet?

A safe-food list does not move a cat's exams. Kittens are seen every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks even when the only menu question is whether a bite of chicken was safe. Adult cats are examined about once a year, and cats from about 10 years old are often examined every six months. Onion, garlic, alcohol, caffeine, grapes, and xylitol are a same-day problem if a cat ate them or is vomiting after table food. A piece of plain cooked meat does not replace bloodwork.
