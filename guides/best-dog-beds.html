Types of Dog Beds

  • Orthopedic/Memory Foam: Best for senior dogs, large breeds, and dogs with arthritis or joint issues
  • Bolster/Pillow: Great for dogs who like to rest their head on something while sleeping
  • Donut/Nest: Ideal for dogs who curl up and like to feel secure
  • Elevated/Cot: Best for hot climates, outdoor use, or dogs that overheat
  • Heated: Perfect for senior dogs, short-haired breeds, or cold climates
  • Cave/Hooded: Great for dogs who like to burrow and feel enclosed

Measure Your Dog First

Before purchasing, measure your dog from nose to tail base while lying down, then add 6-12 inches. Dogs should be able to stretch out fully without hanging off the bed. When in doubt, size up.

How often should I replace my dog's bed?

Most dog beds should be replaced every 1-3 years, depending on quality and usage. Signs it's time for a new bed include flattened foam, visible wear, persistent odors even after washing, or if your dog stops using it.

Are orthopedic beds worth it for young dogs?

While not essential for healthy young dogs, orthopedic beds can help prevent joint problems later in life, especially for large breeds prone to hip dysplasia. They're a good investment for any dog.

How do I clean a dog bed?

Most quality dog beds have removable, machine-washable covers. Wash covers weekly in hot water to kill bacteria and dust mites. Vacuum the foam insert monthly and spot clean as needed. Air out beds in sunlight periodically.
