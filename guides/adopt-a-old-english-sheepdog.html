Finding an Old English Sheepdog to Adopt

If you are set on an Old English Sheepdog, at least know that rescue is an option before you commit to a breeder waiting list. Purebred Old English Sheepdogs reach rescue through owner surrender, divorces, downsizing, and occasionally from breeders themselves when a placement falls through. The dogs have usually been vetted, temperament-tested in a foster home, and priced well below a puppy from a responsible breeder.

The Old English Sheepdog typically weighs 60-100 lbs and lives 10-12 yrs; owner results track strongly to how seriously the breed's unique health and temperament traits are taken. Few breeds combine steady enthusiasm with the Old English Sheepdog's distinctive character quite so effectively.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Old English Sheepdogs have elevated rates of hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, cataracts. Many individual animals never show the conditions in question; when they do, a breed-literate veterinarian usually identifies them sooner.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Old English Sheepdogs

A regular vet schedule based on your Old English Sheepdog's age and breed-specific risks is the best health investment you can make. Adjust the schedule based on your vet's advice.

Life StageVisit FrequencyKey Screenings
Puppy (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation

Old English Sheepdogs should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 1-2 years of age, as large breeds develop structural issues early. Proactive testing tends to pay for itself in avoided complications.
