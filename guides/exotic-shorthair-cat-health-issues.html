Common Health Problems

Exotic Shorthairs are predisposed to several health conditions including PKD, breathing issues, eye problems.

The Exotic Shorthair is distinct among cat breeds: a medium cat with short fur, low energy demands, and predispositions that informed owners should understand.

Genetic Screening

  • Size: medium
  • Energy Level: Low
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 8-15 yrs

Prevention Strategies

Exotic Shorthairs bring a medium build, a moderate shedding pattern, and breed-specific health risk around PKD and breathing issues — each of those shifts routine care in a different direction.
