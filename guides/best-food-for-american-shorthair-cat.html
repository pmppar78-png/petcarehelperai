Nutritional Needs of American Shorthairs

American Shorthairs typically weigh 8-15 lbs and need approximately 200–350 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. With moderate energy levels, most American Shorthairs do well on standard feeding guidelines for their size.

Health Predisposition Summary: American Shorthairs show higher-than-average incidence of HCM, obesity, dental disease based on breed health database data.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for American Shorthairs

Both wet and dry food have advantages for American Shorthairs.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your American Shorthair's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your American Shorthair's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Body condition

American Shorthairs are a sturdy, generally healthy breed, and the feeding problem owners actually see is weight from a bowl that stays full. Feel the ribs without a thick fat pad, and measure a complete cat food into meals. A heart murmur on exam is a reason for an echocardiogram of that cat. The breed does not have a Maine Coon-style DNA test or a standing echo program written for every kitten. Dental tartar is the other common finding, and it is treated by dentistry.

Adult American Shorthairs typically need a measured daily ration based on ideal weight, calorie density, and body condition, split into two or more meals.

What is the best food brand for American Shorthairs?

American Shorthairs gain weight when the bowl stays full, and a grain-free label does not change that. Measure a complete cat food to body condition and split it into meals. A grain allergy is uncommon in this breed, so ask the veterinarian to confirm one before you pay extra for the bag.
