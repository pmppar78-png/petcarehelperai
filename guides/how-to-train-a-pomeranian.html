How to Train a Pomeranian

Pomeranian training. Luxating patella, patellar luxation, and Lively, Bold, Inquisitive belong in the training plan for the Pomeranian.

Training Approach

Pomeranians are moderate-energy toy dogs that benefit from regular but moderate training routines.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies luxating patella, collapsed trachea, dental disease as conditions with higher prevalence in Pomeranians.

Pomeranian Training Challenges

  • Size: small (3-7 lbs)
  • Energy Level: Moderate
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 12-16 yrs
