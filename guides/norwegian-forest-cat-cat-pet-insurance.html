Norwegian Forest Cat Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Norwegian Forest Cats is particularly important given their predisposition to HCM, hip dysplasia, glycogen storage disease.

Glycogen storage disease type IV is the breed-specific fatal disease, alongside hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and hip dysplasia.

Genetic Health Considerations: The Norwegian Forest Cat breed has documented susceptibility to HCM, hip dysplasia, glycogen storage disease.
