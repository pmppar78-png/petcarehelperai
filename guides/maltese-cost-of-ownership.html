Cost of Maltese Ownership

Before committing to ownership, evaluate whether these costs are sustainable long-term for Maltese ownership.

  • Annual food costs: $250–$500 for high-quality dog food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $30–50 per professional session (occasional home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $25–40/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials

Local Vet & Care Considerations

What a typical year of care costs for Maltese Cost Of Ownership depends heavily on where you live. Dental cleanings are the single most region-sensitive line item, ranging from $250 to well over $900 depending on anesthesia protocols and local wage costs. In humid coastal regions, expect a stronger emphasis on parasite prevention year-round; colder inland areas often shift that spending toward joint and cold-weather care instead. Heat waves and cold snaps are worth preparing for in advance; a simple indoor temperature log for a month reveals patterns you would not otherwise notice.
