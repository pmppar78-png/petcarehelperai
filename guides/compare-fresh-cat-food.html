Best Fresh Cat Food Delivery Services Compared

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Comparing Smalls, Nom Nom, and other fresh cat food delivery services. Covers ingredients, pricing, meal plans, and which is best for your cat.

Detailed Comparison

SmallsNom NomOther fresh or raw brands
What it isFresh cat food sold as complete meals, in refrigerated and freeze-dried formsFresh cooked cat recipes shipped on a scheduleCooked or raw cat foods from several companies, each with its own handling rules
Use it forA cat who eats the fresh food and a refrigerator that can hold itA cooked complete diet in place of the current food, changed over several daysOnly after you read whether that package is a complete diet or a supplement
Check before you buyThe life stage on the nutritional adequacy statementThe same statement, and room to keep the food coldA raw recipe needs the handling the label describes. Kidney disease, urinary crystals, or a food allergy may already have a prescribed diet.

Read the nutritional adequacy statement and the life stage. A fresh food that is complete can replace kibble for a cat who eats it. A cat who already has a prescribed diet for kidney disease, urinary crystals, or a food allergy should stay on that diet unless the veterinarian changes it.

Which option is the best value?

Value is a complete feline diet the cat will eat. An untouched pack in the refrigerator is the expensive outcome.

Can I switch later?

Cancel the plan and use or discard what you have. Mix the new food in over several days. A cat who stops eating needs a veterinarian quickly, because a short fast can start hepatic lipidosis.
