Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Bombays is particularly important given their predisposition to HCM, breathing issues, excessive tearing.

A Bombay is a Burmese-derived cat, and the premium is a cat premium. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is the cardiac disease. Some Burmese lines also carry a craniofacial defect that can affect the skull and breathing.

Health Awareness: Bombays have documented breed-level risk for HCM, breathing issues, excessive tearing.
