Breed Character

The Airedale Terrier's reputation in the terrier group reflects generations of purposeful breeding, resulting in a medium dog with predictable but nuanced care requirements.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Airedale Terriers have elevated rates of hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, cancer.
