How Big Do Beagles Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Beagle full size: 20-30 lbs, medium breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Ribs should take a light touch to find, with a waist from above. The appetite works against that waist across a 10-15 year life if the bowl stays generous. Write the weight down after a walk and before breakfast so the number means something. Two puppies from the same litter can aim at different varieties. Ask the breeder which height the parents actually measure, then watch the puppy's own leg.

Buyers who only saw the sire standing on a stack of towels misread height. Set a level at the withers and hold the head in a natural pose. A treat held high fakes an extra bit of leg. The same hound weighed after one long drink looks heavier than the dawn weight, and both figures belong on one line with the date.

The 15-inch Beagles weigh 20 to 30 pounds and shed a moderate coat.

The nose will take a Beagle through a fence gap, so the gate latch matters as much as the food cup.

Long drop ears and a deep chest make a lean hound look heavier from the side than the scale will confirm.

Growth Timeline

A Beagle with energy left at dusk needs a long sniffing walk. Without that outlet the hound bays at the fence.

  • Size: medium (20-30 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 10-15 yrs

Weight Chart by Age

A Beagle's condition is read on the nine-mark chart published by WSAVA, and a lean hound sits on the middle marks.

Male vs Female Size

A wicket and a scale split this hound in two, which beats any guess made beside a bathroom mirror. One variety is 13 inches and under on the AKC standard. The other stands over 13 and up to 15 in. A 13-inch dog can still be sturdy. A 15-inch dog can still look moderate. Use the wicket and the scale together. A photo of a puppy on a couch tells someone neither number.

Factors Affecting Size

Hide kibble in the grass for a Beagle after the walk, so the nose finishes the day.

When They Stop Growing

The youngster is ears and appetite. Early on, the head seems too big and the legs seem borrowed. A few months later the food intake jumps and the outline rounds out if the bowl stays full. Measure every meal from the week the puppy comes home. Medium-sized hounds often look filled out between 10 and 16 months. Height arrives first. Chest and thigh come after. Calling the dog finished at the first adult-looking month, then acting surprised when the scale moves, is a common miss.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Beagles

Weigh the Beagle at the yearly visit so a slow gain shows up on the scale before the outline changes.
