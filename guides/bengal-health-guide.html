If an echocardiogram has already found hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, the policy treats that heart disease as pre-existing. Pyruvate kinase deficiency and Bengal PRA-b, once they are in the medical record, are pre-existing too. Ask how a hereditary heart condition is treated after the waiting period if the echo was still normal at enrollment.

Bengal breeders should show pyruvate kinase deficiency and Bengal PRA-b results. UC Davis lists that pair. Neither test says anything about hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, so an echocardiogram is still the heart screen. A 'clear PK and PRA' kitten can have a thick ventricle. Ask for the echo on the breeding cats, not only the DNA. Patellar luxation, if it is in the line, is an exam. Ancestry percentages that prove the cat is a Bengal are not health testing.
