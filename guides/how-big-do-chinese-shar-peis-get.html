How Big Do Chinese Shar-Peis Get? Size & Growth Guide

Chinese Shar-Pei full size: 45-60 lbs, medium breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Chinese Shar-Peis are a medium breed, reaching 45-60 lbs at full maturity. Medium breeds generally reach full size between 10-16 months.

At 45-60 lbs with a 8-12 yrs lifespan, the Chinese Shar-Pei has a health and temperament profile that rewards close attention rather than generic care. Below, we break down the practical details.

Knowledge of breed-specific characteristics directly translates to better day-to-day care. Plan Chinese Shar-Peis care around a medium body size, light shedding, and the breed's documented predisposition toward skin infections and entropion.

The key to a happy, healthy Chinese Shar-Pei is matching your care approach to their breed characteristics. A sedentary lifestyle carries health risks regardless of breed predisposition — joint stiffness, weight gain, and behavioral issues increase with inactivity.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for medium breed dogs (800–1,200 calories/day)
  • Maintain a weekly grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for skin infections
  • Pet insurance enrolled early typically offers the best value, covering breed-related conditions before they develop

Cost of Chinese Shar-Pei Ownership

Budgeting ahead avoids hard choices later. Typical ongoing expenses for Chinese Shar-Pei ownership.

  • Annual food costs: $400–$800 for high-quality dog food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $45–70 per professional session (weekly home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $35–55/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials
