Siamese Cat Grooming

Grooming Schedule

Siameses have light shedding and require weekly brushing. Regular grooming sessions keep your Siamese's coat healthy and help you bond with your cat.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Siameses have elevated rates of amyloidosis, dental disease, asthma.
