Bombay Cat Grooming

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Grooming guide for Bombay cats with short light-shedding coat. Brushing, bathing, nail trimming, and coat maintenance tips.

Grooming Schedule

Bombays have light shedding and require weekly brushing. Regular grooming sessions keep your Bombay's coat healthy and help you bond with your cat.

Weighing around 6-11 lbs and lifespan of 12-16 yrs, the Bombay benefits from care tailored to its physical and behavioral profile. Not many cat breeds share the Bombay's specific mix of moderate activity, light shedding characteristics, and distinctive health considerations.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies HCM, breathing issues, excessive tearing as conditions with higher prevalence in Bombays. These are population-level trends, not individual certainties. Discuss with your veterinarian which screening tests are recommended for your Bombay Cat.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Bombays

Preventive care reduces both emergency costs and disease severity over your pet's lifetime. Here is a general framework for your Bombay. These are baseline recommendations.

Life StageVisit FrequencyKey Screenings
Kitten (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation
Adult (1-7 years)AnnuallyPhysical exam, dental check, heartworm test, vaccination boosters
Senior (7+ years)Every 6 monthsBlood work, urinalysis, HCM screening, Breathing Issues screening, Excessive Tearing screening

Bombays should receive breed-specific screening for HCM starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Screening before symptoms appear makes a meaningful difference in outcomes.

The Bombay has a short, close black coat that still sheds and still gets oily along the neck. A weekly rubber-curry or a damp cloth keeps the coat flat. Ears, nails, and teeth are the rest of the grooming. This is a muscular cat that likes heat, so a bath in a cold room is a poor plan.
