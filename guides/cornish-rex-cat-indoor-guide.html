Preventing Boredom

The cost difference between catching a condition early versus treating it at an advanced stage is typically 3-5x, not counting quality-of-life impact. Watch for early signs of HCM, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your cat at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Cornish Rex Cats are prone to.

A predictable rhythm around meals, activity, and rest tends to reduce stress for most pets. Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy Cornish Rexs especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.
