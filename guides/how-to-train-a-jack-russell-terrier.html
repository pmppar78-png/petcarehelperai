How to Train a Jack Russell Terrier

Jack Russell Terrier training. Tips for their high energy terrier breed temperament.

Training Approach

Jack Russell Terriers are high-energy terrier dogs that require consistent mental stimulation and structured training sessions. Terriers are spirited and determined, requiring creative training approaches that channel their natural tenacity.

Weighing around 13-17 lbs and lifespan of 13-16 yrs, the Jack Russell Terrier requires attention to its unique health and temperament characteristics. The Jack Russell Terrier stands out among small breeds, weighing 13-17 lbs and carrying a temperament shaped by the terrier group's heritage.

Cost of Jack Russell Terrier Ownership

Ownership costs vary by region, health status, and lifestyle. These ranges reflect national averages for Jack Russell Terrier ownership:

  • Annual food costs: $250–$500 for high-quality dog food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $30–50 per professional session (2–3 times per week home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $25–40/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials
