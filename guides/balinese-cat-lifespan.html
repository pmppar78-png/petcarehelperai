Balinese Cat Lifespan & Longevity Guide

How long do Balinese cats live? Average lifespan of 12-20 yrs, health factors, and tips for maximizing your cats years.

Average Lifespan

The Balinese has an average lifespan of 12-20 yrs. With proper nutrition, exercise, and veterinary care, many Balineses live full, healthy lives.

Weighing around 5-10 lbs and lifespan of 12-20 yrs, the Balinese benefits from care tailored to its physical and behavioral profile. For those considering the Balinese, the breed's combination of moderate shedding, high activity level, and known health predispositions forms the essential baseline for informed ownership.

  • Maintain a 2–3 times per week grooming routine

Balineses should receive breed-specific screening for amyloidosis starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Screening before symptoms appear makes a meaningful difference in outcomes.

Cost of Balinese Ownership

  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $45–70 per professional session (2–3 times per week home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $35–55/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials

What tends to get overlooked about Balinese Cat Lifespan is how much the environment around them shapes day-to-day behavior.
