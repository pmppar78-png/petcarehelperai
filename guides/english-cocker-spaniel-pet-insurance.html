Pet insurance for English Cocker Spaniels is particularly important given their predisposition to ear infections, hip dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy.

Quick Answers

For an English Cocker Spaniel, the claims that move the quote are familial nephropathy, progressive retinal atrophy, and chronic otitis.

Familial nephropathy in an English Cocker Spaniel is medical kidney failure. Progressive retinal atrophy is not restored by surgery. Chronic otitis is treated with medication, and total ear canal ablation is reserved for an end-stage canal. Glaucoma is medical first.
