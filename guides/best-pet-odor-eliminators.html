    Questions Owners Ask

    House odor that starts in the pet is dental disease, otitis, anal sac disease, or urine. The AAHA Dental Care Guidelines are the dental reading. Cornell's feline urinary pages cover the cat who is suddenly urinating outside the box. Enzyme spray is cleaning.

    House odor often starts in the pet: dental disease, an infected ear, anal glands, or urine. Kittens and puppies are examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks. Adult dogs and cats are examined about once a year, and many senior dogs from about 7 years, giant breeds earlier, and senior cats from about 10, are examined twice a year, and the mouth, ears, and rear are part of that hands-on exam. Weight loss plus a new smell, or head shaking, should be seen this week. Spraying the animal with a household odor product can poison it.

    Enzyme spray used every week on the same smell is a repeating purchase that leaves the cause in the pet. Dental disease, an infected ear, anal sac disease, or a cat urinating outside the box each has a visit that stops the smell. Buy the cleaner for accidents. Spend the next dollar on the exam if the odor keeps coming back.
