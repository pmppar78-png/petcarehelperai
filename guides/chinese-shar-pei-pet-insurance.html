Quick Answers

For a Chinese Shar-Pei, the claims that move the quote are amyloidosis and entropion.

Shar-Pei fever and the amyloidosis that follows it are medical. There is no operation that removes the fever disorder. Entropion, which this breed often needs corrected so the lids stop rolling into the cornea, is a real surgery. Fold pyoderma is treated with cleaning and the antibiotics or anti-inflammatories the veterinarian selects, separate from the eyelid surgery.
