How Big Do Labrador Retrievers Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

A Labrador Retriever's growth, puppy through adult build, is laid out in the sections here. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Staying lean matters if hip dysplasia or elbow dysplasia appears. Most of the height is in by 12 months, and most are near adult height by 14 months and can get heavier through 18 to 24 months if the cup stays at a puppy level.

Black, yellow, and chocolate leave the tape untouched, so coat color stays out of the measurement. Stand the dog square and read the height where the neck meets the back, the highest point of the shoulder, with the tape perpendicular to the floor. The tail works as a rudder in the water and can make a youngster look longer than that shoulder mark. Paws look big early, which is only a hint, and the parents' measurements are the better hint. A chunky puppy at 12 weeks should stand beside the mother's elbow before anyone pictures an adult male.

A photo hides which dog is standing there. AKC lists a Labrador's life expectancy as 11-13 years. A 2016 paper in Cell Metabolism described a POMC deletion that blunts satiety in Labrador Retrievers, so the skeleton can sit inside the standard while the dog stays hungry. Write the date beside every monthly number so each gain stays labeled on the card. Stand the dog on the same floor each time, because a rug and a tile can change the reading. AKC asks for a neck long enough to retrieve game, rising from the shoulders with a moderate arch, and for feet that are strong and compact, with well-arched toes and well-developed pads. The topline stays level from the shoulder to the croup while the dog is standing.

After the coat comes in you should still see a waist from above. Take a body-condition score at the one-year visit and match the cup to it.

Weight Chart by Age

Field lines finish leaner and busier. Show lines finish broader, with more bone and a wider chest, and they can look heavy while the scale still sits inside the standard. Broader describes the body outline. The scale still decides how full the bowl should be. The water-repellent jacket hides a thickening middle until a hand goes looking.

Male vs Female Size

Repeat the weigh-in monthly on a single scale, with an empty stomach, and log the date next to the figure. A male who is tall early and still light can land at the top of the male band later. Add a second weigh-in during any month the bowl is being topped off for growth.

Factors Affecting Size

A Labrador Retriever was bred to carry things, so a bumper in the yard gives the mouth a job. A basket of random toys does less for this breed than a retrieve the dog can finish.

When They Stop Growing

A new house or a new baby is when the feeding time and the walk time should stay put. A Labrador Retriever who can predict the walk settles once the shoes are away. Final hip grades from the Orthopedic Foundation for Animals come once the dog is past the second birthday, and an earlier film is only preliminary. PennHIP is a different radiograph and can be taken during adolescence.

How tall and heavy is a full-grown Labrador Retriever?
