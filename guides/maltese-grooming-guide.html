Grooming Schedule

Malteses have minimal shedding and require occasional brushing. While Malteses shed very little, regular grooming is still important for skin health and early detection of lumps or skin issues.

Malteses should receive breed-specific screening for luxating patella starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. The earlier you know, the more you can do about it.
