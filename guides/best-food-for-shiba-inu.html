Nutritional Needs of Shiba Inus

As a medium non-sporting breed with moderate energy levels, the Shiba Inu has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Shiba Inu typically weigh 17-23 lbs and need approximately 800–1,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Food allergies on a seventeen to twenty three pound Shiba Inu keep the adult portion at one and a half to two and a half cups across two meals; a novel protein waits on a diagnosed food allergy.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies allergies, luxating patella, hip dysplasia as conditions with higher prevalence in Shiba Inu.
