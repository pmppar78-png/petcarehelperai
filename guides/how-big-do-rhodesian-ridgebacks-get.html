How Big Do Rhodesian Ridgebacks Get? Size & Growth Guide

Rhodesian Ridgeback full size: 70-85 lbs, large breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Rhodesian Ridgebacks are a large breed, reaching 70-85 lbs at full maturity. Large breeds take 12-24 months to reach their adult size, with most of their height achieved by 12 months and filling out continuing until 18-24 months.

Weighing around 70-85 lbs and lifespan of 10-12 yrs, the Rhodesian Ridgeback comes with breed-specific considerations worth understanding early. The practical information below will help guide your decisions.
