How Big Do French Bulldogs Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

French Bulldog full size: 16-28 lbs, small breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Adult French Bulldogs weigh 16 lb at the light end and 28 lb at the cap.

The adult issues are the airway and the spine, and extra weight makes both work harder. Keep the ribs feelable. Fit a harness to the adult chest. Have the clinic listen to the breathing at each puppy visit as the nostrils and palate finish developing.

People who shop from photos expect a bigger dog. Standing next to a human knee is the real picture: 11 to 13 inches, compact, and finished in height well before some large breeds start their final fill. A hand on the rib cage is how the 28 pound ceiling stays a ceiling.

The adult chest is what a French Bulldog harness has to fit, and the ribs should stay easy to feel.

Growth Timeline

  • Size: small (16-28 lbs)
  • Energy Level: Low
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 10-12 yrs

Weight Chart by Age

Shoulder height for a French Bulldog lands between 11 and 13 inches. AKC caps the breed under a 28-pound ceiling. Adults that people lift usually fall inside a band running from 16 pounds up to that cap. A French Bulldog past that cap makes the airway and the spine work harder. The bat ears add a visual inch the tape leaves out. Measure at the withers, where the neck meets the back, with the dog standing square, or the measurement comes out wrong.

Male vs Female Size

Puppies fill early compared with a giant breed, and they can look finished while they are still soft. Weigh at 8 weeks and again at 12 weeks, then keep the same scale through the early months. The head and the ears often look adult first. The chest widens after that, and the sleek jacket starts to lie tight over ribs a flat hand should still find. A puppy who is already 16 pounds has already used much of that 28 pound cap. A lean youngster who lengthens is a kinder outcome than a round one stalling near the top of the range.

Factors Affecting Size

A French Bulldog can search for kibble hidden under tennis balls in a muffin tin, then practice a short sit in another room. Change the game. Skip buying a new puzzle every week.

When They Stop Growing

Males and females overlap more here than in many breeds. The difference visible on paperwork is bone and chest, while the height stays inside 11 to 13. A broad chest changes the harness fit. Refit the strap when the puppy leaves 12 weeks, because the adult chest is wider than the baby one and a loose harness becomes an escape. Stairs stay a carry while that chest is growing. The published size is small enough to lift and heavy enough, at the top end, to matter for a human back too.
