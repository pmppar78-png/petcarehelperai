Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Persians is particularly important given their predisposition to PKD, breathing issues, eye problems.

Persian premiums are cat premiums with brachycephalic and kidney claims behind them. Polycystic kidney disease is the inherited renal disease, and the flat face brings stenotic nares, tear-duct disease, and corneal sequestra.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies PKD, breathing issues, eye problems as conditions with higher prevalence in Persians.

Polycystic Kidney Disease

For a Persian, the claims that move the quote are polycystic kidney disease, corneal sequestrum, and stenotic nares.

Polycystic kidney disease in a Persian is medical. Ultrasound finds the cysts, and treatment supports kidney function. It is not removed with one operation. A corneal sequestrum can need surgery, and some cats need a procedure for stenotic nares, which is separate from the kidney disease.

For Persians, common pre-existing concerns include PKD and breathing issues. .
