Are German Shepherds Good with Kids? Family Guide

Family Compatibility

German Shepherds are energetic and large, which means they can accidentally knock over small children.

Health Predisposition Summary: German Shepherds show higher-than-average incidence of hip dysplasia, degenerative myelopathy, bloat based on breed health database data.
