Veterinary Care Schedule for Staffordshire Bull Terriers

Keeping up with preventive veterinary care is one of the most important things you can do for your Staffordshire Bull Terrier. Your vet may modify this depending on your pet's history.

Puppy (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation

Staffordshire Bull Terriers should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Most breed-related conditions respond better to early intervention.
