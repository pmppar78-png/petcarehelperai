Staffordshire Bull Terrier training.

Training Approach

Staffordshire Bull Terriers are high-energy terrier dogs that require consistent mental stimulation and structured training sessions.

Health Awareness: Staffordshire Bull Terriers carry known breed-associated risks including hip dysplasia, cataracts, allergies.
