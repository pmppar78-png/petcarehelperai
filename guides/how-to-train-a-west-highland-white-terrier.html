How to Train a West Highland White Terrier

West Highland White Terrier training. Tips for their moderate energy terrier breed temperament.

Training Approach

West Highland White Terriers are moderate-energy terrier dogs that benefit from regular but moderate training routines. Terriers are spirited and determined, requiring creative training approaches that channel their natural tenacity.

Plan for 13-22 lbs of dog and 13-15 yrs of life with a West Highland White Terrier — and plan for an ownership experience that rewards knowing the breed rather than treating it as generic. Advanced Training

Mental stimulation is as important as physical exercise for West Highland White Terrier. Boredom is the root cause of most destructive behavior — not disobedience. Puzzle feeders, scent work, and novel experiences challenge your West Highland White Terrier's mind in ways that a standard walk cannot. Change up the routine regularly: the same toys and the same routes lose their enrichment value quickly.
