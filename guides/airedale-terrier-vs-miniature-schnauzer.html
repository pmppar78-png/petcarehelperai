Pick the Airedale when you want a 50 to 70 pound terrier, a long daily outing, and hand-stripping. Pick the Miniature Schnauzer when the household is closer to 11 to 20 pounds and can keep treats low in fat, because pancreatitis shows up in that breed. Both have wiry coats. Stairs, cars, and the size of the scoop are the daily difference.

Health watchlists differ. Airedale Terrier carries additional risk for hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, cancer. Miniature Schnauzer is more notably predisposed to pancreatitis, urinary stones, cataracts.

Grooming effort is meaningfully different: Airedale Terrier sheds at a moderate level, Miniature Schnauzer at minimal.
