Nutritional Needs of Shetland Sheepdogs

As a small herding breed with high energy levels, the Shetland Sheepdog has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Shetland Sheepdogs typically weigh 15-25 lbs and need approximately 400–800 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Shetland Sheepdogs may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Shetland Sheepdogs

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Shetland Sheepdogs.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Shetland Sheepdog's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Shetland Sheepdog's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Shetland Sheepdog's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Shetland Sheepdogs benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Shetland Sheepdogs may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
