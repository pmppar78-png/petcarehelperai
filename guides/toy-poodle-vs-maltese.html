Toy Poodle vs Maltese

With Family Members

Toy Poodles are about 4 to 6 pounds and need regular clips. Maltese are about 4 to 7 pounds under a white coat that stains with tears and can be kept long or put in a short pet trim. Both can break a leg jumping off furniture. Dental disease shows up early at this weight. Use a kitchen scale for the food, a harness on walks, and a ramp for the couch.
