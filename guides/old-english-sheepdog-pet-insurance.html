Is pet insurance worth it for an Old English Sheepdog?

Autoimmune hypothyroidism in an Old English Sheepdog is treated with thyroid hormone. Congenital deafness is not repaired. Cataracts can be extracted if the retina functions. Hip dysplasia is managed medically or surgically depending on pain, and the thyroid result does not change that choice.
