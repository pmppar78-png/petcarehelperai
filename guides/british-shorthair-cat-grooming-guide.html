Ear & Dental Care

Check a British Shorthair's ears weekly. The dense coat covers the base and hides a small amount of wax until the cat shakes. Wipe only the visible flap unless a veterinarian has shown you otherwise. The round head still gets periodontal disease, so brush if the cat allows it and book a dental when the gums redden. The coat itself needs a weekly comb. Skip the haircut.
