Border Collie

Evidence-based guide to the best dog food for Border Collies based on their medium size, high energy level, and health needs including hip dysplasia.

Nutritional Needs of Border Collies

Border Collies typically weigh 30-55 lbs and need approximately 800–1,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Border Collies may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Border Collies are prone to several health conditions that can be managed or prevented through proper nutrition.

Best Protein Sources for Border Collies

  • Chicken and turkey: Lean, easily digestible proteins ideal for most Border Collies

Supplements Worth Considering

  • Glucosamine & Chondroitin: Essential for joint health, especially important for Border Collies prone to hip dysplasia

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Border Collies

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Border Collies.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Border Collie's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Border Collie's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Border Collie's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Border Collies benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Border Collies may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
