Pet insurance for Birmans is particularly important given their predisposition to HCM, kidney disease, FIP.

Quick Answers

For a Birman, the claims that move the quote are hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, chronic kidney disease, and feline infectious peritonitis.

Birman HCM, when present, is diagnosed by echocardiogram and treated with medication. Chronic kidney disease is diet and monitoring. Feline infectious peritonitis is an infectious disease managed medically.

For Birmans, common pre-existing concerns include HCM and kidney disease. .
