Cornish Rex Cat Grooming

Grooming guide for Cornish Rex cats with short minimal-shedding coat. Brushing, bathing, nail trimming, and coat maintenance tips.

Grooming Schedule

Cornish Rexs have minimal shedding and require occasional brushing. While Cornish Rexs shed very little, regular grooming is still important for skin health and early detection of lumps or skin issues.

The Cornish Rex typically weighs 5-10 lbs and lives 11-15 yrs; owner results track strongly to how seriously the breed's unique health and temperament traits are taken. At 5-10 lbs with a life expectancy spanning 11-15 yrs, the Cornish Rex represents one of the more active and engaging cat breeds available.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Cornish Rexs have elevated rates of HCM, luxating patella, hyperthyroidism. Think of breed predispositions as watchlist items rather than predictions. Many individual animals never show the conditions in question; when they do, a breed-literate veterinarian usually identifies them sooner.
