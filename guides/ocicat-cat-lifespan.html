Cat Lifespan Longevity

Average Lifespan

With proper nutrition, exercise, and veterinary care, many Ocicats live full, healthy lives.

Health Predisposition Summary: Ocicats show higher-than-average incidence of HCM, renal amyloidosis, dental disease based on breed health database data.

Factors Affecting Longevity

  • Size: medium
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Light
  • Lifespan: 12-18 yrs

Life Stages

Three variables drive daily care for Ocicats: their medium size, their light shedding level, and their breed-associated risk of HCM and renal amyloidosis.
