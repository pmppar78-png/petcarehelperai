Routine veterinary care for Devon Rex Cat Cost Of Ownership varies more by region than many owners realize. Dental cleanings are the single most region-sensitive line item, ranging from $250 to well over $900 depending on anesthesia protocols and local wage costs. In humid coastal regions, expect a stronger emphasis on parasite prevention year-round; colder inland areas often shift that spending toward joint and cold-weather care instead.
