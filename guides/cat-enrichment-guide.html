Cat Clicker Training

A cat hunts in short bursts. Two or three wand sessions a day, each ended with a meal, match that pattern. A window seat, a cardboard box, and a puzzle feeder use different behaviors. Rotate toys weekly so last month's mouse is interesting again. A cat that never plays and only sleeps may be painful. Mention the change at the next exam, and note when the playing stopped.
