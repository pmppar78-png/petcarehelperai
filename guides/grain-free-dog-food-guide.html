    Where can I learn more?

    The FDA Center for Veterinary Medicine investigation of diet-associated dilated cardiomyopathy is the document behind the grain-free question. It collected reports of dogs eating grain-free and legume-rich foods. It did not show that dropping grain protects a dog, and a marketing page is not that investigation.

    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    A grain-free label does not move how often a dog is examined. Puppies are seen every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, and adults at least once a year. The FDA collected reports of dilated cardiomyopathy in dogs eating some grain-free, legume-rich diets, so a cough, exercise intolerance, or a new murmur should be examined when it appears. Many dogs from about 7 years, giant breeds sooner, are already seen about every six months, which is a sensible time to talk about that diet with the veterinarian who listens to the heart.
