Norwegian Elkhound vs Siberian Husky: Side-by-Side

Norwegian Elkhound and Siberian Husky look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Norwegian Elkhound is larger at 48-55 lbs, while Siberian Husky typically runs 35-60 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around hip dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy. Norwegian Elkhound carries additional risk for kidney disease. Siberian Husky is more notably predisposed to cataracts. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.
