Money-Saving Tips

Many breed-associated conditions are manageable when detected early but become significantly more complex — and expensive — when diagnosis is delayed. Watch for early signs of allergies, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Bichon Frises are prone to.

Cost of Bichon Frise Ownership

Before committing to ownership, evaluate whether these costs are sustainable long-term for Bichon Frise ownership.

  • Annual food costs: $250–$500 for high-quality dog food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $30–50 per professional session (occasional home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $25–40/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials
