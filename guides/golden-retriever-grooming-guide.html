Golden Retriever Grooming Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Feathering and baths sit under this Golden Retriever heading.

Grooming Schedule

Golden Retrievers need a full comb weekly most of the year, and daily combing during the spring and fall shed.

Feathering on the ears, chest, legs, and tail holds pond water, so the towel follows the swim before the dog gets on the couch. A skipped week lets a mat form under the ear leather. Dry the chest feathers before the dog shakes pond water across the kitchen. A comb that starts at the skin finds the felt early. Part the chest feathers with the comb.

Golden Retrievers show a higher rate of cancer, hip dysplasia, and heart disease. A comb already in the feathers is when a lump gets noticed.

Brushing & Coat Care

Give this retriever a retrieve before breakfast and a place to sleep while the house is quiet. The coat still needs a comb on the days the retrieve is the whole workout.

  • Size: large (55-75 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 10-12 yrs

Bathing

Mats hide behind the ears of this feathered coat, and brushing is a chance to feel for lumps from cancer and for stiffness from hip dysplasia.

Nail Care

Hands find Golden Retriever mats before the eyes do. The coat is a water-repellent double coat with feathering on the ears, the chest, the backs of the legs, and the tail. Behind each ear the hair packs into felt when the brush stays on the easy back. Lift each ear and comb from the skin outward, pinching the base of the mat so the pull stays in dead hair and the skin stays slack. Feathers on the hind legs get a line-brush, a few strands at a time, because a rake dragged through a wet feather rips it.

Ear & Dental Care

Swimming is the grooming trap. The jacket sheds water and still holds damp underneath, and the ear canal stays wet after the feathers look fluffy again. Dry ear openings with cotton, then towel the chest and the tail. Burrs ride home in the feathering. Pick them out once burrs are visible and the dog has leaned over to rest. Nails and the pads get a glance at the same time, since river rock wears nails oddly and sometimes cuts a pad that the long hair hides.

Professional Grooming Costs

Feathers behind the ears and along the hind legs mat first, so the comb starts there. One complete comb-through weekly most of the year, and a daily pass during the spring and fall shed, matches how fast this coat mats. When the undercoat releases, the comb fills faster. If a bath happens, rinse until the water runs clear so soap leaves the feathers. Clippers belong on a matted emergency, and they are a poor haircut for a coat that is supposed to work in water.
