Golden Retriever Grooming Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

The long feathering is the combing work for this retriever.

Grooming Schedule

Golden Retrievers need a full comb weekly most of the year, and daily combing during the spring and fall shed.

AKC describes the outer coat as dense and water-repellent, with a good undercoat that should stay firm and resilient, neither coarse nor silky, lying close to the body. A shave takes off the weather protection that coat is built to give, and it does not make the shedding stop. The color can be any shade of gold, and the shade does not change how the comb moves. During the heavy shed the loose undercoat comes out in thick clumps, and the dog still needs the outer coat left long enough to work in water. A matted emergency is the rare day clippers belong on this breed. The rest of the year the length stays, and the work is combing.

A red patch under wet feathering can show up the day after the dog has been in the water, and that patch needs an appointment while it is still small.

Brushing & Coat Care

Give this retriever a retrieve before breakfast and a place to sleep while the house is quiet. The coat still needs a comb on the days the retrieve is the whole workout.

  • Size: large (55-75 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 10-12 yrs

Bathing

A bath is for mud the hose did not get, and it is uncommon for a coat that sheds water.

Nail Care

Hands find Golden Retriever mats before the eyes do. The coat is a water-repellent double coat with feathering on the ears, the chest, the backs of the legs, and the tail. Lift each ear and pinch the base of a tangle so the pull stays in dead hair and the skin stays slack. Feathers on the hind legs get a line-brush, a few strands at a time, because a rake dragged through a wet feather rips it.

Ear & Dental Care

Swimming is the grooming trap. The jacket sheds water and still holds damp underneath. Dry ear openings with cotton, then towel the chest and the tail. Burrs ride home in the feathering. Pick them out once burrs are visible and the dog has leaned over to rest. Nails and the pads get a glance at the same time, since river rock wears nails oddly and sometimes cuts a pad that the long hair hides.

Professional Grooming Costs

The longest feathers are where the comb should start. A slicker on the outer feather and a comb at the skin are different tools, and the comb is what finds the felt. Skip a hot dryer. Hot air scorches this coat before the dog looks dry. When the undercoat releases, the comb fills faster. If a bath happens, rinse until the water runs clear so soap leaves the feathers.

Where do mats hide in a Golden Retriever coat?

Mats hide behind the ears of this feathered coat, and brushing is a chance to feel for lumps from cancer and for stiffness from hip dysplasia.
