Bichon Frise exercise & Fitness Guide

How much exercise does a Bichon Frise need? Activity recommendations for this small moderate-energy non-sporting breed.

Daily exercise daily. Moderate daily exercise keeps your Bichon Frise healthy and mentally satisfied.

12-18 lbs at maturity, 14-15 yrs lifespan — the Bichon Frise does best in a home where the owner actually understands the breed-level quirks rather than learning them the hard way. Breed standards describe form and function ideals, but real-world Bichon Frises show meaningful individual variation in temperament and health.

Mental Stimulation

No two Bichon Frises are identical. Breed profiles describe tendencies across populations — individual variation is always significant. A sedentary lifestyle carries health risks regardless of breed predisposition — joint stiffness, weight gain, and behavioral issues increase with inactivity.

  • Maintain an occasional grooming routine
