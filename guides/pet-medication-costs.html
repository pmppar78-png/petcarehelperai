Common Pet Medication Costs

This Common Pet Medication Costs page is not a dose chart.

Average costs for common pet medications including antibiotics, pain meds, anti-inflammatories, and chronic condition drugs. Tips for saving on prescriptions.

Average Cost Breakdown

Service/ItemAverage Cost RangeNotes
Flea/tick prevention (monthly)$15 – $50Varies by pet weight and brand
Heartworm prevention (monthly)$10 – $30Required year-round in most regions
Chronic condition meds (monthly)$30 – $200Arthritis, thyroid, seizure, allergy meds
Antibiotic course (7-14 days)$20 – $150Depends on drug type and pet size
