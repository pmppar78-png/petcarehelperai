Nutritional Needs of Vizslas

As a medium sporting breed with high energy levels, the Vizsla has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Vizslas typically weigh 44-60 lbs and need approximately 800–1,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Vizslas may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Health Predisposition Summary: Vizslas show higher-than-average incidence of hip dysplasia, epilepsy, cancer based on breed health database data.

Health-Specific Diet Considerations

Vizslas are prone to several health conditions that can be managed or prevented through proper nutrition.

Best Protein Sources for Vizslas

  • Chicken and turkey: Lean, easily digestible proteins ideal for most Vizslas

Foods to Avoid

  • Macadamia nuts and alcohol
  • Cooked bones (splintering risk)

Supplements Worth Considering

  • Glucosamine & Chondroitin: Essential for joint health, especially important for Vizslas prone to hip dysplasia

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Vizslas

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Vizslas.

Feeding Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Vizsla's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Vizsla's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Vizsla's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Vizslas benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Vizslas may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
