    Where can I learn more?

    A gravy or mixer changes the calorie count of a diet that already met a profile. WSAVA's tools for selecting a pet food explain what 'complete and balanced' means, and the AAFCO statement on the base food is the line to read. The enhancer does not carry its own nutritional adequacy statement in most cases, which is the point of checking.

    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    Meal enhancers poured over kibble change the calorie count of a diet that was already complete. Puppies eating them are examined every 3 to 4 weeks through about 16 weeks of age. Adult dogs need at least one exam a year, and that visit is where a heartworm antigen test belongs if prevention is in use. Dogs around 7 years old, and giant breeds sooner, are often weighed and examined twice a year. Repeated vomiting or blood in the stool after a richer topping is a visit today.

    How can I save money on pet care?

    Gravy and mixers are priced like a small meal and then poured on a diet that was already complete. Add their calories into the daily amount or you pay for the topper and for the weight that follows. Check the AAFCO statement on the complete food under the topper. If the base food is unfinished, you do not need an enhancer to justify it.
