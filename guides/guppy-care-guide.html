Guppy Care and Breeding Guide

Complete guppy care guide covering tank setup, feeding, breeding, fry care, color varieties, and common guppy diseases.

Housing and Enclosure

Guppies need a minimum 10-gallon aquarium with gentle filtration. Maintain water temperature between 72-82°F. They thrive in planted aquariums with live plants that provide hiding spots for fry. Guppies are social and should be kept in groups — use a ratio of 2-3 females per male to reduce stress on females from male attention.

Diet and Nutrition

Feed high-quality flake or micro-pellet food as a staple, supplemented with frozen or freeze-dried brine shrimp, bloodworms, and daphnia. Guppies also enjoy blanched vegetables like zucchini and spinach. Feed small amounts 2-3 times daily — only what can be consumed in 2 minutes.

Health and Veterinary Care

Common guppy health issues include ich, fin rot, velvet disease, and internal parasites. Guppies from pet stores may carry diseases, so quarantine new fish for 2-4 weeks. Maintain clean water with weekly 25% water changes. Watch for clamped fins, white spots, or rapid breathing.

Common Health Issues

  • Ammonia and nitrite poisoning: The most common aquarium problem. It follows an uncycled tank, overfeeding, a dead fish left in the tank, or a filter that stopped. Both should read 0 ppm.
  • Temperature stress: Fish cannot regulate their own body temperature, so every body process follows the water. Use a thermometer, and a thermostat heater for tropical species.
  • Ich and other parasites: Usually brought in on new fish or plants. Quarantine new arrivals in a separate tank before they join the display.
  • Bacterial infections such as fin rot: Usually follow poor water quality or an injury.
  • Medication harm: Copper treatments are toxic to shrimp, snails, and plants and can set back the filter bacteria, so ammonia and nitrite need watching for weeks afterward.

Source: Merck Veterinary Manual: Management of Aquarium Fish.

Stocking and Stress Reduction

  • Float a new fish's bag to match temperature, then mix in tank water slowly before release.
  • Add a few fish at a time so the filter bacteria can catch up with the extra waste.
  • Check adult size, temperament, and water needs before buying a tank mate.
  • Keep hands out of the tank when you can, and wash them before and after maintenance.
  • Use a net only when you must; chasing fish around the tank is a stressor in itself.

Questions Owners Ask

Are guppies good for beginner fish keepers?

Yes. They need at least 10 gallons, gentle filtration, and water at 72 to 82°F. Keep 2 or 3 females per male, and quarantine new fish for 2 to 4 weeks because store guppies often carry disease.

Why do guppies need more females than males?

Males chase females constantly. Two or three females per male spreads that attention out and lowers stress on the females.
