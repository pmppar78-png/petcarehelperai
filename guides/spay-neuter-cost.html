A spay removes the uterus and ovaries through an abdominal incision. A neuter removes the testicles. The posted clinic fee often covers a young, healthy pet and then rises when bloodwork, an in-heat uterus, or a retained testicle is added. Ask which of those items are inside the number on the website, and ask what pain medicine goes home with the animal.
