Prevention Strategies

Lifespan Optimization

Early intervention consistently produces better outcomes and lower costs than reactive treatment for breed-associated conditions. Watch for early signs of luxating patella, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Jack Russell Terriers are prone to.

Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy Jack Russell Terriers especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.
