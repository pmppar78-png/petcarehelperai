Grooming Schedule

Dachshunds have moderate shedding and require 2–3 times per week brushing. Regular grooming sessions keep your Dachshund's coat healthy and help you bond with your dog.

With a typical weight of 16-32 lbs and lifespan of 12-16 yrs, the Dachshund requires thoughtful care tailored to their specific breed characteristics. This guide covers everything you need to know.

Nail Care

The key to a happy, healthy Dachshund is matching your care approach to their breed characteristics. Even lower-energy breeds benefit from daily interaction and enrichment.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for small breed dogs (400–800 calories/day)
  • Maintain a 2–3 times per week grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for IVDD
  • Invest in pet insurance early to cover breed-specific conditions

Beyond the basics, Dachshund owners should be aware of the unique aspects of this breed. As a hound breed, the Dachshund has instincts and behaviors shaped by centuries of selective breeding for specific tasks.

Many experienced Dachshund owners recommend a balanced mix of physical activities and brain games.

Environmental enrichment plays a crucial role in your Dachshund's well-being. Rotate toys regularly, introduce new scents and textures, and vary your walking routes to keep their mind engaged. A mentally stimulated Dachshund is less likely to develop destructive behaviors or anxiety-related issues.

Being proactive about your Dachshund's care can prevent many common problems. Watch for early signs of IVDD, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — obesity exacerbates nearly every health condition Dachshunds are prone to.

With proper care and attention to their breed-specific needs, your Dachshund can live a long, healthy, and happy life of 12-16 yrs.

Establishing a consistent daily routine helps your Dachshund feel secure and reduces stress-related behavioral problems. Include scheduled feeding times, exercise sessions, grooming, and quiet rest periods. Even moderate-energy breeds thrive with predictable schedules.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Dachshunds

Dachshunds should receive breed-specific screening for IVDD starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Early detection significantly improves treatment outcomes and quality of life.

Cost of Dachshund Ownership

Understanding the financial commitment helps you prepare for a lifetime of Dachshund ownership:

  • Annual food costs: $250–$500 for high-quality dog food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $30–50 per professional session (2–3 times per week home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $25–40/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Dachshund right for me?

Dachshunds are small dogs with moderate energy and a lifespan of 12-16 yrs. They require 2–3 times per week grooming and are prone to IVDD.

How much does a Dachshund cost?

Initial costs range from $500-$3,000 for purchase/adoption, plus $1,000-$2,000 in first-year supplies and veterinary care. Annual ongoing costs are typically $1,500-$4,000 depending on health needs.

What health problems do Dachshunds have?

Dachshunds are prone to IVDD, obesity, dental disease. Regular vet checkups and breed-specific screening tests help catch these conditions early for better treatment outcomes.

How long do Dachshunds live?

The average lifespan for a Dachshund is 12-16 yrs. Proper nutrition, regular exercise, preventive veterinary care, and maintaining a healthy weight can help your Dachshund live to the upper end of this range.
