Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Singapuras is particularly important given their predisposition to PKD, uterine inertia, HCM.

Singapura premiums are cat premiums for a very small breed. The inherited disease with a real genetic test is pyruvate kinase deficiency. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, if it occurs, is medical. Uterine inertia can turn a queening into a cesarean.
