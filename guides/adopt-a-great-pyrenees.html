Finding a Great Pyrenees to Adopt

Most people never consider that Great Pyrenees can be adopted rather than bought.

Health Awareness: Watch Great Pyrenees for bloat, hip dysplasia, bone cancer, all documented at breed level.

Breed-Specific Rescues

  • Size: large (85-160 lbs)
  • Energy Level: Low
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 10-12 yrs

Shelter Adoption

Plan Great Pyrenees care around a large body size, heavy shedding, and the breed's documented predisposition toward bloat and hip dysplasia.

What are the most important considerations for adopting a great pyrenees?

A Great Pyrenees is a livestock guardian. It patrols at night and barks at what it decides is a threat. Adopt one for property with room for a night patrol, and ask about hips and bloat.
