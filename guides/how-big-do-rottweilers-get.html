Full-Grown Size

Rottweilers are a large breed, reaching 80-135 lbs at full maturity.

A short jacket makes overfeeding easy to miss. A sleek outline can still hide fat. Factors Affecting Size

Many experienced Rottweiler owners recommend a balanced mix of physical activities and brain games.

The connection between enrichment and behavior is well-documented in veterinary behavioral science. A Rottweiler without adequate mental engagement will find ways to occupy itself — and owners rarely appreciate the results. Invest in variety: rotate toys on a weekly cycle, introduce new textures and objects, and provide opportunities for species-appropriate problem-solving.

When They Stop Growing

Around month number 12 the withers often stop climbing and people declare the project finished. The sternum is still shallow beside the adult he will become. Through the months after 12 and into month number 18 the neck thickens and the chest drops. From there until month number 24 the last muscle and the spring of rib fill in, males especially, sometimes pushing the crest of that 80 to 135 lb band. Girls often settle sooner, inside a lighter share of the same band. Meals stay scooped and jumping stays modest through the whole stretch so the frame finishes before a soft belly does.
