How to Train

Flat-Coated Retriever training.

Training Approach

Flat-Coated Retrievers are high-energy sporting dogs that require consistent mental stimulation and structured training sessions.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Flat-Coated Retrievers have elevated rates of cancer, hip dysplasia, bloat.

Flat-Coated Retriever Training Challenges

  • Size: large
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 8-10 yrs

Cancer Surveillance Protocol

A Flat-Coated Retriever stays puppyish for years. Train with retrieves and food, and forgive a slow teenager. Keep the work happy. The disease to know is histiocytic sarcoma. A new lump, lameness, or a dog that suddenly tires needs a veterinarian.
