Standard Poodle Health Issues

Common health problems in Standard Poodles including hip dysplasia, bloat, Addisons disease. Prevention, symptoms to watch for, and treatment options.

Common Health Problems

Standard Poodles are predisposed to several health conditions including hip dysplasia, bloat, Addisons disease. Understanding these risks allows you to screen early, prevent where possible, and catch problems before they become emergencies.

Weighing around 40-70 lbs and lifespan of 10-18 yrs, the Standard Poodle comes with breed-specific considerations worth understanding early. Few breeds combine boundless energy with the Standard Poodle's distinctive character quite so effectively.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Standard Poodles have elevated rates of hip dysplasia, bloat, Addisons disease. Prevalence varies, and many individuals live full lives without developing these issues. However, breed-aware veterinary care — including targeted screening at appropriate ages — is the most effective prevention strategy.

Prevention Strategies

Knowledge of breed-specific characteristics directly translates to better day-to-day care. Standard Poodles have particular requirements based on their large size, minimal shedding level, and genetic predispositions to hip dysplasia and bloat.

Routine veterinary screenings catch many breed-related conditions at stages where intervention is most effective. Given the breed's health tendencies, proactive screening is important for this breed.

When to See the Vet

Work with your veterinarian to fine-tune these recommendations based on your pet's weight, activity level, and any health considerations.

  • Structure 60-120 minutes of daily movement that matches your pet's drive — a brisk walk alone won't cut it for high-energy breeds
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for large breed dogs (1,400–2,200 calories/day)
  • Maintain an occasional grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for hip dysplasia
  • Pet insurance enrolled early typically offers the best value, covering breed-related conditions before they develop

Lifespan Optimization

Prevention-focused care tailored to breed characteristics reduces both health risks and long-term costs. Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Standard Poodles are prone to.

A stable daily routine serves as the foundation for behavioral wellness, reducing reactivity and stress responses. Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy Standard Poodles especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.
