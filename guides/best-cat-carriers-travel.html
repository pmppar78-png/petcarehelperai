    Where can I learn more?

    Carrier training so a cat can actually be examined is covered in AAFP client material on taking cats to the clinic. Airline crate dimensions and embargoes are on that airline's pet page. USDA APHIS pet-travel rules apply when the trip crosses a border.

    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    Healthy adult pets should visit the vet at least once annually. Kittens, kittens, senior pets, and those with chronic conditions may need more frequent visits — typically every 3-6 months.

    How can I save money on pet care?

    Measure the carrier against the airline's current pet rules before you pay for the ticket and the carrier. A crate refused at the counter is a fare you cannot use. For a cat who only needs to reach a local clinic, a simple secure carrier that the cat has already practiced in is enough. You do not need a travel system for a car ride across town.
