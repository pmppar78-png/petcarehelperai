English Springer Spaniel exercise & Fitness Guide

How much exercise does an English Springer Spaniel need? Activity recommendations for this medium high-energy sporting breed.

Daily exercise daily. This is a high-energy breed that thrives with vigorous activities like running, hiking, fetch, and swimming.

Expect 40-50 lbs at maturity and 12-14 yrs of life with a English Springer Spaniel; the combination of its health pattern and temperament profile is where owner attention pays the largest dividends. Signs of Under-Exercise

Many breed-associated conditions are manageable when detected early but become significantly more complex — and expensive — when diagnosis is delayed. Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions English Springer Spaniels are prone to.

Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy English Springer Spaniels especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.
