Questions Owners Ask

Irish Wolfhounds weigh about 105 to 180 pounds and are sighthounds, so a fence and a leash matter when something runs. Great Danes overlap that weight, live a little longer on many charts, and carry bloat risk in a deep chest. The Wolfhound needs a straight place to stride. The Dane needs a feeding plan the clinic agrees with. Budget giant-breed food and a car that fits the grown dog.

The middle years of an Irish Wolfhound's life are when subtle health shifts begin to appear — slightly slower recovery after exercise, a preference for softer resting spots, or minor changes in appetite.
