Where can I learn more?

Read the sample contract before the premium table. The North American Pet Health Insurance Association publishes average premiums. Read waiting periods, hereditary exclusions, and bilateral clauses. Your clinic's office manager can tell you which insurers actually pay that hospital.

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

An accident-and-illness policy often leaves the wellness exam unpaid, and the exam is still how a murmur, a limp, or a diagnosis gets written down before it is a pre-existing condition. Puppies and kittens are examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, and those visits stay on the calendar before any policy begins. Adult dogs and cats need about one exam a year, and many dogs from about 7 years, giant breeds earlier, and many cats from about 10, are seen about every six months. Enrolling is a paperwork date. The physical exam is a clinic date.

How can I save money on pet care?

The savings are in the deductible and the reimbursement percent you can afford to pay at the desk, and in enrolling before a condition is on the record. Annual billing sometimes costs less than monthly. A policy bought after the cruciate tear is the expensive way to learn about pre-existing conditions.
