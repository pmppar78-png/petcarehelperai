Nutritional Needs of Irish Setters

Irish Setters typically weigh 60-70 lbs and need approximately 1,400–2,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Irish Setters may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Irish Setters

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Irish Setter's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Irish Setter's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Irish Setter's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Irish Setters benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Irish Setters may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
