Best Insurance by Cat Type

Best for Kittens

Lemonade Pet and Spot offer excellent coverage for kittens. Enrolling early means lower premiums locked in for life and no pre-existing conditions to worry about.

Pros for Kittens

  • Lowest premiums when enrolled young
  • Full coverage from day one (after waiting period)
  • Wellness add-ons cover vaccinations, spay/neuter
  • Covers accidents from curious kitten behavior

Best for Indoor Cats

Indoor cats face lower accident risks but similar illness risks. Lemonade and ManyPets offer affordable plans ideal for indoor-only felines.

Pros for Indoor Cats

  • Lower premiums than outdoor cats
  • Focus on illness coverage
  • Dental coverage particularly valuable

Best for Purebred Cats

Persians, Maine Coons, Bengals, and Siamese have breed-specific health risks. Trupanion and Healthy Paws cover hereditary conditions without breed exclusions.

Breed-Specific Concerns

  • Persian: Polycystic kidney disease (PKD), breathing issues, eye conditions
  • Maine Coon: Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM), hip dysplasia
  • Bengal: HCM, progressive retinal atrophy
  • Siamese: Respiratory issues, amyloidosis, dental problems
  • Scottish Fold: Osteochondrodysplasia, arthritis

Best for Senior Cats

Pets Best accepts cats at any age, making it ideal for insuring older cats. Spot also has no upper age limits.

Pros for Senior Cats

  • No age restrictions for enrollment
  • Covers age-related conditions developing after enrollment
  • Accident-only plans available for budget-conscious owners
  • Important for kidney disease, hyperthyroidism, diabetes coverage

What Cat Insurance Typically Covers

Usually Covered

  • Accidents: Falls, bite wounds, poisoning, car accidents, burns
  • Illnesses: Cancer, diabetes, kidney disease, hyperthyroidism, infections
  • Urinary issues: Blockages, UTIs, crystals, FLUTD
  • Emergency care: ER visits, hospitalization, emergency surgery
  • Diagnostics: X-rays, ultrasounds, bloodwork, urinalysis
  • Prescription medications: Insulin, thyroid medication, antibiotics
  • Hereditary conditions: HCM, PKD, hip dysplasia (with most providers)
  • Dental illness: Extractions, stomatitis, periodontal disease (varies by provider)

Usually NOT Covered

  • Pre-existing conditions: Any condition showing signs before coverage
  • Routine care: Vaccinations, annual exams, flea prevention (without wellness rider)
  • Elective procedures: Declawing (where legal), cosmetic procedures
  • Breeding costs: Pregnancy, birthing, fertility treatments
  • Behavioral issues: Some policies exclude these; others cover them

Male Cat Owners: Urinary Blockage Warning

Male cats are prone to urinary blockages, a life-threatening emergency costing $3,000-$6,000. Most policies have 14-30 day illness waiting periods. Enroll your male cat early, as urinary issues can strike suddenly and become pre-existing conditions if they occur before coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is cat insurance cheaper than dog insurance?

Yes, significantly. Cat insurance typically costs 30-50% less than dog insurance. Cats are smaller, have fewer breed-specific orthopedic issues, and generally have lower claim costs than dogs.

Do I need insurance for an indoor cat?

Indoor cats still face illness risks including cancer, diabetes, kidney disease, and dental problems. While accident risk is lower, illness costs can be substantial. Insurance provides peace of mind for unexpected health issues.

What about multi-cat discounts?

Most providers offer 5-10% discounts for insuring multiple cats. ManyPets offers 15%, and Lemonade provides multi-pet discounts as well.
