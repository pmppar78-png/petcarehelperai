Benefits of Fresh Dog Food

Fresh dog food offers several advantages over traditional kibble.

Improved Nutrition

  • Higher digestibility: Dogs absorb more nutrients from gently cooked fresh food
  • Better protein quality: Human-grade meat vs. rendered meat meals
  • No artificial preservatives: Frozen storage eliminates need for chemical preservatives
  • Whole food ingredients: Real vegetables vs. vitamin supplements

Health Benefits Reported by Owners

  • Improved coat condition and skin health
  • Better digestion and smaller, firmer stools
  • Increased energy levels
  • Weight management (precise portioning)
  • Reduced allergy symptoms
  • Better dental health (less tartar buildup)

Convenience

  • Pre-portioned meals eliminate measuring
  • Delivered to your door on a schedule
  • Easy to travel with (frozen portions)
  • No more lugging heavy kibble bags

How to Transition to Fresh Food

Switching to fresh food should be done gradually to avoid digestive upset.

7-10 Day Transition Schedule

  • Days 1-2: 25% fresh food, 75% current food
  • Days 3-4: 50% fresh food, 50% current food
  • Days 5-6: 75% fresh food, 25% current food
  • Days 7-10: 100% fresh food

Tips for Success

  • Slow down further if you notice loose stools
  • Some dogs may experience temporary digestive adjustment
  • Monitor weight and adjust portions as needed
  • Keep fresh food refrigerated or frozen until use

Consult Your Veterinarian

Before making significant diet changes, consult your veterinarian, especially if your dog has health conditions, is on prescription food, or has a history of digestive issues. Fresh food services can accommodate most dietary needs but professional guidance ensures the best transition.
