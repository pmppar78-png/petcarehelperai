A Siberian Husky at 35 to 60 pounds explores and pulls, and training stays independent. A German Shepherd at 50 to 90 pounds usually watches the handler more closely and keeps growing longer. Both shed heavily. The Husky's fence has to be tall and dig-proof. The Shepherd's checklist includes hips and elbows. Choose the Husky for a secure yard and climate-aware runs. Choose the Shepherd for closer teamwork and a larger adult body.
