Bull Terrier vs American Staffordshire Terrier: Side-by-Side

Bull Terrier and American Staffordshire Terrier look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Bull Terrier is larger at 50-70 lbs, while American Staffordshire Terrier typically runs 40-70 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Both breeds share a high energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around heart disease. Bull Terrier carries additional risk for kidney disease, deafness. American Staffordshire Terrier is more notably predisposed to hip dysplasia, allergies. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.
