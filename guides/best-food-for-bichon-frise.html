Bichon Frise

Detailed guide to the best dog food for Bichon Frise based on their small size, moderate energy level, and health needs including allergies.

Nutritional Needs of

As a small non-sporting breed with moderate energy levels, the Bichon Frise has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Bichon Frise typically weigh 12-18 lbs and need approximately 400–800 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Luxating patella on a twelve to eighteen pound Bichon Frise keeps the adult portion at half to one and a half cups across two meals; extra weight lets a loose kneecap slip, so the portion stays lean.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Bichon Frise have elevated rates of allergies, bladder stones, luxating patella.

Bladder stones in a Bichon Frise are managed by the mineral the stone actually is, and a grain-free food does not pick that mineral. The veterinarian reads the urinalysis and chooses the diet. Until a stone is identified, feed a complete dog food to body condition. A grain allergy is uncommon in Bichons and needs a clinic diagnosis.
