Yorkshire Terrier Grooming Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Complete Yorkshire Terrier grooming guide. minimal shedding management, bathing schedule, nail care, and professional grooming costs.

Grooming Schedule

Yorkshire Terriers sit in the small-size category, shed at a minimal level, and carry documented risk for dental disease and luxating patella — those three factors drive most of the daily-care decisions.

Baths are occasional and thorough. Wet onto the skin, shampoo twice, rinse until the water runs clear, and squeeze the coat in a towel before a low dryer. High heat frizzes silk into fuzz and can overheat a patient this small. Comb while this coat is damp, then again when it is dry, resetting the part each time. A pet trim that leaves the body shorter and the face furnished still needs the comb, or the shorter ends mat closer onto the skin where they are harder to see.

Dogs in coat from year 11 through year 15 need a comb by the couch for an evening session. The Yorkshire Terrier coat description at AKC is the film to aim the part at: long, silky, steel blue and tan, divided down the back. The 7-lb ceiling still applies, and a 4-lb adult mats just as fast when that comb stays in a drawer.

Yorkshire Terriers have minimal shedding and require occasional brushing. While Yorkshire Terriers shed very little, regular grooming is still important for skin health and early detection of lumps or skin issues.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies dental disease, luxating patella, collapsed trachea as conditions with higher prevalence in Yorkshire Terriers. These are population-level trends, not individual certainties. Discuss with your veterinarian which screening tests are recommended for your Yorkshire Terrier.

Bathing

Part the hair down the spine and the grooming job is suddenly obvious. This coat is long and silky, traditionally parted down the back, steel blue on the body with tan on the head, chest, and legs. Find the midline from the occiput to the tail set with oiled fingertips and comb each side outward so the part stays straight. Skip that part for a weekend and the silk crosses the spine and dries into a rope.

Ear & Dental Care

A daily comb is the prevention plan. The coat mats onto the skin when dead hair stays tangled against the body. Begin at the feet with a fine metal comb, move up the legs, then the chest, then the ear leather, and reach the back only after the comb teeth touch skin without snagging. A pea-sized knot in the armpit becomes a felted plate if it sits. Hair outweighs the body at 4 lb. It outweighs the body at 7 lb too, so one combing method covers both. Left alone for 3 days, silk back of the ears can felt to the leather. A neglected jacket has taken 5 hours at the salon in the log, the dog wrapped and dried in sections so the skin could breathe between passes.

Professional Grooming Costs

The topknot stays out of the eyes with a small band or a wrap, changed whenever it slips, because a tight band rubs a bald spot. Tears stain the tan face fur. Dab the eye corners with a moist cloth before the comb, then comb the beard so food cannot dry into cement. Hair grows inside the ear canal on many of these dogs. Pluck only the strands the clinic has already shown someone, and spare the rest, because a hard pull inflames the canal.
