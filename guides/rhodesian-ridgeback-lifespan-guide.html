Rhodesian Ridgeback Lifespan: How Long Do They Live

Rhodesian Ridgeback average lifespan of 10-12 yrs, factors affecting longevity, and how to help your Rhodesian Ridgeback live a longer, healthier life.

Average Lifespan

The Rhodesian Ridgeback has an average lifespan of 10-12 yrs. Larger breeds tend to have shorter lifespans, but proper care can help your Rhodesian Ridgeback live to the upper end of this range.

With a typical weight of 70-85 lbs and lifespan of 10-12 yrs, the Rhodesian Ridgeback requires thoughtful care tailored to their specific breed characteristics. What sets the Rhodesian Ridgeback apart from other hound breeds is the specific combination of size, drive, and health profile that defines daily life with this dog.

Breed Health Context: The Rhodesian Ridgeback has documented genetic predispositions to hip dysplasia, dermoid sinus, hypothyroidism. These conditions vary in prevalence and severity — not every Rhodesian Ridgeback will develop them, but awareness enables early detection and proactive management. Discuss breed-specific screening protocols with your veterinarian.

Hip and Joint Health Management

Hip dysplasia — a polygenic condition where the femoral head fails to fit properly within the acetabulum — is a documented concern in the Rhodesian Ridgeback. The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) maintains a breed-specific database showing dysplasia prevalence rates, and the PennHIP evaluation method provides a distraction index that can predict hip laxity as early as 16 weeks of age. For large breeds like the Rhodesian Ridgeback, maintaining lean body condition during growth is one of the most impactful preventive measures, as studies from the Purina Lifespan Study demonstrated that dogs kept at ideal body weight had significantly delayed onset of osteoarthritis. Joint supplements containing glucosamine hydrochloride, chondroitin sulfate, and omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) have demonstrated clinical benefit in peer-reviewed veterinary orthopedic literature when started before symptomatic onset.
