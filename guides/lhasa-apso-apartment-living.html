Can a Lhasa Apso live in an apartment? Lhasa Apsos can adapt well to apartment living. Their small size and moderate energy levels make them more suitable for smaller spaces.

Exercise Solutions

The key to a happy, healthy Lhasa Apso is matching your care approach to their breed characteristics. Consistent daily activity, even in short sessions, contributes more to long-term health than occasional intense exercise.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for small breed dogs (400–800 calories/day)
  • Maintain a 2–3 times per week grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for kidney disease
