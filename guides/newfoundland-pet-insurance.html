Cardiac Health Monitoring

For a Newfoundland, the claims that move the quote are subaortic stenosis and gastric dilatation-volvulus.

Subaortic stenosis in a Newfoundland is followed with echocardiography. Balloon valvuloplasty is an occasional specialist procedure, and most dogs are managed medically. Cystinuria is diet and sometimes stone removal. Gastric dilatation-volvulus is the emergency surgery.
