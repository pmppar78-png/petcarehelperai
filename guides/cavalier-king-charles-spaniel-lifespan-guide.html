Cavalier King Charles Spaniel Lifespan

Cavalier King Charles Spaniel average lifespan of 9-14 yrs, factors affecting longevity, and how to help your Cavalier King Charles Spaniel live a longer, healthier life.

Average Lifespan

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel has an average lifespan of 9-14 yrs. Smaller breeds generally live longer, and well-cared-for Cavalier King Charles Spaniels often exceed average lifespan expectations.

At 13-18 lbs and 9-14 yrs of life expectancy, the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel carries specific care considerations that benefit from early attention. Living with a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel means adapting to a moderate-energy companion that thrives on structure, appropriate exercise, and attentive health monitoring.

Extending Your Cavalier King Charles Spaniel's Life
