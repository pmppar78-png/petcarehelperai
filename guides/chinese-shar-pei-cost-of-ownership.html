Medical Expenses

Few breeds combine steady enthusiasm with the Chinese Shar-Pei's distinctive character quite so effectively. Lack of physical activity affects behavior before it affects weight — restlessness and attention-seeking often precede visible fitness changes.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for medium breed dogs (800–1,200 calories/day)
  • Maintain a weekly grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for skin infections
  • Consider pet insurance while your pet is young and healthy — premiums are lower and pre-existing conditions aren't an issue

Hidden Costs

Money-Saving Tips

Preventive care calibrated to breed profile, rather than generic pet care, reliably shifts long-term outcomes. Watch for early signs of skin infections, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Chinese Shar-Peis are prone to.
