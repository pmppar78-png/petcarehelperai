Money-Saving Tips

Many breed-associated conditions are manageable when detected early but become significantly more complex — and expensive — when diagnosis is delayed. Watch for early signs of deafness, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your cat at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Turkish Angora Cats are prone to.

Consistent daily structure — including predictable meal times, exercise, and rest periods — reduces anxiety and supports behavioral stability. Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy Turkish Angoras especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.

Turkish Angoras should receive breed-specific screening for deafness starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Screening before symptoms appear makes a meaningful difference in outcomes.

Cost of Turkish Angora Ownership

Before committing to ownership, evaluate whether these costs are sustainable long-term for Turkish Angora ownership:

  • Annual food costs: $400–$800 for high-quality cat food
  • Veterinary care: $300–$700 annually for routine visits, plus potential emergency costs
  • Grooming: $45–70 per professional session (2–3 times per week home grooming recommended)
  • Pet insurance: $35–55/month for comprehensive coverage
  • Supplies and toys: $200–$500 annually for bedding, toys, leashes, and other essentials
