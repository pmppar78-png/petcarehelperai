Questions Owners Ask

Arrange French bulldog coverage for airway surgery before anyone writes down the larynx exam. A BOAS grade, stenotic-nare note, or hemivertebra film already in the record is what insurers cite when they deny the later operation. Hyperuricosuria and cystinuria results already on the paperwork are pre-existing findings.

French bulldog airway disease is graded by looking at the nostrils, palate, and larynx, not by a DNA score. The University of Cambridge and other BOAS clinics use a functional exam. Hemivertebrae need spinal radiographs. DNA still has a role for hyperuricosuria, cystinuria, and the SOD1 degenerative-myelopathy risk variant, none of which tell you whether this dog can breathe after a walk. Ask for the airway grade and the spinal films first. Treat a clear 'Frenchie panel' that skips those as incomplete.
