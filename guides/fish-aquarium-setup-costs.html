Aquarium Nitrogen Cycle
Common Aquarium Fish

A working freshwater setup is a tank on a stand rated for the weight, a filter sized above the tank volume, a heater and a thermometer, a liquid test kit for ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate, and a dechlorinator. Cycling means feeding the filter until ammonia and nitrite both read zero before fish are added. Livestock bought the day the tank is filled is the purchase that dies of ammonia. A lid, a siphon, and a bucket are part of the first bill.
