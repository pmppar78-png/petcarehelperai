Akita in an Apartment

Apartment Suitability Score

With adequate daily exercise, Akitas can adapt to apartment living, though a home with a yard is preferable.

Space Requirements

  • Size: large (70-130 lbs)
  • Energy Level: Moderate
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 10-13 yrs

Noise Level

Akitas sit in the large-size category, shed at a heavy level, and carry documented risk for hip dysplasia and bloat — those three factors drive most of the daily-care decisions.
