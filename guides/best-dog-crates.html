Crate Types Explained

  • Wire Crates: Best ventilation, visibility, foldable. Great for home use and crate training.
  • Plastic Crates: More enclosed, airline-friendly, easier to clean. Ideal for travel and nervous dogs.
  • Soft-Sided Crates: Lightweight, portable, comfortable. Only for calm, crate-trained dogs.
  • Heavy-Duty Crates: Steel or aluminum construction. For escape artists and anxious dogs.
  • Furniture Crates: Designed to blend with home decor. Great for living spaces.

Size Guide

Your dog should be able to stand up, turn around, and lie down comfortably. Measure your dog and add 2-4 inches to each dimension.

  • 18-22" (XS): Chihuahuas, Toy Poodles (up to 10 lbs)
  • 24" (S): Pugs, Boston Terriers (11-25 lbs)
  • 30" (M): Beagles, Cocker Spaniels (26-40 lbs)
  • 36" (L): Bulldogs, Australian Shepherds (41-70 lbs)
  • 42" (XL): Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers (71-90 lbs)
  • 48" (XXL): German Shepherds, Rottweilers (91-110 lbs)
  • 54"+ (Giant): Great Danes, Mastiffs (110+ lbs)

Crate Training Tips

Never use the crate as punishment. Introduce the crate gradually with positive associations. Puppies under 6 months should not be crated for more than 3-4 hours. Adult dogs should not exceed 8 hours. Always remove collars when crating to prevent accidents.

Should I get a crate with a divider?

Yes, if you're getting a puppy. A divider lets you buy an adult-sized crate while limiting space during house training. Dogs naturally avoid soiling their sleeping area, but too much space allows them to use one end as a bathroom.

Wire or plastic crate - which is better?

Wire crates offer better ventilation and visibility, making them ideal for home use. Plastic crates feel more den-like and are required for airline travel. Many owners have both types for different situations.

How do I make the crate comfortable?

Add a comfortable bed or pad (if your dog doesn't chew), a safe chew toy, and consider covering wire crates with a blanket for a den-like feel. Place the crate in a family area so your dog feels included.
