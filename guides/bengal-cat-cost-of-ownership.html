Local Vet & Care Considerations

Before budgeting for Bengal Cat Cost Of Ownership, it is worth talking to two or three nearby clinics rather than relying on a single national estimate. An annual wellness visit in a small town can run $45–$85, while the same appointment in a large metro area often lands closer to $110–$180, and emergency after-hours visits can triple that figure. Desert climates tend to push care plans toward hydration monitoring and paw-pad protection, while northern climates place more weight on seasonal coat care and indoor enrichment. Wildfire smoke, ragweed season, and indoor humidity all influence respiratory comfort in ways that a standard wellness checklist does not capture.
