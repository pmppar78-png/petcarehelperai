Rottweiler Grooming Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Complete Rottweiler grooming guide. moderate shedding management, bathing schedule, nail care, and professional grooming costs.

Grooming Schedule

Once the undercoat blows, the curry comes out most evenings for a couple of weeks. Whatever is left of the year, two honest sessions keep the shine and the laundry in a truce. Part the coat along the sternum once a month and hunt for rash from a harness. The VCA coat handling on the Rottweiler matches the routine by the back door: a short jacket that still has a second layer, and a handler who has to go find it with their hands.

Rottweilers have moderate shedding and require 2–3 times per week brushing. Regular grooming sessions keep your Rottweiler's coat healthy and help you bond with your dog.

Plan for 80-135 lbs of dog and 8-10 yrs of life with a Rottweiler — and plan for an ownership experience that rewards knowing the breed rather than treating it as generic. Few breeds combine steady enthusiasm with the Rottweiler's distinctive character quite so effectively.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies hip dysplasia, osteosarcoma, heart disease as conditions with higher prevalence in Rottweilers. These are population-level trends, not individual certainties. Discuss with your veterinarian which screening tests are recommended for your Rottweiler.

Bathing

Short hair over a double coat tricks people into owning zero tools. The hands start at the throat. Stroke 1 with a soft curry lifts the guard hair and knocks yard dust off the shine. Stroke 2, the same curry pressed a little deeper, drags the undercoat out in soft tufts along the thigh, the ribcage, and behind the ear. A slick black jacket can mask a handful of loose wool until that wool is across the rug and on the pants.

Ear & Dental Care

Bathe him rarely. A mild wash and a long rinse clear the yard, and towel the groin, the armpit, and the lip fold until those pockets are actually dry. A damp corner under a heavy body is odor by the next morning. Nails get clipped while that hound is still standing from the curry. Long nails change the foot on hardwood, and can hear them from the hall before seeing them.

Professional Grooming Costs

The leather of the ear gets lifted every grooming sit. It holds warmth. Dab the visible flap and smell the canal. Teeth get a brush while the mouth is already open for one treat. Feet get handled, pads included, since a seed rides home from a trail between them. The palm follows the curry over the rib cage so a lump is felt before it is a phone call. Short hair makes skin easy to read when someone bothers to read it.
