Where can I learn more?

Osteoarthritis Council and AAHA pain guidelines talk about weight loss first, then drugs and diets. A joint diet or an omega-3 dose should be chosen with the clinic that has seen the dog's radiographs. Glucosamine on its own is a weak plan for a dog that cannot climb the stairs.

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

Glucosamine in a kibble does not prove a limp is arthritis. Large-breed puppies are examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks so growth and gait are watched in person. Adult dogs need at least a yearly exam, and a dog who is already lame, and many dogs from about 7 years with giant breeds earlier, should be seen about every six months or sooner if the limp worsens. Hip dysplasia, a cruciate tear, and tick-borne joint disease are separated at the exam. The food does not do that separation.

How can I save money on pet care?

Glucosamine baked into an expensive kibble does not diagnose hip dysplasia or a cruciate tear. You can pay for that food for months and still pay for the orthopedic procedure. Have the limp examined. If the veterinarian wants a joint diet after that, compare it per day with the food you are already feeding, and do not add it on top of full meals.
