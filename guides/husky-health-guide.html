Aging and Senior

Siberian husky eye disease is why the policy date matters. Juvenile cataracts or corneal dystrophy already written on an ophthalmologist's form are pre-existing. A DNA swab does not remove that form from the record. Hip films scored before enrollment are a separate pre-existing joint finding. A progressive retinal atrophy mutation already reported for the line is on the record too. Buy the policy while the annual eye exam is still clean.

Siberian husky screening that changes decisions is an annual ophthalmologist exam and hip radiographs. Juvenile cataracts, corneal dystrophy, and progressive retinal atrophy are found with a slit lamp over the dog's life. A one-time puppy swab misses cataracts that develop after the sale. Some PRA and cataract mutations are testable in certain lines. They do not retire the yearly eye exam. OFA and the Siberian Husky Club of America point buyers at eyes and hips, not at a coat-color bundle.
