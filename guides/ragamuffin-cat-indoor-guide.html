Indoor Living Essentials

The Ragamuffin is distinct among cat breeds: a large cat with long fur, moderate energy demands, and predispositions that informed owners should understand.

Health Predisposition Summary: Ragamuffins show higher-than-average incidence of HCM, PKD, obesity based on breed health database data.
