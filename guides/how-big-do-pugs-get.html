How Big Do Pugs Get? Size & Growth Guide

Pug full size: 14-18 lbs, small breed.

Full-Grown Size

Pugs are a small breed, reaching 14-18 lbs at full maturity.

Growth Timeline

  • Size: small
  • Energy Level: Low
  • Shedding: Heavy
