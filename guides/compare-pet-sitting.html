Best Pet Sitting & Boarding Comparison Guide 2024

Whether you're traveling for work or vacation, finding the right care for your pet is essential. From in-home pet sitters to professional boarding facilities, this guide compares the best options to keep your furry friend safe, happy, and well-cared-for while you're away.

Types of Pet Care Services

In-Home Pet Sitting

A sitter comes to your home to care for your pet, maintaining their routine and environment.

Pros

  • Pet stays in familiar environment (less stress)
  • Maintains regular routine and diet
  • Good for anxious or senior pets
  • Home security benefit (someone checking the house)
  • Ideal for multi-pet households

Cons

  • Less supervision between visits
  • Pet may be lonely without 24/7 presence
  • Relies on individual sitter reliability

In-Home Boarding (Sitter's Home)

Your pet stays at the sitter's home, often receiving more personal attention than facilities.

Pros

  • Home environment (not kennel)
  • More individual attention
  • Often less expensive than facilities
  • Usually 24/7 supervision

Cons

  • Quality varies by sitter
  • May have other pets in home
  • Less structured than facilities
  • Away from pet's familiar environment

Boarding Facilities/Kennels

Professional facilities with trained staff, structured schedules, and often additional services.

Pros

  • Professional staff and facilities
  • Consistent care standards
  • Often have veterinary relationships
  • Structured activities and socialization
  • Climate controlled, secure environments

Cons

  • Can be stressful for some pets
  • Higher risk of illness exposure
  • Less individual attention
  • More expensive than home-based options

House Sitting Exchange

Sitters stay at your home for free in exchange for pet care, often used by travelers.

Pros

  • No per-night fees (just membership)
  • 24/7 in-home care
  • Full home security
  • Great for longer trips

Cons

  • Stranger in your home
  • May need to coordinate timing
  • Less control over sitter selection
  • Annual membership cost

Choosing the Right Option for Your Pet

Best for Anxious Dogs

  • Recommendation: In-home pet sitting (Rover house sitting)
  • Keeps pet in familiar environment
  • Maintains normal routine
  • Avoids stress of new environment or other animals

Best for Social Dogs

  • Recommendation: Camp Bow Wow or Dogtopia
  • Plenty of play and socialization
  • Supervised group activities
  • Tire them out so they sleep well

Best for Cats

  • Recommendation: In-home visits (Rover drop-ins)
  • Cats generally prefer staying home
  • Less stressful than boarding
  • 1-2 visits per day usually sufficient

Best for Senior Pets

  • Recommendation: In-home sitting or experienced home boarder
  • Can maintain medication schedules
  • Familiar environment reduces confusion
  • One-on-one attention for special needs

Best for Extended Travel

  • Recommendation: TrustedHousesitters
  • Most economical for long stays
  • 24/7 in-home care
  • No boarding costs adding up

Best for Last-Minute Needs

  • Recommendation: Wag! or Rover
  • Large network of available sitters
  • Quick booking options
  • Filter by immediate availability

Preparing Your Pet for Boarding

  • Vaccinations: Ensure all required vaccinations are current (most require rabies, distemper, bordetella)
  • Flea/tick prevention: Most facilities require proof of prevention
  • Trial visit: Do a daycare trial or meet-and-greet before overnight stays
  • Bring comfort items: Familiar blanket, toy, or shirt with your scent
  • Medication instructions: Written instructions for any medications
  • Emergency contacts: Provide vet info and emergency contact numbers
  • Special instructions: Feeding schedule, allergies, behavioral notes

Kennel Cough Prevention

Dogs in group settings are at higher risk for kennel cough (Bordetella). Ensure your dog's Bordetella vaccine is current (most facilities require it within 6-12 months). Even vaccinated dogs can contract mild cases, so be prepared for possible coughing after boarding.

Questions to Ask Before Booking

  • What is your pet care experience?
  • How many pets do you care for at once?
  • What is your emergency plan/vet relationship?
  • How much time will my pet spend supervised vs. in a kennel?
  • Can I receive photo/video updates?
  • What are your feeding and medication protocols?
  • What happens if my pet gets sick or injured?
  • What are your cancellation policies?
