Is a Old English Sheepdog right for me?

Old English Sheepdogs are large dogs with moderate energy and a lifespan of 10-12 yrs. They require daily brushing grooming and are prone to hip dysplasia.

How much does a Old English Sheepdog cost?

Initial costs range from $500-$3,000 for purchase/adoption, plus $1,000-$2,000 in first-year supplies and veterinary care. Annual ongoing costs are typically $1,500-$4,000 depending on health needs.

What health problems do Old English Sheepdogs have?

Old English Sheepdogs are prone to hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, cataracts. Regular vet checkups and breed-specific screening tests help catch these conditions early for better treatment outcomes.

How long do Old English Sheepdogs live?

The average lifespan for a Old English Sheepdog is 10-12 yrs. Proper nutrition, regular exercise, preventive veterinary care, and maintaining a healthy weight can help your Old English Sheepdog live to the upper end of this range.
