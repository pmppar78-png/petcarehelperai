Forty-five to sixty pounds, and a short lifespan to plan for

The Chinese Shar-Pei card on this site says 18 to 20 inches, 45 to 60 pounds, and 8 to 12 years. That lifespan is the number that should change how you read the rest of the size guide. This is a medium dog with a guard-dog history from southern China, wrinkled skin, and a muzzle people compare to a hippopotamus. The name refers to a harsh coat. The wrinkles are a medical surface, and they also fool the eye. A heavy Shar-Pei and a correct one can look alike from across the room.

The profile tells owners to treat a fever spike, a swollen hock, or a dog who stops eating as possible Shar-Pei fever, an autoinflammatory disease, and to go the same day. Entropion rolls the eyelid into the eye and scars the cornea. Cherry eye and glaucoma are on the eye list. Folds that stay wet grow yeast. Environmental and food allergies are called common. Before you buy, the page says to ask about familial Shar-Pei fever in the line, eye certifications, and hip evaluations, and to find a veterinarian who has already treated the breed. Embark is linked there as a sponsored DNA test, and it does not replace the eye exam or the fever history.

Food on the profile is listed at $500 to $800 and routine veterinary care at $400 to $800. The vet band is high for a 45 to 60 pound dog because ears, eyes, and skin generate visits that a smooth-coated breed skips. During growth, keep the dog lean. Folds on a fat puppy stay damp, and a damp fold is the yeast you will pay to culture. Dry the muzzle, the toes, and the tail pocket after every bath. Have the clinic show you the ear-canal drying step while the dog is still small enough to hold easily.

Height in this breed is modest. Eighteen to twenty inches does not need a giant crate, and it does need a floor the dog can grip, because a hurried puppy on slick tile injures a joint you will live with for a life that may only run 8 to 12 years. Exercise is moderate. A long forced run in the heat is a bad plan for a brachycephalic, wrinkled dog. Walks, training, and a cool room beat a noon jog.

Write the adult target, inside 45 to 60 pounds, where you scoop the food. Recheck the eyelids at every vaccine visit while the skull is still growing. Treat a squint as the entropion appointment the first time you see it. The size that matters at ten years is the one you kept lean, with eyes that still close over the cornea.
