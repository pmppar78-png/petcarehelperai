French Bulldog

Finding a French Bulldog to Adopt

Rescue French Bulldogs come with a history, not a prediction, and that matters more the first time you try to own the breed.

16-28 lbs at maturity, 10-12 yrs lifespan — the French Bulldog does best in a home where the owner actually understands the breed-level quirks rather than learning them the hard way.

Health Awareness: The breed-level risk profile for French Bulldogs includes brachycephalic syndrome, spinal disorders, allergies.

Breed-Specific Rescues

  • Size: small (16-28 lbs)
  • Energy Level: Low
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 10-12 yrs
