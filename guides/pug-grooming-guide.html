Ear & Dental Care

Wipe a pug's facial folds daily and dry them, or yeast takes the wrinkles. Check the ears, which are poorly ventilated on a brachycephalic head, and call the clinic for odor or head shaking. The crowded mouth needs brushing and an earlier dental than a long-nosed dog. Keep the nails short so they do not scratch the already-irritated face. Skip a heavy bath in hot water. They overheat.

Brachycephalic Airway Considerations

What are the most important considerations for pug grooming health and comfort?

Chat With AI

Pug facial folds need a daily wipe and a full dry, or yeast and bacteria grow in the crease. The nails, the ear canals, and the skin under the tail are the other spots. Pugs overheat because of the short nose, so grooming in a hot room is a bad plan. Anesthesia for a dental is an airway decision: tell the clinic this is a brachycephalic dog and ask how they monitor the throat. Keep the dog lean. A lean face is easier to wipe dry inside the folds.
