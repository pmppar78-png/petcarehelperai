Why Is My Cat Not Grooming Itself

When cats stop grooming: arthritis, obesity, dental pain, depression, and illness. A cat neglecting grooming needs veterinary attention.

Understanding This Symptom

A subtle shift in a cat’s behaviour is often the first and only early warning. Catching it depends entirely on knowing their baseline. This article covers the most common causes, warning signs that indicate an emergency, and what you can expect at the veterinarian.

When to Seek Emergency Care

If you see laboured breathing, seizures, collapse, uncontrolled bleeding, or sudden rear-limb paralysis, go straight to an emergency clinic. Observation is not a safe plan with these signs.

Should I go to the emergency vet?

A cat that stops grooming and is also hiding, breathing fast, or not eating should be seen the same day. A greasy coat that appears over weeks in an older cat can be booked this week.

How much will treatment cost?

The exam looks for mouth pain, obesity that prevents reaching the back, arthritis, and systemic disease. Dental disease and bloodwork are the common add-ons. Ask which one the coat change suggests before you approve a dental.

Can I treat this at home?

Note whether the cat can jump, whether the breath smells, and whether the fur is matted only along the spine. A sanitary clip of mats helps, and a full bath of a sick cat can chill it. Mats tight to the skin should be clipped by someone who can see the skin.
