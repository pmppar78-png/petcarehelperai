    Where can I learn more?

    Small mouths develop periodontal disease early, and the AAHA Dental Care Guidelines are the reading on charting and dental radiographs under anesthesia. The food itself still has to meet an AAFCO dog nutrient profile. Kibble size is a texture choice for a small mouth.

    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    Toy and small puppies are examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, and those visits cover vaccine timing, dental crowding, and the low blood sugar risk of a tiny puppy who skips a meal. Adult small dogs need at least a yearly exam, and the awake mouth check belongs on that visit because periodontal disease starts early in small mouths. Many small dogs are not seniors as early as giant breeds. A collapse, a seizure, or a toy dog who will not eat should be seen the same day.

    How can I save money on pet care?

    Small dogs eat little, so a huge bag goes stale and you buy it twice. Buy a bag you will finish while it is fresh, and compare price per day. The expensive problem in these mouths is periodontal disease found late. Home care between the dental procedure the clinic schedules is what stretches that interval. Dental disease in a tiny breed is still a dental procedure.
