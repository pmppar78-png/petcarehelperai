What are the most important considerations for great dane vs english mastiff?

Great Danes are 110 to 175 pounds and tall. English Mastiffs are heavier still, often 120 to 230 pounds, with looser skin and a very large food bill. Drool and orthopedic risk climb with the Mastiff. Bloat is the Dane headline: a swollen belly and retching that produces nothing means the emergency clinic now. Put nonslip runners down for either puppy before it arrives.
