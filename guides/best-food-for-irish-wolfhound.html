Nutritional Needs of Irish Wolfhounds

As a large hound breed with moderate energy levels, the Irish Wolfhound has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Irish Wolfhounds typically weigh 105-180 lbs and need approximately 1,400–2,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. With moderate energy levels, most Irish Wolfhounds do well on standard feeding guidelines for their size.

Health Awareness: Irish Wolfhounds have documented breed-level risk for heart disease, bloat, osteosarcoma.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Irish Wolfhounds

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Irish Wolfhounds.

Feeding Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Irish Wolfhound's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Irish Wolfhound's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Irish Wolfhound's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Irish Wolfhounds benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Irish Wolfhounds may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
