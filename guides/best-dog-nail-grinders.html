    Where can I learn more?

    Nail length between visits is husbandry. A broken nail, a nail bed that will not stop bleeding, or a mass at the toe is a clinic visit.

    How often should I take my pet to the vet?

    Nail length is a home task between exams, and a toe that suddenly hurts is a veterinary visit. Puppies are seen every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, adults at least yearly, and many dogs from about 7 years, giant breeds earlier, about every six months. A nail that will not stop bleeding, a broken nail bed, or a dewclaw curled into the pad should be seen this week. A mass at the toe can look like a stubborn quick.

    How can I save money on pet care?

    A grinder or a clipper is a small tool. The costly visit is a nail torn off at the base or a toe mass you tried to file down. Learn one method the dog tolerates and stop when the quick is close. A bleeding nail usually stops with styptic powder and pressure, so a quick that bled once is no reason to buy a new tool. If the bleeding will not stop, have the nail seen.
