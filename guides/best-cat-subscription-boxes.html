    Common Questions

    Treats in a monthly box are still food. AAFCO's explanation of pet-food labels and the nutritional adequacy statement is the reading that says whether an item is a complete diet or a snack. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control toxic-food list is the reading for a surprise ingredient. The shipping email is neither.

    The veterinary schedule stays in place beside a monthly box of toys and treats. The kitten vaccine series is still a veterinary visit every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks, an adult cat is still examined about once a year, and many cats from about 10 years old are examined twice a year. Treats in the box add calories, so weight gain is caught on the clinic scale at those visits. A male cat who cannot pass urine is an emergency between deliveries.

    A subscription charges you for toys and treats whether the cat plays with them or not. Cancel or skip a month when the last box is still unopened, and count the treat calories so you are not also paying for weight gain later. Read the AAFCO statement before you pay for a snack on top of a complete diet.
