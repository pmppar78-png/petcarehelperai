Nutritional Needs of Bombays

Bombays typically weigh 6-11 lbs and need approximately 200–350 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. With moderate energy levels, most Bombays do well on standard feeding guidelines for their size.

Genetic Health Considerations: The Bombay breed has documented susceptibility to HCM, breathing issues, excessive tearing.

Health-Specific Diet Considerations

Bombays are prone to several health conditions that can be managed or prevented through proper nutrition.

  • Breathing Issues: Look for foods with nutrients that support breathing issues management
  • Excessive Tearing: Look for foods with nutrients that support excessive tearing management

Best Protein Sources for Bombays

  • Chicken and turkey: Lean, easily digestible proteins ideal for most Bombays

Foods to Avoid

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Bombays

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Bombays.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Bombay's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Bombay's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Bombay's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Bombays benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Bombays may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.

Quick Answers

Heart screening

Bombays come from Burmese lines, and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is reported in that family. The screening test is an echocardiogram. There is no Bombay DNA test that takes the place of the scan. A murmur or harder breathing at rest is a reason to book it sooner than the next wellness visit. Grain-free food does not treat the heart muscle. Feed a complete cat food in measured meals so the ribs stay easy to feel.

Adult Bombays typically need a measured daily ration based on ideal weight, calorie density, and body condition, split into two or more meals.

What is the best food brand for Bombays?

Bombays come from Burmese lines and gain weight on a full bowl. Grain-free food does not prevent that weight gain or the heart muscle disease reported in this family. Measure a Bombay's complete food to ribs you can feel, and split it into meals. A grain allergy is uncommon in Bombays, so a veterinarian would confirm one before the bag changes.
