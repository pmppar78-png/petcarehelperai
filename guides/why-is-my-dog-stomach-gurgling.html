Seek emergency care if this symptom is severe, worsening rapidly, accompanied by other serious symptoms (collapse, difficulty breathing, seizures), or if your dog appears to be in significant pain or distress.

Loud gut sounds with a normal dog can be diet related. Gurgling plus vomiting, a tight belly, or blood in the stool needs an exam. The costs to ask about are an anti-nausea plan, a pancreatitis test, and abdominal films if a foreign body is possible.

Note the last meal, any trash raid, and whether the dog is still passing stool. Skip the next rich treat. A hard swollen abdomen with retching is a leave-now emergency, especially in a deep-chested breed.
