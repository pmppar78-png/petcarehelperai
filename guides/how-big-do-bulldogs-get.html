How Big Do Bulldogs Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Bulldog full size: 40-50 lbs, medium breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Practical Bulldogs care is shaped by three things: medium size, moderate shedding, and a known predisposition to brachycephalic syndrome and hip dysplasia.

Buyers who met only the dam on a sofa misread mass every time. Weigh after a quiet indoor stroll, write the date, and decline to argue with a wish. Two pups can head for different ends of that 40 to 50 pound idea, one lighter female and one heavier male. The face will charm either way. The number on the scale is what the nose and the hips will carry for the remainder of the 8-10 yr often quoted for the breed. The pounds stay on the scale either way. The scale is the only honest witness in that room.

Bulldogs are a medium breed, reaching 40-50 lbs at full maturity. Medium breeds generally reach full size between 10-16 months.

Plan for 40-50 lbs of animal and 8-10 yrs of companionship with a Bulldog; the breed-specific care considerations are the kind it pays to read up on before day one. Let's examine the important details.

Weight Chart by Age

Females weigh as adults sit near 40 pounds. Males sit near 50. Those are the figures to give when someone asks how big this dog gets, and attach the shape in one shared breath: a wrinkled face, an undershot jaw, a nose pushed short, a dense low body. Pounds and skull are the conversation. A tape at the shoulder is a side note people use to feel organized. The AKC weight figures on the Bulldog stay in the puppy packet so a charming photo stops pretending to be a scale.

Male vs Female Size

The youngster resembles a loaf earlier than other puppies. Past 3 months old the wrinkles are already a daily project and the chest is wide for the weeks he has been alive. When month 5 arrives, hunger can outrun the airway if the bowl is bottomless, so measure every meal and count breaths after he eats. The notes show the body settling from month 10 into month 16. Some look done when month 10 arrives and then add a surprising thickness by month 16. The undershot jaw is obvious long before the final pounds. People fall for the face and forget to book a weigh-in.

Factors Affecting Size

Many experienced Bulldog owners recommend puzzle toys and interactive feeders for mental stimulation without overexertion.

Understanding your Bulldog's instinctual drives makes enrichment more effective. Rather than generic toy rotation, tailor activities to what this breed was developed to do. Working breeds benefit from task-oriented challenges; scent-driven breeds thrive with nose work; social breeds need interactive play rather than solo activities.

When They Stop Growing

Ribs hide under a smooth coat and a broad chest. Hands do the reading a glance will fake. Slight pressure should find them, on the female near 40 and the male near 50. Extra fat stacked over those ribs makes breathing harder and hips angrier for a shadow who already bargains for air. Midday heat and couch launches stay out of the growth months. A cool floor and a measuring cup do more for the adult outline than any speech about potential.
