What are the most important considerations for french bulldog vs pug?

French Bulldogs at 16 to 28 pounds and Pugs nearer 14 to 18 pounds share the short muzzle. Pugs have deeper facial folds and a curled tail that should be checked for irritation. Frenchies bring spinal and whelping concerns. Both overheat. A harness, a cool room, and a clinic that handles airway surgery belong on the list for either breed. The Pug is usually the lighter dog to carry.

French Bulldog vs Pug: Side-by-Side

French Bulldog and Pug look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. French Bulldog is larger at 16-28 lbs, while Pug typically runs 14-18 lbs.

Both breeds share a low energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Lifespan: French Bulldog typically lives 10-12 yrs; Pug 13-15 yrs. Pug generally has the longer-term care window, which affects insurance math and the point at which senior diagnostics become the dominant cost line.

Health watchlists differ. French Bulldog carries additional risk for spinal disorders, allergies. Pug is more notably predisposed to eye problems, obesity.

Grooming effort is meaningfully different: French Bulldog sheds at a moderate level, Pug at heavy.
