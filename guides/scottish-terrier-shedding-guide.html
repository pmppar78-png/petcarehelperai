Reducing Shed Hair

Prospective Scottish Terrier owners should know that this small terrier breed demands an informed approach to nutrition, exercise, and preventive health management. Consistent daily activity, even in short sessions, contributes more to long-term health than occasional intense exercise.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for small breed dogs (400–800 calories/day)
  • Maintain a 2–3 times per week grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for bladder cancer
