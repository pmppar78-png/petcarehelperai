Scottish Terrier Shedding

Dead hair in a Scottish Terrier jacket sits in the wiry furnishings until a comb pulls it, and the shed stays moderate across the year. A metal comb to the skin behind the beard removes more coat than a bristle brush on the outline.

Shedding Level

Scottish Terriers have a moderate shedding level. Regular grooming keeps shedding manageable and your Scottish Terrier's coat healthy.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies bladder cancer, von Willebrand disease, Scotty cramp as conditions with higher prevalence in Scottish Terriers.

Reducing Shed Hair

Furniture & Clothing Protection

A Scottish Terrier sheds little if the wiry coat is hand-stripped on a schedule. A neglected coat sheds dead hair onto furniture and mats into the furnishings. Strip or have a groomer strip it, and comb the beard after meals so food stays off the sofa. A shaved Scottie grows back a soft coat that sheds more and protects less. The throw on the chair is for the beard drips as much as the hair.
