Shedding Level

Scottish Terriers have a moderate shedding level. Regular grooming keeps shedding manageable and your Scottish Terrier's coat healthy.

Weighing around 18-22 lbs and lifespan of 12 yrs, the Scottish Terrier has specific care needs shaped by its genetics and build. Prospective Scottish Terrier owners should know that this small terrier breed demands an informed approach to nutrition, exercise, and preventive health management.

Breed-Specific Health Profile: Research identifies bladder cancer, von Willebrand disease, Scotty cramp as conditions with higher prevalence in Scottish Terriers. Reducing Shed Hair

Prospective Scottish Terrier owners should know that this small terrier breed demands an informed approach to nutrition, exercise, and preventive health management. Consistent daily activity, even in short sessions, contributes more to long-term health than occasional intense exercise.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for small breed dogs (400–800 calories/day)
  • Maintain a 2–3 times per week grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for bladder cancer
  • Securing pet insurance before any health issues arise gives you the broadest coverage at the most reasonable cost

When Shedding Indicates Problems

Knowing what to watch for gives you a real head start on breed-related problems. Watch for early signs of bladder cancer, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Scottish Terriers are prone to.
