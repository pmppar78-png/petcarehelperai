Nutritional Needs of Devon Rexs

As a small cat breed with high energy levels, the Devon Rex has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other cats.

Devon Rexs typically weigh 5-10 lbs and need approximately 400–800 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Devon Rexs may need 20-30% more calories than the average cat of their size.

Your Devon Rex's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Screening

Published reports include hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in the Devon Rex. The breed also has hereditary myopathy from a COLQ mutation, which shows up as weak neck muscles and fatigue, and no food replaces that gene. Devon Rex cats have a high metabolic rate, so feed enough complete cat food to hold weight, measured to body condition.
