Dog Dental Chew Buying Guide

What is the VOHC Seal?

The Veterinary Oral Health Council (VOHC) independently tests dental products and awards their seal only to products that meet strict standards for plaque and tartar reduction. Look for this seal when choosing dental chews - it's the closest thing to a guarantee of effectiveness.

How Dental Chews Work

  • Mechanical Action: Chewing scrapes plaque off teeth like brushing
  • Texture Design: Ridges, bristles, and shapes increase tooth contact
  • Ingredients: Some contain enzymes or chemicals that reduce bacteria
  • Saliva Stimulation: Chewing increases saliva which naturally cleans teeth

Choosing the Right Size

  • Always select the appropriate size for your dog's weight
  • Too small = choking hazard and ineffective
  • Too large = difficult to chew and may be left uneaten
  • Chews should take at least 5-10 minutes to finish for effectiveness

Safety First

Always supervise your dog with dental chews. Choose the correct size to prevent choking. If your dog tends to swallow large pieces, try a harder chew or supervise closely. Dental chews are a supplement to, not a replacement for, professional dental cleanings and daily brushing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I give my dog dental chews?

Most dental chews are designed for daily use. One appropriately-sized chew per day is typically recommended. Check the package for specific guidelines as calorie content varies. Factor dental chews into your dog's daily calorie intake to prevent weight gain.

Can dental chews replace brushing?

No, dental chews are a helpful supplement but don't replace brushing or professional cleanings. The gold standard is daily brushing with enzymatic toothpaste. Dental chews help between brushings and are better than nothing for dogs who won't tolerate brushing.

Are dental chews safe for puppies?

Most dental chews are designed for adult dogs with permanent teeth. Check the package for age recommendations. Puppies under 6 months should avoid most dental chews. Choose puppy-specific teething treats for young dogs.
