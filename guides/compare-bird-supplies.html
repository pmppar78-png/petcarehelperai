Best Bird Supply Stores Compared

Comparing online bird supply stores for cages, food, toys, and accessories. Covers Chewy, PetSmart, specialty stores, and pricing.

Detailed Comparison

ChewyPetSmartA bird specialty shop
What it isAn online store for repeat orders of pellets, toys, and some cagesA walk-in store for seed, perches, and small cagesA shop that stocks flight cages, stainless bowls, and species diets
Use it forA pellet the bird already eats, on a schedule so you do not run outA perch, a toy, or a test kit you need todayThe cage the bird will live in, with bar spacing and flight dimensions published
Check before you buyThat the pellet is for this species. A macaw pellet is the wrong size and recipe for a budgie.Bar spacing and cage dimensions on the tag. Many store cages are travel cages.The minimum flight size for the species, and the return policy on a custom cage

Order the staple pellet where shipping is reliable. Buy the cage from a seller that prints bar spacing and the flight dimensions. A pretty cage with the wrong bar gap is the expensive mistake.

Quick Answers About Best Bird Supply Stores Compared

Which option is the best value?

Value is a cage the bird can move in and a pellet formulated for that species. Toys are the small part of the bill.

Can I switch later?

A food brand can be changed over a week or two by mixing the new pellet into the old one. A cage is a one-time purchase. Measure the door and the room before it ships.
