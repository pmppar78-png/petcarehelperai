Common Questions About Abyssinian Cat Health Guide

What is the best insurance for this breed?

Enroll before protein appears in the urine. Renal amyloidosis already in the chart is treated as pre-existing. An rdAc result or a PKLR result does not open a claim on its own. Read the hereditary-illness clause and the illness waiting period in the policy.

Should I do genetic testing?

Abyssinians have two DNA tests that are worth naming: progressive retinal atrophy rdAc in CEP290, and pyruvate kinase deficiency in PKLR. UC Davis prices them as health tests, and Somalis share both. Renal amyloidosis, which this breed is known for, does not have a definitive clear-the-line genetic test. A cat can be PRA-normal and PK-normal and still develop amyloidosis. Ask for those two results, and ask how the cattery follows urine protein in older cats, because the DNA will not answer that.
