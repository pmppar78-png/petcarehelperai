Quick Answers About

The useful policy is the one that can pay a rabbit dental or stasis claim in your state. A low premium on a dog-and-cat policy does not cover the rabbit.

You can apply to the other company. A condition already in the veterinary record, including a claim on the first policy, can be pre-existing on the second. Read the waiting period before you cancel the policy you have.
