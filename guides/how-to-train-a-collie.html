How to Train

Collie training. Tips for their moderate energy herding breed temperament.

Training Approach

Collies are moderate-energy herding dogs that benefit from regular but moderate training routines.

The Collie's reputation in the herding group reflects generations of purposeful breeding, resulting in a large dog with predictable but nuanced care requirements.

Collie Training Challenges

  • Size: large
  • Energy Level: Moderate
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 12-14 yrs

Socialization

For Collies, the inputs that matter most are a large frame, a heavy shedding coat, and breed-level risk for collie eye anomaly and hip dysplasia.
