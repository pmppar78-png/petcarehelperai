Mental Stimulation

The Rhodesian Ridgeback stands out among large breeds, weighing 70-85 lbs and carrying a temperament shaped by the hound group's heritage. Consistent daily activity, even in short sessions, contributes more to long-term health than occasional intense exercise.

  • Provide 30–60 minutes of daily exercise appropriate to their energy level
  • Feed a high-quality diet formulated for large breed dogs (1,400–2,200 calories/day)
  • Maintain a weekly grooming routine
  • Schedule breed-appropriate health screenings for hip dysplasia
  • Invest in pet insurance early to cover breed-specific conditions
