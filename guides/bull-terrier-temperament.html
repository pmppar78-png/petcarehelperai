What are the most important considerations for bull terrier temperament?

A Bull Terrier is comic, strong, and capable of a fixation, including tail chasing, that should be interrupted early. Many are affectionate with their own people and hard on other dogs. Hereditary nephritis and deafness in white dogs are the health facts.
