Best Time to Insure Your St. Bernard

Enroll your St. Bernard as early as possible — ideally as a puppy or kitten. Pre-existing conditions are never covered, so insuring before health issues develop is critical. St. Bernards are prone to hip dysplasia, which can develop as early as 1-2 years of age.

How much does St. Bernard pet insurance cost?

NAPHIA's 2025 State of the Industry report, compiled by Willis Towers Watson from year-end 2024 premiums, puts US dog accident-and-illness coverage at $62.44 a month ($749.29 a year) and accident-only coverage at $16.10 a month. Enrollment age, ZIP code, deductible, reimbursement percentage, and annual limit set the quote. For a St. Bernard, the claims that move the quote are gastric dilatation-volvulus, entropion, and osteosarcoma.

Is pet insurance worth it for a St. Bernard?

Gastric dilatation-volvulus in a St. Bernard is an emergency operation, and entropion is a smaller one. Heart disease in this breed is medical monitoring and medication, not that gastropexy. Osteosarcoma, if it appears, is amputation and often chemotherapy.

What pre-existing conditions affect St. Bernard insurance?

Any condition diagnosed before enrollment is excluded. For St. Bernards, common pre-existing concerns include hip dysplasia and bloat. Early enrollment is key.
