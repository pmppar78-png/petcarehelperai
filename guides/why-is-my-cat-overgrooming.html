Go the same day if the skin is raw and infected or the cat is also hiding and off food. Weeks of overgrooming a bald belly can be booked this week. A male cat that is overgrooming the belly and visiting the litter box often should be seen now, because the belly grooming can be bladder pain.

Overgrooming a bald belly or a raw spot needs an exam for fleas, pain, allergies, and stress. Cytology of the skin and a flea check come first. A behavior drug is a later conversation. Ask what the first visit will rule out before you buy another collar.

Look for flea dirt at the base of the tail and film the grooming. A cone lets a hot spot heal. Stop scented diffusers in that room. A cat that overgrooms and also pees outside the box should have a urine test. A new toy does not answer bladder pain.
