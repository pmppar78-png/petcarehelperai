How Big Do Basset Hounds Get? Size & Growth Guide

Basset Hound full size: 40-65 lbs, medium breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Basset Hounds are a medium breed, reaching 40-65 lbs at full maturity. Medium breeds generally reach full size between 10-16 months.

Weighing around 40-65 lbs and lifespan of 12-13 yrs, the Basset Hound benefits from care tailored to its physical and behavioral profile. We cover the essential details in the sections that follow.
