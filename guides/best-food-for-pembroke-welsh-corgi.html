Pembroke Welsh Corgi

Owner-focused guide to the best dog food for Pembroke Welsh Corgis based on their medium size, high energy level, and health needs including hip dysplasia.

Nutritional Needs of Pembroke Welsh Corgis

Pembroke Welsh Corgis typically weigh 25-30 lbs and need approximately 800–1,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Pembroke Welsh Corgis may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Pembroke Welsh Corgis have elevated rates of hip dysplasia, degenerative myelopathy, obesity.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Pembroke Welsh Corgis

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Pembroke Welsh Corgis.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Pembroke Welsh Corgi's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Pembroke Welsh Corgi's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Pembroke Welsh Corgi's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Pembroke Welsh Corgis benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Pembroke Welsh Corgis may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
