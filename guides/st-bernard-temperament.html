St. Bernard Temperament & Personality Guide

St. Bernard temperament traits, personality, and behavior. What to expect from this low-energy working breed with family, kids, and other pets.

Personality Foundations

The St. Bernard is known for being a low-energy working breed with a distinctive personality. As a working breed, they are loyal, protective, and often form strong bonds with their primary caretaker.

Weighing around 120-180 lbs and lifespan of 8-10 yrs, the St. Bernard benefits from care tailored to its physical and behavioral profile. Whether you are researching the St. Bernard for the first time or deepening your knowledge as a current owner, the breed's working lineage is the foundation for understanding their needs.

St. Bernards should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 1-2 years of age, as large breeds develop structural issues early. Screening before symptoms appear makes a meaningful difference in outcomes.
