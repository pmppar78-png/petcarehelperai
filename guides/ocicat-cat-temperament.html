Personality Foundations

The Ocicat is known for being a high-energy cat breed with a distinctive personality. Their unique blend of traits makes them well-suited for the right owner and lifestyle.

At 6-15 lbs and with a typical 12-18 yrs lifespan, the Ocicat rewards owners who understand the breed's specific quirks rather than treating it as a generic pet. Alertness and Guarding

Early intervention consistently produces better outcomes and lower costs than reactive treatment for breed-associated conditions. Watch for early signs of HCM, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your cat at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Ocicat Cats are prone to.
