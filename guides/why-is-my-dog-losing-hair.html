Go sooner if the hair loss comes with a rash that is spreading by the hour, facial swelling, or a dog that is also lethargic and drinking heavily. Symmetric coat loss in a dog that feels fine can wait for a regular dermatology-style appointment.

The workup often includes a skin scraping for mites, a fungal culture or lamp check, and bloodwork when the pattern looks hormonal. Cytology is cheaper than a biopsy. Ask the order the clinic wants before you approve all of it.

Note whether the dog is itchy, whether the skin is greasy or smooth, and which areas are bald. Stop any new topical oil or dip. A bald patch that is also scabby can be ringworm, which spreads to people, so wash hands after handling.
