How much does Havana Brown pet insurance cost?

NAPHIA's 2025 State of the Industry report, compiled by Willis Towers Watson from year-end 2024 premiums, puts US cat accident-and-illness coverage at $32.21 a month ($386.47 a year) and accident-only coverage at $9.17 a month. Enrollment age, ZIP code, deductible, reimbursement percentage, and annual limit set the quote. For a Havana Brown, the claims that move the quote are hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and bladder stones.

Is pet insurance worth it for a Havana Brown?

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in a Havana Brown is diagnosed by echocardiogram and treated with medication. A bladder stone can need a cystotomy, and a respiratory infection is medical. Those are three different claims.

What pre-existing conditions affect Havana Brown insurance?

Any condition diagnosed before enrollment is excluded. For Havana Browns, common pre-existing concerns include HCM and calcium oxalate stones. Early enrollment is key.
