What are the most important considerations for golden retriever vs german shepherd?

Goldens at about 55 to 75 pounds tend to greet everyone. German Shepherds at 50 to 90 pounds tend to watch everyone. Exercise is high for both. The Golden's coat and ears need more weekly care. Hips belong on the exam list for the Shepherd, and joints for the Golden. Decide which doorway behavior you want, then check that you can exercise that choice every day.
