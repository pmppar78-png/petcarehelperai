Best Pet Boarding

Comparing Rover, Wag!, Camp Bow Wow, and traditional boarding facilities. Covers pricing, services, safety, and which is best for your pet.

Detailed Comparison

RoverWagCamp Bow Wow or a local kennel
What it isA marketplace of people who board pets in their homesA marketplace of caregivers for walks, sitting, and boardingA facility. Camp Bow Wow is a daycare and boarding chain. A local kennel or veterinary hospital is a facility stay at a fixed address.
Use it forA dog or cat who is calm in a house, after a meet-and-greetThe same home-care arrangement, with a written handover of medicationsA dog who already does well in a play group, or an owner who wants a published vaccine policy and a street address
Who is home during the day, and which vaccines the sitter requiresThe same meet-and-greet, plus how medications are givenWhether someone is on the property overnight, and where the pet goes if it becomes ill

A home sitter suits a pet who settles in a house. A facility suits a dog who already copes with daycare, or an owner who wants the pet at a fixed address. Visit once before the trip you cannot cancel.

The cheaper sit is the one that matches the pet. Group play is a poor value for a dog who fights. A home sitter is a poor value for a dog who needs a midday medication and an empty house.

Book a different sitter or kennel for the next trip. For the current trip, the cancellation window is the host's. Read it before you pay.
