French Bulldog vs Bulldog: Side-by-Side

French Bulldog and Bulldog look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Bulldog is larger at 40-50 lbs, while French Bulldog typically runs 16-28 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Both breeds share a low energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Lifespan: French Bulldog typically lives 10-12 yrs; Bulldog 8-10 yrs. French Bulldog generally has the longer-term care window, which affects insurance math and the point at which senior diagnostics become the dominant cost line.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around brachycephalic syndrome. French Bulldog carries additional risk for spinal disorders, allergies. Bulldog is more notably predisposed to hip dysplasia, skin infections. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.
