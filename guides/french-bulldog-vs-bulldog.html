What are the most important considerations for french bulldog vs bulldog?

French Bulldogs are 16 to 28 pounds. The Bulldog is heavier, often around 40 to 50 pounds, with deeper skin folds and a lower tolerance for heat. Both are brachycephalic. Wrinkle care dominates the Bulldog's week. Spinal and airway questions dominate the Frenchie's clinic visits. Air conditioning matters for both in summer. French Bulldog vs Bulldog: Side-by-Side

Both breeds share a low energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Health watchlists differ. French Bulldog carries additional risk for spinal disorders, allergies. Bulldog is more notably predisposed to hip dysplasia, skin infections.
