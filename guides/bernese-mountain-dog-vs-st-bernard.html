Bernese Mountain Dog vs St. Bernard: Side-by-Side

Bernese Mountain Dog and St. Bernard look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. St. Bernard is larger at 120-180 lbs, while Bernese Mountain Dog typically runs 70-115 lbs.

Energy level is the practical differentiator for most households: Bernese Mountain Dog is classified as moderate-energy and St. Bernard as low-energy.

Lifespan: Bernese Mountain Dog typically lives 6-8 yrs; St. Bernard 8-10 yrs. St. Bernard generally has the longer-term care window, which affects insurance math and the point at which senior diagnostics become the dominant cost line.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around hip dysplasia, bloat. Bernese Mountain Dog carries additional risk for cancer. St. Bernard is more notably predisposed to heart disease.
