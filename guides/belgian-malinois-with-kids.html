Are Belgian Malinois Good with Kids? Family Guide

Family Compatibility

Belgian Malinois are energetic and large, which means they can accidentally knock over small children.

Genetic Health Considerations: The Belgian Malinois breed has documented susceptibility to hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy.

Age-Appropriate Interactions

  • Size: large (40-80 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Moderate
  • Lifespan: 14-16 yrs

Health Monitoring

Belgian Malinois bring a large build, a moderate shedding pattern, and breed-specific health risk around hip dysplasia and elbow dysplasia — each of those shifts routine care in a different direction.

Quick Answers

What are the most important considerations for belgian malinois with kids?

A Belgian Malinois is a poor match for a house run by children. The dog is fast, mouthy when aroused, and needs an adult who trains. A child can throw a toy in a structured game. A child should not be the one correcting the dog. Confirm hip and elbow scores.
