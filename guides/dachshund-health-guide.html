Key Questions

Dachshund coverage is about disc surgery. Intervertebral disc herniation that has already caused pain or weakness is the pre-existing event these contracts exclude. A CDDY genotype does not open that exclusion by itself and does not promise that a later hemilaminectomy will be paid. Buy the plan while the dog is still walking normally, after you have read the orthopedic waiting period.

Disc disease in dachshunds is associated with an FGF4 retrogene insertion on chromosome 12, the chondrodystrophy variant labs report as CDDY. UC Davis and other labs offer the test. It does not tell you whether this dog will rupture a disc. Many dogs with the variant never lose the ability to walk, and dogs can herniate for other reasons. Lafora disease testing matters in miniature wirehaired lines. Back pain is still a neurology problem, not a genotype. Do not skip insurance or crate-rest planning because a report said 'carrier.'
