Cocker Spaniel Health Issues

Common health problems in Cocker Spaniels including ear infections, cataracts, hip dysplasia. Prevention, symptoms to watch for, and treatment options.

Common Health Problems

Cocker Spaniels are predisposed to several health conditions including ear infections, cataracts, hip dysplasia. Understanding these risks allows you to screen early, prevent where possible, and catch problems before they become emergencies.

Weighing around 20-30 lbs and lifespan of 10-14 yrs, the Cocker Spaniel has specific care needs shaped by its genetics and build. The Cocker Spaniel's care profile reflects its breeding history: size, coat type, energy level, and health predispositions all interact.
