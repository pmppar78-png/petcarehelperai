Miniature Pinscher Health Issues

Common health problems in Miniature Pinschers including luxating patella, Legg-Calve-Perthes, progressive retinal atrophy. Progressive retinal atrophy and Fearless, Fun-Loving, Proud belong in the health list for the Miniature Pinscher.

Common Health Problems

Miniature Pinschers are predisposed to several health conditions including luxating patella, Legg-Calve-Perthes, progressive retinal atrophy.

Few breeds combine boundless energy with the Miniature Pinscher's distinctive character quite so effectively.

Health Awareness: Miniature Pinschers show elevated breed-level risk for luxating patella, Legg-Calve-Perthes, progressive retinal atrophy.

Genetic Screening

  • Size: small (8-12 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Light
  • Lifespan: 12-16 yrs

Prevention Strategies

Miniature Pinschers sit in the small-size category, shed at a light level, and carry documented risk for luxating patella and Legg-Calve-Perthes — those three factors drive most of the daily-care decisions.
