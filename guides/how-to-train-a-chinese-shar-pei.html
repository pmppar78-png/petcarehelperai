How to Train a Chinese Shar-Pei

Chinese Shar-Pei training. Tips for their moderate energy non-sporting breed temperament.

Training Approach

Chinese Shar-Peis are moderate-energy non-sporting dogs that benefit from regular but moderate training routines. Their intelligence and temperament make them responsive to positive reinforcement training methods.

Expect 45-60 lbs at maturity and 8-12 yrs of life with a Chinese Shar-Pei; the combination of its health pattern and temperament profile is where owner attention pays the largest dividends. Living with a Chinese Shar-Pei means adapting to a moderate-energy companion that thrives on structure, appropriate exercise, and attentive health monitoring.
