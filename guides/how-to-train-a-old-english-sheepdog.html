How to Train a Old English Sheepdog: Complete Guide

Old English Sheepdog training. Tips for their moderate energy herding breed temperament.

Training Approach

Old English Sheepdogs are moderate-energy herding dogs that benefit from regular but moderate training routines. As a herding breed, they have strong working instincts and excel at agility, obedience trials, and trick training.

Weighing around 60-100 lbs and lifespan of 10-12 yrs, the Old English Sheepdog requires attention to its unique health and temperament characteristics. The Old English Sheepdog stands out among large breeds, weighing 60-100 lbs and carrying a temperament shaped by the herding group's heritage.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Old English Sheepdogs have elevated rates of hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, cataracts. Prevalence varies, and many individuals live full lives without developing these issues. However, breed-aware veterinary care — including targeted screening at appropriate ages — is the most effective prevention strategy.

Hip and Joint Health Management

Hip dysplasia — a polygenic condition where the femoral head fails to fit properly within the acetabulum — is a documented concern in the Old English Sheepdog. The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) maintains a breed-specific database showing dysplasia prevalence rates, and the PennHIP evaluation method provides a distraction index that can predict hip laxity as early as 16 weeks of age. For large breeds like the Old English Sheepdog, maintaining lean body condition during growth is one of the most impactful preventive measures, as studies from the Purina Lifespan Study demonstrated that dogs kept at ideal body weight had significantly delayed onset of osteoarthritis. Joint supplements containing glucosamine hydrochloride, chondroitin sulfate, and omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) have demonstrated clinical benefit in peer-reviewed veterinary orthopedic literature when started before symptomatic onset.
