Seventeen to twenty-three pounds, and a coat that blows twice a year

The Shiba Inu profile describes a dog of about 13.5 to 16.5 inches and roughly 17 to 23 pounds, while the stats table on the breed page lists height as 13 to 17 inches. Lifespan is 13 to 16 years. This size guide uses the 17 to 23 pound adult line. They look finished early, which is why people stop weighing them. A thick double coat hides a winter gain. The waist you could see in August disappears under guard hair by January, and the scale is the only witness.

Health items the profile says are worth asking a breeder about are atopy, patellar luxation, glaucoma, and cataracts. Hip scores matter less than in a giant breed. They are still worth filing. A Shiba who rubs its face raw may have allergies, and the profile points to VCA's description of atopic dermatitis. A food trial is a veterinary plan. Face rubbing and red skin have a symptom page on this site for the night you are deciding whether to wait until morning. Have the clinic check the kneecaps while the dog is young, and ask what an eye certification on the parents actually showed.

The coat blows twice a year in a cloud that fills a vacuum. Size the grooming budget for those weeks, and size the house for a dog who is fastidious and intensely independent. Training works when it is a trade. A harness has to fit the adult chest, which is broader than the puppy chest that looked "almost done" at five months. Measure it again at a year. Escape is a size issue: a 20 pound dog fits through a gap a Labrador would notice, and a locked gate is part of the adult setup.

They can live with older children who respect a dog that will leave. A home that wants every visiting child and every sidewalk dog tolerated is a poor match, and the profile says cats are a predation problem unless the Shiba was raised with them. That temperament sits on a small-to-medium frame for 13 to 16 years. Exercise is moderate. A bored Shiba still digs and still bolts, and the adult weight does not make the bolt safer.

Write an adult target inside 17 to 23 pounds. Weigh monthly through the first two winters. If the ribs vanish, cut the cup before you buy a different bag because the label showed a wolf. Keep the eye and skin notes from the breeder in the same folder as the vaccine record. The compact body is the easy sell. The twice-yearly coat and the gate latch are the size-related work that continues after the dog has stopped growing.
