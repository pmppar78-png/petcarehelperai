What is the best insurance for this breed?

Neonatal isoerythrolysis kills kittens in a breeding cattery. It is not a treatment bill for the British Shorthair already living with you. The illness that matters for a pet British Shorthair is hypertrophic cardiomyopathy after an echocardiogram has documented it. Enroll while that study is still normal, then check whether heart medicine started after the illness waiting period is eligible.

Should I do genetic testing?

British Shorthair breeding decisions should include blood type. Type B is common in the breed, and a type B queen bred to a type A tom can lose kittens to neonatal isoerythrolysis. That is a bench blood type, not a coat panel. HCM still needs an echocardiogram because no single mutation clears the breed. PKD1 testing is useful when Persian cats are behind the pedigree. A pet kitten's owner needs the parents' echo reports more than a color genotype.
