Cardiac Health Monitoring

For a Doberman Pinscher, the claims that move the quote are dilated cardiomyopathy, von Willebrand disease, and wobbler syndrome.

Dilated cardiomyopathy in a Doberman is diagnosed by echocardiogram and Holter monitor and treated with cardiac medication. There is no single surgery for it, and von Willebrand disease is a clotting disorder managed around injuries and operations. Wobbler syndrome sometimes does go to spinal surgery.

For Doberman Pinschers, common pre-existing concerns include dilated cardiomyopathy and von Willebrand disease. .
