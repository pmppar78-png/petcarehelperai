How Big Do Portuguese Water Dogs Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Portuguese Water Dog full size: 35-60 lbs, medium breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Portuguese Water Dogs are a medium breed, reaching 35-60 lbs at full maturity. Medium breeds generally reach full size between 10-16 months.

Plan for 35-60 lbs of animal, a 11-13 yrs lifespan, and a distinct combination of health considerations and temperament that matters more than a species-level view would suggest. The details below reflect current veterinary knowledge and breed data.

Factors Affecting Size

Several breed-specific considerations deserve attention beyond routine care protocols. As a working breed, the Portuguese Water Dog has instincts and behaviors shaped by centuries of selective breeding for specific tasks.

When They Stop Growing

Preventive screening is most valuable when tailored to documented breed risks rather than applied as a generic checklist. Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Portuguese Water Dogs are prone to.

Set up regular times for meals, activity, grooming, and rest. High-energy Portuguese Water Dogs especially benefit from knowing when their exercise time is coming — it helps them settle during calmer periods.
