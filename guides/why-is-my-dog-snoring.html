Should I go to the emergency vet?

A brachycephalic dog that is snoring louder and also gagging, turning blue, or cannot cool off needs the emergency clinic. New snoring in a long-nosed dog that is otherwise well can be mentioned at an exam.

How much will treatment cost?

The visit looks at the nostrils, the palate, and the body weight. Chest films are added when the dog also coughs or tires easily. Airway surgery is a separate surgical estimate for dogs that cannot breathe through a walk.

Can I treat this at home?

Keep the dog lean and cool, use a harness, and film the sleep noise. Weight loss changes the airway more than a new bed does. A dog that sleeps with the elbows out and the gums pale should be seen promptly.
