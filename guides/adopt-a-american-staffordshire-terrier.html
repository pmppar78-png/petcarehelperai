American Staffordshire Terrier

Finding an American Staffordshire Terrier to Adopt

Adopting an American Staffordshire Terrier is not the bargain option — it is the informed option. Rescue American Staffordshire Terriers come with a known temperament, a known size, often a completed vaccination schedule, and volunteers who will tell you the truth about whether the dog is right for your home.

Genetic Health Considerations: The American Staffordshire Terrier breed has documented susceptibility to hip dysplasia, heart disease, allergies.

Breed-Specific Rescues

  • Energy Level: High
