Common Questions

What are the most important considerations for labrador retriever vs golden retriever?

The Labrador here is about 55 to 80 pounds with a short dense coat. The Golden is often 55 to 75 pounds with feathers on the legs and ears that need drying. Both retrieve, both gain weight easily, and both should have hips evaluated. Labs are more likely to counter-surf. Goldens are more likely to need a weekly brush. Spend an afternoon with adult dogs once the puppy pictures start to look the same.
