Best Cat GPS Trackers Compared

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Comparing GPS trackers designed for cats including Tractive, Tabcat, and other lightweight options. Covers range, battery life, and cat-specific features.

Detailed Comparison

TractiveTabcatApple AirTag
What it isA GPS collar that sends its position over a cellular network. Tractive states that live tracking needs a subscription because the tracker uses a SIM.A radio-frequency tag and a handheld finder. Tabcat describes it as a homing device with no satellite and no cellular plan.A Bluetooth item finder. A location appears when a nearby Apple device hears the tag.
Use it forA cat who is allowed far enough from home that you need a mapA cat who hides in the house, under the deck, or in the next yardA backup tag on a breakaway collar, with the limits of a crowdsourced Bluetooth network
Check before you buyCollar weight, a breakaway buckle, and the current plan price on Tractive's siteThat you will be close enough to walk toward the tag with the handsetThe weight on a breakaway collar. This is sold as an item finder, not as a live GPS collar.

Use Tractive when the cat leaves the block and you need a map. Use Tabcat when the cat is nearby and you want a finder without a monthly plan. Either tag still belongs on a collar that releases if the cat snags it.

Which option is the best value?

Value follows how far the cat goes. A subscription GPS tracker is the useful tool for a cat who leaves the neighborhood. A radio finder is the useful tool for a cat who hides under the porch.

Can I switch later?

A Tractive plan can be cancelled. Read the current term on Tractive's plan page before you pay for a year. A Tabcat tag has no plan to cancel.
