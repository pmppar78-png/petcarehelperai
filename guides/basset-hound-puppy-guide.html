Socialization Window

House Training

Housetrain a basset on a leash to the same door, after meals and after naps, and reward the outdoor event immediately. They follow their nose. An off-leash puppy in the yard is following scent. Leash it to the door if you want the lesson to stick. Use a crate sized so the puppy can stand and turn, and keep sessions short. A stubborn hound still learns when the routine is boringly consistent.
