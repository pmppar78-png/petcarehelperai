Common Questions

A new, loud, continuous yowl in an older cat should be seen soon, and now if the cat is also blind-looking, circling, or unable to walk. Hyperthyroidism, high blood pressure, pain, and cognitive change are on the list. A cat that yowls at the bedroom door at breakfast is a different pattern.

The exam includes a thyroid check and, in an older cat, a blood pressure reading. Bloodwork and a urine test follow when the drinking has also changed. Ask whether blood pressure is in the quote. It is easy to skip and it matters.

Note the time of day and film a yowl. Check the litter box for straining. Extra play and a feeding puzzle help a bored young cat and do not fix a hypertensive one.
