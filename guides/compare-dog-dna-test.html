A breed percentage is an estimate. Two companies can name different breeds for the same dog because each compares the sample with its own reference dogs. Buy a health-marker test when the question is a drug sensitivity or a breed-linked disease. Buy a breed-only test when the question is curiosity about a mix.

The health-marker panel is the part with a practical use. A breed percentage on its own does not change a vaccine or a diet. Compare the kit that includes health markers with the kit that does not, using the price printed on that product today.

These are one-time kits. You can send a second company's swab later. A breed-only result does not become a health panel. That requires a new test of the kit that includes health markers.
