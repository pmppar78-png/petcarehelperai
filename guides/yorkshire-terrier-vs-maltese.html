Yorkshire Terrier vs Maltese: Side-by-Side

Yorkshire Terrier and Maltese look superficially similar to new owners but differ in ways that matter for daily care. Maltese is larger at 4-7 lbs, while Yorkshire Terrier typically runs 4-7 lbs. That size gap shows up in feeding volume, crate size, vehicle space, and how much joint-stress management each dog needs over their lifetime.

Both breeds share a moderate energy level, so the differentiator here is temperament, not exercise volume. Watch how each individual dog responds to training pressure, novelty, and time alone — that tells you more than the AKC group label.

Lifespan: Yorkshire Terrier typically lives 11-15 yrs; Maltese 12-15 yrs. Maltese generally has the longer-term care window, which affects insurance math and the point at which senior diagnostics become the dominant cost line.

Health watchlists differ. Both breeds share concerns around dental disease, luxating patella, collapsed trachea. These aren’t guaranteed diagnoses — they’re the conditions responsible vets screen for, and they shape insurance underwriting more than most owners realize.
