Compare plans covering Manx syndrome, arthritis, constipation, monthly costs, and coverage recommendations.

Pet Insurance Overview

Pet insurance for Manxs is particularly important given their predisposition to Manx syndrome, arthritis, constipation.

Manx syndrome is a spinal malformation that causes urinary and fecal incontinence and hind-limb trouble. The tailless look is not itself a procedure.

Known Health Risks: Genetic screening data shows Manxs have elevated rates of Manx syndrome, arthritis, constipation.

Manx syndrome is a spinal and nerve defect in tailless Manx cats, and it is the condition that decides whether a policy is worth buying. Ask whether congenital disease and spinal surgery are covered, and enroll before a vet writes a gait problem into the record. A tailless kitten with an already-noted spine defect may be excluded for the only illness you were insuring against.
