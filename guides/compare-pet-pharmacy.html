Best Online Pet Pharmacies Compared

Comparing online pet pharmacies for prescription and OTC medications. Covers 1-800-PetMeds, Chewy Pharmacy, PetCareRx, and pricing.

Detailed Comparison

Chewy Pharmacy1-800-PetMedsPetCareRx
What it isAn online pharmacy that requests the prescription from the clinicAn online pet pharmacy for prescription and over-the-counter productsAn online pharmacy that fills from a veterinarian's prescription
Use it forA refill of the same drug, strength, and count the veterinarian authorizedThe same match of drug, strength, and quantityThe same prescription, if that product is in stock and the pharmacy can ship to your state
Check before you buyThe clinic may require an exam before it authorizes a refillA valid prescription for anything labeled Rx, and a pharmacy license that covers your stateThe concentration. A lower price on a different strength is a different prescription.

Compare the same drug, strength, and quantity. Your veterinarian can refuse to authorize a particular pharmacy, and a new exam is sometimes required before a refill.

Ask the clinic which pharmacies it will authorize before you create an account. The price on the website becomes a fill only after the veterinarian approves that pharmacy.

Common Questions

Which option is the best value?

Value is the prescribed product, at a price you can see before you pay, from a pharmacy licensed to ship it to you. A cheaper listing for a different strength is not the same order.

Can I switch later?

You can send the next refill to a different pharmacy. The clinic has to authorize that pharmacy. Do not create another strength by splitting tablets.
