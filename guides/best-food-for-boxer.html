Nutritional Needs of Boxers

As a large working breed with high energy levels, the Boxer has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Boxers typically weigh 50-80 lbs and need approximately 1,400–2,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. Given their high energy levels, active Boxers may need 20-30% more calories than the average dog of their size.

Health Awareness: Boxers have documented breed-level risk for cancer, heart disease, hip dysplasia.

Wet Food vs Dry Food for Boxers

Both wet and dry food have advantages for Boxers.

Feeding Mistakes to

  • Overuse of treats: Treats should make up no more than 10% of your Boxer's daily caloric intake.
  • Ignoring body condition: Use the body condition score (BCS) system to assess your Boxer's weight rather than relying solely on scale numbers.

Age-Specific Nutrition Considerations

Your Boxer's nutritional needs change significantly throughout their life.

Adult Boxers benefit from a protein content of 22-30%.

Senior stage (7+ years): Older Boxers may need fewer calories but higher-quality protein to maintain muscle mass.
