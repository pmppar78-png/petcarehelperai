Abyssinian Cat Lifespan & Longevity Guide

How long do Abyssinian cats live? Average lifespan of 9-15 yrs, health factors, and tips for maximizing your cats years.

The Abyssinian has an average lifespan of 9-15 yrs. With proper nutrition, exercise, and veterinary care, many Abyssinians live full, healthy lives.

Amyloidosis Risk and Monitoring

Renal amyloidosis — the abnormal deposition of amyloid protein in kidney tissue — is a documented genetic predisposition in Abyssinian cats. Unlike PKD, amyloidosis does not yet have a commercially available genetic test, making clinical monitoring essential. Serial monitoring of urine protein-to-creatinine ratio (UPC) can detect proteinuria before azotemia develops. The condition typically presents in young to middle-aged cats (1-5 years) with progressive renal failure. Abyssinian owners should discuss baseline kidney screening with their veterinarian, including annual bloodwork panels that track SDMA (a more sensitive early marker than creatinine alone) alongside standard renal parameters.
