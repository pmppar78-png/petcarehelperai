What are the most important considerations for how big do rat terriers get?

Rat Terriers vary from about 10 to 25 pounds, and the variety on the puppy paperwork (toy through standard) tells you which crate to buy. A 10-pound adult and a 25-pound adult from different lines need different food volumes. Weigh every month until two weights in a row match, then set the cup and leave it there.
