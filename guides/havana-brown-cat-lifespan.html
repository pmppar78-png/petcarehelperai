The Havana Brown has an average lifespan of 8-13 yrs. With proper nutrition, exercise, and veterinary care, many Havana Browns live full, healthy lives.

The Havana Brown runs about 6-10 lbs at maturity with a typical 8-13 yrs life expectancy; both its health pattern and its temperament are specific enough to deserve deliberate attention.
