Personality Overview

The Flat-Coated Retriever is known for being a high-energy sporting breed with a distinctive personality. Sporting breeds like the Flat-Coated Retriever are typically friendly, eager to please, and excellent with families.

Weighing around 60-70 lbs and lifespan of 8-10 yrs, the Flat-Coated Retriever does best with an owner who understands its breed-specific quirks. Here's a comprehensive look at what you need to consider.
