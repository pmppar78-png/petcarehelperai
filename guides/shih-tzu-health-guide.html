Buy Shih Tzu coverage before the airway and the knees are graded. A brachycephalic exam that already describes stenotic nares, or a patella score already written down, is the pre-existing note. Renal-dysplasia marker tests do not clear a line. Buy the policy without waiting for one. Disc pain that has already happened will not be treated as a new illness.

Shih tzu renal dysplasia has been sold with marker tests that did not hold up as a way to clear a line. Do not pay for a renal-dysplasia 'clear' as if it were PKD in Persians. What you can verify is a brachycephalic airway exam, patellas, an eye exam, and hip films if the breeder did them. Intervertebral disc disease comes from the long back. It is not a cheek-swab result. Ask how the breeder follows kidneys in older dogs, because a normal puppy panel does not answer that.
