Bringing home a Staffordshire Bull Terrier puppy is exciting but requires preparation. Medium breed puppies typically reach full size by 12-15 months.

At 24-38 lbs with a 12-14 yrs lifespan, the Staffordshire Bull Terrier has a health and temperament profile that rewards close attention rather than generic care. What sets the Staffordshire Bull Terrier apart from other terrier breeds is the specific combination of size, drive, and health profile that defines daily life with this dog.

First-Year Health Milestones

Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Staffordshire Bull Terriers are prone to.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Staffordshire Bull Terriers

Regular veterinary visits allow early detection of breed-associated conditions, when treatment is most effective. The recommended schedule for your Staffordshire Bull Terrier. Adjust the schedule based on your vet's advice.

Life StageVisit FrequencyKey Screenings
Puppy (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation
Adult (1-7 years)AnnuallyPhysical exam, dental check, heartworm test, vaccination boosters
Senior (7+ years)Every 6 monthsBlood work, urinalysis, Hip Dysplasia screening, Cataracts screening, Allergies screening

Staffordshire Bull Terriers should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 3-5 years of age or earlier if symptoms appear. Proactive testing tends to pay for itself in avoided complications.
