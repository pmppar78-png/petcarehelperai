Standard Poodles have minimal shedding and require occasional brushing. While Standard Poodles shed very little, regular grooming is still important for skin health and early detection of lumps or skin issues.

Veterinary Care Schedule for Standard Poodles

Preventive care reduces both emergency costs and disease severity over your pet's lifetime. Here is a general framework for your Standard Poodle. Your vet may modify this depending on your pet's history.

Puppy (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation

Standard Poodles should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 1-2 years of age, as large breeds develop structural issues early. Most breed-related conditions respond better to early intervention.
