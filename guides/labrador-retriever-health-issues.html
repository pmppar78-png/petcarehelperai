Labrador Retriever Health Issues

Common health problems in Labrador Retrievers including hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, obesity. Prevention, symptoms to watch for, and treatment options.

Common Health Problems

Labrador Retrievers are predisposed to several health conditions including hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, obesity. Understanding these risks allows you to screen early, prevent where possible, and catch problems before they become emergencies.

Weighing around 55-80 lbs and lifespan of 10-13 yrs, the Labrador Retriever has specific care needs shaped by its genetics and build. The Labrador Retriever's reputation in the sporting group reflects generations of purposeful breeding, resulting in a large dog with predictable but nuanced care requirements.

Health Awareness: Labrador Retrievers carry known breed-associated risks including hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, obesity. A screening schedule tuned to those specific risks — which your vet can outline — is one of the highest-leverage moves you make as an owner, because most of these conditions are easier to treat earlier than later.

Prevention Strategies

Breed-appropriate routines pay for themselves in reduced friction and fewer avoidable issues. For Labrador Retrievers, the inputs that matter most are a large frame, a heavy shedding coat, and breed-level risk for hip dysplasia and elbow dysplasia.

Lifespan Optimization

Breed-aware prevention usually beats reactive treatment on both cost and quality-of-life measures. Watch for early signs of hip dysplasia, maintain regular veterinary visits, and keep your dog at a healthy weight — excess weight worsens most of the conditions Labrador Retrievers are prone to.

Puppy (0-1 year)Every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks, then at 6 and 12 monthsVaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter (consult AVMA guidelines on optimal timing) consultation

Labrador Retrievers should receive breed-specific screening for hip dysplasia starting at 1-2 years of age, as large breeds develop structural issues early. Catching problems early gives you more treatment options and better odds.
