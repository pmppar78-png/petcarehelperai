Labrador Retriever Health Issues

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Common Health Problems

Labradors are prone to hip and elbow dysplasia, so keeping them lean is one of the most useful things an owner can do.

A sour smell in the ear, or a head that keeps shaking, needs a clinic the same day. The same mouth that retrieves will take a sock from the laundry pile or a corn cob from the trash. Vomiting, a quiet dog, or a painful belly after a counter raid needs a clinic the same day, because those objects are common surgical finds in this retrieving breed.

A 2016 paper in Cell Metabolism described a POMC deletion that blunts satiety in Labrador Retrievers, and the same deletion shows up in flat-coated retrievers. An empty bowl is a poor guide to the scoop. The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals accepts a final hip radiograph at 2 years of age, meaning 24 months or later, while a film at 1 year stays preliminary and PennHIP can start at 16 weeks.

Weighing 55-80 lbs, with a lifespan of 11-13 yrs, a Labrador Retriever still wants a job when the walk is over.

Ask whether the parents were cleared of exercise-induced collapse before the puppy comes home.

Genetic Screening

A Labrador Retriever needs a real retrieve or a swim on ordinary days. A short stroll on some days and a marathon on others leaves the dog keyed up at night.

  • Size: large (55-80 lbs)
  • Energy Level: High
  • Shedding: Heavy
  • Lifespan: 11-13 yrs

Prevention Strategies

The heavy chest and the retrieving mouth are the two things that change an ordinary week: measure the cup, and dry the ears after a swim.

When to See the Vet

A young Labrador Retriever who was fetching hard and then folded in the grass should be worked up for exercise-induced collapse until another answer is proven. The mutation sits in DNM1. Between episodes the dog looks ordinary, which is why the diagnosis is easy to doubt. Watch for weakness after intense work plus excitement, followed by a retriever ready to work again later that afternoon. A quiet loop around the block rarely triggers it. A frantic retrieve marathon can trigger that collapse. Shorter retrieves are how owners manage it, and a clinic visit separates this collapse from heat stress or a seizure.

Health Testing

Lifespan Optimization

Hips and elbows are the pair to ask about by name. Dysplasia in either joint shows up as stiffness after rest, a short stride, or a puppy who bunny-hops into the truck. Ask for the parents' hip papers and elbow papers, the kind filed for review, before a limp is written off as a sprain. The prcd form of retinal atrophy is the eye disease to mention with night hesitation: a Labrador Retriever that balks at dusk or misses a curb. Centronuclear myopathy is the puppy who looks weak and awkward, with a poor rise from the floor, long before any athletic career starts.
