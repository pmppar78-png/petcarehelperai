Medical Expenses

Hidden Costs

Hidden Newfie costs are drool on walls and clothes, a food bill sized for a giant, ear infections after swimming, and joint or bloat surgery. Price a gastropexy conversation and a hip film with your own clinic, and buy towels as if they were equipment. A puppy estimate that uses a Labrador's food line will be wrong by the second year.
