What are the most important considerations for golden retriever vs labrador retriever?

Goldens are often 55 to 75 pounds, with longer ear leather that stays wet after a swim. Labs are 55 to 80 pounds, short-coated, and more likely to eat what they find and to head for water. Both should have hips and elbows evaluated in a careful breeding plan. A Lab sheds in seasonal spikes. A Golden needs a brush most weeks. Spend time with adult dogs if the puppy photos look identical.
