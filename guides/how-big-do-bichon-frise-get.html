Bichons finish small, about 12 to 18 pounds, and the powder-puff coat hides both ribs and a round belly. Use a kitchen scale every month through the first year. Book a groom every four to six weeks while the dog is still short, because a mat at 10 pounds is the same salon job as a mat at 16.

Get Pet Care

A 12 to 18 pound dog inside a round white coat

The Bichon Frise card lists 9.5 to 11.5 inches, 12 to 18 pounds, and a lifespan often 14 to 15 years. The powder-puff trim makes a correct dog and a heavy dog look like the same cloud. A kitchen scale, once a month through the first year and after every groom, is the only honest size chart. Small dogs of this weight usually stop adding height well before a herding breed does. What keeps changing is the coat, and a groomer can take an inch off the outline in an afternoon without the dog having lost a pound.

The profile's medical pattern is specific. Calcium-containing bladder stones and other urinary crystals show up often. Blood in the urine or straining that returns needs imaging. Luxating patellas cause a skip in the gait. Teeth crowd in a short muzzle and rot if nobody brushes. Allergies drive ear infections and paw licking under hair that hides the redness until the dog smells. Cataracts are a reason to ask what an ophthalmologist saw in the parents. None of that is a reason to guess a dose at home. It is a reason the first-year visits include a urine check, a kneecap check, and a look in the mouth.

Exercise on the profile is a moderate 30 to 45 minutes of play and walking, which is why an apartment can work. The cheerful, curious temperament is real, and so is door barking. Book a groom every four to six weeks once the adult coat is in, and comb to the skin between visits so mats never sit on the body. A matted coat changes the way you read the ribs. You cannot feel a waist through felted hair, and a shaved recovery groom is how a neglected coat ends, with the skin irritated underneath.

Food portions for a 12 to 18 pound adult are small enough that treats erase them. Count the training bites in the daily cup. A puppy who looks "done" at six months can still be soft through the chest. Keep the growth food until the veterinarian says the dog has leveled off, then hold the weight inside the card's band for a life that often runs 14 to 15 years. Extra pounds on a luxating kneecap are the part of that lifespan you can still change.

Ask the clinic to show you how a normal patella feels and what blood in the urine looks like in a dog this white. Write the adult target weight, somewhere inside 12 to 18 pounds, on the food bin. The coat is the decoration. The scale is the size. Recheck that target after the first adult groom, when the trim has changed the outline and the pounds have not.
