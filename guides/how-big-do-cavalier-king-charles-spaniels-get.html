How Big Do Cavalier King Charles Spaniels Get? Size & Growth Guide

This page is educational reference material for preparing questions. A licensed veterinarian examines the animal, makes the diagnosis, and sets the treatment.

Cavalier King Charles Spaniel full size: 13-18 lbs, small breed. Growth timeline from puppy to adult, weight chart, and when they stop growing.

Full-Grown Size

Food volume keeps the chart honest. Use one cup, one time of day, and a hand on the ribs every Sunday. Feathering can outpace muscle, and a fluffy outline gets praised while the scale climbs past 18. Save the praise for the number. A dog kept between roughly 13 and 18 pounds, with a withers height of 12 to 13 in, is the dog the registry described. Write the Sunday weight in one shared notebook as the withers reading so a fluffy week cannot masquerade as a growth spurt. The cup stays level. Praise waits for the number on the page.

Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are a small breed, reaching 13-18 lbs at full maturity. Small breeds mature faster, typically reaching adult size by 8-12 months.

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel typically weighs 13-18 lbs and lives 9-14 yrs; the breed has its own set of quirks, and outcomes track closely to how well the owner understands them. Let's examine the important details.

Weight Chart by Age

Height and weight both have a printed home. Adult Cavalier King Charles Spaniels stand 12-13 in and weigh 13-18 lb. Feathering gets smoothed, then read height at the scapula, because ear fringe and chest silk make a small spaniel look taller in a photograph than on a wicket. Males and females share that inch band and that pound band. Chasing a separate male column wastes the afternoon.

Male vs Female Size

Much of the height arrives from month 8 to month 12. At the 8-month mark the head can look finished while the chest is still narrow. By month 12 a lot of the height is set, and what remains is rib spring, muscle, and feather. Weigh again past the 9-month mark and past month 14. Month 9 catches a puppy racing ahead of a destination set at 13-18 lb. Month 14 catches the late fill that softens a lean yearling if the bowl never changed.

Factors Affecting Size

Mental stimulation is as important as physical exercise for Cavalier King Charles Spaniel. Boredom is the root cause of most destructive behavior — not disobedience. Puzzle feeders, scent work, and novel experiences challenge your Cavalier King Charles Spaniel's mind in ways that a standard walk cannot. Change up the routine regularly: the same toys and the same routes lose their enrichment value quickly.

When They Stop Growing

The opening year should land near those adult figures. Feel ribs through the silk. A spaniel who looks big-boned past the peak of the range, say at nearly 20 pounds, has left the 18-pound end, and the lap-dog frame carries that mass into the years when mitral valve disease is already the screening priority. Condition gets another look in year 3, then a fresh look once year 5 arrives, when feathering is heavy and eyes lie about the waist.
