Local Vet & Care Considerations

What a typical year of care costs for Bombay Cat Cost Of Ownership depends heavily on where you live. Dental cleanings are the single most region-sensitive line item, ranging from $250 to well over $900 depending on anesthesia protocols and local wage costs. In humid coastal regions, expect a stronger emphasis on parasite prevention year-round; colder inland areas often shift that spending toward joint and cold-weather care instead.
