Where can I learn more?

Spring itch in a dog is examined for fleas, mites, and infection before it is called a pollen allergy. Facial swelling or hives the same day as a walk is a visit now.

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

Itching that starts every spring should be on the exam list before the season opens. By July the skin is often already infected. The clinic can separate fleas, pollen, and a food allergy. Recheck if the itch is uncontrolled after the plan starts. Cytology of a hot spot is a short visit and worth doing early.

How can I save money on pet care?

Several shampoos, sprays, and grocery diets bought for spring itch, before an exam, usually miss fleas, mites, or a skin infection. One visit that identifies the cause replaces that basket. Itch medicine, if the veterinarian prescribes it, has a follow-up. The veterinarian chooses the drug and the dose.
