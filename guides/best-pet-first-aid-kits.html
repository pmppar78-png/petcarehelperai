Where can I learn more?

The American Red Cross cat and dog first aid materials describe what a handler can do on the way to a clinic. Merck Veterinary Manual emergency chapters cover the conditions a kit cannot finish: trouble breathing, a seizure that continues, a bloated abdomen, and a blocked male cat. The pouch list is not those chapters.

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

A kit is for the minutes between home and a clinic, for example pressure on a bleeding nail on the way in. Puppies and kittens are still examined every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks. Adult dogs and cats are examined about once a year, and senior dogs, often from about 7 years and earlier in giant breeds, and senior cats from about 10, are commonly examined every six months; the kit only covers the trip to the clinic. Trouble breathing, a seizure that does not stop, a bloated abdomen, or a male cat who cannot urinate is the emergency hospital.
