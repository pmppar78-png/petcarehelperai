Nutritional Needs of Rhodesian Ridgebacks

As a large hound breed with moderate energy levels, the Rhodesian Ridgeback has specific nutritional requirements that differ from other dogs.

Rhodesian Ridgebacks typically weigh 70-85 lbs and need approximately 1,400–2,200 calories per day, depending on age, activity level, and metabolism. With moderate energy levels, most Rhodesian Ridgebacks do well on standard feeding guidelines for their size.

Health Predisposition Summary: Rhodesian Ridgebacks show higher-than-average incidence of hip dysplasia, dermoid sinus, hypothyroidism based on breed health database data.
