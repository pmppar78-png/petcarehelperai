How Big Do Norwegian Elkhounds Get? Size & Growth Guide

Full-Grown Size

Norwegian Elkhounds are a medium breed, reaching 48-55 lbs at full maturity. Medium breeds generally reach full size between 10-16 months.

Weighing around 48-55 lbs and lifespan of 12-15 yrs, the Norwegian Elkhound requires attention to its unique health and temperament characteristics. We cover the essential details in the sections that follow.

Early intervention consistently produces better outcomes and lower costs than reactive treatment for breed-associated conditions.
