Breed and species photographs on this site are self-hosted copies of freely licensed files. A photo was kept only when the file title, description, or categories identified that species or breed. Look-alike animals, stamps, maps, and files that showed a different subject stayed off the page.

Wikimedia Commons was searched first. Research-grade iNaturalist observations licensed CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA were checked when Commons had no file we could identify. Flickr, Pexels, and Unsplash were not queried, because no API key was available for those sites.

665 photographs are self-hosted as WebP, with a large image up to 960 pixels wide and a 250 pixel thumbnail. 17 profiles still use the neutral “Photo coming soon” illustration. Those profiles are listed after the credit tables.
