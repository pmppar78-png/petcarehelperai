    When to Seek Emergency Care

    Go to an emergency vet immediately if your pet shows: Understanding how this applies specifically to your pet helps you avoid common pitfalls. Owners who take the time to research this aspect typically report better outcomes and fewer surprises.

    • Difficulty breathing or blue/pale gums
    • Inability to urinate (especially male cats)
    • Bloated abdomen (dogs) - may indicate GDV/bloat
    • Seizures lasting more than 2-3 minutes or multiple seizures
    • Collapse or inability to stand
    • Severe bleeding that won't stop
    • Known toxin ingestion
    • Major trauma (hit by car, fall from height)
