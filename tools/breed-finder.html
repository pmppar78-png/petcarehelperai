How Our Breed Matcher Works

Our algorithm considers multiple factors to find breeds that match your lifestyle:

  • Living Space: Some breeds need room to run, others thrive in apartments
  • Activity Level: Matching dog energy with owner energy prevents frustration
  • Experience: Some breeds need experienced handlers for their strong personalities
  • Family Situation: Child-friendly breeds are essential for families
  • Time Availability: Some breeds need constant companionship
  • Grooming Needs: Coat maintenance varies dramatically between breeds
