How the Calculator Works

This calculator uses the Resting Energy Requirement (RER) formula, which is the standard veterinary method for calculating pet caloric needs:

  • RER = 70 × (body weight in kg)^0.75
  • This base value is then multiplied by factors for age, activity, body condition, and neutering status
  • Puppies and kittens need more calories (up to 2-3x RER)
  • Senior pets typically need fewer calories
  • Active working dogs may need significantly more

Important Feeding Tips

For Dogs

  • Divide meals: Feed adult dogs 2 meals per day, puppies 3-4 meals
  • Monitor weight: Weigh monthly and adjust portions as needed
  • Treats count: Treats should be no more than 10% of daily calories
  • Fresh water: Always provide clean, fresh water
  • Transition slowly: Change foods gradually over 7-10 days

For Cats

  • Multiple small meals: Cats do better with 3-4 small meals or puzzle feeders
  • Wet food benefits: Helps with hydration, important for urinary health
  • Don't free-feed: Measured portions prevent obesity
  • Variety: Rotating proteins can prevent food allergies

When to Consult a Veterinarian

While this calculator provides a good starting point, consult your vet if:

  • Your pet has specific health conditions (diabetes, kidney disease, allergies)
  • Your pet needs to lose or gain significant weight
  • You're feeding a prescription diet
  • Your pet is pregnant or nursing
  • Your pet has unusual appetite changes
