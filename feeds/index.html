For Content Republishers & Syndicators

If you're republishing our content on your website, blog, or platform, please follow these technical requirements to ensure proper SEO attribution and avoid duplicate content issues.

Required: Canonical Tag Implementation

Add a canonical tag in the <head> section of your republished page pointing to our original URL:

<link rel="canonical" href="https://petcarehelperai.com/guides/[article-path]">

Example: If republishing our Dog Training Guide:

<link rel="canonical" href="https://petcarehelperai.com/guides/dog-training">

Attribution Format

Include visible attribution at the beginning or end of the republished content:

<p>Originally published by <a href="https://petcarehelperai.com/">Pet Care Helper AI</a>.
Republished with permission.</p>

RSS Feed Integration

Our feeds include all necessary metadata for automated syndication:

  • guid: Permanent article identifier (use as canonical URL)
  • link: Original article URL for linking
  • description: Article summary for previews
  • content:encoded: Extended content with HTML formatting
  • category: Topic tags for content organization
  • pubDate: Original publication date
  • dc:creator: Content author/source

Feed Specifications

Technical details for developers and system integrators:

  • Format: RSS 2.0 with Atom, Content, and Dublin Core extensions
  • Encoding: UTF-8
  • Update Frequency: New content added regularly; feeds refreshed daily
  • TTL: 1440 minutes (24 hours recommended cache)
  • Namespaces:
    • xmlns:atom - Atom syndication extensions
    • xmlns:content - Content module for full article text
    • xmlns:dc - Dublin Core for authorship metadata
