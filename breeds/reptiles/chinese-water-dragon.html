Chinese water dragon questions

Why is my water dragon's nose raw?

They run the glass when they can see reflections or too much room beyond the tank. Cover three sides, add cover inside, and treat a raw nose as an injury. It gets infected if you wait.

How big a cage does an adult need?

Give them at least 6 by 3 by 6 feet for adults. A 40-gallon tank is a baby cage. Water to swim in and branches above it are both required.

Are they beginner lizards?

Babies look easy in a shop. Adults need space, humidity of 70 to 80 percent, and a vet who understands metabolic bone disease.

Humidity of 70 to 80 percent comes from the pool, misting, and live plants, with enough airflow that the glass does not stay fogged all day. A large water area they can submerge in, filtered and changed, is part of the heat plan as well as the swimming plan.

Insects first, and the jaw

The diet is omnivore and primarily insects. Gut-loaded roaches and crickets, dusted with calcium, are the core. Vegetables and a little fruit can be offered to animals that take them. Feed on a schedule and remove leftovers from the water so the pool stays a swimming place. Salmonella precautions are ordinary handwashing. A skittish dragon that is handled constantly spends its day hiding at the bottom of the cage.

Metabolic bone disease is the health watch-item because this species basks. A soft jaw, a swollen limb, or tremors when climbing means the UVB or the calcium failed. A lizard that stops eating insects while the basking branch still reads near 95°F needs a reptile veterinarian. Respiratory noise can follow a cold, wet cage with no warm branch, so the thermostat and the UVB stay on the same checklist as the filter. Build the 6 by 3 by 6 foot space first. A juvenile in a short tank learns to rub its nose on the lid and then arrives at adult size with a scar.
