Fire-Bellied Toad Overview

The Fire-Bellied Toad is a small (2 in) amphibian known for being active, colorful. With a lifespan of 10-15 years, this species is a rewarding pet for committed keepers. As a beginner-level species, the Fire-Bellied Toad is an excellent choice for newcomers to reptile and amphibian keeping.

Their diet of small insects requires a supply of live or prepared insects. Temperature requirements of 68-77°F make a cool, controlled environment essential for their wellbeing.

Enclosure Setup & Requirements

Enclosure Size

  • Minimum: 10+ gal semi-aquatic
  • Type: Terrarium with appropriate substrate and water features.
  • Security: Secure lid to prevent escapes.

Environmental Requirements

  • Temperature: 68-77°F - use a quality thermostat with every heat source.
  • Humidity: 70-80% - monitor with a digital hygrometer.
  • Lighting: Low-level lighting; many species are sensitive to bright light.
  • Substrate: Coconut fiber, sphagnum moss, or ABG mix.
  • Hides & Decor: Plants, cork bark, and water features.

Diet & Feeding

  • Primary Diet: Small insects.
  • Feeding Schedule: Every 1-3 days depending on species size and metabolism.
  • Supplements: Calcium with D3 and multivitamin dusting on feeder items.
  • Water: Fresh water available at all times in an appropriately sized dish.

Common Health Issues

  • Skin Infections: Bacterial or fungal infections from poor water quality.
  • Parasites: Both internal and external parasites can affect Fire-Bellied Toads. Annual fecal exams recommended.
  • Chytrid Fungus: A serious fungal disease; maintain clean conditions and quarantine new animals.
  • Stress: Fire-Bellied Toads can become stressed from improper husbandry, handling, or enclosure placement. Ensure proper setup and gentle interaction.

Veterinary Care

Find a reptile/exotic vet before you need one. Many health issues in amphibians are caused by husbandry problems. Regular checkups and fecal testing help prevent serious issues with your Fire-Bellied Toad.

Handling & Temperament

  • Temperament: Active, Colorful.
  • Handling: Minimize handling - amphibians have permeable skin and can absorb oils and chemicals from your hands. Wet hands or gloves recommended.
  • Acclimation: Allow 1-2 weeks to settle in before handling.

Is This Amphibian Right for You?

Fire-Bellied Toads Are Great For:

  • First-time reptile/amphibian keepers
  • Keepers committed to proper long-term care
  • Those who can provide proper temperature and humidity control
  • People who can provide live or prepared food consistently

Fire-Bellied Toads May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Those unable to maintain proper environmental conditions
  • People wanting a completely hands-off pet
  • Those wanting a pet to handle frequently
