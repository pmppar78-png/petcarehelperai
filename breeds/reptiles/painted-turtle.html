Painted turtle questions

How big is a painted turtle tank?

55 gallons or more. A hatchling in a 10-gallon bowl will outgrow it. Add swimming room, a filter rated well above the tank, and a dock that dries completely.

Can I sell or buy a tiny painted turtle?

In the United States, turtles with a shell under 4 inches generally cannot be sold as pets. The rule exists because small turtles spread Salmonella.

Do painted turtles need a heat lamp?

Yes. Water around 75 to 82°F, a basking spot near 90°F, and UVB are what the shell needs. A lamp-free tank grows soft shells.
