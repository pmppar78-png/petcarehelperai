Mud Turtle Habitat Essentials

A ramp, a UVB lamp over a dry rest area, and a canister or hang-on filter are the gear that keeps a 3 to 5 inch mud turtle in 75 to 82°F water. Gasping at the surface or a crooked float is a respiratory sign for a veterinarian who treats turtles.

Food on the bottom

They eat pellets, insects, and worms. Feed modestly. A fat mud turtle stuffed into its shell is overfed. Calcium and a varied week beat a single tin of shrimp. Hatchlings under 4 inches fall under the US sales limit tied to Salmonella. Wash after every water change.

They can bite. They also climb. A tight lid saves a turtle from the hallway. Swollen eyes or a refusal to eat belongs with a chelonian vet. The ARAV list is the practical way to find one.

Mud turtle questions

What is the difference between a mud turtle and a musk turtle?

Mud turtles are usually Kinosternon, with a hinge that can close the shell more than a musk turtle can. Musk turtles are Sternotherus. Both are small bottom-walkers with long lives.

Do mud turtles stay in the water?

Mostly, but they wander on land more than people expect, including nesting females. A secure lid and a land spot both matter.

Are mud turtles easy?

Care is beginner. They are hardy when the water is clean and the tank offers shallow water, a ramp, and a place to rest. They still live 30 to 50 years.
