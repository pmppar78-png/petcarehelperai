African Clawed Frog Overview

The African Clawed Frog is a medium (4-5 in) amphibian known for being peaceful, hardy. With a lifespan of 15-30 years, this species is a long-term companion requiring years of dedicated care. As a beginner-level species, the African Clawed Frog is an excellent choice for newcomers to reptile and amphibian keeping.

Their diet of pellets, bloodworms is straightforward and readily available. Temperature requirements of 68-77°F make a cool, controlled environment essential for their wellbeing.

Enclosure Setup & Requirements

Enclosure Size

  • Minimum: 10+ gal aquatic
  • Type: Aquatic or semi-aquatic setup with filtration.
  • Security: Secure lid to prevent escapes.

Environmental Requirements

  • Temperature: 68-77°F - use a quality thermostat with every heat source.
  • Water Quality: Clean, dechlorinated water with appropriate filtration.
  • Lighting: Low-level lighting; many species are sensitive to bright light.
  • Substrate: Smooth gravel or bare-bottom for easy cleaning.
  • Hides & Decor: Plants, cork bark, and water features.

Diet & Feeding

  • Primary Diet: Pellets, bloodworms.
  • Feeding Schedule: Every 1-3 days depending on species size and metabolism.
  • Supplements: Calcium with D3 and multivitamin dusting on feeder items.
  • Water: Clean, filtered water is the habitat itself - maintain with regular water changes.

Common Health Issues

  • Skin Infections: Bacterial or fungal infections from poor water quality.
  • Parasites: Both internal and external parasites can affect African Clawed Frogs. Annual fecal exams recommended.
  • Chytrid Fungus: A serious fungal disease; maintain clean conditions and quarantine new animals.
  • Stress: African Clawed Frogs can become stressed from improper husbandry, handling, or enclosure placement. Ensure proper setup and gentle interaction.

Veterinary Care

Find a reptile/exotic vet before you need one. Many health issues in amphibians are caused by husbandry problems. Regular checkups and fecal testing help prevent serious issues with your African Clawed Frog.

Handling & Temperament

  • Temperament: Peaceful, Hardy.
  • Handling: Minimize handling - amphibians have permeable skin and can absorb oils and chemicals from your hands. Wet hands or gloves recommended.
  • Acclimation: Allow 1-2 weeks to settle in before handling.

Is This Amphibian Right for You?

African Clawed Frogs Are Great For:

  • First-time reptile/amphibian keepers
  • Keepers committed to proper long-term care
  • Those who can provide proper aquatic habitat and filtration
  • People who can provide live or prepared food consistently

African Clawed Frogs May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Those unable to maintain proper environmental conditions
  • People wanting a completely hands-off pet
  • Those wanting a pet to handle frequently
