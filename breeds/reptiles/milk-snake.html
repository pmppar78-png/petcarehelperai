Red bands, and a kingsnake appetite

Milk snakes are the tricolor Lampropeltis that used to sit in one species and are now split into several. A typical adult is 2 to 5 feet, because a Pueblan and a large Honduran are not the same length. Lifespan runs about 15 to 20 years for this Medium (2-5 ft) animal at 72-85°F in a 20-40 gal enclosure. Care level is beginner for the common captive forms: mice, a thermostat, a secure tub.

Keep the warm side near 72 to 85°F and humidity around 40 to 60 percent. A moist hide fixes most sheds. Twenty to forty gallons is a fair cage, and a 5-foot animal needs the long one. They climb. Clip the lid. Keep a single snake. The instinct to eat other snakes does not go away because the meal is a mouse.

Know the species you paid for

Honduran, Pueblan, Sinaloan, and Nelson's milk snakes are the usual captive names. Eastern milk snakes from the United States are less common as well-started pets and are protected in places. Buy captive-bred and keep the label. Babies are nervous and musk. Adults settle if you support the body and skip handling on feeding day.

Stuck eye caps and a snake that loses weight while refusing food need a reptile vet. The directory is ARAV.

Milk snake questions

Which milk snake do shops sell?

Usually a Central American form such as a Honduran or Pueblan milk snake. The eastern milk snake is a different, often protected animal. Ask for the species, not only the red color.

Do milk snakes need other snakes as food?

No. Captive milk snakes eat mice. They will still eat a snake that shares the cage. House them alone.

Are the bands a coral snake warning?

The pattern mimics coral snakes. A captive-bred milk snake from a known species is a harmless rodent eater wearing a coral-snake pattern. Wild identification is a different problem. Do not catch tricolor snakes.

Colorful, calm, and kept singly

Milk snakes are Lampropeltis in the 2 to 5 foot range, living about 15 to 20 years. Care is beginner. Temperament is docile and colorful. A captive-bred milk snake from a known species is a harmless rodent eater in the kingsnake group. Wild identification of tricolor snakes is a different problem, and wild snakes should stay outside. The enclosure is 20 to 40 gallons for adults, scaled to the species, with a secure lid. They eat other snakes. One animal per cage..

Hold 72 to 85°F on a thermostat, warm hide at the top of the band, cool hide at the bottom. Humidity of 40 to 60 percent, plus a moist box during blue eyes, supports a clean shed. Skip a desert basking layout. These are terrestrial colubrids. Measure the hides, because a lamp on the screen can leave the snake cold underneath while the air at the lid looks warm.
