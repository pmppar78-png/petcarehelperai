Dig them up rarely

Constant excavation stresses them and collapses the burrows they just built. Check weight every so often by offering food at the entrance rather than by a daily grab. Captive-bred skinks exist and settle better than imports. House them alone unless you have a large tub and experience. Two animals in a small tank is a fight you will not see until a tail is chewed.

Fire skink questions

Why don't I see my fire skink?

They burrow. A visible fire skink all day may be a skink with nowhere to dig. Judge health by weight, sheds, and fresh droppings, not by a daily photo.

What substrate do they need?

Several inches of a soil mix that is damp in the lower layer and drier on top. Hold 60 to 70 percent humidity. Swamp mud is too wet. Desert sand is too dry.

Do they bite?

They can. They are shy, not aggressive. Most bites happen when a hand digs them up. Scoop substrate from below instead of grabbing.
