Rankin's dragon questions

Are Rankin's dragons just small bearded dragons?

They are a different species, Pogona henrylawsoni. Care looks similar: UVB, heat, insects, greens. Adult size is about 10 to 12 inches, and plan around a 6 to 10 year lifespan.

Can they live in a group?

Sometimes, if the cage is large, the animals are similar in size, and there is more than one basking spot. A bully still has to be removed. Watch tails and food.
