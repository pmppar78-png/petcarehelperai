Woma python questions

Why did my woma bite me at feeding time?

Womas hunt by smell and touch. They have no heat pits, and they often miss. Use long tongs, and keep hands out of the strike path. The bite is a feeding error more than a mood.

Do woma pythons burrow?

Yes. Deep, dry substrate is part of the setup.

Are they beginner snakes?

Care is beginner to intermediate. Size and care are manageable. The food response is the part that surprises new keepers.
