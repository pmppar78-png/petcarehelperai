Display first

Feed with tongs. Clean the cage by moving the gecko into a secure container with a cup or by letting it stay in its cork while you work. Free handling is how keepers get a lizard locked onto a thumb. Captive-bred tokays exist and are less frantic than imports. Wild-caught groups are a parasite and a bite problem together. Do not release them. Introduced tokays are already a nuisance in places far from their original range.

A bite that breaks skin should be washed. A gecko that stops eating or cannot cling to glass needs a reptile vet. ARAV is the directory.

Tokay gecko questions

Do tokay geckos tame down?

A few captive-bred individuals tolerate a hand. Most do not. They are aggressive, defensive, and vocal. Believe that before you buy one for a child.

How bad is the bite?

They bite and hold. The jaws are strong for a lizard this size. Peel them off carefully. Do not shake. The lesson is to stop handling, not to try again tonight.

Why do they yell?

The to-kay call is how they got the name. It is loud, it is often at night, and the call is the species doing what tokays do.

Insects and a loud shed

Gut-load the insects and dust calcium. A tokay that only gets occasional crickets will thin out inside the cork and still bark. Weigh the animal when you can do it safely, or track droppings and body fill at the tube entrance. Handling is a specialist job. Most tokays are display animals with a bite. Captive-bred babies tame better than wild adults, and even those babies can revert to defensive as they mature.

A finished Tokay Gecko shed includes the eye caps and the tail tip when the moist hide is used at 80-90°F and humidity 60 to 80 percent. Stuck skin in a 60 to 80 percent cage usually means the misting never reached the tube the gecko actually uses, or the cage dried for weeks. A swollen toe needs a reptile veterinarian. Respiratory noise in a cold, stale, wet tank is the other failure. Ten to twenty years of a vocal 10 to 14 inch gecko is a vertical cage at 80 to 90°F, a lid that locks, and neighbors who can hear the call.
