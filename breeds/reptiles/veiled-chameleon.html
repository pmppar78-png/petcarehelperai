Veiled chameleon questions

How long do veiled chameleons live?

Males more often reach the long end. Females can die younger, especially if they retain eggs. Buy the animal knowing the years are short.

Can two veiled chameleons share a cage?

No. They are territorial. A pair in one screen cage is a fight you will notice after one animal is on the floor, dark, and thin.

Do they need a glass tank?

A tall screen cage, about 2 by 2 by 4 feet for an adult, moves air. A glass box holds stale humidity and is a common way these lizards get respiratory infections.
