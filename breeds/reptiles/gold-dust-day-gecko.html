Gold dust day gecko questions

Can I hold a gold dust day gecko?

No. They are display-only. The skin tears if you grab them. Herd them into a cup for cage moves.

Do they need UVB?

Yes. They are day-active. UVB plus calcium-dusted insects and a fruit puree is the diet pattern that keeps bones solid.

Are they legal everywhere?

They are established as an introduced lizard in Hawaii and a few other warm spots. Releasing one is how that happens. Check local rules before you buy, and never dump a pet.
