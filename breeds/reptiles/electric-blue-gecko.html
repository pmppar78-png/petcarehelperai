Diet & Nutrition

Most Electric Blue Day Gecko (Lygodactylus williamsi) thrive on a diet of appropriately sized insects (crickets, dubia roaches, mealworms) dusted with calcium and vitamin D3 supplements. Some species also accept fruit-based diets or commercial gecko food.

Nutrition for Electric Blue Day Gecko (Lygodactylus williamsi) is inseparable from the broader environmental context in which feeding occurs. Feeding your Electric Blue Day Gecko (Lygodactylus williamsi) without ensuring access to appropriate post-feeding temperatures can result in food decomposing in the digestive tract rather than being properly processed—a potentially serious and even life-threatening situation.

Supplementation protocols for Electric Blue Day Gecko (Lygodactylus williamsi) address nutritional gaps that are nearly impossible to fill through diet alone in captive conditions. The specific supplementation schedule depends on the species, age, reproductive status, and UVB exposure of your individual Electric Blue Day Gecko (Lygodactylus williamsi). Multivitamin supplements are typically provided less frequently to avoid hypervitaminosis.
