Emerald tree boa questions

Do emerald tree boas like to be held?

No. They are defensive, and they are a display animal. Long teeth and a bad attitude toward hands are normal. Watch them on the perch.

What is the cage shape?

Taller than it is wide, with horizontal perches the snake can drape over. Heat from above has to be guarded so the snake cannot burn its belly on a lamp.

How long do they live?

About 15 to 25 years. Advanced care for that whole span, or the snake fails earlier.
