Map turtle questions

Why is one map turtle huge and the other tiny?

Females of many Graptemys species are several times the bulk of males. A pair sold as matching babies will not stay matching, and adults should not be surprised by that.

Are map turtles a beginner turtle?

Care is intermediate. They are active, they bask, and they foul the water. Some species are also protected and should not be wild-caught.

What do map turtles eat?

Pellets, insects, and vegetables. Several species lean toward snails and other hard prey. Offer calcium. Skip a diet of only feeder goldfish.
