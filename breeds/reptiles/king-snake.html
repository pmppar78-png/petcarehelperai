King snake questions

The page covers the genus. California kings, Mexican black kings, milk snakes, and Florida kings do not share one adult size. 3 to 6 feet is the spread across the group.

Can two king snakes live together?

No. They eat snakes. A shared cage is a feeding plan, a one-snake rule with teeth behind it.

Are king snakes beginners?

Many of the common captive species are, and care is beginner. A secure lid and a thawed-mouse diet are the skills. The lifespan is still 15 to 25 years.
