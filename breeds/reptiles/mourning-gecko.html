Mourning gecko questions

Do mourning geckos need a male?

No. They are parthenogenetic. Females lay fertile eggs without a male. A starter pair is already a future crowd.

Can they live together?

Yes. They are communal, which is unusual for geckos. Give lots of cover, several feeding spots, and a plan for the babies.

What do they eat?

Small insects and fruit puree or a crested-gecko diet. Calcium matters, especially once eggs start showing up in the cork.
