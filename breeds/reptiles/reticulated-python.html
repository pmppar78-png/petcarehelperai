The longest snake people try to keep

Reticulated pythons, Malayopython reticulatus, are the length record holders in the pet trade. Mainland animals are often 10 to 20 feet or more. That length comes with a 15 to 25 year lifespan, and the care is expert. Island localities sold as dwarfs or super dwarfs stay shorter and are still powerful constrictors. A seller who will not name the locality is selling you a surprise.

They are injurious wildlife under US federal rules, in the same large-constrictor category as Burmese pythons. Import is still illegal, and so is shipping them to Hawaii, the District of Columbia, or U.S. territories. After USARK v. Zinke in 2017, federal law does not forbid transport between states in the lower 48. Some states ban keeping them at all. A cage of 8 by 4 by 4 feet is not an adult mainland cage. An adult needs a locked room, a temperature gradient of about 78 to 92°F, and humidity around 60 to 70 percent.

Intelligence cuts both ways

Retics learn routines. They also learn that the cage door means food. That is the unpredictable part. Feed with a plan, tongs, and your hands out of the way. Never alone with a large adult. Never around the neck. A rabbit-sized meal is a housing and a safety problem of its own.

Have a reptile vet before the animal is huge. ARAV lists clinics. This is an expert animal for a keeper who already has a giant-snake plan.

Reticulated python questions

What does dwarf reticulated python mean?

Island and super-dwarf localities stay smaller than mainland retics, which often pass 15 feet and can pass 20. Dwarf is still a large, strong snake. Ask which locality the parents were.

Are reticulated pythons legal to transport?

Import is still banned, and so is shipment to Hawaii, the District of Columbia, or U.S. territories. After USARK v. Zinke in 2017, federal law does not ban transport between states in the lower 48. Possession rules still vary by state and city.

Are they predictable pets?

They are intelligent and sometimes unpredictable. A calm juvenile can be a dangerous adult. Expert care includes a second handler and a cage you cannot improvise.

Expert heat for a very long snake

Reticulated pythons are among the longest snakes people try to keep, about 10 to 20 feet or more, living roughly 15 to 25 years. Care is expert. Temperament is intelligent and can be unpredictable. The enclosure minimum is 8 by 4 by 4 feet, and large adults need more than that minimum. Locks, frames, and the room itself have to survive an animal that can move furniture. Check the law. Many places restrict giant pythons. A hatchling's price is a poor guide to the adult project.

Hold 78 to 92°F with every heat source on a thermostat. Measure warm and cool zones, because a room-sized cage grows cold corners. Guard lamps. Humidity of 60 to 70 percent supports shedding and should come with ventilation. A large soak tub will be emptied as a chore. Use a low tube as daylight for Reticulated Python if you want a photoperiod. The temperature job stays 78-92°F across 15 to 25 years. A desert basking chart does not fit a giant python. Stay inside the stated band.

Large prey and breathing

The diet is large prey items, sized to the snake, with long intervals for big adults. Feed so the snake does not learn that a person is food. Tongs, a consistent place, and a second adult in the room are the minimum once the animal is large. Intelligence cuts both ways. A retic can habituate to a routine and can also ambush a careless entry. One person should not be the only handler.

Labored breathing in a Reticulated Python belongs with a reptile veterinarian, along with the hygrometer reading beside 78-92°F across 15 to 25 years. Mucus, bubbling, or open-mouth breathing needs a reptile veterinarian who has agreed to see an animal this size. Transport is a planned bin and a helper. Incomplete sheds mean the 60 to 70 percent humidity failed. Have the clinic relationship before the snake is huge. Fifteen to twenty-five years of a 10 to 20 foot python is a housing, legal, and safety decision, with 78 to 92°F on a thermostat in a cage that already locks.
