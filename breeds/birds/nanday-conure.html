Nanday Conure Overview

The Nanday Conure, also known as the Black-Hooded Parakeet or Nanday Parakeet, is a striking parrot recognized by its distinctive black head contrasting with bright green body and blue flight feathers. Native to the Pantanal region of South America, Nandays have established feral populations in several US states including Florida, California, and Texas.

Nandays are intelligent, social birds that can make wonderful companions. They have better talking ability than many conures, with some learning extensive vocabularies. However, they are notorious for being among the loudest conures, with piercing screams that can be heard from great distances. This noise level makes them suitable only for those who can accommodate it.

Critical Noise Warning

Nanday Conures are one of the loudest parrot species relative to their size. Their piercing calls can be painfully loud and carry over long distances. They are absolutely unsuitable for apartments, condos, or homes with close neighbors.

Natural Habitat

Nanday Conures originate from:

  • Range: Southeast Bolivia, southwest Brazil, Paraguay, northern Argentina
  • Habitat: Pantanal wetlands, palm groves, savanna woodlands
  • Feral Populations: Established in Florida, California, Texas, and elsewhere
  • Social Groups: Live in large, noisy flocks of 10-50+ birds
  • Conservation: Least Concern in native range; thriving in captivity

Temperament & Personality

Nanday Conures have distinctive personalities:

  • Intelligent: Among the smarter conure species; quick learners
  • Affectionate: Form strong bonds and enjoy cuddling
  • Social: Thrive on interaction; can become depressed if neglected
  • Playful: Energetic and entertaining
  • Bold: Confident, not easily intimidated
  • Vocal: Love to "talk" and make noise
  • Nippy: Can be beaky; need consistent training
  • Loyal: Devoted to their owners

Housing Requirements

Nandays need appropriate housing:

  • Minimum Cage: 24"W x 24"D x 30"H
  • Bar Spacing: 3/4 to 1 inch
  • Material: Sturdy powder-coated or stainless steel
  • Perches: Natural wood perches of varying diameters
  • Toys: Plenty of chewing and foraging toys
  • Play Stand: Essential for out-of-cage time
  • Location: Away from kitchen but in social area

Diet & Nutrition

Balanced nutrition for Nanday health:

  • Pellets (50-60%): High-quality conure/parrot pellets
  • Vegetables (25-30%): Dark leafy greens, carrots, peppers, broccoli
  • Fruits (10-15%): Berries, apple, grape, mango
  • Seeds/Nuts (5%): Limited; as treats only
  • Fresh Water: Changed daily

Foods to Avoid

  • Avocado (toxic)
  • Chocolate and caffeine
  • Alcohol
  • Fruit pits and apple seeds
  • Onions and garlic
  • High-salt foods

Common Health Issues

Nanday Conures may experience:

  • Conure Bleeding Syndrome: Vitamin K deficiency causing abnormal bleeding
  • Proventricular Dilatation Disease: Viral disease
  • Psittacine Beak and Feather Disease: Viral infection
  • Feather Plucking: Often stress-related
  • Respiratory Infections: From poor air quality
  • Self-Mutilation: In severe stress cases
  • Obesity: If diet is seed-heavy

Training

Nandays respond well to training:

  • Step-Up: Foundation command; usually learned quickly
  • Positive Reinforcement: Very effective with treats and praise
  • Talking: Can learn many words and phrases with practice
  • Trick Training: Intelligent enough for complex tricks
  • Bite Prevention: Address nipping consistently
  • Socialization: Important for well-adjusted behavior

Noise & Vocalization

Nandays are extremely vocal:

  • Volume: Among the loudest conures; can cause hearing discomfort
  • Frequency: Multiple screaming sessions throughout the day
  • Flock Calls: Natural behavior; will call to locate family
  • Talking: Better ability than Sun Conures; can develop vocabulary
  • Management: Cannot eliminate screaming; part of their nature
  • Living Situation: Must have detached home or rural property

Compatibility

Household considerations:

  • Children: Can be good with respectful older children
  • Other Pets: Supervision required
  • Other Birds: May do well with other conures
  • Family Bonding: Can bond with multiple family members
  • Time Requirements: 2-4 hours daily interaction
  • Noise Acceptance: All household members must accept the volume

Is a Nanday Conure Right for You?

Ideal Owners Have:

  • Detached home with no close neighbors
  • Very high noise tolerance
  • Time for daily interaction and training
  • Interest in a talking bird
  • Long-term commitment (20-30 years)
  • Patience for training challenges

Nanday Conures May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Anyone in attached housing
  • Noise-sensitive individuals
  • Those seeking a quiet companion
  • First-time bird owners
  • Homes with young children

Related Species

Consider these related species:

  • Green-Cheek Conure - Much quieter alternative
  • Sun Conure - Similar noise level, more colorful
  • Jenday Conure - Similar characteristics
  • Peach-Faced Lovebird - Smaller, less noise
