Parrotlet questions

Are parrotlets good for a child?

No. They bite, they are quick, and care is intermediate. A child can watch. An adult keeps the bird.

Which parrotlet is the pet?

Usually a Pacific parrotlet, one of several Forpus species. This page covers the genus at 4.5 to 5.5 inches. Confirm the species, because care overlaps and the birds are still different.

Do they talk?

Some do. Talking is moderate. The voice is big for the body, and so is the bite.

Blue marks the male Pacific parrotlet

The usual pet is Forpus coelestis, the Pacific parrotlet, one of seven Forpus species from . Males show blue on the rump and the wings, and females are green in those places, so the sex is often visible without a laboratory test. Adults are about 4.5 to 5.5 inches and 28 to 33 grams, and they often live 15 to 20 years or longer. The voice is a chirp, and the bite is still a parrot bite, which is why care sits at intermediate. Two birds sold as a pair can still draw blood, and a single parrotlet with one consistent person is often the calmer house. Weigh them weekly, because a gram lost on a bird near 30 grams is a large share of the body.
