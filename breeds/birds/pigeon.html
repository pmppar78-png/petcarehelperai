Domestic Pigeon Overview

The Domestic Pigeon is a medium (12-14 in) pigeon that has captivated bird enthusiasts worldwide.

As a beginner-level bird, the Domestic Pigeon is an excellent choice for first-time bird owners who are ready to provide proper care. Their diet of pigeon mix, grit, greens requires consistent quality and variety.

Diet and Nutrition Tips

A balanced diet is critical for your Domestic Pigeon's longevity and quality of life.

  • Pellet base: A high-quality pelleted diet should form 60-70% of your Domestic Pigeon's food intake.
  • Toxic foods: Never feed avocado, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, onion, garlic, or fruit pits to your Domestic Pigeon.

Exercise Requirements

Adequate flight time and physical activity are essential for your Domestic Pigeon's physical and mental health.

  • The cage should allow your Domestic Pigeon to fully extend and flap their wings.
  • Include foraging toys, puzzle feeders, and chew toys appropriate for your Domestic Pigeon's size.
  • Social interaction: Spend quality interactive time with your Domestic Pigeon daily.

Training Advice

Training your Domestic Pigeon builds trust, provides mental stimulation, and makes daily care much easier.

  • End on a positive note before your Domestic Pigeon loses interest or becomes frustrated.
  • Target training: Teach your Domestic Pigeon to touch a target stick.

Grooming Essentials

Regular grooming keeps your Domestic Pigeon healthy, comfortable, and looking their best.

  • Wing clipping: Discuss with your avian vet whether wing clipping is appropriate for your Domestic Pigeon's safety and lifestyle.
