Green male, red female, picky digestion

Eclectus parrots are Eclectus roratus, from New Guinea, nearby islands, and the Australian Cape York. They can live 30 to 50 years, the noise is low to moderate, talking is excellent, and care is intermediate to advanced. The advanced part is the food. They do well on vegetables, sprouts, fruit, and a pellet made for them. A fatty seed mix, the bowl that keeps a cockatiel alive, is a poor staple here. Toe tapping and stress-colored feathers show up when the diet is off. Fix the bowl before you decide the bird is badly trained.

They are calmer than a macaw and still chew, scream at dusk, and bond hard. Talking can be very clear. A talking eclectus is still a 30-year roommate. Subspecies differ in size. Ask which one you are buying.

Fresh food is the daily chore

Eclectus questions

Why is one bird green and the other red?

Males are green. Females are red and purple. They are the same species, Eclectus roratus. A shop that calls the red bird a different kind of parrot is wrong about the sex, not looking at a second species.

What is different about the diet?

Fresh food comes first, on a low-fat diet made for this species. Seed and a heavy nut habit bother eclectus more than they bother many other parrots. Vegetables and a measured pellet are the base.

Care is intermediate to advanced because the diet is easy to get wrong for that whole span.

Vosmaeri and red-sided are different receipts

Vosmaeri eclectus parrots are the larger birds, with yellow trim on the tail. Red-sided eclectus parrots are the other common pet form. Size shifts inside the 14 to 17 inch band, so the subspecies goes on the sales paper before anyone buys the cage. Males are green and females are red and purple in both forms. A shop that files the red bird as a second species has the sex backward. The diet that suits them is vegetables, sprouts, fruit, and a pellet made for this bird. A fatty seed mix is a poor staple, and toe tapping shows up when the food is off. Lives of 30 to 50 years and unusually clear speech are the rest of the commitment.
