The loudest common cockatoo

Moluccan cockatoos are Cacatua moluccensis. Adults are about 20 to 24 inches, with a salmon-pink wash under the wings and a tall crest. They often live 65 to 80 years or more. The noise is extreme, care is expert, and this is the most demanding cockatoo. That ranking is fair. The bird is big, loud, emotional, and built to spend the day in contact with a flock. A person who is gone at work is not that flock.

Add a play area that can be destroyed and replaced. Screaming at dawn and dusk is normal. Screaming all day means the bird is under-slept, under-entertained, or hormonal, on top of being a Moluccan. Plucking is common in bored birds and also has medical causes. A bald chest is a vet visit.

Do not buy this bird to see if it works

Rescues are full of Moluccans whose first home wanted a cuddle. If you proceed, have a second caregiver, a house that can take the noise, and an avian vet from AAV. A fluffed, quiet Moluccan needs a vet the same day. Read the quiet bird page so you know the look.

Is Moluccan Cockatoo

Appendix I, and a salmon crest

Cacatua moluccensis is a cockatoo of Seram in the Moluccas, listed on CITES Appendix I. The crest is salmon-pink, which separates it in a photograph from the white crest of an umbrella cockatoo. Adults are about 20 to 24 inches and often live 65 to 80 years or more. The voice sits among the loudest in the cockatoo group, and the bird wants flock contact for much of the day. A paperworked captive-bred bird, a second caregiver, and an avian veterinarian are the minimum plan if someone still proceeds. A bald chest can be medical or the result of an empty day, and either reading starts at the clinic.
