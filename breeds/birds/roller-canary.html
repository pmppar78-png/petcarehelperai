Roller Canary Overview

The Roller Canary is a small (5 in) canary that has captivated bird enthusiasts worldwide. Their moderate (rolling song) noise level makes them better suited for homes where some vocalization is acceptable.

Their diet of seeds, greens, egg food requires consistent quality and variety.

A conversation with your avian veterinarian ensures these general guidelines get adapted to your Roller Canary's unique needs, age, and overall condition.
