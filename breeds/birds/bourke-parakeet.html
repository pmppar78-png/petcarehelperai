Bourke's parakeet, Neopsephotus bourkii, is a quiet Australian grass parakeet that feeds at dawn and dusk. The colors are soft pink and brown. A long cage suits it better than a tall narrow one, and the diet is a parakeet pellet or seed plus sprouted seed and greens. They are calmer than many other parakeets and can startle if the room goes from bright to dark in one switch.
