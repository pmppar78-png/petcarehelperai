Color that does not make them sturdy

Gouldian finches, Erythrura gouldiae, are about 5 inches. Six to eight years is a reasonable life for a captive-bred bird kept warm and dry. Wild birds are an Australian conservation story. Pets should be captive-bred. They need a flight cage, not a canary show cage, and a group if you know how to watch for bullying. Males in breeding condition can harass each other.

Seed, greens, egg food, and a calcium source are the diet. A vitamin powder shaken onto seed and then knocked off is not a diet. They are native to warm country. A drafty window in January kills more Gouldians than a missed millet spray.

The click in the breath

Air-sac mites are the classic Gouldian illness. So are coccidia and a bird that is egg bound if you let them breed without a plan. Do not breed them because the colors would be pretty. AAV is the clinic list. A finch on the floor is the quiet bird page.

Gouldian finch questions

Are Gouldians good for beginners?

Usually no. They are less forgiving of cold, drafts, and a seed-only diet than a society finch. Start with societies if you have never kept finches.

Can I hold a Gouldian?

No. They stress, and a grab injures them. The color is for watching in a flight cage.

What are air-sac mites?

Sternostoma mites that live in the airway. A finch that clicks when it breathes, or that wipes its beak and gasps, needs a vet. The treatment is a drug, not a different seed.

Three head colors, one airway mite

Erythrura gouldiae is an Australian estrildid finch about 5 inches long, and captive lives often run 6 to 8 years when the room stays warm and dry. Red, black, and yellow or orange heads are genetic color morphs in aviary birds. Wild Gouldians are a conservation species, so the bird to take home is captive-bred. The classic airway parasite is the air-sac mite Sternostoma tracheacolum, and a finch that clicks when it breathes needs an avian veterinarian. Seed, greens, egg food, and a calcium source are the diet, offered in a flight cage kept out of drafts. A decorative show cage built for one canary is the wrong house for a Gouldian. Head color is only a morph. The click of an air-sac mite is the finding that changes the plan, and a different seed mix leaves that parasite where it is.
