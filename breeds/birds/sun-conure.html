Yellow, orange, and very loud

Sun conures are Aratinga solstitialis, from northeastern South America, where the wild birds are endangered. Pets should be captive-bred. Adults are about 12 inches and 3.5 to 4.5 ounces. They can live 25 to 30 years, the noise is very high, and care is intermediate. People buy the color. The scream is the part that gets the bird rehomed.

A cage of at least 24 by 24 by 30 inches is only a minimum, and hours outside it are the rest of the day. Pellets, vegetables, fruit, and a little seed are the diet. Seed as the whole bowl is how these birds get fat. They want to be on a person. A sun conure left alone in a covered cage becomes a plucker or a screamer, often both. Biting during a hormonal season is common. It is not a bird that failed training in one afternoon.

Sun conure questions

How loud is a sun conure?

Noise is very high. A contact scream carries through a house and into a neighbor's window. An apartment is a poor match.

Do sun conures talk?

A few words are possible. Talking is limited. The bird you are buying is a screamer that likes to be on you, not a quiet talker.

How long do sun conures live?

About 25 to 30 years. Plan who takes the bird if you cannot.
