Red and green, and the size of a toddler

Green-wing macaws, Ara chloropterus, are about 34 to 37 inches and 2.5 to 3.5 pounds. Lifespan is often 50 to 60 years or more. They have a reputation for being the calmer large macaw. Reputation is not a puppy class. They bond hard, they bite hard, and they need a person who can read a hormonal season without turning it into a wrestling match.

Pellets, vegetables, fruit, and a measured handful of nuts are the diet. A seed-only macaw is a vitamin deficiency with feathers. Chew toys are lumber, not a plastic supermarket bird toy. Captive-bred only.

Fifty years of noise

Have two people who can handle the bird, a will, and an avian vet from AAV who has seen a macaw. Heavy-metal poisoning from a cheap toy is a real emergency. A fluffed green-wing that will not eat is the quiet bird page.

Green-wing macaw questions

Are green-wings quieter than scarlets?

Some are steadier. The noise is still high. Do not buy one as the quiet macaw. The neighbors will correct that plan.

How big is the cage?

A minimum around 40 by 50 by 65 inches is already a small room, and the bird still needs hours outside it. Measure the door the cage has to come through.

Do they talk?

Many learn words. Many scream more than they talk. Speech is a bonus on a bird you can house for 50 years, not the reason to buy one.
