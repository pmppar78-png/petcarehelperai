Pet Bird Species Guide

Explore our comprehensive directory of pet bird species. From small finches to large parrots, each species page includes care requirements, diet information, housing needs, lifespan, and personality traits to help you find the perfect feathered companion.

Parrots & Parakeets

Parrots are intelligent, social birds known for their ability to mimic speech and form strong bonds with their owners. They range from small parakeets to large macaws and require significant mental stimulation.

Small Parrots & Parakeets

Budgerigar (Parakeet)

ParrotBeginner

Most popular pet bird worldwide. Intelligent, playful, and can learn to talk. Lifespan 5-10 years.

Cockatiel

ParrotBeginner

Gentle, affectionate birds with distinctive crests. Great whistlers. Lifespan 15-25 years.

Lovebird

ParrotSpirited

Small but feisty with big personalities. Bond strongly with owners. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Parrotlet

ParrotBold

Tiny parrots with huge personalities. Can learn tricks and some words. Lifespan 15-20 years.

Lineolated Parakeet

ParrotQuiet

Calm, quiet parakeets perfect for apartments. Enjoy climbing more than flying. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Bourke's Parakeet

ParrotGentle

Gentle, quiet grass parakeets with rosy coloring. Great for calm households. Lifespan 8-15 years.

Medium Parrots

Conure

ParrotPlayful

Playful, affectionate clowns. Various species from Green-Cheeked to Sun Conures. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Green-Cheeked Conure

ParrotCuddly

Quieter than other conures, very cuddly. Great apartment parrots. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Sun Conure

ParrotVibrant

Stunning yellow and orange plumage. Beautiful but loud. Lifespan 25-30 years.

Quaker Parrot (Monk Parakeet)

ParrotTalkative

Excellent talkers with charming personalities. Illegal in some states. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Caique

ParrotEnergetic

High-energy clowns of the parrot world. Playful, bouncy, and entertaining. Lifespan 25-30 years.

Senegal Parrot

ParrotCalm

Quieter Poicephalus parrots with calm demeanor. Bond closely with one person. Lifespan 25-30 years.

Pionus Parrot

ParrotEasy-going

Quieter, easier-going medium parrots. Less demanding than many species. Lifespan 25-40 years.

Indian Ringneck Parakeet

ParrotIntelligent

Excellent talkers with clear voices. Need experienced owners. Lifespan 25-30 years.

Rainbow Lorikeet

ParrotSpecialized Diet

Stunning colors but require nectar-based diet. Very active and playful. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Large Parrots

African Grey Parrot

ParrotMost Intelligent

Considered the most intelligent parrot species. Exceptional talkers. Lifespan 40-60 years.

Amazon Parrot

ParrotVocal

Colorful, vocal, and entertaining. Can be feisty. Multiple species available. Lifespan 40-70 years.

Eclectus Parrot

ParrotUnique

Males green, females red - extreme sexual dimorphism. Calm and gentle. Lifespan 30-50 years.

Cockatoo

ParrotDemanding

Affectionate but extremely demanding. Need experienced owners. Lifespan 40-70 years.

Umbrella Cockatoo

ParrotCuddly

Very cuddly but prone to behavioral issues. Require significant time investment. Lifespan 40-60 years.

Moluccan Cockatoo

ParrotAdvanced

Largest cockatoo with salmon-pink coloring. Extremely loud and needy. Lifespan 65-80 years.

Macaw

ParrotLarge

Largest parrots with stunning colors. Need huge enclosures. Lifespan 50-80+ years.

Blue & Gold Macaw

ParrotSocial

Most common large macaw. Social and trainable. Lifespan 50-60 years.

Scarlet Macaw

ParrotStriking

Brilliant red, yellow, and blue plumage. Can be nippy. Lifespan 50-75 years.

Hyacinth Macaw

ParrotRare

Largest flying parrot, stunning cobalt blue. Endangered and extremely expensive. Lifespan 60+ years.

Finches & Canaries

Finches and canaries are small, active birds that are generally hands-off pets. They're valued for their beautiful songs and colorful plumage. Best kept in pairs or small flocks.

Finches

Zebra Finch

FinchBeginner

Hardy, active finches with cheerful chirps. Easy to breed. Keep in pairs. Lifespan 5-10 years.

Society Finch (Bengalese)

FinchSocial

Extremely social, often used as foster parents. Peaceful community birds. Lifespan 6-12 years.

Gouldian Finch

FinchStunning

Rainbow-colored Australian finches. More delicate, need experienced care. Lifespan 6-8 years.

Java Finch (Java Sparrow)

FinchGentle

Larger finches with pink beaks. Can become tame. Illegal in some states. Lifespan 5-10 years.

Owl Finch (Bicheno Finch)

FinchActive

Distinctive owl-like face markings. Active and entertaining. Lifespan 5-10 years.

Star Finch

FinchPeaceful

Attractive red faces with white-spotted bodies. Very peaceful community finches. Lifespan 6-8 years.

Cordon Bleu

FinchDelicate

Beautiful blue African finches. Need warm temperatures and experienced care. Lifespan 5-8 years.

Spice Finch (Nutmeg Mannikin)

FinchHardy

Brown scalloped pattern, very hardy. Easy to keep in mixed finch aviaries. Lifespan 6-10 years.

Canaries

Canary (General)

FinchSinging

Famous for beautiful songs (males). Many varieties available. Prefer to be kept alone or in pairs. Lifespan 10-15 years.

American Singer Canary

FinchSong

Bred for beautiful singing voice. Combination of Roller and Border canaries. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Roller Canary

FinchSong

German song canary with unique rolling notes. Valued for song quality. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Red Factor Canary

FinchColor

Bred for orange-red coloring. Require color-fed diet to maintain plumage. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Gloster Canary

FinchType

Small canary with distinctive "Beatles-like" corona (crest). Bred for appearance. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Other Pet Birds

Beyond parrots and finches, several other bird species make wonderful pets, each with their own unique characteristics and care requirements.

Ringneck Dove

OtherGentle

Gentle, quiet birds with soothing cooing sounds. Easy to tame. Lifespan 12-15 years.

Diamond Dove

OtherSmall

Tiny Australian doves with red eye rings. Peaceful, soft cooing. Lifespan 10-12 years.

Fancy Pigeon

OtherVariable

Many fancy breeds with unique features. Intelligent and can be very tame. Lifespan 15-20 years.

Button Quail

OtherGround Dweller

Tiny ground birds often kept in aviaries. Clean up fallen seeds. Lifespan 3-5 years.

Coturnix Quail

OtherDual Purpose

Hardy quail kept as pets or for eggs. Easy to care for. Lifespan 2-5 years.

Backyard Chickens

OtherProductive

Many breeds for eggs, meat, or pets. Social and entertaining. Lifespan 5-10 years.

Toucan

OtherExotic

Iconic large-billed birds. Require specialized diet and large enclosures. Lifespan 20-25 years.

Mynah Bird

OtherTalkative

Excellent mimics rivaling parrots. Active and entertaining. Lifespan 12-25 years.

Additional Bird Species

Finch (General)

FinchSocial

Small, social birds valued for their songs and colors. Best kept in pairs or groups.

Galah (Rose-Breasted Cockatoo)

ParrotSocial

Pink and grey cockatoos known for playful, affectionate personalities. Lifespan 40-70 years.

Goffin's Cockatoo

ParrotClever

Smallest cockatoo species, exceptionally clever and playful. Lifespan 30-40 years.

Green-Winged Macaw

ParrotGentle Giant

Second largest macaw, known as the gentle giant. Affectionate and calm. Lifespan 60-80 years.

Jenday Conure

ParrotColorful

Colorful South American conure closely related to Sun Conures. Playful and social. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Masked Lovebird

ParrotBold

Striking black-headed lovebird with bright coloring. Feisty and social. Lifespan 10-15 years.

Military Macaw

ParrotSocial

Green macaw with red forehead patch. Social and even-tempered. Lifespan 50-60 years.

Nanday Conure

ParrotVocal

Black-hooded conure known for being vocal and highly social. Loyal companions. Lifespan 20-30 years.

Peach-Faced Lovebird

ParrotPopular

Most popular lovebird species with beautiful coloring. Spirited and affectionate. Lifespan 15-25 years.
