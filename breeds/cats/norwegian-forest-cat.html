A climber that sheds its winter

Norwegian Forest Cats weigh about 12 to 16 pounds on this profile, sometimes more in males, and often live 14 to 16 years. They are athletic, patient with children, and independent in the sense that they sit beside you rather than demanding a lap. They mature slowly. The cat that looks finished at a year old is not; bone and judgment keep going for several more years. Give them something tall and sturdy. A flimsy condo built for a 9-pound pet will tip.

The coat needs a rake through the undercoat, especially when it blows. Outdoor time belongs in a secure enclosure or a harness, not in a hope that a hunting cat will respect the road.

One genetic test that is not optional

GSD IV is the paperwork. Affected kittens waste and die, and carriers should not be bred together. Pyruvate kinase deficiency is a second DNA test that explains some anemias. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy needs ultrasound when there is a murmur or a family history. Hips can be dysplastic in a cat this size, which looks like a hesitation to jump rather than a dramatic limp. Weight hides under the coat. Feel the ribs.

Food is often $500 to $900. A routine year totals about $1,550 to $3,100. Kittens commonly cost $800 to $1,500. The Merck Veterinary Manual covers glycogen storage diseases and cardiomyopathy. A young kitten that fades, or an adult that stops eating, uses the not-eating guide and the checker without a multi-day experiment.

Norwegian Forest Cat questions

What is GSD IV?

Glycogen storage disease type IV breaks glucose storage. Affected kittens often die young. A DNA test exists, and a breeder who does not run it on breeding cats is skipping the one disease this breed is famous for in veterinary genetics.

Do they take a long time to grow up?

Yes. Many are not fully mature until around four or five years. A leggy youngster that still acts like a teenager at three is on schedule.

How much do they shed?

A lot, and then more in spring. The coat is a weather system: long guard hairs, a woolly undercoat, and mats if you skip the belly and the trousers. Budget grooming time or a groomer.
