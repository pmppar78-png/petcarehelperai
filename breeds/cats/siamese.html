They narrate the house

A Siamese is a medium cat, roughly 6 to 14 pounds, with a pale body and darker points on the face, ears, legs, and tail. The points exist because the pigment enzyme works in cooler skin. Social, vocal, and very intelligent is not marketing. This cat will follow you into the bathroom and explain the day. Activity is high. Shedding and grooming needs are low. Lifespan is one of the long ones: 15 to 20 years is a fair expectation.

They are usually excellent with children and with other pets, provided the other pet can tolerate a conversationalist. A dog that wants to be left alone on a bed may not.

Company is husbandry

Under-stimulated Siamese cats yell, overgroom, and sometimes develop a fixation on wool or plastic. The fix is boring and daily: play that ends in a meal, a window, a puzzle feeder, and either a person or a second cat who likes contact. Training is real. Clicker work and harness walks suit this breed better than most.

Feed a measured, complete cat food. They are lean by type and still get fat if the bowl is open all day, and a fat Siamese is harder to assess for the dental disease they are prone to. Brush teeth if the cat will allow it, or plan on professional dentals. The cost card sets dental care aside at $100 to $400 for a reason.

What to screen and what to spend

Progressive retinal atrophy exists in the breed and has a DNA test. Crossed eyes and a kinked tail show up in old photos and are not a personality. Amyloidosis is discussed more in Oriental and Abyssinian relatives; if you are buying from a cattery that mixes those lines, ask what they know about liver and kidney disease in their older cats. Routine yearly cost is about $1,300 to $2,600.

Apartments are fine. Silence is not. A first-time owner who likes interaction will do well. A first-time owner who wanted a British Shorthair and got talked into "something prettier" will be exhausted by month two. The TICA standard describes the look. Clinical dental and eye questions belong with your vet, with the VCA cat pages as background. A cat that stops eating needs the not-eating guide the same day, not after a weekend of waiting.

Living with the voice

Why is my Siamese so loud?

The breed talks. Comments, demands, and greetings are normal, and the volume is part of why the quick facts list vocalization as very high. A quiet household that wants a decorative cat will not enjoy this.

Do they need another pet?

They need someone. A second friendly cat, or a person who is actually home, prevents a lot of the yelling and the curtain-climbing. A Siamese alone 12 hours is an unhappy project.

Dental disease and, in some related lines, amyloidosis are the health issues that shorten that if you ignore them.

What does the color change mean?

Pointed cats are born pale and darken on the cooler parts of the body. A dark cat that has been running a fever, or a cat whose points fade oddly, is worth mentioning to a vet. The coat is temperature-sensitive pigment, not dye.
