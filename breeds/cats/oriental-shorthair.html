The voice comes in every color

Oriental Shorthairs weigh about 6 to 12 pounds, live roughly 12 to 15 years, and wear a short sleek coat that sheds little and shows every rib. Activity and vocalization are both very high. They comment on the refrigerator, the other cat, and your meeting. Crossed eyes show up and usually do not ruin vision. The personality is the Siamese one: in your lap, in your bag, unhappy if the house goes quiet.

Plan real play sessions, a climbing route, and preferably a second cat that is not terrified of being talked at. Children are usually fine because the cat wants the audience. Grooming is a weekly cloth, not a salon plan.

Liver, teeth, lungs

Amyloid in the liver can make an Oriental very sick with vague signs: not eating, vomiting, a painful abdomen. Do not wait a weekend. Progressive retinal atrophy is worth a DNA or eye conversation with the breeder. Asthma shows up as a cough or a wheeze that owners call a hairball. Dental disease is common enough that the cost lines include dentistry. A heart murmur should be imaged. Megaesophagus is uncommon and serious if swallowing suddenly fails.

Routine yearly costs often run about $1,300 to $2,600. Kittens are commonly $600 to $1,500. VCA covers feline asthma and dental disease. Vomiting plus hiding is the vomiting page and the checker. Two quiet days without food is already late.

Oriental Shorthair questions

Are Oriental Shorthairs just Siamese in other colors?

In body and voice, that is the practical truth. The difference buyers feel is the color, not the volume. If a talking cat would bother you, this breed will bother you.

Can an Oriental Shorthair be left alone?

A workday with another friendly cat is sometimes tolerable. A single cat alone from breakfast to dinner is how you get a miserable, noisy animal. They are social first and beautiful second.

What disease is specific to this family?

Hepatic amyloidosis, protein deposits in the liver, is reported in Orientals and Siamese. A cat that suddenly will not eat, vomits, or seems painful in the belly needs a clinic the same day. Dental disease is the slower, more common problem.
