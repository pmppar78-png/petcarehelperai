Oriental Shorthair Overview

The Oriental Shorthair is essentially a Siamese cat in a rainbow of colors and patterns. Developed by crossing Siamese with other breeds to introduce new colors while maintaining the sleek, elegant body type, Orientals come in over 300 color and pattern combinations - more than any other breed. They have the same wedge-shaped head, large ears, and almond-shaped eyes as their Siamese cousins.

With their long, tubular bodies, big ears, and distinctive personalities, Orientals are often compared to elegant aliens or supermodels of the cat world. They share the Siamese's vocal nature and intense desire for human companionship.

Personality & Temperament

Oriental Shorthairs share the Siamese personality in a colorful package:

  • Extremely Vocal: They will "talk" constantly, commenting on everything and expecting responses.
  • Highly Social: They crave human attention and don't tolerate being left alone.
  • Intelligent: Very clever cats who can learn tricks and solve puzzles.
  • Energetic: They are athletic and love to climb, jump, and play.
  • Affectionate: They form intense bonds with their people and are true lap cats.
  • Attention-Seeking: They want to be involved in everything you do.
  • Playful: They maintain kitten-like energy and playfulness into adulthood.

Common Health Issues

Oriental Shorthairs share health concerns with their Siamese relatives:

Cardiac Conditions

  • Dilated Cardiomyopathy: Heart muscle disease that can affect this breed.

Genetic Conditions

  • Amyloidosis: Liver disease from protein deposits, more common in Oriental breeds.
  • Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA): Genetic eye condition causing vision loss.
  • Crossed Eyes (Strabismus): Common but usually doesn't affect vision.

Other Concerns

  • Dental Disease: Prone to gingivitis and periodontal disease.
  • Asthma: Can be prone to feline asthma.
  • Lymphoma: Higher rates of certain cancers have been reported.
  • Megaesophagus: Rare condition affecting swallowing.

Health Screening Recommendation

Before getting an Oriental Shorthair, ask breeders about heart screening, PRA testing, and amyloidosis history. Consider Basepaws DNA testing for genetic health screening.

Activity Level & Exercise

Oriental Shorthairs are extremely active cats:

  • Interactive Play: 30+ minutes of active play daily
  • Climbing: They need tall cat trees and vertical space
  • Fetch: Many love playing fetch
  • Puzzle Toys: Mental stimulation is essential
  • Training: They excel at learning tricks
  • Companion: Consider a second cat for companionship

Nutrition & Feeding

Proper nutrition supports Oriental Shorthair health:

  • High-Quality Protein: Lean protein supports their athletic build
  • Dental Health: Consider dental-specific foods
  • Active Cat Formula: Foods that support their high energy needs
  • Sensitive Stomach: Some may need easily digestible formulas
  • Hydration: Water fountains encourage drinking

Grooming Requirements

Oriental Shorthairs have minimal grooming needs:

  • Brushing: Once weekly to remove loose fur
  • Coat Type: Short, fine coat that's nearly self-maintaining
  • Bathing: Rarely needed
  • Nail Trimming: Every 2-3 weeks
  • Dental Care: Daily brushing recommended due to dental disease predisposition
  • Ear Cleaning: Weekly checks of their large ears

Is an Oriental Shorthair Right for You?

Oriental Shorthairs Are Great For:

  • Those who want an interactive, vocal companion
  • People who are home frequently
  • Families who enjoy active, playful cats
  • Multi-cat households
  • Those who enjoy "conversations" with their cats

Oriental Shorthairs May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Those seeking a quiet, independent cat
  • People away from home for long hours
  • Apartment dwellers with noise-sensitive neighbors
  • Those wanting low-maintenance pets
  • People who prefer calm, sedate cats

Related Cat Breeds to Consider

If you're interested in Oriental Shorthairs, you might also consider:

  • Siamese - The original colorpoint ancestor
  • Burmese - Similarly social, less vocal
  • Devon Rex - Playful and engaging
  • Abyssinian - Athletic and intelligent
