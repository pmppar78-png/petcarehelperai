A Persian face in point color

Himalayans weigh about 7 to 14 pounds, males larger, and are listed at 9 to 15 years. The short end of that range is what the skull and the kidneys do. They are calm, low-activity cats with a softer voice than a Siamese and a heavier coat than makes any sense in July. Children can live with them if the children are gentle and the cat can leave. Strangers get a polite distance.

Wipe the eyes every day. The tear film overflows because the face is flat, and the fur stains and then infects the skin fold. Corneal ulcers follow a scratch that a normal-faced cat would have blinked away. Snoring can be the airway. Labored breathing is not a quirk. Dental crowding comes with the short jaw.

Kidneys you can test before you buy

Polycystic kidney disease is common in Persian-related cats and has a DNA test. Do not buy a kitten from someone who has never heard of PKD1. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and progressive retinal atrophy are the other reasons a "healthy line" needs images and an eye exam, not a slogan. Heatstroke and hair mats are the owner-side failures.

A routine year often totals about $1,750 to $3,400, with vet care and grooming both expensive. Kittens commonly cost $1,500 to $3,500. Merck describes polycystic kidney disease and brachycephalic airway problems. Eye pain, open-mouth breathing, or a skipped day of food uses the checker and the not-eating guide.

Himalayan questions

Is a Himalayan a Persian?

In body and skull, yes: a brachycephalic longhair. The colorpoint pattern is the extra. Breathing problems, tear staining, and polycystic kidney disease are Persian problems that come with the face.

How much grooming is required?

Daily combing, or mats form against the skin. Professional grooming is often $400 to $800 a year. A shaved cat in summer is sometimes the kinder choice if the coat has already felted.

Can they handle heat?

Poorly. The nose is short, the coat is heavy, and they do not pant their way out of a hot room. Air conditioning is part of the setup, not a luxury.
