Marcelled coat, marathon play

Cornish rex cats weigh about 5 to 10 pounds and often live 12 to 15 years. The mutation waves the coat, including the whiskers. They are intelligent and very active, and they want company. A Cornish that is the only pet and the only project in an empty apartment will invent projects you do not like.

Children and other pets are usually a good match if play is not rough on the thin skin. They sunburn on exposed ears and bellies. A windowsill at noon in July is not harmless. Offer shade the way you would for a hairless cat, even though this cat is not fully nude.

Heart and kneecap, again, in a different breed

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and patellar luxation show up here too. Do not copy a Devon myopathy article onto a Cornish and call it the same disease. Ask the breeder what they actually test. A cat that breathes with effort at rest needs a clinic the same day. VCA explains HCM in cats. Skipping meals around that breathing change is the not-eating page.

Cornish rex questions

Devon or Cornish?

Cornish rex have a wavy coat and a more wedge-shaped head. Devons have a pixie face and a coat that is often patchier. They are separate breeds. A 'rex' kitten with no breed named is a guess.

Do they need baths?

Sometimes, because skin oils sit on a thin coat. Do not bathe on a schedule you invented from a dog. Greasy skin or a rash is a vet question first.

Are they climbers?

Yes. They treat shoulders, curtain rods, and refrigerators as pathways. Breakable shelves need a plan before the cat does.
