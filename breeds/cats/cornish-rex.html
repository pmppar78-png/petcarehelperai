A wave, not a Devon

Cornish Rex cats weigh about 5 to 10 pounds and often live 12 to 15 years. The mutation thins the coat into a ripple and breaks the whiskers. They are not hairless, and they are not Devons. Activity is very high. They are usually excellent with children and other pets because they want the game. Food motivation is real. A counter is not secure because you put a napkin on it.

Cold houses need a heated bed. Sun through a window can burn a back that has almost no fur. Ears grow wax you can see from across the room. Grooming supplies are a small line, about $50 to $100, which is skin care more than brushing.

Knees and a heart murmur

Luxating patellas show up and cause a skip that owners call a funny hop. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is the reason a murmur gets an ultrasound rather than a note that the cat is excited. There is no need to import the Devon myopathy story onto this breed; ask about the heart and the knees instead. Dental disease happens under the elegant face. A Cornish that stops eating is still an emergency-prone cat because any lean cat tips into hepatic lipidosis quickly.

A routine year often totals about $1,200 to $2,250. Kittens are commonly $1,000 to $2,000. TICA registers both Rex breeds as separate. Appetite loss uses the not-eating page. Open-mouth breathing uses the checker on the way out the door.

Cornish Rex questions

How is a Cornish Rex different from a Devon Rex?

Different genes, different heads. Cornish cats are finer, with a tighter wave and often short or curly whiskers. The Devon has its own muscle disease. Do not let a listing treat the names as the same cat.

Do Cornish Rex cats need special skin care?

They need warmth, sunscreen-free shade, and a check of ears and nails because the coat does not protect much. Oils collect. A weekly wipe beats a heavy bath that strips what little coat they have.

Are they quiet?

No. Activity is very high and the voice is moderate. They climb, steal food, and want to be in the middle of the room. A lap happens after the zoomies, not instead of them.
