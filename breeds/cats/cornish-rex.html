Cornish Rex Overview

The Cornish Rex originated in Cornwall, England in 1950, when a curly-coated kitten named Kallibunker was born to a barn cat. The breed is named after the Rex rabbit, which has a similarly soft, wavy coat. Unlike most cats that have three types of hair (guard, awn, and down), Cornish Rex have only the soft, curly down layer.

With their egg-shaped heads, large ears, arched backs, and long, elegant legs, Cornish Rex cats have a distinctive Greyhound-like appearance. They are the athletes of the cat world, known for their incredible speed, agility, and love of acrobatic play that continues well into their senior years.

Temperament & Personality

Cornish Rex cats are known for their distinctive personalities:

  • Perpetual Kittens: They maintain kitten-like playfulness and energy throughout their lives.
  • Acrobatic: Exceptional jumpers and climbers who love to perform aerial feats.
  • Heat Seekers: Their thin coats drive them to find the warmest spots, including your lap.
  • People-Oriented: They thrive on human interaction and don't like being left alone.
  • Intelligent: Quick learners who enjoy puzzle toys and can be trained to fetch and do tricks.
  • Adventurous: Curious explorers who want to investigate everything in their environment.

Common Health Issues

Cornish Rex cats have some health considerations:

Cardiac Conditions

  • Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM): The most common heart disease in cats; regular screening recommended.

Other Health Concerns

  • Hypotrichosis: Some may develop bald patches or thin coat areas.
  • Patellar Luxation: Kneecap displacement can occur in some individuals.
  • Sunburn Risk: Thin coat provides less protection from UV rays.
  • Temperature Sensitivity: Susceptible to cold; may need extra warmth.
  • Skin Sensitivity: More prone to skin irritation and allergies.

Care Requirements

Cornish Rex cats have specific care needs:

  • Temperature Comfort: Keep your home warm; provide heated beds and sunny spots.
  • High Activity: Multiple play sessions daily to satisfy their energy levels.
  • Vertical Space: Tall cat trees and climbing shelves are essential.
  • Mental Stimulation: Puzzle feeders and interactive toys to engage their intelligence.
  • Companionship: They need company - consider a second cat or dog companion.
  • Indoor Only: Best kept indoors due to temperature sensitivity and thin coat.

Grooming Needs

Cornish Rex have minimal but specialized grooming needs:

  • Coat Care: Gentle stroking with hands or a chamois cloth is sufficient.
  • Bathing: More frequent baths (every 2-4 weeks) due to oil buildup on thin coat.
  • Ear Cleaning: Regular cleaning needed due to wax accumulation.
  • Nail Trimming: Every 2-3 weeks.
  • Skin Protection: Limit sun exposure; consider pet-safe sunscreen for outdoor access.
  • Dental Care: Regular brushing recommended.

Nutrition & Feeding

Cornish Rex have higher metabolic needs:

  • Higher Calorie Intake: Their high activity level and body temperature regulation burns more calories.
  • High-Quality Protein: Animal protein should be the primary ingredient.
  • Multiple Meals: Three to four smaller meals daily suit their fast metabolism.
  • Avoid Overfeeding: Despite higher needs, monitor weight to prevent obesity.
  • Fresh Water: Always available; many prefer running water fountains.

Is a Cornish Rex Right for You?

Cornish Rex Are Great For:

  • Active families who enjoy playful, entertaining pets
  • People who want an athletic, acrobatic cat
  • Those who love affectionate, people-oriented cats
  • Homes with other pets (they're very social)
  • People with warm homes who can provide cozy environments

Cornish Rex May Not Be Ideal For:

  • People away from home frequently
  • Those seeking a calm, independent cat
  • Cold climate homes without adequate heating
  • Those who want minimal interaction with their cat
  • People looking for a low-energy companion

Related Breeds to Consider

If you're interested in Cornish Rex, you might also consider:

  • Devon Rex - Similar but different genetic mutation
  • Sphynx - Hairless with similar warmth needs
  • Selkirk Rex - Curly coat with different texture
  • Oriental Shorthair - Similar athletic build and personality
