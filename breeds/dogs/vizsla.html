A pointer that sits on your feet

Vizslas weigh about 44 to 60 pounds and stand 21 to 24 inches, with a rust coat, almost no grooming, and a lifespan around 12 to 14 years. They were bred to hunt close to a person and they never really got the memo about personal space. That is pleasant on a couch and miserable if everyone leaves for work. Crate training and short absences have to start before the dog has practiced panic.

Exercise is not a luxury add-on. Plan field time, swimming, or a long retrieve with impulse control, most days. A fenced yard they patrol alone does not count. They are usually excellent with children and good with other dogs when they are tired enough to remember manners.

Seizures, skin, hips

Epilepsy shows up often enough to ask a breeder for ages of onset in the relatives, not a blanket "they're fine." Sebaceous adenitis needs a vet and often a biopsy; owners waste months calling it allergies. Hips should be scored. Allergies and ear infections happen even on a short coat. Some lines see more lymphoma or hemangiosarcoma, so a sudden weakness in a middle-aged Vizsla is an emergency clinic, not a rest day.

Grooming is the cheap line, about $100 to $200. A routine year still lands near $1,700 to $3,400 once food and insurance are in it. VCA explains epilepsy and allergic skin disease. A first seizure uses the checker on the drive. Itchy, scaling skin uses the itching guide.

Vizsla questions

Can a Vizsla stay home alone?

Poorly, unless alone-time was trained in small pieces. This is a contact breed. A dog that panics, destroys the crate, or screams for hours is not being stubborn. It is the temperament meeting an empty house.

What is sebaceous adenitis in Vizslas?

An inflammatory attack on the skin glands. Hair thins, the skin scales, and a biopsy is how it is confirmed. It is not the ordinary shed of a double coat, because Vizslas barely have one.

How much running is enough?

The stats table says very high, an hour to two and then more. A sniff walk plus a retrieve session beats a single frantic sprint. They still want to be touching you when it is over.
