American Water Spaniel Overview

The American Water Spaniel is the state dog of Wisconsin and one of only a few breeds developed entirely in the United States. Created in the Fox and Wolf River valleys of Wisconsin in the mid-1800s, this versatile hunting spaniel was bred to work from both boats and shore, retrieving waterfowl and flushing upland game. Their distinctive curly or marcel-patterned brown coat is water-resistant, and their compact, muscular build gives them excellent swimming ability. Despite being relatively rare (only about 3,000 exist), the American Water Spaniel is an outstanding multi-purpose hunting dog and a devoted family companion with an enthusiastic, people-loving personality.

Temperament & Personality

The American Water Spaniel is known for being happy, eager, charming.
