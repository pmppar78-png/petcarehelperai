Gray, fast, and bad at being alone

Weimaraners weigh about 55 to 90 pounds and stand 23 to 27 inches. Lifespan is often 10 to 13 years. They are hunting dogs with a short silver coat and a need to be in the same room as their person. Two hours of running without company still fails them. The useful day has real mileage plus a person attached to the other end of it. Children do better when they are old enough not to be run over. Other dogs are a coin flip if the Weimaraner was not socialized on purpose.

The coat is low work, about $100 to $200 a year. The brain is not. A bored Weimaraner eats drywall and then looks hurt that you noticed.

Stomach, hips, growing bones

Bloat is the emergency: a tight abdomen, unproductive retching, a dog that cannot settle. Emergency clinic. Talk about tacking the stomach before the sterilization surgery, and split meals. Hip dysplasia is common enough to demand parental scores. Hypertrophic osteodystrophy hits some puppies with hot, swollen, exquisitely painful legs. That is not growing pains you ice at home. Keep young dogs lean and off forced pavement jogging until the growth plates close.

A routine year often runs about $2,100 to $4,250. VCA's library covers gastric dilatation-volvulus in owner language. Retching or a puppy that will not stand uses the checker immediately.

Weimaraner questions

Are Weimaraners good apartment dogs?

Only for someone who is out working the dog for hours and home the rest of the time. Separation distress is common. A gray dog in a crate ten hours is a welfare problem, not a lifestyle hack.

What is HOD in a Weimaraner puppy?

Hypertrophic osteodystrophy: painful, swollen legs in a growing dog, often with fever. It is a same-day vet visit. Do not wait to see if the puppy walks it off.

How do you reduce bloat risk?

Feed two or three meals, skip hard running right after eating, and ask about a gastropexy at spay or neuter. Raised bowls are not a proven fix. Learn the retching-and-swollen-belly signs before you need them.
