A herding dog in a small coat

Shetland Sheepdogs weigh about 15 to 25 pounds and stand 13 to 16 inches. Lifespan is often 12 to 14 years. They are bright, devoted, and sound-sensitive. A harsh voice is too much tool. They herd children by nipping heels, bark at the hallway, and blow their undercoat all over the house twice a year. Exercise is high for the size, 1 to 2 hours, and puzzle work counts. Grooming often runs $300 to $600 if you are not line-brushing the mane yourself.

They can be shy with strangers. Early exposure, done quietly, matters more than flooding them at a street fair. The stats table's "excellent with kids" assumes the kids are not chaotic and the dog has a place to retreat.

Drug sensitivity and skin

MDR1, an ABCB1 mutation, is common enough that the test should happen before surgery or a new parasite drug. Hand the paper to the clinic. Collie eye anomaly, progressive retinal atrophy, and cataracts are the eye file. Von Willebrand disease affects clotting. Dermatomyositis scars the face and can involve muscle. Hypothyroidism, epilepsy, hip dysplasia, and patent ductus arteriosus show up often enough to ask about. Gallbladder mucoceles are overrepresented in Shelties: vomiting, lethargy, and belly pain in a middle-aged dog can be a surgical emergency, not a rich meal.

A routine year often totals about $1,650 to $3,300. Merck describes collie eye anomaly and MDR1. Vomiting with pain uses the vomiting page and the checker. A new limp uses the limping guide.

Sheltie questions

Are Shelties small Collies?

They look like a rough Collie scaled down and they share collie eye anomaly and MDR1 drug sensitivity. They are their own breed, about 13 to 16 inches. Do not buy a tiny Collie and call it a Sheltie.

Why does my Sheltie bark so much?

Herding dogs announce everything. You can lower it with training. You will not delete it. Apartment neighbors and a bored Sheltie are a bad pair.

What is dermatomyositis?

A Collie and Sheltie disease of skin and muscle. Hairless, scaly patches on the face or legs, sometimes a stiff gait, need a vet rather than a new shampoo. It is inherited. Ask the breeder what they have seen.
