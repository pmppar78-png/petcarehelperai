Seven to ten years, said plainly

A Great Dane can clear 32 inches and weigh 110 to 175 pounds. The temperament line, friendly and patient, is often true and still irrelevant if you cannot lift them into a car or pay for a giant-breed dental. Lifespan is 7 to 10 years. Count on senior habits, orthopedic beds, and twice-yearly bloodwork by age five or six, not at eleven.

Bloat, gastric dilatation-volvulus, is the emergency to memorize. The stomach fills and can twist, and the dog dies without surgery. Signs are a hard swollen belly, unproductive retching, restlessness. Emergency clinic, immediately. Talk about a prophylactic gastropexy before the spay or neuter. Feed two or three meals, not one trough, and give the stomach a quiet hour afterward. Raised bowls are not a proven fix; some research ties them to more bloat, so skip that shortcut.

Heart, bone, growth

Dilated cardiomyopathy is common. An echocardiogram, repeated, tells you more than a stethoscope. Osteosarcoma and wobbler syndrome (a cervical spinal problem) are the other reasons a limp or a wobble in a Dane is imaged early. Puppies should eat a large-breed diet and stay lean while the growth plates are open. Forced jogging on pavement is how people break a dog that is already going to be enormous.

Food alone often runs $1,200 to $2,400 a year. The whole routine total is about $3,150 to $6,300, and a torsion surgery sits far above that. Drug doses and anesthesia scale with the body, so "just a dental" is priced like a big dog. Merck has the clinical account of GDV. Retching or collapse uses the checker only as a formality on the drive. A new limp uses the limping page while you book radiographs.

Giant-breed questions

What does bloat look like in a Great Dane?

A swelling, tight abdomen, retching that brings up little or nothing, drooling, and a dog that cannot settle. Great Danes have the highest risk of stomach torsion of any breed. Go to an emergency hospital. Do not wait to see if it passes.

Should a Dane have a gastropexy?

Many surgeons tack the stomach to the body wall at spay or neuter so it cannot twist. Ask before the surgery date. The pexy is not a license to feed one huge meal and then play fetch.

How long do Great Danes live?

Often 7 to 10 years. Heart disease, bone cancer, and bloat take dogs inside that window. They are seniors by five or six. Buy the dog knowing the decade is short.

How do you feed a Dane puppy?

Large-breed puppy food, measured, without extra calcium to 'help them grow.' Pushing calories produces a faster puppy and a worse adult skeleton. Split meals. Skip hard running right after eating.
