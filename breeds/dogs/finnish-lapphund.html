Finnish Lapphund Overview

The Finnish Lapphund is a medium herding breed known for being friendly, alert, agile.

Finnish Lapphunds are exceptional family dogs that get along wonderfully with children of all ages.

Temperament & Personality

Finnish Lapphunds have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Agile: Finnish Lapphunds show remarkable agile in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Finnish Lapphunds are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Finnish Lapphunds are typically friendly with strangers and other dogs.

Common Health Issues

Finnish Lapphunds are generally healthy dogs, but like all breeds, they can be prone to certain conditions.
