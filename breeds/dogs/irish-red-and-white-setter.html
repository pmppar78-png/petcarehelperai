Temperament & Personality

Irish Red and White Setters have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Courageous: This defining trait makes the Irish Red and White Setter a standout companion that bonds deeply with their family.
  • Spirited: Their spirited nature means they are always eager to please and participate in family activities.
  • Friendly: Irish Red and White Setters show remarkable friendly in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Irish Red and White Setters are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Irish Red and White Setters can be selective with strangers but warm up with proper introductions.
