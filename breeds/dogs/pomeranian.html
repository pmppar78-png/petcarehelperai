A throat the size of a straw

Pomeranians are spitz dogs shrunk to 3 to 7 pounds and 6 to 7 inches. They live about 12 to 16 years, act like they weigh 40, and get hurt when someone treats that confidence as armor. Toddlers step on them. They fall from sofas. They pick fights with dogs that do not understand the costume. Older, careful children are the match the stats table is pointing at.

The honking cough of tracheal collapse is the signature problem. Harness, not collar. Weight control, because fat makes the airway worse. A soft spot on the skull of a tiny puppy is a reason to protect the head, not a trick to show guests. Luxating patellas cause a skip in the gait. Crowded teeth need brushing and, later, dentistry. Some puppies crash if a meal is skipped; adults should not be free-fed into a round coat to prevent that.

The coat leaves twice a year

Expect a blow: clumps of undercoat, a slightly naked look, then regrowth. Alopecia X, sometimes called black-skin disease, is a different pattern, with symmetrical hair loss and dark skin, and it needs a vet so you are not treating a hormone problem as a bad brush. Grooming often costs $400 to $800 a year. Exercise is a short walk and indoor play, 30 to 45 minutes, not a jog beside a bike.

A routine year totals about $1,500 to $3,050. They can be stubborn in training and noisy at the door. VCA describes collapsing trachea clearly. Cough plus blue gums, or a dog that will not get up, uses the checker. A skip in the hind leg uses the limping guide.

Pomeranian questions

What is a teacup Pomeranian?

A marketing size, not a breed. The tiny end of 3 pounds is where tracheal collapse, low blood sugar in puppies, and broken legs get more likely. A sturdy pup in the breed's normal range is the safer buy.

Why is my Pomeranian coughing?

Collapsing trachea is common and sounds like a goose honk, worse with excitement or a collar. Use a harness. Have a veterinarian separate that cough from a reverse sneeze, heart disease, or an infection.

Do Pomeranians shed?

Yes, despite the small body. The undercoat blows, sometimes until the dog looks moth-eaten, and alopecia X can leave a bald tail and trunk that is not the ordinary shed. A groomer at $400 to $800 a year prevents mats.
