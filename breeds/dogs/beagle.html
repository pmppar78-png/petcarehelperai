The nose is in charge

Beagles were pack hounds for rabbits. Merry, friendly, and curious is fair, and it leaves out the part where the dog goes deaf to you the moment a squirrel crosses the path. There are two height varieties, 13 inches and 13 to 15 inches, both usually 20 to 30 pounds. Lifespan is a genuine bright spot: 10 to 15 years is realistic with weight control.

Exercise needs are moderate to high, and the useful kind is sniffing. A 40-minute neighborhood walk where the dog is allowed to read the ground tires a beagle more honestly than a sprint. They do well with other dogs and with kids. A single beagle left alone all day may sing about it. A second dog can help, or it can mean two voices.

Ears, waistline, gates

Long, heavy ears trap moisture. Weekly checks and drying after baths prevent a lot of the yeast infections this breed collects. Obesity is the other everyday disease. A beagle will finish the cat's food, the trash, and the counter loaf, then look underfed. Measure meals. Secure bins.

Fence the yard and check the bottom. They dig and they squeeze. Microchip and a collar tag matter because a beagle on a scent does not wander home on a schedule. Trainability is moderate and scent-driven: they learn fast when food or smell is the point, and they ignore a repeated "come" that has never paid off.

Less common, still worth knowing

Cherry eye, epilepsy, hypothyroidism, and disc disease show up in the breed. A seizure, a sudden reluctance to jump, or a red third eyelid is a clinic visit. Routine vet care is often a relatively gentle $300 to $500 a year; insurance is often $300 to $600. The whole routine year is about $1,450 to $2,900.

Apartments work if the neighbors can live with hound talk and you will walk the dog on a leash every day. Rural homes work if the fence is real. The AKC Beagle page covers the standard; ear and seizure decisions belong with your vet. Repeated vomiting or a lost appetite is laid out on the vomiting guide, and the checker is there when you are standing in the kitchen trying to decide.

Beagle ownership, without the cartoon

Do Beagles bark a lot?

They bay. A hound voice carries through apartment walls and across fences. Training can lower the chatter. It will not turn a beagle into a quiet companion breed.

Are there two sizes?

Yes. The 13-inch variety and the 13- to 15-inch variety are both beagles. Weight usually lands between 20 and 30 pounds.

Can they be off leash?

Rarely, and only in a fenced area. A scent trail outranks your recall. Invisible fence and a rabbit are a bad bet.

What do they cost each year?

The breed card totals about $1,450 to $2,900 for routine care, which is modest next to the brachycephalic breeds. Ear infections and a lost-dog search are the expenses people forget.
