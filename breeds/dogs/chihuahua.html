Small enough to be injured by a hug

Chihuahuas are 2 to 6 pounds and 5 to 8 inches, with a lifespan often 14 to 16 years. They are loyal, noisy, and unaware of the size difference when they challenge a Lab. The stats table is right that children are a variable and that other Chihuahuas are often preferred company. Carry them everywhere and they never learn to walk politely. Let them resource-guard the sofa and you have built a dog that snaps.

Bones break from a drop off the couch or a stepped-on paw. They chill fast. In an open yard a hawk is a real risk to a dog this size. Twenty to thirty minutes of movement is enough exercise. It is not optional just because they live in a bag.

Teeth, knees, trachea, brain

Dental disease is so predictable that the cost lines include $200 to $600 for teeth. Brush them. Crowded mouths lose teeth early. Luxating patellas and Legg-Calvé-Perthes cause the skip and the young-dog hip pain. Collapsing trachea is why the collar stays in the drawer and the harness stays on. Heart murmurs, including valve disease in older dogs and a patent duct in some pups, need a listen at every visit. Hydrocephalus, fluid on the brain, shows up more in extreme apple heads: a dome, a struggle to learn, sometimes seizures. Prominent eyes scratch easily.

A routine year is often about $1,100 to $2,400, one of the lower totals on the dog pages, until a dental or a fracture. VCA's library covers dental disease and tracheal collapse. A broken-looking leg is the limping guide immediately. Wobbly weakness uses the checker.

Chihuahua questions

Is a molera on a Chihuahua dangerous?

A molera is an open fontanel, a soft spot that may never close. It is not the same thing as hydrocephalus, though apple-headed dogs get both. Protect the skull from falls and from other pets. A domed puppy that seems dull or circles needs a veterinarian, not a smaller sweater.

Why do Chihuahuas shake?

Cold, fear, excitement, and low blood sugar can all do it. A sweater helps in winter. A puppy that is wobbly and limp may be hypoglycemic and needs food and a clinic, not a video.

Are Chihuahuas good with children?

Older children who sit down to greet them, sometimes. Toddlers and a 3-pound dog are a fracture waiting to happen. They also bond hard to one person and will defend that lap.
