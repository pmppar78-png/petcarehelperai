White hair and a serious dental bill

Maltese are toy companions, often under 7 pounds, with a lifespan commonly quoted at 12 to 15 years. The coat is hair that mats against the skin if it is not combed. Many owners keep a puppy cut and still have to dry the beard and the sanitary area. Tear staining is housekeeping. Eye pain is not.

They are affectionate and can be sharp when startled. Other dogs are fine if the other dog understands "tiny." Exercise is indoor play plus a short walk. They chill easily. A sweater is reasonable in a cold apartment, and it does not fix a cough.

Shunt, knees, and a collapsing windpipe

Portosystemic shunts, patellar luxation, and tracheal collapse are the three to know by name. A puppy that seems drunk after eating needs a vet, not a theory about teething. Dental extractions are nearly a rite of passage if the mouth is ignored until age six. Merck covers portosystemic shunts. A honking cough on a walk is worth a visit before it becomes a crisis, and the checker helps you sort the night.

Maltese questions

How do you stop tear staining?

Wipe the face daily with a cloth and keep the hair short around the eyes. Staining itself is often cosmetic. A swollen eye or thick discharge is not staining. It is a corneal or a tear-duct problem.

Are they fragile?

Yes. Falls from sofas and rough play break legs. They belong with older children, or with adults who will not carry them on a staircase in socks.

Do they bark?

They alarm-bark. A Maltese that is never taught a quiet will narrate the hallway. The size does not make the noise small.

Hair that keeps growing past the floor

The American Kennel Club standard is a toy companion at 7 to 9 inches, with the weight held under 7 pounds, and a white coat of hair that grows continuously. A puppy cut reduces the daily work and the beard and the sanitary area still need to be dried. Mats form against the skin when the comb stops. Portosystemic shunts, patellar luxation, and tracheal collapse are the three diseases to know by name. A puppy that seems unsteady after a meal needs a veterinarian. Dental disease is common enough that a mouth check by about two years of age beats waiting until extractions are the entire plan. These dogs chill easily, and a sweater in a cold apartment is a reasonable piece of care.
