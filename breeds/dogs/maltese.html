White hair, crowded teeth

Maltese are toy companions of 4 to 7 pounds and 7 to 9 inches, often living 12 to 15 years. The coat is hair, not fur. It grows, it mats against the skin, and the grooming line is often $400 to $1,000 a year unless you keep a short clip and still comb it. They are gentle and playful, better with older children than with kids who run and grab. Other dogs usually go fine if the Maltese is not carried like a fragile purse and then dropped into a scuffle.

Teeth are the predictable failure. The mouth is tiny, tartar arrives early, and many lose teeth if nobody brushes. The cost lines set aside $200 to $600 for dental care on purpose. Start with gauze on a puppy before you need anesthesia on a six-year-old.

Shunts, knees, throats

Portosystemic shunts appear in some lines. A puppy that stays tiny and acts strange after meals needs a bile-acid test. Luxating patellas cause a hop. Collapsing tracheas honk when the dog is excited or yanked by the neck; use a harness. Low blood sugar can flatten a very small puppy that missed breakfast. Tear staining is managed with a wipe and an eye exam, not with a dye that hides an ulcer.

A routine year often totals about $1,500 to $3,200. Trainability is decent and patience helps; they are not robots. VCA covers periodontal disease and liver shunts. A puppy that wobbles after eating, or a dog that cannot breathe comfortably, uses the checker. A hop uses the limping guide.

Maltese questions

Why do Maltese have rusty tear stains?

Porphyrins in tears stain a white coat. Wipe the face daily. Staining plus squinting, redness, or a bad smell is eye or skin disease, not a cosmetic you cover with powder.

Are Maltese hypoallergenic?

They shed very little. People allergic to saliva and dander can still react. A visit with an adult dog tells you more than the stats-table word.

How small is too small?

The breed is already 4 to 7 pounds. A tinier puppy sold as a teacup has more risk of low blood sugar, a collapsing trachea, and broken bones. Skip the size upgrade that is actually a size downgrade.
