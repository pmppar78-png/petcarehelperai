Japanese Spitz Overview

The Japanese Spitz is a small to medium non-sporting breed known for being loyal, intelligent, playful.

Japanese Spitzs are exceptional family dogs that get along wonderfully with children of all ages.

Temperament & Personality

Japanese Spitzs have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Intelligent: Their intelligent nature means they are always eager to please and participate in family activities.
  • Playful: Japanese Spitzs show remarkably playful behavior in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Japanese Spitzs are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Japanese Spitzs are typically friendly with strangers and other dogs.

A Japanese Spitz is a small white spitz with a stand-off coat that blows twice a year. They bark, they shed in clumps, and they are more companion than sled dog. Brush to the skin during the blow or the undercoat felts. Patellar luxation is the small-dog knee issue to mention at the wellness visit. Daily care for a Japanese Spitz is a brush, a walk, and a plan for the bark. They are bright and they will announce every passerby.
