Croatian Sheepdog Overview

The Croatian Sheepdog is a medium herding breed known for being agile, alert, intelligent.

Croatian Sheepdogs are good family dogs that do well with respectful children. Their agile nature makes them ideal for active families who enjoy outdoor activities.
