Norwegian Elkhound Overview

The Norwegian Elkhound is a medium hound breed known for being bold, loyal, playful.

Norwegian Elkhounds are good family dogs that do well with respectful children. Their bold nature makes them ideal for active families who enjoy outdoor activities.

Temperament & Personality

Norwegian Elkhounds have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Bold: This defining trait makes the Norwegian Elkhound a standout companion that bonds deeply with their family.
  • Playful: Norwegian Elkhounds show remarkably playful behavior in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Norwegian Elkhounds are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Norwegian Elkhounds can be selective with strangers but warm up with proper introductions.

Temperament is Bold, Loyal, Playful.

Hip and Joint

The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) reports a hip dysplasia prevalence of approximately 8.4% in evaluated Norwegian Elkhounds (medium breed, typical weight 48-55 lbs).

Norwegian Elkhounds' moderate build and northern heritage provide good baseline joint health. Their weight management is important as the breed tends toward stockiness. Concurrent eye screening for progressive retinal atrophy is recommended.

Exercise Guidelines: Moderate-distance hiking and walking suit the breed's endurance background. Cold-weather activities are well-tolerated. Their natural prey drive provides motivation for varied exercise activities.
