Short years, heavy dog

A Rottweiler is a guardian breed that matures into 80 to 135 pounds and 22 to 27 inches. The lifespan line, 9 to 10 years, is the fact to sit with before the puppy photos. Osteosarcoma, cancer of the bone, is grimly common. A limp that does not fade in a middle-aged Rottweiler needs radiographs, not another week of "he slept wrong." Lymphoma and other tumors show up often enough that a yearly hands-on check of the body is a habit, and insurance bought before the first lump is the only version that helps.

They are loyal and trainable, and the stats table is honest that they need an experienced handler. Same-sex fights are a known pattern. Children can live with a Rottweiler that was taught manners and is never left to "figure the toddler out." A dog of this size that was not socialized becomes everyone's problem, including the dog's.

Heart, joints, airway of the larynx

Subaortic stenosis is a congenital narrowing below the aortic valve. Breeding dogs should have a cardiac exam, not only a hip form. Hips and elbows dysplastic often; cruciate tears follow extra weight. Bloat is possible in this chest: unproductive retching and a swelling abdomen are an emergency drive.

Juvenile laryngeal paralysis and polyneuropathy is a recessive disease you can DNA-test. Affected pups develop noisy breathing and a failing gait. Two carriers should not be bred. The AKC breed list is a fine starting description; the health paperwork is the part that decides whether you stay in the living room.

Budget about $2,700 to $6,300 a year for food, routine care, insurance in the $600 to $1,200 band, and gear, then keep a separate number for oncology. A new limp is the situation on the limping guide. Collapse, a hard stomach, or vomiting that will not stop goes through the checker on the way to the clinic.

Rottweiler questions worth asking

Are Rottweilers good for first-time owners?

Usually no. They learn fast and they also test a soft handler. Same-sex dog friction is common. The right home has already trained a large dog and will keep this one social and busy.

Why is the lifespan listed as 9 to 10 years?

Bone cancer, other tumors, heart defects, and joint disease take dogs early. A promise of a 14-year Rottweiler without health records is a sales line.

What should a Rottweiler breeder show you?

Hip and elbow scores, a cardiac exam for subaortic stenosis, and a DNA result for juvenile laryngeal paralysis and polyneuropathy. Ask who in the last two generations died of cancer and at what age.

How much does a Rottweiler cost in a year?

Food alone is often $800 to $1,500. The full routine year on this profile runs about $2,700 to $6,300 before a cancer workup or a cruciate repair.
