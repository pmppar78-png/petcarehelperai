Hip and Joint Health in the Belgian Tervuren

The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) reports a hip dysplasia prevalence of approximately 5.2% in evaluated Belgian Tervuren (medium-large breed, typical weight 45-75 lbs).

Belgian Tervuren share similar orthopedic profiles with other Belgian shepherd varieties. Their herding and protection work creates diverse joint stress patterns. Epilepsy is a concurrent breed concern that may affect activity management.

Exercise Guidelines: Varied herding and obedience activities distribute stress across multiple joints. Their intelligence responds well to structured rehabilitation exercises when joint management is needed.
