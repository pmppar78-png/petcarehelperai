Belgian Tervuren Overview

The Belgian Tervuren is a large herding breed known for being courageous, alert, devoted.

Belgian Tervuren are good family dogs that do well with respectful children. Their courageous nature makes them ideal for active families who enjoy outdoor activities.

Temperament & Personality

Belgian Tervuren have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Courageous: This defining trait makes the Belgian Tervuren a standout companion that bonds deeply with their family.
  • Devoted: Belgian Tervuren show remarkable devoted in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Belgian Tervuren are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Belgian Tervuren can be selective with strangers but warm up with proper introductions.

Belgian Tervuren have a dense double coat that sheds year-round with heavier blows in spring and fall — regular brushing is non-negotiable, and many owners schedule professional grooming during peak shedding seasons. Their intensity and high work drive mean training is a consistent investment; without clear structure and mental challenges, behavioral problems can emerge that cost more to address later. Hip dysplasia and progressive retinal atrophy are the primary health screens recommended by the breed community.

Hip and Joint

The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) reports a hip dysplasia prevalence of approximately 5.2% in evaluated Belgian Tervuren (medium-large breed, typical weight 45-75 lbs).

Belgian Tervuren share similar orthopedic profiles with other Belgian shepherd varieties. Their herding and protection work creates diverse joint stress patterns. Epilepsy is a concurrent breed concern that may affect activity management.

Exercise Guidelines: Varied herding and obedience activities distribute stress across multiple joints. Their intelligence responds well to structured rehabilitation exercises when joint management is needed.
