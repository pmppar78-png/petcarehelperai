Hip and Joint Health in the Portuguese Water Dog

The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) reports a hip dysplasia prevalence of approximately 12.6% in evaluated Portuguese Water Dogs (medium breed, typical weight 35-60 lbs).

Portuguese Water Dogs have moderate dysplasia risk. Their swimming heritage makes aquatic therapy a natural fit for joint management. GM1 gangliosidosis (a storage disease) should also be screened for via DNA testing.

Exercise Guidelines: Swimming is the ideal exercise — it's in the breed's DNA. Water work provides excellent joint conditioning without impact stress. Regular swimming builds hip-stabilizing muscle groups naturally.
