Thirteen pounds of full-time work

Jack Russell terriers are about 10 to 15 inches and 13 to 17 pounds, often living 13 to 16 years. They are fearless, loud, and clever. Other dogs are a project because many Russells start fights they cannot finish. Children need to be old enough not to treat a terrier like a wind-up toy. Prey drive means the hamster, the cat, and the squirrel are not friends by default.

Exercise is one to two hours and a job: earthdog, agility, or a flirt pole used with rules. A walk to the mailbox is an insult. They dig out of fences. Hardware cloth along the bottom is part of the yard.

Eyes, knees, and a short leg bone

Primary lens luxation is the emergency and the DNA test. Patellar luxation and Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease make a small dog skip. Deafness occurs. Teeth crowd. VCA explains lens luxation. A cloudy, painful eye the same afternoon it starts is the visit, and the checker should push you toward the car.

Jack Russell terrier questions

Are Jack Russells apartment dogs?

Only if the hours of running and training happen anyway. The size fits. The brain does not. A bored Russell digs, barks, and kills the household routine.

What is lens luxation?

The lens slips out of place and the eye becomes painful and cloudy. It is an emergency, and the breed has a DNA test for the primary form. A squinting terrier is not 'just dramatic.'

Jack, Parson, or Russell?

Registries split these terriers by height and by club. Ask which standard the parents were bred to. The care is similar. The adult size is not identical.

Two club standards under an old shared name

The 10 to 15 inch and 13 to 17 pound span covers two American Kennel Club breeds that once shared a name in casual speech. The Parson Russell terrier is the taller, squarer dog, and the Russell terrier is the shorter one. Match the dog in front of you to that club's standard. Primary lens luxation is the eye emergency, and ADAMTS17 is the DNA test breeders run for it. Patellar luxation, Legg-Calve-Perthes disease, deafness, and crowded teeth fill out the health list. The work these terriers need runs one to two hours and includes a job, because a walk to the mailbox leaves the brain underemployed. Hardware cloth along the bottom of the fence is part of the yard, since digging out is a hobby.
