Dogue de Bordeaux Overview

The Dogue de Bordeaux is a large working breed known for being loyal, affectionate, courageous.

Dogues de Bordeaux are good family dogs that do well with respectful children.

A Dogue de Bordeaux is a short-faced giant. Heat, hips, heart disease, bloat, and facial folds are the care. Lifespan is shorter than a Labrador's. Do not jog a puppy or leave the dog in a warm room to toughen up. A red eye from an in-turned lid needs a vet before the cornea scars.
