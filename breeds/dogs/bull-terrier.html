Comic, muscular, and medically odd

Bull terriers stand about 21 to 22 inches and weigh 50 to 70 pounds. Lifespan is often 12 to 13 years. They are playful and stubborn. Other dogs are a serious management issue for many adults. Children can do well with supervision, because the play style is body-slamming even when the mood is friendly. Exercise is one to two hours. Training sessions stay short, because the independent streak ends a long lecture.

The short coat is easy. The skin is not always easy. Allergies and a tendency to fixate on a toy or a tail are the daily picture in some lines. A fenced yard matters. These dogs are strong enough to pull a person off balance.

Kidneys, ears, and skin that is not a rash

Hereditary nephritis and polycystic kidney disease show up. A young bull terrier that drinks and urinates more than its littermates needs bloodwork and urine, not a bigger water bowl as the whole plan. Deafness testing, heart checks, and patellar exams belong in the puppy paperwork. Merck discusses hereditary nephritis. Increased thirst is also a reason to open the symptom notes and book labs.

Bull terrier questions

Are bull terriers deaf?

White bull terriers have a higher rate of deafness. A BAER test tells you whether one ear or both are dead. A dog deaf in both ears can live well and cannot be trained with a shout across a yard.

What is lethal acrodermatitis?

A fatal genetic disease of bull terrier puppies, with skin lesions and a failure to thrive. It is not the ordinary allergy. A breeder should be able to talk about it.

Why does my bull terrier chase its tail?

Some spin as play. Compulsive spinning that interrupts life can be medical or behavioral, and it is known in the breed. Film it and take it to a vet before you laugh it off for a year.
