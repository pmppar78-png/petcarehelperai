Lagotto Romagnolo Overview

The Lagotto Romagnolo is an ancient Italian breed originally developed as a water retriever in the marshlands of Romagna, Italy. When the marshes were drained in the 19th century, the breed transitioned to its current role as the world's finest truffle hunting dog. "Lagotto" comes from "Lago" (lake), reflecting its water dog heritage.

With their distinctive curly, wool-like coat and teddy bear appearance, Lagotti (plural) have gained popularity as family companions. They're known for their exceptional nose, intelligence, and affectionate nature. The breed combines working drive with a loving temperament, making them versatile companions for active families.

Temperament & Personality

The Lagotto Romagnolo has a wonderfully balanced temperament.

  • Affectionate & Loving: Lagotti form strong bonds with their families and enjoy being close to their people at all times.
  • Intelligent & Trainable: Bred for complex work finding truffles, they're quick learners who enjoy mental challenges.
  • Moderate Energy: Active when working or playing but can settle well indoors. Not hyperactive.
  • Alert but Not Aggressive: They'll alert to visitors but are generally friendly once introduced.
  • Strong Work Ethic: They love having a job, whether that's truffle hunting, scent work, or other activities.

Common Health Issues

Lagotto Romagnolo are generally healthy but have some breed-specific concerns.

Neurological Conditions

  • Benign Familial Juvenile Epilepsy (BFJE): A hereditary condition causing seizures in puppies. Most outgrow it by 8-13 weeks. DNA test available.
  • Lagotto Storage Disease (LSD): A fatal neurological disease appearing around 4 months. DNA test available - always verify parents are tested.

hip and joint issues

  • Hip Dysplasia: Screen breeding dogs. Occurs at moderate rates in the breed.
  • Elbow Dysplasia: Less common but should be screened in breeding stock.

Other Concerns

  • Juvenile Cataracts: Can occur in young dogs. Eye exams recommended.
  • Ear Infections: Curly hair in ears can trap moisture. Regular cleaning essential.
  • Incorrect Coat: Some dogs develop overly soft coats prone to matting.

Health Screening Recommendation

Essential: Ask breeders for DNA tests for BFJE and LSD (Lagotto Storage Disease). These are serious genetic conditions with available tests. Also verify hip evaluations and eye exams. .

Exercise & Activity Requirements

  • Daily Exercise: 60+ minutes of activity including walks, play, and training
  • Swimming: Many Lagotti love water due to their retriever heritage
  • Scent Work: Outstanding at nose work, tracking, and truffle hunting
  • Fetch & Retrieval: Natural retrievers who enjoy fetch games
  • Mental Stimulation: Puzzle toys and training sessions are essential

Training Tips for

Lagotti are highly trainable and eager to work: A care plan fitted to this particular Lagotto Romagnolo almost always produces better behavior and better health markers.

  • Start Early: Begin training and socialization as soon as you bring puppy home.
  • Positive Methods: They respond wonderfully to reward-based training.
  • Keep It Interesting: Lagotti are smart and can get bored with repetition.
  • Harness Their Nose: Incorporate scent games into training for maximum engagement.
  • Prevent Digging: Their truffle-hunting instincts may lead to digging. Provide appropriate outlets.

Nutrition & Feeding

  • Quality Protein: Support their active lifestyle with good protein sources.
  • Omega Fatty Acids: Important for maintaining their curly coat health.
  • Moderate Calories: Adjust portions based on activity level to prevent weight gain.
  • Joint Support: Consider supplements with glucosamine for joint health.

Grooming Requirements

The Lagotto's curly coat requires regular maintenance.

  • Professional Grooming: Every 6-8 weeks for trimming and maintenance
  • Rustic Look: Coat should look natural, not sculpted. Avoid over-grooming.
  • Ear Care: Regular cleaning and hair removal from ear canals to prevent infections

Lagotti Are Great For:

  • Those interested in dog sports like scent work or agility
  • Families with children (patient and playful)
  • Owners who will provide mental stimulation

Lagotti May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Those who dislike regular grooming requirements
  • Sedentary households without time for exercise
  • People who travel frequently without their dog
  • Those wanting a guard dog (they're too friendly)
  • Owners unable to provide mental challenges

People who live with a Lagotto Romagnolo tend to develop a deep appreciation for the breed's personality — the affectionate, keen, undemanding, trainable nature becomes part of the household's rhythm.
