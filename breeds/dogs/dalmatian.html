Spots, stamina, and a urine problem

Dalmatians stand about 19 to 24 inches and weigh 45 to 70 pounds. They are active coaching dogs, not couch statues. An under-exercised Dalmatian is a shedder that barks and paces. Families who actually move do well. Families who wanted a spotted ornament do not.

They can be good with kids and with other dogs if socialized. The coat sheds all year. Short hair still covers the house. They are not hypoallergenic, and the famous movie did not include the ear cleaning or the urine crystals.

BAER tests and a blocked male

Buy from someone who BAER-tests and who will talk about urate stones. A male Dalmatian that strains and produces no urine is an emergency obstruction. Hip dysplasia and skin allergies are the other common items. VCA explains bladder stones. Straining is also the moment to use the checker only if you are already on the way to a clinic.

Dalmatian questions

Are Dalmatian puppies born spotted?

They are born white. The spots come in over the first weeks. A puppy that stays mostly white can still hear, and a heavily spotted puppy can still be deaf. Color is not a hearing test.

How do you test hearing?

A BAER test. Clapping in the kitchen is not a diagnosis. Deaf Dalmatians live well with hand signals and a leash, and they should not be off leash near a road.

Why do they form stones?

Most Dalmatians excrete uric acid in the urine in a way other dogs do not. Stones can block a male dog. Diet changes belong to a vet who has seen the urine, not to a forum recipe.

The SLC2A9 variant and the BAER test

Many Dalmatians carry a mutation in SLC2A9 that changes how the body handles uric acid. Urate stones are the clinical result, and a veterinarian is who chooses any diet change that follows. A male that strains and produces no urine is an emergency obstruction. BAER testing is the hearing check, because deafness associated with the spotted coat can sit in one ear or in both. Puppies are born white and the spots come in over the first weeks. The dog those tests apply to stands about 19 to 24 inches and weighs about 45 to 70 pounds, and it needs real daily work. Hip dysplasia and skin allergy are the other items that show up often enough to expect them on a clinic list.
