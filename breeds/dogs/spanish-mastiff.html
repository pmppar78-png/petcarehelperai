Temperament & Personality

Spanish Mastiffs have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Determined: Their determined nature means they are always eager to please and participate in family activities.
  • Noble: Spanish Mastiffs show remarkable noble in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Spanish Mastiffs are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Calm: Their energy level is low, requiring gentle daily walks and some playtime.
  • Social: Spanish Mastiffs can be selective with strangers but warm up with proper introductions.
