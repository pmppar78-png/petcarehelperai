Same dog, two coats

Collies stand about 22 to 26 inches and weigh 50 to 75 pounds, with a lifespan around 12 to 14 years. Rough or smooth is a grooming decision: the long coat needs real brushing and often $300 to $600 a year, and the short coat does not make the eyes or the drug sensitivity go away. They are devoted, often excellent with children, and brighter than the old television joke. They also bark, herd, and take a harsh voice personally.

Exercise on the stats table is moderate, about an hour. That can be a walk plus training. It cannot be nothing because the dog looks calm in a photo. Shedding on the rough variety is a seasonal event that fills a vacuum.

Eyes and a drug list

Collie eye anomaly is a developmental defect you can DNA-test. Severity runs from a small scar in the back of the eye to detachment and blindness. Progressive retinal atrophy is separate and also testable. MDR1 is common in both coats. Do the test before a surgery date, and do not dose ivermectin from a farm store. Dermatomyositis, the skin-and-muscle disease shared with Shelties, shows up as scarring on the face. Hips, bloat, and epilepsy are the rest of the file worth asking about.

A routine year often totals about $1,800 to $3,250. Merck describes collie eye anomaly and MDR1 without the Lassie soundtrack. A new limp or a seizure uses the limping page and the checker.

Collie questions

What is the difference between a rough and a smooth Collie?

Coat. Rough Collies have the long mane. Smooth Collies have a short coat and the same skull, the same MDR1 risk, and the same eye disease. They are one breed. Grooming cost follows the coat you actually bought.

Why does MDR1 matter before a vet visit?

Many Collies carry an ABCB1 mutation that lets certain parasite drugs, sedatives, and an antidiarrheal reach the brain. A DNA result in the chart prevents a dangerous dose. Tell the clinic even if the visit seems routine.

Are Collies good with children?

Usually, if the household is not chaotic and someone gives them about an hour of exercise plus a job that is not herding the toddler. They are sensitive. Hard corrections make them shut down.
