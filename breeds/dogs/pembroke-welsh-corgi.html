A short herding dog with a long back

Pembroke Welsh corgis stand about 10 to 12 inches and weigh 25 to 30 pounds. Lifespan is often 12 to 15 years. They are bold, loud, and convinced the hallway is a flock that should move. The double coat sheds in clumps twice a year and lightly the rest of the time. A rake undercoat tool matters more than a bath.

They do well with kids who are not frightened of a dog that comments on everything, and they may try to herd those kids. Exercise is a real hour, not a lap of the kitchen. Stairs are fine for a fit adult and a bad idea as a repeated jump from the couch.

Disc disease is not myelopathy

A Pembroke that screams and will not stand may have a disc extrusion. That is an emergency, especially if the toes no longer feel a pinch. Degenerative myelopathy comes on slowly, without pain, and a DNA test for SOD1 tells you risk rather than the whole diagnosis. Hip dysplasia and eye disease are on the breeder list too. VCA explains disc disease. Sudden weakness is the limping and weakness page.

Pembroke Welsh corgi questions

Are corgis bad for their backs?

The body shape raises the risk of a slipped disc. Jumping off beds, extra weight, and tug games that wrench the neck add to it. A ramp is cheaper than spinal surgery.

What is the difference between IVDD and DM?

Intervertebral disc disease is a sudden, painful disc injury. Degenerative myelopathy is a slow, painless loss of coordination tied to an SOD1 variant. They are not the same disease and they are not treated the same way.

Do they herd children?

Often. Nipping ankles is the breed doing its old job. Teach a settle, and do not reward the nip by running away.

People say corgi and almost always mean the Pembroke: 10 to 12 inches, 25 to 30 pounds, often living 12 to 15 years, with little or no tail. The Cardigan is the other corgi, larger, with a tail, and it should not be shoved into a Pembroke care plan or a Pembroke breeding. If a seller cannot say which breed the papers name, walk away.

Pembrokes are friendly and busy. They comment on the mail carrier. They will herd a toddler. Training is usually easy if you are more interesting than the squirrel, and it falls apart if the dog is bored. A puzzle toy and a short walk do not replace a job, but they cover a Tuesday.
