Keeshond Overview

The Keeshond is a medium non-sporting breed known for being friendly, outgoing, lively.

Keeshonds are exceptional family dogs that get along wonderfully with children of all ages.

Temperament & Personality

Keeshonds have a distinctive personality that endears them to their owners.

  • Outgoing: Their outgoing nature means they are always eager to please and participate in family activities.
  • Lively: Keeshonds show remarkable lively in various situations and environments.
  • Alert: Keeshonds are naturally watchful and will alert their families to unusual activity.
  • Social: Keeshonds are typically friendly with strangers and other dogs.

Over a 12-15 years span, weigh a Friendly, Outgoing, Lively Keeshond against 17-18 in and against Dental issues.
