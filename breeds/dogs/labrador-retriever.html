Two Labradors, one name

Labs were the AKC's most registered breed for 31 years, through 2022, and then French Bulldogs passed them. Popularity did not make them one dog. A field-line Lab is a lean retrieving machine that wants a job after breakfast. A bench-line Lab is broader, often calmer in the house, and still a sporting dog. Black, yellow, and chocolate are the accepted colors. A "silver" puppy is a dilute, not a fourth breed, and dilute coats can come with skin trouble.

Adults land about 55 to 80 pounds and 21.5 to 24.5 inches, with a dense water-shedding coat that dumps hair on the sofa. Lifespan is often 10 to 12 years. They are listed as excellent with children and other dogs, which holds when someone is actually training them. A Lab left to invent a hobby will choose socks, corn cobs, and the bathroom trash.

The hunger is partly genetic

A 2016 paper in Cell Metabolism found a POMC deletion in a large share of Labradors that weakens the signal to stop eating. The same variant shows up in flat-coated retrievers. That is why "just feed less" fails if the cup is not measured. Feel the ribs. Look for a waist from above. Extra fat loads the hips, elbows, and cruciate ligaments, and it shortens the years you get.

Puppy-proof for swallowing, not for china. Foreign-body surgery is a Lab classic. Use part of the measured ration as training food so treats are not a second dinner. VCA's pet library has a plain page on obesity and on gastrointestinal foreign bodies if you want the clinical list.

Joints, ears, and collapse

Hip and elbow dysplasia are common enough that breeding dogs should have orthopedic scores, not a shrug. Stiffness after rest around middle age is arthritis until a vet says otherwise. Ear infections follow the floppy ear and the swimming. Dry the canals after water. A weekly look beats a monthly head-shake.

Exercise-induced collapse is a DNM1 mutation: a fit dog can wobble and go down after hard play, then recover. DNA testing identifies it. Progressive retinal atrophy (prcd) and centronuclear myopathy are the other results worth holding in your hand before you pay a deposit. Intense fetch in the heat is a poor test of a dog you have not screened.

Food, routine care, insurance, grooming, and gear often run about $1,800 to $4,300 a year before a knee surgery or an after-hours endoscopy. Repeated vomiting, or a dog that ate something it should not have, belongs on the dog vomiting guide and the urgency checker.

Labrador questions

Why is my Labrador always hungry?

A 2016 Cell Metabolism paper described a POMC deletion that is common in Labradors and blunts the stop-eating signal. Measured meals and a locked trash can are the management, not a longer lecture.

What is the difference between field and bench Labs?

Field, often called American, lines are usually leaner and busier, bred toward hunt tests. Bench, often called English, lines are stockier and often settle sooner. Both are Labradors. Meet the parents and watch the energy, because coat color does not pick the line.

Which DNA tests matter before buying a Lab?

Ask for hip and elbow scores, an eye exam, and DNA results for exercise-induced collapse, prcd progressive retinal atrophy, and centronuclear myopathy. A breeder who cannot show those is guessing.

How much exercise does a Labrador need?

The stats table lists 1 to 2 hours a day. Swimming, retrieve with a stop-and-wait, and sniffing work burn energy. Endless ball launching can make an obsessive dog rather than a tired one.
