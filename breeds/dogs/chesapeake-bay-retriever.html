Hip and Joint Health in the Chesapeake Bay Retriever

The Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) reports a hip dysplasia prevalence of approximately 17.1% in evaluated Chesapeake Bay Retrievers (large breed, typical weight 55-80 lbs).

Chessies have a higher dysplasia rate than other retrievers, possibly related to their powerful, muscular build developed for cold-water retrieval. Their waterproof coat and love of swimming make aquatic therapy a natural fit.

Exercise Guidelines: Swimming is ideal — the breed was literally made for it. Cold-water conditioning builds joint-supporting musculature. Limit high-impact land activities during growth phases.
