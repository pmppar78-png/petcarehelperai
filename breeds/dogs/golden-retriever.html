A retrieving dog that got marketed as furniture

Golden Retrievers were built in Scotland to pick up shot birds in cold water. The modern pet still has that mouth, that coat, and that need to be doing something with a person. Friendly, intelligent, and devoted is an accurate temperament line. It does not mean the adolescent dog will nap while you finish a work call.

Males often land around 65 to 75 pounds, females closer to 55 to 65, with the height band in the quick facts of 21.5 to 24 inches. Field-bred dogs are usually leaner and hotter to work than the heavier show lines. Either way, expect a large sporting dog with a high exercise need and a 10- to 12-year lifespan.

Work the dog or the dog will invent a job

A useful day has a morning walk long enough to loosen the dog, a training fragment (retrieve to hand, place, loose-leash), and an evening outing. Swimming is kind to joints if the dog is taught an exit and you skip blue-green algae. Endless chuck-it sessions can wreck shoulders and build an obsessive dog. Mix in sniffing.

Goldens are excellent with kids and other dogs when they are tired and taught manners. A bored golden mouths hands, countersurfs, and greets guests by launching. Trainability is genuinely high. Use it early, before the 70-pound body decides that jumping is a greeting.

The health record you should read before the puppy photo

Ask for hips, elbows, eyes, and a heart clearance. Subaortic stenosis shows up in the breed, and hip dysplasia is common enough that a parent without an OFA or PennHIP number is a gamble. Ichthyosis, a flaky skin condition, has a DNA test in some lines.

Cancer is the part nobody puts in the shelter ad. Hemangiosarcoma can look like a normal Tuesday until a dog collapses from a bleeding spleen. Lymphoma and mast cell tumors are also over-represented. None of that makes the breed a mistake. It means a $500 to $1,000 insurance line on the cost card is rational, and a sudden pale gum or a bloated, restless abdomen is an emergency rather than a wait-until-Monday problem. The Merck Veterinary Manual is the reference clinicians use; your own vet is the one who knows this dog.

Ears infect easily. Dry the canals after swimming. The coat mats behind the ears and in the feathering if you only brush the back.

What you will spend

Food for a large, active dog runs roughly $600 to $1,200 a year, and the full routine year lands around $2,100 to $4,800. Grooming at a salon is optional if you brush and bathe at home, until the feathers felt into a pelt. Puppies from health-tested parents are often $2,000 to $3,500. A rescue golden can be cheaper up front and still carry the same cancer and joint odds.

Apartments work only when the person is the exercise plan. A first-floor door and a nearby field matter more than square footage. If you are unsure whether a limp or a vomiting spell can wait, the vet checker sorts urgent from monitor, and the limping guide covers the joint side.

Golden Retriever questions that change the decision

How much exercise does a Golden Retriever need?

Plan on one to two hours a day, split between a real walk, retrieve or swim, and a brain task such as scent work. A yard the dog paces in is not a substitute.

Why do people talk about cancer and this breed?

Goldens have higher rates of hemangiosarcoma, lymphoma, and mast cell tumors than many other breeds. It is a reason to keep insurance, learn what sudden collapse looks like, and buy from breeders who track how their older dogs died.

Do Goldens shed enough to matter?

Yes. The double coat sheds hard in spring and fall and drops hair the rest of the year. Weekly brushing is maintenance; a deshedding session is a seasonal chore, not an optional spa day.

Are they a good first dog?

They are a good first dog for someone who likes training and has time every day. They are a poor first dog for a household that wants a calm pet by eight months old.
