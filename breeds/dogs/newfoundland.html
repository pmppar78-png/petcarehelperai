A water dog the size of a person

Newfoundlands weigh about 100 to 150 pounds and stand 26 to 28 inches. Lifespan is often 9 to 10 years, which is the giant-breed math people skip while looking at the sweet face. They are usually gentle with children and other dogs, moderate in exercise, 30 to 60 minutes, and miserable in heat. The double coat sheds in a way that will ruin a dark sofa. Drool is a feature. If that sentence bothers you, this is the wrong dog.

They swim as if the water were the original living room. A life that includes a safe shore fits them. A life that is a hot patio and a food bowl does not. Slips on hardwood are how giants tear cruciates; runners and rugs are equipment.

Aortic stenosis and stones

Subaortic stenosis is the congenital heart disease of this breed. Ask for a cardiologist clearance on the parents, not a breeder's "the puppy vet listened." Fainting, exercise intolerance, or a murmur that was never imaged needs a specialist. Cystinuria, an inherited stone disease, has a DNA test in Newfoundlands and shows up as straining or blood in the urine. Hips, elbows, and bloat are the other large-dog bills. A hard abdomen and unproductive retching is the same emergency it is in any deep chest.

VCA explains subaortic stenosis in plain language. Collapse or retching uses the checker on the way to the hospital. A limp that lasts uses the limping guide.

Newfoundland questions

Are Newfoundlands nanny dogs?

They are famously patient with children, and a calm adult often earns that reputation. A teenager of this size still knocks people down and drools on them. Patience is not a supervision plan.

What heart problem do Newfoundlands get?

Subaortic stenosis, a narrowing below the aortic valve. Breeding dogs should have a cardiologist's exam. A puppy murmur is not something to outgrow without imaging. Affected dogs can faint or die suddenly.

How much does the coat and the food cost?

Food is often $1,000 to $2,000 a year. Grooming and shedding tools add $400 to $800. A routine year on this profile runs about $3,100 to $6,100 before a heart workup or a bloat surgery.
