Gentle, and enormous

English mastiffs stand about 27 to 30 inches and can weigh 120 to 230 pounds. The lifespan is 6 to 10 years. They are dignified and often good with children and other dogs when socialized. Exercise is a half hour of walking, not jogging. Puppies should stay lean while the bones grow. Pushing a mastiff puppy to look impressive is how you buy dysplasia.

They drool, they snore, and they take up a couch by existing. Floors need traction. A mastiff that slips on hardwood can tear a ligament standing up. Boarding, grooming tubs, and some clinic tables are simply too small. Ask before you need them.

Stomach, heart, hips

Discuss gastropexy when the dog is altered. It does not make bloat impossible, and it makes a twist less likely. A swollen belly with unproductive retching is still an emergency drive. Dilated cardiomyopathy and hip dysplasia belong on the adult vet plan. Merck describes gastric dilatation-volvulus. The vomiting page is the household copy of that emergency.

English mastiff questions

How long do English mastiffs live?

Often 6 to 10 years. Buy one only if that calendar is acceptable. Cancer, bloat, and orthopedic disease are common reasons the life is short.

Are they protective?

They are imposing and usually steady. A mastiff is a poor choice if you want a dog that will be trained to attack. Size plus poor manners is already a lawsuit.

What does ownership cost?

Food for a giant dog, a car that fits, and insurance. A routine year is expensive before anyone mentions a gastropexy or a cruciate repair.
