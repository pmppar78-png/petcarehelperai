Californian Rabbit Overview

The Californian Rabbit is a large (8-11 lbs) rabbit that makes an excellent pet for beginners and families.

Californian Rabbits thrive with companionship and are best kept with compatible cage mates.

A Californian rabbit is a large domestic rabbit with a white body and dark points, bred as a commercial meat rabbit and also kept as a pet. It is not a California kingsnake, not a reptile, and not a wild cottontail from the state of California. This card lists 8 to 11 pounds, a lifespan of 5 to 10 years, moderate activity, beginner care, and a minimum pen of 4 by 2 feet. The points sit on the nose, ears, feet, and tail. The rest of the coat stays white if the rabbit is actually this breed.

Large means the pen and the hay bill, not a bigger version of a dwarf that can live in a cage sold for a guinea pig. The 4 by 2 foot line is a minimum floor. A meat-breed body needs room to lie stretched out, and a wire grid under an 8 to 11 pound rabbit cuts the hocks. Solid flooring or deep bedding is part of the setup. Social needs are pairs or groups. Bond a rabbit to a rabbit. Do not add a snake, a guinea pig, or a cat and call it companionship.

Training Advice

Hay, pellets, and vegetables are the diet line, and hay is the part that has to be unlimited. The teeth never stop growing. A Californian on a pellet-heavy ration gets fat and still manages to grow molar spurs, which is a poor trade. Leafy greens can join the hay. A bowl of carrots and yogurt drops cannot. Five to ten years is a wide lifespan band. The short end of it arrives early when the diet is sweet and the floor is wire.

Grooming Essentials

Beginner on the card is about a sturdy domestic rabbit with ordinary rabbit rules, not about an animal that tolerates neglect. They are moderate in activity and still need time out of the pen to move. Meat-breed history means a full body and a fast juvenile growth rate. Feed a growing Californian enough hay and a sensible pellet portion. Do not free-feed a rich mix until it cannot groom its own rear.

Temperament & Personality

The name collision with the kingsnake is worth killing off once. A white rabbit with dark points does not need a heat lamp, a hide box at 85 F, or a diet of mice. It needs hay, floor space, and a vet who treats rabbits. If the animal in the photo is scaled and limbless, it is on the wrong page. Five to ten years of a large rabbit is the commitment this card is actually describing.
