Dwarf Hotot Rabbit Overview

The Dwarf Hotot Rabbit is a small (2.5-3.5 lbs) rabbit that makes an excellent pet for beginners and families.

A dwarf Hotot is a small white rabbit with dark bands around the eyes. It is not a full-size Blanc de Hotot, which carries a similar eyeliner mark on a much larger body. Mixing the names puts the wrong adult weight in the plan. This card lists a small adult, 2.5 to 3.5 pounds, a lifespan of 7 to 10 years, moderate activity, beginner care, and a minimum of 4 by 2 feet. Seven to ten years is a long time for a rabbit that looks like it could live in a carrier.

Hay, pellets, and vegetables are the diet, and hay is the staple. A 2.5 to 3.5 pound rabbit has the same continuously growing teeth as a giant. The mouth is smaller, so spurs and a dropped appetite show up on a body with less reserve. Keep grass hay in front of them all day. Measure the pellets. A dwarf that free-feeds a rich mix becomes a round rabbit that cannot reach its own cecotropes, and those missed cecotropes are food, not trash.

Training Advice

The floor should be solid, not wire. A light rabbit still gets sore hocks on a grid, and the 4 by 2 foot minimum is the floor size, not a suggestion that a wire breeding cage from a shed is close enough.
