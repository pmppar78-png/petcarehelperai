Recommended for Gerbils

Desert rats that need a tunnel

Gerbils weigh about 2 to 4 ounces and often live 3 to 5 years. They are awake in bursts through the day and evening. A 10-gallon tank with an inch of bedding is not enough. They want deep bedding to dig, a wheel, and a lid, because they chew plastic and they kick bedding out of short cages.

Pairs do better than singles. Watch for a gerbil that is barbered bald or sleeping apart and bleeding. That pair is over. Seizures happen in some lines and look terrifying. A one-off seizure still deserves a note to a vet. A gerbil that is seizing and not recovering needs help the same day.

Scent glands, teeth, and the California rule

The orange scent gland on the belly is normal. A sore that grows is not. Teeth overgrow if the gerbil cannot chew. A fat face can be an impacted cheek pouch. ASPCA covers small-mammal basics. A gerbil that ignores food is small enough to crash quickly, which is why the not-eating page matters.

Gerbil questions

Can gerbils live alone?

They can, and many are miserable that way. A same-sex pair from a young age is the usual plan. Adult strangers often fight. Do not add a new gerbil to a grieving one without a careful split cage.

Why are they illegal in some places?

California bans gerbils as pets. Other states do not. Check your state before you treat a pet-store tank as permission.

What is a degloved tail?

The skin can pull off a gerbil tail if you grab it. Never pick a gerbil up by the tail. The injury often means amputating what is left.
