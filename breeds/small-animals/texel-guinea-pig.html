Texel Guinea Pig Overview

The Texel Guinea Pig is a medium (1.5-2.5 lbs) guinea pig that makes a wonderful pet for those with some small animal experience.

Texel Guinea Pigs thrive with companionship and are best kept with compatible cage mates.

A texel guinea pig is a long-haired guinea pig with a curly coat. It is not a texel sheep, and it is not a silkie. The sheep is a livestock breed that shares a name. The silkie, or sheltie, is a guinea pig whose long hair is straighter and sweeps back off the face. The texel curls. Size on the card is medium, 1.5-2.5 lbs. . Care level is intermediate because of that coat. People who search the name and expect either a sheep or a smooth longhair will mismanage the hair in front of them.

The curls mat. High grooming need is the whole intermediate rating. A texel that is combed once a week and otherwise left to look charming will felt. Mats pull on the skin, collect urine, and hide sores. The curl is tighter than a silkie's wave, so a routine copied from a silkie is often not enough. Work the coat down to the skin often enough that you never discover a mat by accident. Bathing a felted curl makes the mat worse. Cut the problem out only if you can do it without the skin, or have someone do it who has done a texel before. This is not a sheep shear on a schedule. It is a guinea pig that mats.

Training Advice

The diet is hay, pellets, vegetables, and vitamin C. The curls do not change the fact that a guinea pig needs vitamin C and needs hay in the pen all day. Fruit juice in a curly coat is how mats start in earnest. Keep treats small and the hay constant. A texel at 1.5-2.5 lbs is easy to overfeed because the coat adds visual bulk. Weigh the animal. A dense coat over a thin body is the opposite problem, and you will not see it if you only look at the silhouette. Intermediate care is the comb and the scale, plus the ordinary cavy diet, for 5-7 years.

Grooming Essentials

Social needs are social, and the card says to keep them in pairs. Space is 7.5 sq ft minimum. That minimum is tight once two curly coats are sharing it, and a pair is what the social line is asking for. One texel alone in 7.5 sq ft meets the floor and misses the pair. Two texels need enough room to pass, and both need the grooming. A cagemate will not keep the curls open. Activity is moderate. They are not a high-drive animal whose coat you can ignore because they are busy. They are a medium guinea pig that sits in its own hair.
