English Angora Rabbit Overview

The English Angora Rabbit is a medium (5-7.5 lbs) rabbit that makes a rewarding but challenging pet for experienced keepers. Their low activity level and social social nature make them engaging and entertaining companions.

The English Angora is the wool rabbit with furnishings on the face and the ears, a coated head rather than the cleaner face of a French Angora. This card lists a medium adult, 5 to 7.5 pounds, a lifespan of 7 to 12 years, low activity, advanced care, and a minimum of 4 by 2 feet. . The furnishings are the breed point and the husbandry problem. Wool grows into the eyes, the ear edges, and the cheeks, and it mats there faster than a keeper expects from a body that only weighs about 5 to 7.5 pounds.

Grooming is heavier than on a French Angora. The French coat is a job. The English coat adds the head. Miss the face and the eye weeps under a curtain of fiber. Miss the ears and the tips felt into hard plugs. A full comb-through on a schedule, not a pat on the top of the cloud, is the advanced label on this card. Low activity means they will not exercise a mat out of the coat. They will sit, eat, and grow more wool on top of the tangle.
