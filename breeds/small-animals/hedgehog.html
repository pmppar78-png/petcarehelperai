The pet hedgehog in the United States is usually an African pygmy hybrid, Atelerix albiventris and its crosses, not the European hedgehog of garden stories. Adults weigh about half a pound to a pound and a half and often live 4 to 6 years, sometimes longer. They are nocturnal, solitary, and intermediate in care level because of heat, diet, and the number of clinics that will see them.

Warm floor, insects, a wheel

Give a solid-floor enclosure of at least 2 by 4 feet. Smooth sides or a lid, because they climb and fall. Keep the room or the cage around 72 to 80°F with a thermostat. Below that, they can slip into torpor. Bedding should be paper or fleece that cannot wrap a toe; avoid wire floors. A solid wheel, around 12 inches, lets them run without twisting a back.

Feed a high-quality insectivore diet or a low-fat cat food as a base, plus insects such as crickets and mealworms. They get fat easily, and a fat hedgehog cannot roll well and is harder to warm evenly. Fresh water every day. Handling goes better in the evening, with a patient person and a towel. Daytime handling of a sleeping hedgehog produces a ball of spines and a fair complaint.

Wobbling, legality, vets

Wobbly hedgehog syndrome is a progressive paralysis. There is no cure, and a hedgehog that trips, falls to one side, or cannot close its body needs a vet who knows the species so you are not guessing between that, an injury, and a middle-ear infection. Mites and dental disease are the more fixable reasons for a visit. California and Hawaii are not places to keep this animal, and some other jurisdictions agree. Look it up locally.

The VCA exotic pet pages and a clinic found through ARAV are the practical medical path. A hedgehog that stops eating declines quickly; use the not-eating guide and the checker. Setup often costs $100 to $250, then insects and a heat bill. They suit a night-owl adult who has checked the law. They do not suit a child's daytime petting zoo.

Hedgehog nights and laws

Are hedgehogs legal?

Not in every state. California and Hawaii restrict or ban them, and some cities do too. Check state and city rules before you buy, because a seller shipping across state lines does not make possession legal.

What is anointing?

A hedgehog meets a new smell and froths saliva onto its spines. It looks like a seizure and is normal. Actual wobbling, falling, or paralysis is not anointing and needs a vet.

How warm should the cage be?

About 72 to 80°F. A cold hedgehog goes into a dangerous torpor that looks like sleep and can kill. Use a thermostat-controlled heat source they can move away from.

Can they live with another hedgehog?

No. They are solitary. Housing two together produces injuries, not a friendship.
