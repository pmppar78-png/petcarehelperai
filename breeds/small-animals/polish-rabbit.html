Polish Rabbit Overview

The Polish Rabbit is a small (2.5-3.5 lbs) rabbit that makes an excellent pet for beginners and families.

Polish Rabbits thrive with companionship and are best kept with compatible cage mates.

The Polish rabbit on this page is the small, short-faced US breed. It is not a Polish chicken, and it is not a person. Size is small, 2.5-3.5 lbs. . People search the name and land on poultry or on a demonym. This card is a rabbit, a compact domestic breed with a short face and upright ears. It is not a lop, and it is not a large white meat breed. The 2.5-3.5 lb body is the one the rest of the card is about.

Care level is beginner, activity is moderate, and social needs are social. Beginner does not mean a rabbit that can share a room with a dog. A Polish is easy to injure if a dog shares the room. Four pounds of rabbit, at the top of the 2.5-3.5 lb range, is prey-sized to a dog that has never hurt a pet before. Curiosity is enough. A paw, a grab, or a shake ends a 5-6 year lifespan in a second. The pen has to be secure, with a lid or a height the dog cannot reach into, in a room the dog does not enter unsupervised.
