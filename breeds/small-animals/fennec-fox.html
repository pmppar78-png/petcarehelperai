Daily Care Routine for Fennec Foxs

Establishing a consistent daily care routine ensures your Fennec Fox stays healthy and thriving. Begin each day by checking food and water supplies, removing soiled bedding from high-traffic areas, and spending a few minutes observing your pet for any changes in behavior, appetite, or appearance. Monthly tasks include thorough equipment checks, weight monitoring, and nail trimming if applicable. Keeping a care log helps you track patterns and provides useful information for veterinary visits.
