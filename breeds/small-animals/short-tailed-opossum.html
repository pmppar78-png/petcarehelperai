Daily Care Routine for Short-Tailed Opossums

Establishing a consistent daily care routine ensures your Short-Tailed Opossum stays healthy and thriving. Begin each day by checking food and water supplies, removing soiled bedding from high-traffic areas, and spending a few minutes observing your pet for any changes in behavior, appetite, or appearance. Ensure habitat conditions (temperature, humidity) are within appropriate ranges.

Weekly deep cleaning of the habitat, including full bedding changes and surface disinfection with pet-safe cleaners, prevents bacterial buildup and respiratory issues. Monthly tasks include thorough equipment checks, weight monitoring, and nail trimming if applicable. Keeping a care log helps you track patterns and provides useful information for veterinary visits.
