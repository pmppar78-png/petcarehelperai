A decade, if the tank is actually tropical

Land hermit crabs are often sold as a few dollars of beach souvenir. The honest setup is a tank with deep substrate for molting, heat, humidity high enough that the gills work, a pool of dechlorinated fresh water, and a pool of marine salt water. Both pools. They are not fish and they are not a desert animal that lives on a dry sponge.

Painted shells can be toxic and too small. Offer natural shells. Food is a mix of plants, protein, and calcium, not a single pellet forever. They need darkness and quiet while they molt, which can take weeks.

Molts, shells, and a dead crab that looked asleep

A crab out of its shell is in crisis. A crab that smells of decay is already dead. Do not rinse them in tap water for fun. ASPCA exotic-pet cautions apply. There is no honest version of this animal as a pocket pet for a weekend.

Hermit crab questions

How long do hermit crabs live?

The long end is 10 years and more, sometimes decades. Most pet crabs die in months from a dry tank, tap water, or a shell glued shut. The lifespan is a husbandry score.

Do they need friends?

They are social. A group in a large tank does better than one crab in a souvenir cage. They also fight over shells. Extra empty shells in several sizes are equipment, not clutter.

What if my crab buries itself?

It may be molting. Do not dig it up. A molt is when they are soft and easily killed by a helpful owner. Isolate a molting crab only if the others are chewing on it.
