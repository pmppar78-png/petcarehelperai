A small rabbit with a wool wig

Lionheads weigh about 2.5 to 3.75 pounds and often live 7 to 10 years. Hay is the diet. Pellets are a measured handful. Vegetables are a side. Fruit is a rare treat. The mane does not change that. A rabbit fed mostly pellets and carrots is a dental and a stasis case in progress.

They need room to hop, a litter box, and a floor that is not wire. Cages sold as "rabbit starter kits" are usually a crate you would not leave a cat in. Exercise for several hours a day is the housing plan, or a pen that is already that large.

Teeth, guts, and a mat

Cheek teeth overgrow and the rabbit drools. The gut stops when pain or a bad diet slows it. No droppings and no interest in hay is an emergency the same day. ASPCA rabbit notes are worth reading before the first missed meal. The not-eating page applies the hour you notice.

Lionhead rabbit questions

How often do you groom a lionhead?

Several times a week, and daily when the mane is shedding. Mats pull on skin. A heavily matted rabbit may need a vet to clip the coat, because rabbit skin tears under household clippers.

Can a lionhead live alone?

Rabbits are social. A bonded pair is the better life if you can neuter and introduce them properly. A single rabbit with a person who actually sits on the floor can work. A single rabbit in a hutch cannot.

What is GI stasis?

The gut slows and the rabbit stops eating and producing droppings. It is painful and it kills. Syringe-feeding and a vet the same day are the response, not a wait to see if the rabbit 'perks up.'
