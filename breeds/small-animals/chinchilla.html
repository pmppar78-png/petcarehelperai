Chinchillas are crepuscular rodents from the dry, cool Andes, with fur so dense that water is the wrong cleaning method. Adults weigh about 1 to 1.5 pounds and often live 15 to 20 years. Activity is high when they are awake, which is evening and night. Care level is intermediate to advanced mostly because of heat, chewing, and the length of the commitment.

Cool room, tall cage, hay

Use a multi-level cage at least about 3 by 2 by 2 feet, with solid shelves and no wire they can catch a leg in. Keep ambient temperatures preferably in the 60s, and treat anything sustained over 75°F as an emergency risk. A tile in the cage gives a cool belly rest. Air conditioning is a husbandry tool in warm climates.

Feed unlimited grass hay and a measured chinchilla pellet. Treats are a rare raisin or a small piece of dried herb, not a yogurt drop. Teeth grow continuously. Hay and safe wood to chew are how you avoid a dental disaster. A dust bath of proper chinchilla dust, offered several times a week for a short period, replaces bathing. Water baths mat the coat.

Night noise and vet bills

They jump, bark, and chew. A bedroom is a poor location. Cords, baseboards, and wooden furniture are all fair game during out-of-cage time, which should be supervised. Same-sex pairs can work. Two strangers shoved together often do not.

Dental disease, heatstroke, and gastrointestinal slowdown are the big three. A chinchilla that stops eating or produces tiny, scant droppings needs a vet the same day. The VCA chinchilla material is a clear primer, and the not-eating page matches the urgency. The checker will not cool a hot room for you. Setup is often $200 to $500 for a safe cage, then pellets and hay for up to two decades. Chinchillas suit a cool house and a person who likes a shy, fast animal at dusk. They are a bad pet for a warm apartment and a child who wants to cuddle something every afternoon.

Chinchilla heat and dust

Can chinchillas get wet?

Avoid water baths. Their dense coat takes forever to dry and stays damp against the skin. Offer a dust bath several times a week and remove it so they do not overuse it.

What temperature is too hot?

Keep the room under about 75°F, cooler if you can. Heatstroke kills chinchillas. An air-conditioned room is equipment, not a comfort.

How long do they live?

Often 15 to 20 years. A chinchilla is a youth's pet and a midlife pet if you are the same person.

Do they need a friend?

They can live singly if you are present, and same-sex pairs sometimes work when introduced carefully. A bad pairing is a fight you cannot undo by adding a second dust bath.
