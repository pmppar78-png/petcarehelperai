Domestic ferrets are polecat descendants that ended up as household carnivores: long, low, and faster than the furniture layout suggests. They weigh about 1.5 to 4 pounds and often live 6 to 10 years. Social needs are high. Care level is intermediate to advanced. The cage is a bedroom. The house, for a few hours a day, is the actual enclosure.

Proof the room, then open the door

A multi-level cage with solid floors and bedding they cannot swallow is the start. Recliner chairs, rubber soles, and gaps under appliances are the injuries. They cache food, steal small objects, and bite in play as kits. A kit nip that is answered with a time-out and a toy fades. A kit that was never handled stays a handful. Most ferrets do better with another ferret than alone, once introductions are supervised.

Feed a ferret-specific food with animal protein at the top of the list. Water in a heavy bowl and a bottle. Litter boxes work if you put them where the ferret already backs into a corner, and you will still clean up misses. The smell is real and smaller than the rumor if the ears, the bedding, and the box stay on a schedule. Bathing often makes the smell worse.

The diseases of middle age

In the United States, adrenal gland disease and insulinoma show up so often that a ferret fund should exist by age three. Hair loss, itchiness, a swollen vulva, or a male that starts smelling stronger can be adrenal disease. Staring spells, drooling, and weakness can be low blood sugar from an insulinoma. Neither is a wait-and-see internet fix. Find a vet who sees ferrets before you need one after hours. The Merck Veterinary Manual has the clinical summaries.

California and Hawaii do not allow pet ferrets. Other places require vaccines against canine distemper and rabies, using products appropriate for ferrets. Routine yearly cost is several hundred dollars before those diseases. A ferret that skips a meal is an emergency relative to a dog that skips one; use the not-eating guide and the checker. This animal suits a home that wants a busy, ridiculous carnivore and has checked the law. It does not suit a cage in a child's room that stays shut all evening.

Ferret time, diet, and legality

Are ferrets legal in my state?

Not everywhere. California and Hawaii prohibit pet ferrets, and some cities add their own rules. Confirm with your state before you buy an animal you may not be allowed to keep.

What do they eat?

A high-protein, high-fat, low-carbohydrate diet made for ferrets, or a carefully balanced raw or freeze-dried carnivore diet. Dog food and sugary treats are a poor match for an obligate carnivore.

How much work is the daily routine?

They sleep about 14 to 18 hours and then they are intensely awake. That awake block needs a ferret-proofed room, not only a cage. They will find the one gap behind the fridge.

What diseases should you budget for?

Adrenal disease and insulinoma are common in middle-aged and older ferrets in the United States. Both need a vet who sees ferrets, and both cost more than the adoption fee.
