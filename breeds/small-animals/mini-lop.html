Recommended for Mini Lop Rabbits

Floppy ears, ordinary rabbit rules

Mini lops weigh about 4.5 to 6.5 pounds. A well-kept one can live anywhere from 7 years into the teens. Hay all day, a handful of pellets, and greens are the menu. They are social. A bonded, neutered pair is easier than a lonely rabbit that chews the baseboard because nobody came home.

The lop is cute and it hides ear disease. Check under the flap. Teeth still meet badly in this breed, the way they do in many small rabbits. A wet chin is dental pain until a vet says otherwise.

Stasis does not wait for Monday

A mini lop that hunches, grinds its teeth, or leaves the hay untouched needs pain control and a clinic, not a new toy. Droppings that shrink to nothing are the same emergency. VCA explains gastrointestinal stasis. Use the appetite page on the way.

Mini lop questions

Why do lop ears get infected?

They fold and they stay damp. A smell, a head shake, or a rabbit that resents a touch on the ear needs a vet. Do not pour vinegar or leftover dog ear cleaner down a lop ear.

Are they calmer than other rabbits?

Some are. The ear shape does not guarantee a lap rabbit. Sit on the floor and let the rabbit choose. Picking one up whenever you want a cuddle makes a kicker.

How big is the enclosure?

A pen of at least 4 by 2 feet is a start, plus hours out of it. A hutch in the yard is not a life, and outdoor rabbits meet heat, flies, and predators.
