Recommended for Rabbits

Size and daily care

Mini lops weigh about 4.5 to 6.5 pounds. A well-kept one can live anywhere from 7 years into the teens. Hay all day, a handful of pellets, and greens are the menu. They are social. A bonded, neutered pair is easier than a lonely rabbit that chews the baseboard because nobody came home.

The lop is cute and it hides ear disease. Check under the flap. Teeth still meet badly in this breed, the way they do in many small rabbits. A wet chin is dental pain until a vet says otherwise.

Gastrointestinal stasis

A mini lop that hunches, grinds its teeth, or leaves the hay untouched needs pain control and a clinic the same day. Droppings that shrink to nothing are the same emergency. VCA explains gastrointestinal stasis. Use the appetite page on the way.

Breed origin

The American Rabbit Breeders Association lists the Mini Lop at a maximum of 6.5 pounds and dates recognition to 1980. The rabbits Bob Herschbach showed in the 1970s were the German Klein Widder. Herb Dyck and other breeders pushed the name and the type until ARBA accepted them. A British "mini lop" is a different rabbit. If you are reading a UK care sheet, check the weight before you copy the pen size. The 4.5 to 6.5 pound range on this page is the US breed. A Holland Lop is a smaller breed, with its own weight standard.

Housing

The questions below already set a pen of at least 4 by 2 feet, with hours outside it, and they already reject a yard hutch. The House Rabbit Society housing page goes further: a resting space several times the stretched-out length of the rabbit, and a play area on the order of a 4 by 4 foot exercise pen, for several hours a day, in a room where the household actually lives. Wire floors cause sore hocks. Give a solid floor or a resting board, a litter box big enough to lie in, and a hide with two exits so a rabbit is never cornered. Indoor temperatures that suit you usually suit them. Heat near and above 80°F does not, because rabbits do not cool themselves by panting the way a dog does. Outdoor rabbits meet flies, predators, and that heat. A yard hutch fails once the temperature nears 80°F.

Diet

Grass hay is the main food. A Mini Lop in this weight class should have timothy or another grass hay in front of it all day. Alfalfa is for growing youngsters and for a doe the veterinarian has put on a higher-calcium plan. A sedentary adult eats grass hay. Plain pellets, about a quarter cup a day for a rabbit around this size, keep the calories honest. A packed cup or two of leafy greens (romaine, cilantro, herbs, a little carrot top) rides along. Fruit and carrot chunks are teaspoon treats. A water bowl is easier to drink from than a bottle, and it is also easier to soil, so it gets washed daily. Droppings that shrink, a rabbit that will not touch the hay, or a hunch with tooth grinding is gastrointestinal stasis. VCA's explanation is already linked above. Pain control and a clinic are the response. A toy will not restart the gut.

Common health problems

Folded ears stay warm and damp. A smell, head shaking, or a rabbit that ducks when you lift the flap needs a veterinarian who owns an otoscope. Vinegar and leftover dog ear cleaner do not belong in a lop ear, which the questions already say. The same small-rabbit jaw that looks cute grows molars that spike into the tongue. A wet chin, dropping food, or a preference for soft greens over hay is dental pain until the mouth is examined under the light, often with sedation the clinic chooses. Do not buy a molar rasp for the kitchen.

Unspayed does develop uterine disease often enough that exotic clinics recommend a spay rather than waiting for blood in the urine. Ask about timing before the first birthday. Head tilt can be an inner-ear infection or Encephalitozoon cuniculi. Either one is a veterinary diagnosis. Do not try a livestock dewormer at home. Flystrike, maggots on a soiled rear, is an emergency the same day, which is another reason the rabbit lives indoors where you see the hindquarters.

Grooming and handling

Trim nails before they curl and catch in carpet. A slick table and a towel burrito beat a chase around the pen. The scent glands beside the groin and the soft feces they eat at night are normal. A matted rear is not. Comb it and fix the diet before flies find it. Sit on the floor and let the rabbit climb into a lap. Lifting whenever a person wants a cuddle teaches kicking, and a kick from a 6-pound rabbit can break its own back if the hind end dangles. Support the chest and the pelvis or do not pick the rabbit up.

Enrichment

Cardboard boxes, a dig box of shredded paper, willow balls, and a pile of hay they can waste are the enrichment. Plastic baby toys lose to incisors. A rabbit with only a pen and a food dish will take the baseboard, the rug, and the charging cable. Give the legal chew so the illegal one is less interesting. A bonded, neutered partner is social enrichment. Two strangers dropped in a pen is a fight. Bonding is a slow process on neutral ground, and it fails often enough that you want a second pen ready.

Costs in October

US planning figures for October 2026. An exercise pen and a covered litter setup often land between $60 and $150. Hay is the monthly bill that surprises people: about $20 to $40 if you buy it by the box rather than by the decorative handful. Pellets and greens add roughly $15 to $25. The adoption fee may be $40 to $90. A spay or neuter at an exotic clinic is often $200 to $600, and an exam alone is commonly $80 to $200. Insurance for rabbits exists and often excludes dental and pre-existing ear disease, so read the policy before you count on it. The cheap rabbit from a parking-lot sale becomes the expensive rabbit when the molars are done.

Legal status

California's restricted-species rule for lagomorphs exempts domesticated races of rabbits. A Mini Lop is that domestic animal. A cottontail from the yard is not, and keeping native wildlife is a separate offense. Cities and landlords still ban outdoor hutches, breeding, or rabbits entirely. Hawaii's plant-quarantine list is aimed at other species, and a move there still deserves a call to the Department of Agriculture before the crate is booked. ARBA's breed page is arba.net/12472. It tells you type and weight. It does not tell you your city's zoning.

Suitability

The care level on the table says beginner. That holds for a person who will feed hay, litter-train, and sit on the floor. It does not hold for a household that wants a cage pet visited on weekends. Children can offer greens. Children who chase or squeeze will produce a rabbit that lives under the couch. Dogs and cats can share a house with a rabbit only when the rabbit has a secure room and the other animals are trained to leave it. A terrier that is curious about a rabbit is close to a bite. Two rabbits are kinder than one if you can bond them. One rabbit with a human who is actually home is kinder than a lonely rabbit in a shut bedroom.

Before you buy

Ask the adult weight of the parents so you are not buying a French Lop mix under a Mini Lop name. Watch the rabbit eat hay. A wet chin, a head tilt, sneezing, or dirty front paws from eye discharge are reasons to leave. Ask whether it is neutered, which sex it is, and whether it has lived with another rabbit. Feel the belly only if the seller is comfortable, and decline a rabbit that is thin over the spine. Have the pen built and the hay in the house before the ride home. A rabbit that sits in a cardboard carrier for three days while you buy a cage is already behind on gut motility.

Mini lop questions

Why do lop ears get infected?

They fold and they stay damp. A smell, a head shake, or a rabbit that resents a touch on the ear needs a vet. Do not pour vinegar or leftover dog ear cleaner down a lop ear.

Are they calmer than other rabbits?

Some are. The ear shape does not guarantee a lap rabbit. Sit on the floor and let the rabbit choose. Picking one up whenever you want a cuddle makes a kicker.

How big is the enclosure?

A pen of at least 4 by 2 feet is a start, plus hours out of it. A yard hutch exposes the rabbit to heat, flies, and predators.

How is a US Mini Lop different from a Holland Lop?

The ARBA Mini Lop maxes out at 6.5 pounds and was accepted in 1980 from German Klein Widder stock. A Holland Lop is a smaller breed. A British rabbit sold as a mini lop is a different breed from the ARBA Mini Lop.

How much hay and pellets does a Mini Lop eat?

Grass hay stays in the rack all day. Plain pellets are about a quarter cup a day for a rabbit in the 4.5 to 6.5 pound range, plus a cup or two of leafy greens. Save alfalfa hay for growing youngsters.

What does a Mini Lop cost each month?

As of October 2026, hay often runs $20 to $40 a month, pellets and greens about $15 to $25, and an exotic exam $80 to $200 before tests. A spay or neuter is commonly $200 to $600. The purchase price is the small line on the budget.
