Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) Overview

The Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) is a large (4-5 in) amphibian originating from South America (Argentina, Paraguay, Bolivia). Known for being aggressive, vocal, unique appearance, this species has captivated hobbyists and herpetology enthusiasts around the world. With a lifespan of 15-20 years, the Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) represents a significant long-term commitment that prospective owners should consider carefully before acquiring.

As a intermediate-level species, the Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) is well-suited for keepers who have some experience with amphibians or are willing to invest time in learning proper husbandry techniques. Their natural habitat is aquatic to semi-aquatic, which informs the type of enclosure setup required for optimal health and wellbeing in captivity.

Bizarre flattened appearance with enormous mouth; known for loud screaming vocalizations when threatened; will bite when handled; resembles a living pancake

Temperament & Behavior

The Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) is characterized as aggressive, vocal, unique appearance. Understanding their natural behaviors is essential for providing appropriate care and setting realistic expectations as an owner.

  • Activity pattern: Active at various times, often becoming more animated during feeding and during dawn/dusk periods.
  • Handling tolerance: As an aquatic species, handling should be minimized. When necessary, use wet hands or aquarium-safe nets. Amphibian skin absorbs chemicals from human hands.
  • Social behavior: Can be housed individually or in small groups with adequate space and resources for each animal.
  • Feeding response: Typically accepts food readily once established. New arrivals may need a few days to settle in before feeding.

Size & Physical Characteristics

Adult Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s reach large (4-5 in), though size can vary based on sex, diet quality, and genetic lineage. Their physical appearance reflects adaptations to their natural habitat and lifestyle.

Juveniles are typically smaller and may display different coloration or patterns than adults. Growth rate depends on feeding frequency, temperature, and overall care quality. Expect most growth to occur during the first 1-2 years of life, with growth slowing significantly after reaching adult size.

Lifespan & Longevity

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s typically live 15-20 years in captivity when provided with proper care. Key factors influencing lifespan include:

  • Water/habitat quality: The single most important factor. Clean, properly conditioned water and appropriate humidity prevent the majority of health problems that shorten amphibian lives.
  • Temperature stability: Maintaining 75-82°F consistently, without rapid fluctuations, reduces stress and supports immune function.
  • Diet quality: A varied diet with proper supplementation (calcium and vitamins) supports long-term health. Gut-loading feeder insects dramatically improves nutritional value.
  • Stress reduction: Proper enclosure design with adequate hiding spots, appropriate lighting, and minimal disturbance promotes longevity.
  • Preventive care: Quarantining new animals, maintaining clean conditions, and monitoring for early signs of illness all contribute to maximum lifespan.

Diet & Nutrition

Proper nutrition is foundational to Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) health. Their primary diet consists of earthworms, fish, shrimp, roaches.

Feeding Guidelines

  • Primary foods: Earthworms, fish, shrimp, roaches. Offer variety to ensure complete nutrition.
  • Feeding frequency: Feed every 2-3 days for juveniles, 2-3 times weekly for adults. Adjust based on body condition.
  • Portion size: Offer only what can be consumed in 10-15 minutes. Remove uneaten food to prevent water contamination.
  • Supplementation: Aquatic amphibians typically get adequate vitamins from a varied diet of quality foods. Occasionally supplement with vitamin-enriched foods.
  • Hydration: Aquatic species obtain water directly from their environment. Maintain clean, dechlorinated water at all times.

Feeding Safety

Never feed wild-caught insects, as they may carry pesticides or parasites. Always use captive-bred feeder insects from reputable suppliers. Avoid feeding insects larger than the width between your Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)'s eyes to prevent choking and impaction.

Housing & Habitat Requirements

Enclosure Size & Type

  • Minimum size: 20+ gallon shallow aquatic
  • Recommended: Larger is always better. More space provides greater environmental stability and allows for more natural behaviors.
  • Type: Glass aquarium with secure mesh or solid lid. Fully aquatic species need appropriate water depth and filtration.
  • Security: Amphibians are surprisingly good escape artists. Ensure all openings are secured with no gaps larger than the animal's head.

Environmental Parameters

  • Temperature: 75-82°F. Use a reliable digital thermometer to monitor. A low-wattage heat mat on a thermostat can maintain appropriate temperatures. Avoid heat lamps that can dehydrate amphibians.
  • Humidity: Aquatic/Semi-aquatic. Maintain clean, dechlorinated water. Use a quality filter and perform regular water changes (20-30% weekly).
  • Lighting: Standard aquarium lighting on a 12-hour cycle. Avoid direct sunlight which can overheat the water.
  • Substrate: Large smooth river rocks, bare bottom, or fine sand. Avoid gravel small enough to be ingested.
  • Decor: Smooth rocks, driftwood, live or silk plants, and PVC pipe hides. Avoid sharp decorations that can damage delicate skin.

When to See an Exotic Vet Immediately

Seek veterinary care if your Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) shows: lethargy lasting more than 48 hours, refusal to eat for more than one week, visible skin lesions or discoloration, abnormal swelling or bloating, difficulty breathing or gasping, loss of coordination, or rapid weight loss. Amphibians hide illness instinctively, so visible symptoms often indicate the condition has progressed significantly.

Grooming & Maintenance

  • Enclosure cleaning: Perform 20-30% water changes weekly using dechlorinated water at the same temperature. Clean filter media monthly (rinse in old tank water, never tap water). Remove waste and uneaten food daily.
  • Water quality: Test water parameters weekly: ammonia and nitrite should always be 0 ppm, nitrate under 20 ppm. Use a quality dechlorinator for all water additions.
  • Shedding: Amphibians shed their skin regularly (often eating the shed skin). Ensure proper humidity to facilitate clean shedding. Retained shed can indicate dehydration or skin infection.

Exercise & Enrichment

  • Natural behaviors: Provide an environment that allows swimming freely, exploring tank furnishings, and interacting with water current.
  • Feeding enrichment: Rather than always placing food in the same spot, vary feeding locations to encourage natural foraging and hunting behaviors.
  • Environmental complexity: A well-furnished enclosure with multiple hides, climbing surfaces, and varied terrain provides ongoing mental stimulation.
  • Live plants: Live aquatic plants improve water quality, provide shelter, and create a more natural, stimulating environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s live?

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s typically live 15-20 years in captivity with proper care. Lifespan depends heavily on water quality, temperature stability, diet variety, and stress levels. Providing optimal husbandry conditions is the most important factor in achieving maximum lifespan. Regular health monitoring and prompt treatment of illness also contribute significantly to longevity.

What do Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s eat?

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s eat earthworms, fish, shrimp, roaches. Variety is essential for balanced nutrition. Most amphibians benefit from gut-loaded insects (fed nutritious food 24 hours before offering to your pet) and calcium/vitamin D3 dusting on feeder items at every other feeding for juveniles and twice weekly for adults. Overfeeding is a common problem that leads to obesity and related health issues.

Are Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s good pets for beginners?

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s are rated as intermediate-level pets. They require some prior experience with amphibian keeping or a willingness to research thoroughly before acquiring one. Understanding water chemistry, temperature control, and amphibian health signs is important.

What size enclosure does a Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog) need?

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s require a minimum of 20+ gallon shallow aquatic. Larger enclosures are always better as they provide more stable environmental conditions and allow for more natural behaviors. Water depth, filtration capacity, and surface area for gas exchange are more important than raw volume. A quality filter rated for at least twice your tank size is recommended.

What temperature do Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s need?

Budgett's Frog (Hippo Frog)s require temperatures of 75-82°F. A reliable thermometer (digital probe type recommended) should be used to monitor temperatures. Avoid placing enclosures near windows, heating vents, or other sources of temperature fluctuation. Consistent temperatures within the recommended range are more important than hitting exact numbers.
