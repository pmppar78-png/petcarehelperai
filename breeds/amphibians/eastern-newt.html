Two animals, one life

Eastern newts, Notophthalmus viridescens, are the common newts of eastern North America, and they do not stay one shape. The juvenile eft is bright red, granular, and terrestrial. Years later it returns to ponds as an olive adult with red spots and a paddle tail. A keeper who buys "a newt" without asking which stage will build the wrong tank. Adults want a semi-aquatic 10-gallon. Efts want land, hiding spots, and moisture, not a swimming pool they cannot climb out of.

Adults are about 3 to 5 inches including the tail. The temperature cell says 60 to 70°F. The lifespan cell says 12 to 15 years, which is a long captive adult, not a guarantee for an eft scooped out of a ditch. Care level is intermediate because of that life cycle and because the skin is toxic.

Leave the pond alone

Bloodworms, brine shrimp, and small worms feed aquatic adults. Efts take tiny invertebrates on land. Do not collect either stage from a park pond. Eastern newts can carry amphibian diseases, many states protect native amphibians, and a wild eft does not owe you a decade in a plastic box.

The red color is aposematic. Toxin in the skin is real. Wash after contact, keep them away from mouths, and do not house them with other species.

Fuzzy skin is not a molt you wait out

White fluff, a newt that cannot dive, or an eft that dries into a corner needs help the same week, not after a forum thread. ARAV lists clinics. Use the checker if you are unsure it is urgent, and the not-eating page if the worms sit untouched. The quick facts put the animal at about $5 to $15 and the setup at $75 to $150. That is for a captive animal whose stage you actually know.

Eastern newt questions

Why is my eastern newt bright red and living on land?

That is the eft, the juvenile stage. Efts wander damp woods for years, then turn olive, grow a finned tail, and go back to water as adults.

How long do eastern newts live?

The quick facts list 12 to 15 years. That is the long end for a captive adult. Wild efts often never get that far, and a wild-caught animal should not be your plan.

Are eastern newts poisonous to touch?

They carry tetrodotoxin in the skin, especially the red eft. Handling is for moving the animal, then you wash. They are not a children's handheld pet.

What do adult eastern newts eat?

Bloodworms, brine shrimp, and small worms, in cool water around 60 to 70°F. Efts need tiny land prey and a damp terrestrial setup instead.
