The southeastern climber, not the Australian one

American green tree frogs are Dryophytes cinereus, the smooth green frogs with a pale stripe along the jaw, native to the southeastern United States. People confuse them with White's tree frogs because both get called "green tree frog" in a shop. White's frogs are the chunky Australian dumpies, twice the bulk, with a much longer life. If the frog in front of you is delicate, bright green, and the size of a thumb, you are on the right page.

The quick facts list 6 to 10 years, 72 to 80°F, humidity 50 to 70 percent, and a tall 18 by 18 by 24 inch vivarium. They are nocturnal and vocal. . That means the husbandry is straightforward, not that the frog enjoys being passed around a party.

Night insects and a water dish

Crickets, small moths, and fruit flies, calcium-dusted, are the diet. Feed in the evening when they are awake. A frog that is asleep on the glass at noon is not sick. A frog that never hunts after dark might be. Give vertical bark and plants, a bowl big enough to soak, and misting that dries back rather than a constant drip wall. Too much standing water on the skin is a different problem from drought.

Males call with a repeated nasal note. One frog is charming. Three males in a bedroom are a negotiation with whoever else lives there.

Captive-bred, and what to watch

Buy captive-bred. American green tree frogs are common in the wild and still a poor collecting project. Sticky, discolored skin or a climber that sits on the floor for days needs a vet. Find one via ARAV. The not-eating guide fits a frog that ignores crickets, and the checker sorts whether that wait can be overnight. The animal is often $5 to $15. The tall setup is about $100 to $250.

American green tree frog questions

Is this the same frog as White's tree frog?

No. American green tree frogs stay about 1.5 to 2.5 inches and often live 6 to 10 years. White's tree frogs are larger, calmer, and commonly live into the high teens.

Why does my green tree frog yell at night?

Males call. A loud, nasal quonk is normal after lights-out, especially in a humid room. It is the reason some people move the tank out of the bedroom.

What setup do they need?

A tall vivarium around 18 by 18 by 24 inches, 72 to 80°F, and humidity near 50 to 70 percent, with branches and a water dish. They are climbers, not burrowers.

Are American green tree frogs beginner pets?

The quick facts say beginner, and that is fair for a captive-bred frog if you can tolerate the calling and you feed small insects. They are a weak choice if you want an animal to hold.
