The Flame Hawkfish is described as semi-aggressive, perching predator and is considered reef safe with caution monitor closely in reef tanks as individual specimens may develop coral-nipping behavior.

  • Compatible tankmates: Semi-aggressive community fish of similar size. Introduce simultaneously when possible to reduce territorial aggression.
  • Stocking density: Marine fish require more space per individual than freshwater fish.

Hawkfish do not have a swim bladder that lets them hover the way a wrasse does. They perch, then launch. Give this one a stable rock pile with ledges at several heights, not a bare sand field.
