Green Chromis Overview

The Green Chromis is a small (3 in) saltwater species originating from the Indo-Pacific. Known for being peaceful, schooling, this species is rated beginner care level, making it an excellent choice for aquarists new to saltwater keeping. With a lifespan of 8-15 years, this is a rewarding species that can be enjoyed for many years with proper care.

Temperament & Compatibility

The Green Chromis is described as peaceful, schooling and is considered reef safe, making it compatible with coral-dominated aquariums.

  • Compatible tankmates: Other peaceful community fish of similar size. Avoid keeping with aggressive or highly territorial species.
  • Stocking density: Marine fish require more space per individual than freshwater fish.

Thirty gallons can hold a modest school of 3-inch fish if the tank is long and the filtration is ahead of the bioload. It cannot hold a school plus a pile of other fish and still look like beginner care. They are peaceful toward other species and less peaceful toward a stray chromis added months later. Buy the group once. Replacing them one at a time is how you get a veteran that harasses every newcomer until the school never reforms.

Chromis viridis is kept at 72 to 82°F and a specific gravity of 1.020 to 1.025. pH should sit between 8.1 and 8.4 above the rock. Keep nitrate under 10 ppm if corals share the water and under 20 ppm if the tank is fish-only. Ammonia and nitrite at 0 ppm are the cycling lesson this fish is often used to skip and should not be. Alkalinity at 8 to 12 dKH, calcium at 380 to 450 ppm, and magnesium at 1250 to 1350 ppm matter when the school is in a reef, which is a fair place for them because they are reef safe and they swim above the corals rather than eating them.

The 8-to-15-year lifespan is the surprise in a fish that costs less than a meal. It only shows up if the school stays intact and the tank stays mature. Feed them in the open water so every fish eats, not only the bold one at the front. Tank Specifications

  • Minimum size: 30 gallon. Larger is strongly recommended for parameter stability.
  • Sump systems recommended for tanks over 50 gallons.
  • Water flow: Moderate to strong flow using powerheads or wavemakers. Provide areas of both higher and lower flow within the tank.
  • Lighting: Full-spectrum reef lighting if keeping with corals. LED systems offer energy efficiency and controllable spectrum.
  • Aquascaping: Live rock providing caves, overhangs, and swim-through passages. Ensure open swimming space for active species.

Diet & Nutrition

The Green Chromis is classified as omnivore. Proper nutrition is essential for maintaining health, coloration, and immune function.

  • Primary diet: Omnivore: flakes, pellets, mysis, brine shrimp.
  • Feeding frequency: 2-3 small feedings per day. Small frequent meals are better than one large feeding.
  • Variety: Rotate between different food types to ensure complete nutrition. Offer a mix of frozen foods, pellets, and algae-based foods.
  • Avoid: Overfeeding is the most common feeding mistake.

Grooming & Maintenance

  • Equipment maintenance: Clean protein skimmer cup 2-3 times weekly. Replace filter media monthly. Calibrate refractometer regularly.
