Coral Beauty Angelfish Overview

The Coral Beauty Angelfish is a small (4 in) saltwater species originating from the Indo-Pacific. Known for being semi-aggressive, hardy dwarf angel, this species is rated intermediate care level, making it well-suited for hobbyists with some marine aquarium experience.

One of the hardiest dwarf angelfish; beautiful blue and orange coloration; occasional coral nipping makes reef compatibility unpredictable

  • Minimum size: 70 gallon. Larger is strongly recommended for parameter stability.
  • Sump systems recommended for tanks over 50 gallons.
  • Water flow: Moderate to strong flow using powerheads or wavemakers. Provide areas of both higher and lower flow within the tank.

  • Aquascaping: Live rock providing caves, overhangs, and swim-through passages. Ensure open swimming space for active species.
