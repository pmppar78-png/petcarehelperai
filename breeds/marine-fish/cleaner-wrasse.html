Temperament & Compatibility

The Bluestreak Cleaner Wrasse is described as peaceful, obligate cleaner and is considered reef safe, making it compatible with coral-dominated aquariums.

It is not an ich cure. Marine ich is a parasite with a life cycle that a single wrasse cannot break by picking at a few white spots. Buying a cleaner wrasse to treat a sick tank adds a starving fish to a sick tank. Quarantine, the right diagnosis, and a treatment plan that does not depend on another animal are how ich is handled. A utility fish that usually dies is not a tool. Do not recommend one, and do not accept one, as a shortcut around husbandry.

If an experienced system already has a cleaner that is eating frozen foods, the numbers on the card still apply. Specific gravity belongs between 1.020 and 1.025. Keep pH in the 8.1 to 8.4 band if you keep one at all. Nitrate should sit under 10 ppm on a reef and under 20 ppm in a fish-only tank. Alkalinity from 8 to 12 dKH, calcium from 380 to 450 ppm, and magnesium from 1250 to 1350 ppm belong to the reef around it, not to a special wrasse recipe. Ammonia and nitrite at 0 ppm are assumed. The tank is 55 gallons because the fish ranges and advertises, not because it will stay in a nano as a medic.
