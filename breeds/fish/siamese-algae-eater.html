Siamese Algae Eater Overview

The Siamese algae eater is the cyprinid that actually eats black beard algae, and the adult size on this card is 6 inches with a lifespan of about 10 years. A fish that reaches 6 inches needs 20 gallons or more. Temperament is listed as peaceful and the difficulty as beginner. Those words describe Crossocheilus oblongus and Crossocheilus langei, not every slim fish with a black stripe that a shop has labeled algae eater.

Flying foxes and Chinese algae eaters are the look-alikes, and they are not this fish. A Chinese algae eater grows larger, gets more aggressive, and has a different mouth. A flying fox wears a similar stripe and does not do the same job on black beard. On a true Siamese algae eater the dark stripe continues into the tail, and the mouth is not a big sucker parked under the head. They are useful against black beard when they are hungry and the algae is the soft new growth, not a foot-long forest that already took over a neglected tank. They also need a normal diet.
