Housing & Environment Requirements

A minimum tank size of 10+ gallons is recommended for Ember Tetras. Maintain stable water parameters: temperature 72-82°F, pH 6.5-7.5, and regular water changes of 25% weekly. Provide appropriate substrate, hiding places, and peaceful tankmates. A quality filtration system and heater are essential.

Diet & Nutrition

Feed Ember Tetras a varied diet appropriate for their species. Use micro-pellets, crushed flakes, and small frozen foods like baby brine shrimp. Feed small amounts 1-2 times daily, only what can be consumed in 2-3 minutes. Overfeeding leads to water quality issues. Vary the diet to ensure complete nutrition.

Common Health Issues

Common health issues in Ember Tetras include ich (white spot disease), fin rot, bacterial infections, and stress-related conditions. Maintaining excellent water quality is the best prevention. Signs of illness include loss of color, clamped fins, abnormal swimming, visible spots or lesions, loss of appetite, and gasping at the surface. Quarantine new fish for 2-4 weeks before adding to the main tank. Have aquarium salt and broad-spectrum medications on hand for emergencies.

Daily Care Routine for Ember Tetras

Successful Ember Tetra keeping depends on consistent daily observation and maintenance. Each day, check water temperature, observe fish behavior and appetite, ensure equipment (heater, filter, air pump) is functioning properly, and remove any uneaten food or visible debris. Take a few minutes to watch your fish closely - changes in behavior, coloration, or swimming patterns are often the first indicators of stress or disease.

Weekly maintenance should include a 25% water change using dechlorinated water at the correct temperature, glass cleaning, and water parameter testing (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH). Monthly tasks include filter media rinsing (in tank water, never tap water), equipment inspection, and plant trimming if applicable. Keeping a log of water parameters and observations helps identify trends before they become problems.

What size tank do Ember Tetras need?

A Ember Tetras does best with at least 10+ gallons — bigger water volumes give you more stable chemistry, extra swim space, and flexibility when you want to add tankmates. A larger tank also means more room for aquascaping and creates a more impressive display.

What fish are compatible with Ember Tetras?

With their peaceful nano temperament, Ember Tetras are compatible with many community fish of similar size and temperament. Good tankmates include other peaceful species that share similar water parameter requirements. Always research specific compatibility before adding new species to your aquarium.

How often should I feed Ember Tetras?

Feed Ember Tetras 1-2 times daily, offering only what they can consume within 2-3 minutes. Overfeeding is one of the most common mistakes in fishkeeping, leading to water quality issues and health problems. A varied diet of high-quality flakes, pellets, and occasional frozen or live foods provides optimal nutrition. Fasting one day per week can benefit digestive health.

Are Ember Tetras good for beginners?

Yes, Ember Tetras are excellent for beginning fishkeepers. Their hardy nature and tolerance of minor water parameter fluctuations make them forgiving while you learn. Start with a properly cycled tank and maintain regular maintenance habits.
