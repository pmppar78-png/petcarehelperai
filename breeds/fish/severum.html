Severum Cichlid Overview

Severums are large South American cichlids in the genus Heros. Adults reach 8 to 10 inches and live 8 to 10 years. An adult pair starts at 55 gallons, and that is before you add roommates. Temperament is semi-peaceful and the difficulty is intermediate. A juvenile with bars and a round body looks like a community fish. The adult is a deep, heavy cichlid that digs, eats plants, and wants a partner more than it wants a shoal of small tetras.

They are often kept as a pair. A bonded pair in 55 gallons can work if the tank is arranged for them and not for a delicate aquascape. They will uproot stems, shove gravel, and defend a spawning site when they are ready. Semi-peaceful means they are not peacock bass and they are not harmless. Fast, deep-bodied tankmates that can take a shove are safer than angelfish with long fins or a school of small bottom dwellers. Many keepers simply give the pair the tank.

The diet is the plant problem. Severums eat vegetation. A planted showcase becomes salad unless you feed enough greens that the leaves on the stems are less interesting, and even then some species of plant will not survive. Blanched spinach, peas, spirulina pellets, and a cichlid staple with some meaty frozen food match an omnivore that leans green. Feed on a schedule. An underfed severum does not become a polite algae eater. It strips the tank and then gets thin anyway.

The card lists a setup in the broad band of about 100 to 500 dollars or more, fish around 5 to 100 dollars or more each, annual maintenance around 200 to 500 dollars, and food around 50 to 150 dollars a year. The 55-gallon minimum is where that setup money should go. A cheaper 30-gallon tank saves cash and fails the adult size. Intermediate care is filtration and water changes sized for an 8-to-10-inch cichlid that digs in the mulm, plus the judgment to separate a pair that is beating each other up instead of bonding.

They recognize a routine and they will beg at the glass, which makes overfeeding easy. A thick fish in a 55-gallon tank with shredded plants and a neglected filter is the usual failure, not a mysterious incompatibility. Give them length, heavy decor they cannot stack against the heater, and food that includes plants you intended to sacrifice. The 8-to-10-year lifespan fits that kind of tank. It does not fit a juvenile left in a community until the day it swallows a tetra and uproots the last sword.

Temperament & Behavior

Name the fish as a severum when you buy it, and assume it will reach the top of the 8-to-10-inch range. Gold, green, and turquoise forms are still Heros and still need 55 gallons. A pair needs more than the minimum if they are sharing the tank with other large cichlids. Semi-peaceful is a description of a calm pair with space, not a promise that three adults and a school of small fish will sort themselves out.

Housing & Environment Requirements

A minimum tank size of 55+ gallons is recommended for Severum Cichlids. Provide appropriate substrate, hiding places, and peaceful tankmates. A quality filtration system and heater are essential.

Diet & Nutrition

Feed Severum Cichlids a varied diet appropriate for their species. Overfeeding leads to water quality issues. Vary the diet to ensure complete nutrition.

Severum Cichlid outcomes over months and years track the quality of sustained husbandry more than the quality of any individual piece of gear rather than copied from general fish templates.

Common Health Issues

Common health issues in Severum Cichlids include ich (white spot disease), fin rot, bacterial infections, and stress-related conditions. Maintaining excellent water quality is the best prevention. Quarantine new fish for 2-4 weeks before adding to the main tank. Have aquarium salt and broad-spectrum medications on hand for emergencies.

For Severum, prevention is always better than treatment.

Grooming & Maintenance

Remove uneaten food promptly. Trim live plants as needed. Monitor equipment function daily. Replace filter media according to manufacturer recommendations.

Beginner-Friendly Rating: Intermediate

Severum Cichlids are suitable for keepers with some experience.

Before acquiring a Severum Cichlid, research thoroughly and prepare the habitat in advance. Join online communities and forums dedicated to fishkeeping to connect with experienced keepers who can offer guidance specific to Severum Cichlid care.

Socialization & Enrichment

Rearranging decorations periodically provides novelty. For Severum Cichlids specifically, consider their natural habitat when designing your aquascape - this helps encourage natural behaviors and reduces stress.

Feeding enrichment is another important aspect. Live and frozen foods provide hunting stimulation that dry foods cannot. This knowledge helps you detect health issues early and optimize care.

Where to Find Severum Cichlids

Purchase Severum Cichlids from reputable local fish stores (LFS) that maintain clean, well-cared-for aquariums. Online sellers with strong reviews and live arrival guarantees are another option.

Daily Care Routine for Severum Cichlids

Successful Severum Cichlid keeping depends on consistent daily observation and maintenance.

What size tank do Severum Cichlids need?

Size the tank at 55+ gallons or greater for a Severum Cichlids. Bigger volumes stabilise parameters and widen your aquascaping and tankmate options.

What fish are compatible with Severum Cichlids?

With their semi-peaceful temperament, Severum Cichlids are compatible with many community fish of similar size and temperament. Good tankmates include other peaceful species that share similar water parameter requirements. Always research specific compatibility before adding new species to your aquarium.

How often should I feed Severum Cichlids?

Feed Severum Cichlids 1-2 times daily, offering only what they can consume within 2-3 minutes. Fasting one day per week can benefit digestive health.

Are Severum Cichlids good for beginners?

Severum Cichlids are suitable for fishkeepers with some experience.

Long-Term Commitment & Responsible Ownership

Owning Severum Cichlids is a commitment that spans their full lifespan of 8-10 years. Aquariums require daily attention, weekly maintenance, and ongoing expense. Many fishkeeping communities have members willing to help in emergencies.

Plan for equipment upgrades as fish grow and needs change over time.

Is Severum Cichlid Right for Your Aquarium?

Fit between a Severum and a household is usually decided by three questions: Can the environment be sustained at the required stability? Can the daily care cadence be maintained year-round, including during travel and busy periods? Is the household prepared for the typical lifespan and commitment the species requires? Two out of three rarely works; all three needs to be a clear yes.

Severum Cichlid May Be Great For:

  • A cichlid keeper with 55 gallons or more for a Heros severum, and tankmates too big to eat
  • Someone who will let plants be eaten or skip live plants
  • An aquarist who feeds a varied diet and does not add African mbuna

Severum Cichlid May Not Be Ideal For:

  • A community of neon tetras
  • A 20-gallon tank for a juvenile that will become a heavy cichlid
  • Anyone who wants a peaceful angelfish substitute without reading about adult size

The best way to know if a Severum Cichlid is right for you is to spend time around one before committing. Visit a breeder, a rescue, or a fellow keeper. The firsthand experience of care routines, feeding, and habitat maintenance tells you more than any guide can. If it still feels right after that, you are probably ready.
